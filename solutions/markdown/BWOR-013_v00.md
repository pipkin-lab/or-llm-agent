# Operations Research Model: Investment Portfolio Optimization

## 1. Decision Variables

Let the decision variables represent the amount of capital (in thousand yuan) allocated to each product:

*   $x_A$: Amount of fund invested in Product A (in thousand yuan)
*   $x_B$: Amount of fund invested in Product B (in thousand yuan)
*   $x_C$: Amount of fund invested in Product C (in thousand yuan)
*   $x_E$: Amount of fund reserved for emergency use (in thousand yuan)

**Domain:**
$x_A, x_B, x_C, x_E \ge 0$

## 2. Objective Function

The goal is to maximize the total profit generated from the investments in Products A, B, and C. The emergency fund ($x_E$) typically yields a negligible or zero return in this context, so it is excluded from the profit calculation.

**Maximize Total Profit ($Z$):**

$$ Z = 0.05x_A + 0.07x_B + 0.10x_C $$

*(Note: The coefficients represent the ROI rates: 5% = 0.05, 7% = 0.07, 10% = 0.10. The resulting $Z$ is in thousand yuan.)*

## 3. Operational Constraints

### Constraint 1: Minimum Investment in Product A
At least 300 thousand yuan must be invested in Product A.
$$ x_A \ge 300 $$

### Constraint 2: Maximum Allocation per Product (Diversification)
No single product may receive more than 35% of the total fund (1000 thousand yuan).
$$ 35\% \times 1000 = 350 $$

*   For Product A:
    $$ x_A \le 350 $$
*   For Product B:
    $$ x_B \le 350 $$
*   For Product C:
    $$ x_C \le 350 $$

*(Note: This constraint automatically limits the investment in A to the range $[300, 350]$.)*

### Constraint 3: Emergency Reserve
At least 10% of the fund (100 thousand yuan) must be reserved for emergency use.
$$ x_E \ge 100 $$

### Constraint 4: Total Fund Limitation
The total amount invested in products plus the emergency reserve must not exceed the total available fund (1000 thousand yuan). In standard portfolio models where the entire fund is allocated, this is an equality constraint.
$$ x_A + x_B + x_C + x_E = 1000 $$

---

## Final Structured Model

**Problem:** Maximize $Z = 0.05x_A + 0.07x_B + 0.10x_C$

**Subject to:**

1.  **Minimum A:** $x_A \ge 300$
2.  **Max A:** $x_A \le 350$
3.  **Max B:** $x_B \le 350$
4.  **Max C:** $x_C \le 350$
5.  **Min Emergency:** $x_E \ge 100$
6.  **Budget Balance:** $x_A + x_B + x_C + x_E = 1000$
7.  **Non-negativity:** $x_A, x_B, x_C, x_E \ge 0$
