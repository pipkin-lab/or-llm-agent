To formulate the Linear Programming model, we must carefully analyze the data provided in the table, paying close attention to the blank cells which imply specific constraints (or lack thereof) on the composition of the candy brands.

### 1. Analysis of the Problem Data

**Decision Variables:**
Let $x_i$ be the total kilograms of candy brand $i$ produced per month, where $i \in \{X, Y, Z\}$.
Let $a_{ij}$ be the kilograms of raw material $j$ used in brand $i$.

However, it is more standard and efficient to define the decision variables directly as the amount of each raw material used in each brand.
Let $x_{ij}$ be the amount (in kg) of raw material $j$ used in the production of brand $i$ per month.
- $i \in \{X, Y, Z\}$
- $j \in \{A, B, C\}$

The total production of brand $i$ is $X_i = x_{iA} + x_{iB} + x_{iC}$.

**Parameters from Table 1-17:**

*   **Raw Material Costs ($c_j$):**
    *   $c_A = 2.00$
    *   $c_B = 1.50$
    *   $c_C = 1.00$
*   **Monthly Usage Limits ($U_j$):**
    *   $U_A = 2000$
    *   $U_B = 2500$
    *   $U_C = 1200$
*   **Processing Costs ($p_i$):**
    *   $p_X = 0.50$
    *   $p_Y = 0.40$
    *   $p_Z = 0.30$
*   **Selling Prices ($s_i$):**
    *   $s_X = 3.40$
    *   $s_Y = 2.85$
    *   $s_Z = 2.25$

**Composition Constraints (Proportions):**
The table provides percentage constraints for some brands/materials. Blank cells mean there is **no specific constraint** on that material for that brand, other than non-negativity.

*   **Brand X:**
    *   Material A: $\ge 60\%$ of total $X$.
    *   Material B: Blank (No constraint).
    *   Material C: Blank (No constraint).
    *   *Note:* Since A is at least 60%, the sum of B and C is at most 40%.

*   **Brand Y:**
    *   Material A: $\ge 30\%$ of total $Y$.
    *   Material B: Blank (No constraint).
    *   Material C: $\le 50\%$ of total $Y$.

*   **Brand Z:**
    *   Material A: Blank (No constraint).
    *   Material B: Blank (No constraint).
    *   Material C: $\le 60\%$ of total $Z$.

### 2. Objective Function

The goal is to **maximize profit**.
Profit = Total Revenue - Total Raw Material Cost - Total Processing Cost.

*   **Revenue for brand $i$:** $s_i \times (\sum_{j} x_{ij})$
*   **Raw Material Cost for brand $i$:** $\sum_{j} c_j x_{ij}$
*   **Processing Cost for brand $i$:** $p_i \times (\sum_{j} x_{ij})$

Total Profit $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Maximize } Z = \; & \sum_{i \in \{X,Y,Z\}} \left[ s_i \left(\sum_{j} x_{ij}\right) - \sum_{j} c_j x_{ij} - p_i \left(\sum_{j} x_{ij}\right) \right] \\
= \; & \sum_{i \in \{X,Y,Z\}} \sum_{j \in \{A,B,C\}} (s_i - p_i - c_j) x_{ij}
\end{aligned}
$$

Let's calculate the coefficient for each $x_{ij}$: $(s_i - p_i - c_j)$.

**For Brand X ($s_X=3.40, p_X=0.50 \Rightarrow s_X-p_X = 2.90$):**
*   $x_{XA}: 2.90 - 2.00 = 0.90$
*   $x_{XB}: 2.90 - 1.50 = 1.40$
*   $x_{XC}: 2.90 - 1.00 = 1.90$

**For Brand Y ($s_Y=2.85, p_Y=0.40 \Rightarrow s_Y-p_Y = 2.45$):**
*   $x_{YA}: 2.45 - 2.00 = 0.45$
*   $x_{YB}: 2.45 - 1.50 = 0.95$
*   $x_{YC}: 2.45 - 1.00 = 1.45$

**For Brand Z ($s_Z=2.25, p_Z=0.30 \Rightarrow s_Z-p_Z = 1.95$):**
*   $x_{ZA}: 1.95 - 2.00 = -0.05$
*   $x_{ZB}: 1.95 - 1.50 = 0.45$
*   $x_{ZC}: 1.95 - 1.00 = 0.95$

**Objective Function:**
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 0.90x_{XA} + 1.40x_{XB} + 1.90x_{XC} + 0.45x_{YA} + 0.95x_{YB} + 1.45x_{YC} - 0.05x_{ZA} + 0.45x_{ZB} + 0.95x_{ZC} $$

### 3. Constraints

**A. Raw Material Availability Constraints:**
The total usage of each raw material across all brands cannot exceed the monthly limit.

1.  Material A: $x_{XA} + x_{YA} + x_{ZA} \le 2000$
2.  Material B: $x_{XB} + x_{YB} + x_{ZB} \le 2500$
3.  Material C: $x_{XC} + x_{YC} + x_{ZC} \le 1200$

**B. Product Composition Constraints:**
These constraints relate the amount of a specific material to the total production of that brand.
Let $T_X = x_{XA} + x_{XB} + x_{XC}$, $T_Y = x_{YA} + x_{YB} + x_{YC}$, $T_Z = x_{ZA} + x_{ZB} + x_{ZC}$.

*   **Brand X:**
    *   $x_{XA} \ge 0.60 T_X$
    *   $\Rightarrow x_{XA} \ge 0.60(x_{XA} + x_{XB} + x_{XC})$
    *   $\Rightarrow 0.40x_{XA} - 0.60x_{XB} - 0.60x_{XC} \ge 0$
    *   (Or simply $0.4x_{XA} \ge 0.6x_{XB} + 0.6x_{XC}$)

*   **Brand Y:**
    *   $x_{YA} \ge 0.30 T_Y$
        *   $\Rightarrow x_{YA} \ge 0.30(x_{YA} + x_{YB} + x_{YC})$
        *   $\Rightarrow 0.70x_{YA} - 0.30x_{YB} - 0.30x_{YC} \ge 0$
    *   $x_{YC} \le 0.50 T_Y$
        *   $\Rightarrow x_{YC} \le 0.50(x_{YA} + x_{YB} + x_{YC})$
        *   $\Rightarrow -0.50x_{YA} - 0.50x_{YB} + 0.50x_{YC} \le 0$
        *   (Or $x_{YC} \le 0.5x_{YA} + 0.5x_{YB}$)

*   **Brand Z:**
    *   $x_{ZC} \le 0.60 T_Z$
        *   $\Rightarrow x_{ZC} \le 0.60(x_{ZA} + x_{ZB} + x_{ZC})$
        *   $\Rightarrow -0.60x_{ZA} - 0.60x_{ZB} + 0.40x_{ZC} \le 0$
        *   (Or $0.4x_{ZC} \le 0.6x_{ZA} + 0.6x_{ZB}$)

**C. Non-negativity Constraints:**
$x_{ij} \ge 0$ for all $i, j$.

### 4. Final Mathematical Model

**Model Type:** Linear Programming (LP)

**Index Sets:**
*   $I = \{X, Y, Z\}$: Set of candy brands.
*   $J = \{A, B, C\}$: Set of raw materials.

**Parameters:**
*   $c_j$: Cost of raw material $j$ per kg.
    *   $c_A=2.00, c_B=1.50, c_C=1.00$
*   $U_j$: Monthly limit of raw material $j$.
    *   $U_A=2000, U_B=2500, U_C=1200$
*   $p_i$: Processing cost of brand $i$ per kg.
    *   $p_X=0.50, p_Y=0.40, p_Z=0.30$
*   $s_i$: Selling price of brand $i$ per kg.
    *   $s_X=3.40, s_Y=2.85, s_Z=2.25$

**Decision Variables:**
*   $x_{ij}$: Amount (kg) of raw material $j$ used in brand $i$. Continuous, $x_{ij} \ge 0$.

**Objective Function:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Maximize } Z = \; & 0.90x_{XA} + 1.40x_{XB} + 1.90x_{XC} \\
& + 0.45x_{YA} + 0.95x_{YB} + 1.45x_{YC} \\
& - 0.05x_{ZA} + 0.45x_{ZB} + 0.95x_{ZC}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Subject to Constraints:**

1.  **Raw Material Availability:**
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x_{XA} + x_{YA} + x_{ZA} &\le 2000 \\
    x_{XB} + x_{YB} + x_{ZB} &\le 2500 \\
    x_{XC} + x_{YC} + x_{ZC} &\le 1200
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  **Composition Constraints:**
    *   *Brand X (A $\ge$ 60%):*
        $$ 0.4x_{XA} - 0.6x_{XB} - 0.6x_{XC} \ge 0 $$
    *   *Brand Y (A $\ge$ 30%):*
        $$ 0.7x_{YA} - 0.3x_{YB} - 0.3x_{YC} \ge 0 $$
    *   *Brand Y (C $\le$ 50%):*
        $$ -0.5x_{YA} - 0.5x_{YB} + 0.5x_{YC} \le 0 $$
    *   *Brand Z (C $\le$ 60%):*
        $$ -0.6x_{ZA} - 0.6x_{ZB} + 0.4x_{ZC} \le 0 $$

3.  **Non-negativity:**
    $$ x_{ij} \ge 0, \quad \forall i \in \{X,Y,Z\}, j \in \{A,B,C\} $$
