# Operations Research Model: Construction Assignment Problem

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be a binary decision variable defined for all $i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ and $j \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$:

$$
x_{ij} = 
\begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if construction company } A_i \text{ is assigned to build store } B_j \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

## 2. Objective Function

**Minimize** the total construction cost (in thousand yuan):

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{5} \sum_{j=1}^{5} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

where $c_{ij}$ represents the cost quotation (in thousand yuan) from company $A_i$ to build store $B_j$, as given in Table 5-10.

## 3. Operational Constraints

### Assignment Constraints for Construction Companies (Supply Constraints)
Each construction company must be assigned to exactly one store:

$$
\sum_{j=1}^{5} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}
$$

### Assignment Constraints for Stores (Demand Constraints)
Each store must be constructed by exactly one construction company:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{5} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall j \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}
$$

### Binary Constraints
All decision variables must be binary:

$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}, \forall j \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}
$$
