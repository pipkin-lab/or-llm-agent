### Final Output Structure

#### 1. Decision Variables
*   $x_{i,d}$: Binary variable, 1 if student $i$ is assigned to work on day $d$, 0 otherwise.
*   $h_{i,d}$: Non-negative continuous variable representing the number of hours student $i$ works on day $d$.

#### 2. Objective Function
Minimize Total Weekly Wage:
$$ \text{Min } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{6} \sum_{d=1}^{5} w_i h_{i,d} $$

#### 3. Operational Constraints

**A. Daily Coverage Constraints**
The total hours worked by all students on each day must equal the lab's operating hours (14 hours).
$$ \sum_{i=1}^{6} h_{i,d} = 14, \quad \forall d \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\} $$

**B. Individual Availability Constraints**
A student cannot work more hours than their maximum available hours for that day.
$$ h_{i,d} \le \text{MaxHours}_{i,d}, \quad \forall i \in \{1,\dots,6\}, \forall d \in \{1,\dots,5\} $$
*(Where $\text{MaxHours}_{i,d}$ are the values from Table 5-9)*

**C. Assignment Linking Constraints**
A student can only work hours if they are assigned to the schedule for that day.
$$ h_{i,d} \le 14 x_{i,d}, \quad \forall i, \forall d $$
*(Note: 14 is the max possible hours in a day, acting as the big-M coefficient)*

**D. Minimum Weekly Work Hours Constraints**
Undergraduate students (IDs 1-4) must work at least 8 hours per week.
$$ \sum_{d=1}^{5} h_{i,d} \ge 8, \quad \forall i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\} $$

$$ \sum_{d=1}^{5} h_{i,d} \ge 7, \quad \forall i \in \{5, 6\} $$

**E. Variable Domain Constraints**
$$ x_{i,d} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall i, \forall d $$
$$ h_{i,d} \ge 0, \quad \forall i, \forall d $$
