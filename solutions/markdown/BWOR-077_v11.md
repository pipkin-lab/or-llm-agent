**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Programming)

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
*   $W = \{A, B, C, D, E\}$: Set of workers, indexed by $w$.
*   $S = \{I, II, III, IV, V\}$: Set of assembly line stations, indexed by $s$.

**Parameters:**
*   $E_{w,s}$: Production efficiency (pieces/min) of worker $w$ at station $s$.

| Worker | Station I | Station II | Station III | Station IV | Station V |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **A** | 2 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 7 |
| **B** | 3 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 6 |
| **C** | 2 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 1 |
| **D** | 5 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 5 |
| **E** | 3 | 7 | 6 | 2 | 4 |

### 2. Decision Variables

*   $x_{w,s}$: Binary variable, where:
    $$
    x_{w,s} = \begin{cases} 
    1 & \text{if worker } w \text{ is assigned to station } s \\
    0 & \text{otherwise}
    \end{cases}
    $$
    for all $w \in W, s \in S$.

*   $Z$: Continuous variable representing the overall production capacity (bottleneck rate) of the assembly line (pieces/min).

### 3. Objective Function

Since this is a serial assembly line where every unit must pass through all 5 stations in sequence, the overall production capacity is limited by the slowest station (the bottleneck). We aim to maximize this minimum throughput.

**MAXIMIZE**
$$ Z $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**Worker Assignment Constraints (Each worker assigned to exactly one station):**
$$
\sum_{s \in S} x_{w,s} = 1 \quad \forall w \in W
$$

**Station Staffing Constraints (Each station assigned exactly one worker):**
$$
\sum_{w \in W} x_{w,s} = 1 \quad \forall s \in S
$$

**Bottleneck Capacity Constraints (The system throughput cannot exceed the capacity of any individual station):**
$$
Z \leq \sum_{w \in W} E_{w,s} x_{w,s} \quad \forall s \in S
$$

**Domain Constraints:**
$$
x_{w,s} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall w \in W, s \in S
$$
$$
Z \geq 0
$$
