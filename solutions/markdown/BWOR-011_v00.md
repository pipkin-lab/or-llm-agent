# Operations Research Problem Analysis: Electricity Supply Allocation

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ denote the amount of electricity (in units) supplied from Power Station $i$ to City $j$.

*   $i \in \{1, 2\}$ where $1$ represents Station I and $2$ represents Station II.
*   $j \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ where $j$ represents City 1, City 2, and City 3 respectively.

The decision variables are:
*   $x_{11}$: Units from Station I to City 1
*   $x_{12}$: Units from Station I to City 2
*   $x_{13}$: Units from Station I to City 3
*   $x_{21}$: Units from Station II to City 1
*   $x_{22}$: Units from Station II to City 2
*   $x_{23}$: Units from Station II to City 3

**Domain:** $x_{ij} \ge 0$ for all $i, j$.

## 2. Objective Function

The goal is to minimize the total cost of supplying electricity. Based on Table 3-33, the unit costs ($c_{ij}$) are:
*   $c_{11} = 15, c_{12} = 18, c_{13} = 22$
*   $c_{21} = 21, c_{22} = 25, c_{23} = 16$

**Minimize:**
$$ Z = 15x_{11} + 18x_{12} + 22x_{13} + 21x_{21} + 25x_{22} + 16x_{23} $$

## 3. Operational Constraints

### A. Supply Capacity Constraints (Source)
The problem states the plan must "use up all available electricity from both stations."
*   Station I Capacity: 400 units
*   Station II Capacity: 450 units

$$ x_{11} + x_{12} + x_{13} = 400 $$
$$ x_{21} + x_{22} + x_{23} = 450 $$

### B. Demand Constraints (Destination)
The problem specifies specific ranges or fixed values for each city's received supply.

**City 1:**
*   Requirement: Can be reduced by up to 30 units from the base demand of 320.
*   Minimum: $320 - 30 = 290$
*   Maximum: $320$
*   Constraint:
    $$ 290 \le x_{11} + x_{21} \le 320 $$

**City 2:**
*   Requirement: Supply must remain fixed at 250 units.
*   Constraint:
    $$ x_{12} + x_{22} = 250 $$

**City 3:**
*   Requirement: Supply must be at least 270 units, up to 350 units.
*   Constraint:
    $$ 270 \le x_{13} + x_{23} \le 350 $$

### C. Non-negativity
$$ x_{ij} \ge 0 \quad \forall i \in \{1,2\}, j \in \{1,2,3\} $$

---

### Summary of Model

**Minimize:**
$$ Z = 15x_{11} + 18x_{12} + 22x_{13} + 21x_{21} + 25x_{22} + 16x_{23} $$

**Subject To:**
1.  $x_{11} + x_{12} + x_{13} = 400$
2.  $x_{21} + x_{22} + x_{23} = 450$
3.  $x_{11} + x_{21} \ge 290$
4.  $x_{11} + x_{21} \le 320$
5.  $x_{12} + x_{22} = 250$
6.  $x_{13} + x_{23} \ge 270$
7.  $x_{13} + x_{23} \le 350$
8.  $x_{ij} \ge 0 \quad \forall i,j$
