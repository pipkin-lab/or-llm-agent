## Model Type
**LP** (Linear Programming)

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $I = \{A1, A2, A3, A4\}$: Set of supply origins, $|I| = 4$
- $J = \{B1, B2, B3, B4, B5, B6\}$: Set of demand destinations, $|J| = 6$

**Parameters:**
- $s_i$: Supply available at origin $i$ (units)
  - $s_{A1} = 50$
  - $s_{A2} = 40$
  - $s_{A3} = 60$
  - $s_{A4} = 31$
  - Total Supply $S = 50 + 40 + 60 + 31 = 181$

- $d_j$: Demand required at destination $j$ (units)
  - $d_{B1} = 30$
  - $d_{B2} = 50$
  - $d_{B3} = 20$
  - $d_{B4} = 40$
  - $d_{B5} = 30$
  - $d_{B6} = 11$
  - Total Demand $D = 30 + 50 + 20 + 40 + 30 + 11 = 181$

- $c_{ij}$: Unit transportation cost from origin $i$ to destination $j$

| Origin/Dest | $B1$ | $B2$ | $B3$ | $B4$ | $B5$ | $B6$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$A1$** | 2 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 5 |
| **$A2$** | 4 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| **$A3$** | 3 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 1 |
| **$A4$** | 4 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 |

**Feasibility Check:**
Total Supply ($181$) = Total Demand ($181$). The problem is balanced.

## Decision Variables

- $x_{ij}$: Quantity of goods shipped from origin $i \in I$ to destination $j \in J$.
- **Domain:** $x_{ij} \ge 0$ and $x_{ij}$ is continuous (CONTINUOUS).
  *(Note: While physical units are often discrete, standard transportation problems are typically solved as LPs where integrality holds naturally due to the total unimodularity of the constraint matrix, or treated as continuous flows. Given the magnitudes, continuous is the standard formulation unless "integer units" is explicitly specified.)*

## Objective Function

Minimize the total transportation cost:

$$ \min Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij} $$

Expanded:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min Z = \quad & 2x_{A1,B1} + 1x_{A1,B2} + 3x_{A1,B3} + 3x_{A1,B4} + 3x_{A1,B5} + 5x_{A1,B6} \\
+ & 4x_{A2,B1} + 2x_{A2,B2} + 2x_{A2,B3} + 4x_{A2,B4} + 4x_{A2,B5} + 4x_{A2,B6} \\
+ & 3x_{A3,B1} + 5x_{A3,B2} + 4x_{A3,B3} + 2x_{A3,B4} + 4x_{A3,B5} + 1x_{A3,B6} \\
+ & 4x_{A4,B1} + 2x_{A4,B2} + 2x_{A4,B3} + 1x_{A4,B4} + 2x_{A4,B5} + 2x_{A4,B6}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Operational Constraints

**1. Supply Constraints (Equality):**
Since Total Supply = Total Demand, all supply must be shipped. Each origin $i$ must send out exactly its supply amount.

$$ \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} = s_i, \quad \forall i \in I $$

Specifically:
- $\sum_{j=1}^{6} x_{A1,j} = 50$
- $\sum_{j=1}^{6} x_{A2,j} = 40$
- $\sum_{j=1}^{6} x_{A3,j} = 60$
- $\sum_{j=1}^{6} x_{A4,j} = 31$

**2. Demand Constraints (Equality):**
Each destination $j$ must receive exactly its demand amount.

$$ \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} = d_j, \quad \forall j \in J $$

Specifically:
- $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i,B1} = 30$
- $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i,B2} = 50$
- $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i,B3} = 20$
- $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i,B4} = 40$
- $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i,B5} = 30$
- $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i,B6} = 11$

**3. Non-negativity Constraints:**

$$ x_{ij} \ge 0, \quad \forall i \in I, j \in J $$
