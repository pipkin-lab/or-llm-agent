**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Program)
*Note: While the space variables can be continuous in theory, warehouse space is often rented in discrete units. However, given the large magnitudes (1000s of m²), treating them as continuous is standard and numerically stable. If strict integrality is required for "square meters," declare $x_{i,k}$ as INTEGER. Given the context of "area," CONTINUOUS is the more robust default unless specified otherwise. For this solution, we define them as CONTINUOUS ($x_{i,k} \ge 0$) as area is a continuous resource, but note that in practice, if the warehouse rents in whole m², they would be integers. The linearity holds in both cases.*

### Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
*   $I = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of months (1 to 4).
*   $K = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of possible contract durations (1 to 4 months).

**Parameters:**
*   $D_i$: Required warehouse area (m²) in month $i$.
    *   $D_1 = 1500$
    *   $D_2 = 1000$
    *   $D_3 = 2000$
    *   $D_4 = 1200$
*   $C_k$: Rental fee per square meter for a contract of duration $k$ months (yuan/m²).
    *   $C_1 = 28$
    *   $C_2 = 45$
    *   $C_3 = 60$
    *   $C_4 = 73$

**Derived Indices:**
*   A contract of duration $k$ signed at the beginning of month $i$ covers months $i, i+1, \dots, i+k-1$.
*   Valid combinations $(i, k)$ are those where $i + k - 1 \le 4$ (i.e., the contract does not extend beyond the 4-month planning horizon).

### Decision Variables

Let $x_{i,k}$ be the amount of warehouse space (in m²) rented via a contract of duration $k$ months signed at the beginning of month $i$.
*   Domain: $x_{i,k} \ge 0$ for all valid $(i, k)$.
*   Invalid $(i, k)$ pairs (where the contract exceeds month 4) are not defined or fixed to 0.

Valid variables:
*   $i=1$: $x_{1,1}, x_{1,2}, x_{1,3}, x_{1,4}$
*   $i=2$: $x_{2,1}, x_{2,2}, x_{2,3}$ (since $2+3-1=4$, $x_{2,4}$ would go to month 5, so invalid)
*   $i=3$: $x_{3,1}, x_{3,2}$ (since $3+2-1=4$, $x_{3,3}$ would go to month 5, so invalid)
*   $i=4$: $x_{4,1}$ (since $4+1-1=4$, $x_{4,2}$ would go to month 5, so invalid)

### Objective Function

Minimize the total rental cost. The cost for a contract is the area rented multiplied by the unit cost for that duration.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{k \in K_{valid(i)}} C_k \cdot x_{i,k}
$$

Explicitly:
$$
Z = 28(x_{1,1} + x_{2,1} + x_{3,1} + x_{4,1}) + 45(x_{1,2} + x_{2,2} + x_{3,2}) + 60(x_{1,3} + x_{2,3}) + 73(x_{1,4})
$$

### Operational Constraints

For each month $t \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, the total rented space from all active contracts must be at least the required demand $D_t$. A contract $x_{i,k}$ is active in month $t$ if $i \le t \le i+k-1$.

**Month 1 ($t=1$):**
Contracts starting in month 1 with duration $k \ge 1$ are active.
$$
x_{1,1} + x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} + x_{1,4} \ge 1500
$$

**Month 2 ($t=2$):**
Contracts starting in month 1 with duration $k \ge 2$ are active, plus contracts starting in month 2 with duration $k \ge 1$.
$$
x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} + x_{1,4} + x_{2,1} + x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} \ge 1000
$$

**Month 3 ($t=3$):**
Contracts starting in month 1 with duration $k \ge 3$ are active, plus contracts starting in month 2 with duration $k \ge 2$, plus contracts starting in month 3 with duration $k \ge 1$.
$$
x_{1,3} + x_{1,4} + x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} + x_{3,1} + x_{3,2} \ge 2000
$$

**Month 4 ($t=4$):**
Contracts starting in month 1 with duration $k=4$ are active, plus contracts starting in month 2 with duration $k \ge 3$ (only $k=3$ is valid), plus contracts starting in month 3 with duration $k=2$, plus contracts starting in month 4 with duration $k=1$.
$$
x_{1,4} + x_{2,3} + x_{3,2} + x_{4,1} \ge 1200
$$

**Non-negativity:**
$$
x_{i,k} \ge 0 \quad \forall (i,k) \text{ defined above}
$$
