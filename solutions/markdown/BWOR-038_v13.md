### 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_{m,p}$ be the number of units of product $p$ processed on machine $m$.
Since products go through two stages, we need to track flow through Stage A and Stage B separately to ensure balance. However, since the machine sets for Stage A ($A1, A2$) and Stage B ($B1, B2, B3$) are disjoint, we can define variables for each machine-product pair directly.

*   **Stage A Variables:**
    *   $x_{A1, I}, x_{A1, II}$: Units of Product I and II processed on $A1$.
    *   $x_{A2, I}, x_{A2, II}, x_{A2, III}$: Units of Product I, II, and III processed on $A2$.
    *   *Note:* Product III can ONLY be on $A2$. So $x_{A1, III} = 0$ (variable not defined or fixed to 0).

*   **Stage B Variables:**
    *   $x_{B1, I}, x_{B1, II}$: Units of Product I and II processed on $B1$. (Based on Table: II is allowed on B1).
    *   $x_{B2, I}, x_{B2, III}$: Units of Product I and III processed on $B2$. (Based on Table: II is NOT allowed on B2).
    *   $x_{B3, I}$: Units of Product I processed on $B3$.

**Domain:**
All $x_{m,p} \ge 0$ and CONTINUOUS.
(While products are discrete, in large-scale production planning with high capacities (thousands of hours), continuous relaxation is standard and usually yields integer or near-integer solutions, or is accepted as the LP formulation. If strict integrality is required, they would be IntVars, but LP is the standard model type for this "production plan" question unless specified otherwise. Given the "yuan" and "hours" precision, Continuous is appropriate).

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize Total Profit.
Profit per unit of product $p$ on machine $m$ in Stage $k$:
$Profit_{m,p} = SellingPrice_p - RawMaterialCost_p - (ProcessingTime_{m,p} \times CostPerHour_m)$

Note: The raw material cost is incurred once per unit of final product. Since each unit passes through Stage A and Stage B, we must be careful not to double-count raw material.
Standard approach:
Total Revenue = $\sum_{p} s_p Q_p$
Total Cost = $\sum_{p} r_p Q_p + \sum_{m \in A} \sum_{p} (t_{m,p} x_{m,p} c_m) + \sum_{m \in B} \sum_{p} (t_{m,p} x_{m,p} c_m)$
Where $Q_p$ is the total production of product $p$.
$Q_p = \sum_{m \in A} x_{m,p} = \sum_{m \in B} x_{m,p}$ (by flow balance).

So, $Z = \sum_{p} (s_p - r_p) Q_p - \sum_{m \in A} \sum_{p} t_{m,p} x_{m,p} c_m - \sum_{m \in B} \sum_{p} t_{m,p} x_{m,p} c_m$

Let's compute the net contribution coefficient for each variable $x_{m,p}$.
For a unit of $p$ processed on $m$, it contributes to the production quantity $Q_p$.
It is easier to write the objective in terms of $x_{m,p}$ by distributing the revenue and material cost.
Since $Q_p = \sum_{m \in A} x_{m,p}$, we can substitute $Q_p$ into the objective.
$Z = \sum_{p} (s_p - r_p) (\sum_{m \in A} x_{m,p}) - \sum_{m \in A} \sum_{p} t_{m,p} c_m x_{m,p} - \sum_{m \in B} \sum_{p} t_{m,p} c_m x_{m,p}$

This can be grouped by variable $x_{m,p}$:
For $m \in A$: Coeff $= (s_p - r_p) - t_{m,p} c_m$
For $m \in B$: Coeff $= - t_{m,p} c_m$  (Revenue and Material cost are already accounted for in the Stage A sum term? No, wait.)

If we define $Q_p = \sum_{m \in A} x_{m,p}$, then the term $(s_p - r_p)Q_p$ is counted once. The processing costs are subtracted for both stages.
So,
$Z = \sum_{p} \sum_{m \in A} [(s_p - r_p) - t_{m,p} c_m] x_{m,p} - \sum_{p} \sum_{m \in B} [t_{m,p} c_m] x_{m,p}$

Let's verify this logic.
If I produce 1 unit of I on A1 and B1.
$Q_I = 1$.
Revenue - Material = $1.25 - 0.25 = 1.00$.
Processing Cost A1 = $5 \times 0.05 = 0.25$.
Processing Cost B1 = $6 \times 0.06 = 0.36$.
Total Profit = $1.00 - 0.25 - 0.36 = 0.39$.

Using the formula:
Term for $x_{A1, I}$: $(1.25 - 0.25) - (5 \times 0.05) = 1.00 - 0.25 = 0.75$.
Term for $x_{B1, I}$: $-(6 \times 0.06) = -0.36$.
Sum = $0.75 - 0.36 = 0.39$. Correct.

So the objective is:
**Maximize** $Z = \sum_{p \in P} \sum_{m \in \{A1, A2\}} \left( (s_p - r_p) - t_{m,p} c_m \right) x_{m,p} - \sum_{p \in P} \sum_{m \in \{B1, B2, B3\}} \left( t_{m,p} c_m \right) x_{m,p}$

Let's calculate the specific coefficients for the final formulation.

**Stage A Coefficients ($C^A_{m,p} = (s_p - r_p) - t_{m,p} c_m$):**

*   **Product I** ($s-r = 1.00$):
    *   $A1$: $1.00 - (5 \times 0.05) = 1.00 - 0.25 = 0.75$
    *   $A2$: $1.00 - (7 \times 0.03) = 1.00 - 0.21 = 0.79$
*   **Product II** ($s-r = 2.00 - 0.35 = 1.65$):
    *   $A1$: $1.65 - (10 \times 0.05) = 1.65 - 0.50 = 1.15$
    *   $A2$: $1.65 - (9 \times 0.03) = 1.65 - 0.27 = 1.38$
*   **Product III** ($s-r = 2.80 - 0.50 = 2.30$):
    *   $A2$: $2.30 - (12 \times 0.03) = 2.30 - 0.36 = 1.94$
    *   $A1$: Not allowed.

**Stage B Coefficients ($C^B_{m,p} = - t_{m,p} c_m$):**

*   **Product I**:
    *   $B1$: $-(6 \times 0.06) = -0.36$
    *   $B2$: $-(4 \times 0.11) = -0.44$
    *   $B3$: $-(7 \times 0.05) = -0.35$
*   **Product II**:
    *   $B1$: $-(8 \times 0.06) = -0.48$ (Assuming allowed on B1 based on table)
    *   $B2, B3$: Not allowed.
*   **Product III**:
    *   $B2$: $-(11 \times 0.11) = -1.21$
    *   $B1, B3$: Not allowed.

**Objective Function:**
Max $Z = 0.75 x_{A1,I} + 0.79 x_{A2,I} + 1.15 x_{A1,II} + 1.38 x_{A2,II} + 1.94 x_{A2,III}$
$- 0.36 x_{B1,I} - 0.44 x_{B2,I} - 0.35 x_{B3,I} - 0.48 x_{B1,II} - 1.21 x_{B2,III}$

*(Note: Variables for disallowed combinations are omitted as they are 0)*

### 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Machine Capacity Constraints:**
For each machine $m$, total processing time $\le$ Available Hours.

*   **A1:** $5 x_{A1,I} + 10 x_{A1,II} \le 6000$
*   **A2:** $7 x_{A2,I} + 9 x_{A2,II} + 12 x_{A2,III} \le 10000$
*   **B1:** $6 x_{B1,I} + 8 x_{B1,II} \le 4000$
*   **B2:** $4 x_{B2,I} + 11 x_{B2,III} \le 7000$
*   **B3:** $7 x_{B3,I} \le 4000$

**2. Flow Balance Constraints (Stage A to Stage B):**
The quantity of each product exiting Stage A must equal the quantity entering Stage B.
$Q_p^{A} = \sum_{m \in A} x_{m,p}$
$Q_p^{B} = \sum_{m \in B} x_{m,p}$
$Q_p^{A} = Q_p^{B}$

*   **Product I:**
    $x_{A1,I} + x_{A2,I} = x_{B1,I} + x_{B2,I} + x_{B3,I}$
*   **Product II:**
    $x_{A1,II} + x_{A2,II} = x_{B1,II}$
    *(Note: Only $B1$ is allowed for II in Stage B based on table interpretation)*
*   **Product III:**
    $x_{A2,III} = x_{B2,III}$
    *(Note: Only $A2$ and $B2$ are allowed for III)*

**3. Non-negativity:**
$x_{m,p} \ge 0$ for all defined variables.
