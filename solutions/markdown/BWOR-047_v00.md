# OR Problem Analysis: Shipyard Production Planning

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $N_t$ be the number of ships produced in Year $t$ under **normal** production.
Let $O_t$ be the number of ships produced in Year $t$ under **overtime** production.
Let $I_t$ be the inventory of ships available for delivery (including initial stock and previous production) at the **end** of Year $t$, after satisfying the demand for Year $t$.

*   Time index $t \in \{1, 2, 3\}$.
*   Variables $N_t, O_t$ are non-negative integers.
*   Variable $I_t$ is a non-negative integer.

**Specific Variables:**
*   $N_1, O_1, I_1$
*   $N_2, O_2, I_2$
*   $N_3, O_3, I_3$

## 2. Objective Function

**Minimize Total Cost** ($Z$)

The total cost consists of:
1.  **Production Costs**: Normal cost + Overtime cost.
    *   Normal costs: $500 N_1 + 600 N_2 + 550 N_3$
    *   Overtime costs: $(500+70)O_1 + (600+70)O_2 + (550+70)O_3 = 570 O_1 + 670 O_2 + 620 O_3$
2.  **Holding/Storage Costs**: Cost is incurred for ships stored at the end of each year. The problem states "each year of delay results in an additional maintenance and storage loss of 40 thousand yuan per ship." This is typically modeled as holding cost on the inventory level $I_t$ at the end of year $t$.
    *   Holding costs: $40 I_1 + 40 I_2 + 40 I_3$

**Objective Function:**
$$ \min Z = 500 N_1 + 570 O_1 + 600 N_2 + 670 O_2 + 550 N_3 + 620 O_3 + 40(I_1 + I_2 + I_3) $$

## 3. Operational Constraints

### A. Production Capacity Constraints
The sum of normal and overtime production in each year cannot exceed the maximum capacity.

*   **Year 1:**
    *   Normal: $N_1 \le 2$
    *   Overtime: $O_1 \le 3$
*   **Year 2:**
    *   Normal: $N_2 \le 4$
    *   Overtime: $O_2 \le 2$
*   **Year 3:**
    *   Normal: $N_3 \le 1$
    *   Overtime: $O_3 \le 3$

### B. Inventory Balance Constraints
Inventory at the end of year $t$ is equal to inventory at the end of year $t-1$ plus production in year $t$, minus demand in year $t$.
*   Initial Inventory ($I_0$) = 2 ships.
*   Demand ($D_t$) = 3 ships per year for $t=1, 2, 3$.

*   **Year 1 Balance:**
    $$ I_1 = I_0 + N_1 + O_1 - D_1 $$
    $$ I_1 = 2 + N_1 + O_1 - 3 $$
    $$ I_1 = N_1 + O_1 - 1 $$

*   **Year 2 Balance:**
    $$ I_2 = I_1 + N_2 + O_2 - D_2 $$
    $$ I_2 = I_1 + N_2 + O_2 - 3 $$

*   **Year 3 Balance:**
    $$ I_3 = I_2 + N_3 + O_3 - D_3 $$
    $$ I_3 = I_2 + N_3 + O_3 - 3 $$

### C. Final Inventory Constraint
The shipyard requires 1 spare ship in storage at the end of the third year.

*   $$ I_3 = 1 $$

### D. Non-negativity and Integrality Constraints
*   $N_t, O_t, I_t \ge 0$
*   $N_t, O_t, I_t \in \mathbb{Z}$ (Integers)
*   Implicitly, $I_t$ cannot be negative (no backlogging allowed as ships must be delivered at the end of the year).

### Summary of Constraints for Solving
1.  $N_1 \le 2, \quad O_1 \le 3$
2.  $N_2 \le 4, \quad O_2 \le 2$
3.  $N_3 \le 1, \quad O_3 \le 3$
4.  $I_1 = N_1 + O_1 - 1$
5.  $I_2 = I_1 + N_2 + O_2 - 3$
6.  $I_3 = I_2 + N_3 + O_3 - 3$
7.  $I_3 = 1$
8.  $N_t, O_t, I_t \ge 0$ and integer.
