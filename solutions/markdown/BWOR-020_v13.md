Model Type: MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)

### Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $C = \{A, B, C, D, E\}$: Set of customers.
*   $J = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of trucks.

**Parameters:**
*   $d_c$: Demand (units) for customer $c$.
    *   $d_A = 1$
    *   $d_B = 2$
    *   $d_C = 3$
    *   $d_D = 4$
    *   $d_E = 8$
*   $Cap_j$: Capacity (units) of truck $j$.
    *   $Cap_1 = 2$
    *   $Cap_2 = 6$
    *   $Cap_3 = 8$
    *   $Cap_4 = 11$
*   $c_j$: Cost of using truck $j$.
    *   $c_1 = [NOT\_PROVIDED]$
    *   $c_2 = [NOT\_PROVIDED]$
    *   $c_3 = [NOT\_PROVIDED]$
    *   $c_4 = [NOT\_PROVIDED]$
    *(Note: The problem statement defines the cost symbolically as $c_j$. No specific numerical values are provided in the text.)*

### Decision Variables

*   $x_{c,j} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable. $x_{c,j} = 1$ if truck $j$ serves customer $c$, 0 otherwise.
*   $z_j \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable. $z_j = 1$ if truck $j$ is used (assigned to at least one customer), 0 otherwise.

### Objective Function

Minimize the total transportation cost:
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{j \in J} c_j z_j $$

### Operational Constraints

**1. Customer Coverage (Each customer must be served by exactly one truck):**
For each customer $c \in C$:
$$ \sum_{j \in J} x_{c,j} = 1 $$

**2. Truck Capacity (Total demand assigned to a truck cannot exceed its capacity):**
For each truck $j \in J$:
$$ \sum_{c \in C} d_c x_{c,j} \leq Cap_j $$

**3. Logical Linking (If a truck serves any customer, it is considered "used"):**
For each truck $j \in J$:
$$ \sum_{c \in C} x_{c,j} \leq M z_j $$
*(Where $M$ is a sufficiently large constant, e.g., $M = |C| = 5$. Alternatively, simply $z_j = 1$ if $\sum x_{c,j} > 0$. Since costs are positive, the solver will naturally set $z_j=0$ if no customers are assigned, but explicit linking ensures correctness if costs were negative, though here they are likely positive. A tighter constraint is $\sum_{c \in C} x_{c,j} \leq 5 z_j$.)*

**4. Conflict Constraints (Specific trucks cannot serve specific pairs of customers):**
*   A truck cannot deliver to both Customer A and Customer C:
    For each truck $j \in J$:
    $$ x_{A,j} + x_{C,j} \leq 1 $$

*   A truck cannot deliver to both Customer B and Customer D:
    For each truck $j \in J$:
    $$ x_{B,j} + x_{D,j} \leq 1 $$

**5. Non-negativity and Integrality:**
$$ x_{c,j} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall c \in C, j \in J $$
$$ z_j \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall j \in J $$
