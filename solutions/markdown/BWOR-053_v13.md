## Model Type: MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Program)

**Note:** While the continuous relaxation of this model may yield fractional solutions, the physical interpretation (hours worked by a specific student on a specific day) naturally suggests continuous hours. However, since the problem does not explicitly require integer hours and hours are typically treated as continuous in workforce scheduling, we will use **Continuous** variables for hours worked. If strict integer hours are required, change domains to INTEGER. Given the context of "hours" in a lab setting, continuous is standard unless specified otherwise. *Correction*: In many textbook scheduling problems of this type, hours are treated as continuous. I will declare them as **Continuous** (NumVar). If the problem implies whole hours, they would be Integer. I will use Continuous as the default for time allocation unless "integer hours" is specified.

Let's double-check standard conventions. Usually, labor hours are continuous. I will proceed with Continuous variables for $x_{i,t}$.

    *   $t=4$: Thursday

*   $MinH_{UG}$: Minimum weekly hours required for undergraduates = 8 hours.
*   $MinH_{Grad}$: Minimum weekly hours required for graduates = 7 hours.

### Decision Variables

Let $x_{i,t}$ be the number of hours student $i$ works on day $t$.
*   Domain: $x_{i,t} \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ for all $i \in I, t \in T$.

### Objective Function

Minimize the total wage payment:
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{t \in T} w_i \cdot x_{i,t} $$

Substituting the coefficients:
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = 10.0(x_{1,1} + x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} + x_{1,4} + x_{1,5}) + 10.0(x_{2,1} + x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} + x_{2,4} + x_{2,5}) + 9.9(x_{3,1} + x_{3,2} + x_{3,3} + x_{3,4} + x_{3,5}) + 9.8(x_{4,1} + x_{4,2} + x_{4,3} + x_{4,4} + x_{4,5}) + 10.8(x_{5,1} + x_{5,2} + x_{5,3} + x_{5,4} + x_{5,5}) + 11.3(x_{6,1} + x_{6,2} + x_{6,3} + x_{6,4} + x_{6,5}) $$

### Operational Constraints

**1. Daily Coverage Constraints:**
The total hours worked by all students on any given day must equal the lab's operating hours (14 hours).
$$ \sum_{i \in I} x_{i,t} = 14, \quad \forall t \in T $$

Explicitly:
*   **Monday:** $x_{1,1} + x_{2,1} + x_{3,1} + x_{4,1} + x_{5,1} + x_{6,1} = 14$
*   **Tuesday:** $x_{1,2} + x_{2,2} + x_{3,2} + x_{4,2} + x_{5,2} + x_{6,2} = 14$
*   **Wednesday:** $x_{1,3} + x_{2,3} + x_{3,3} + x_{4,3} + x_{5,3} + x_{6,3} = 14$
*   **Thursday:** $x_{1,4} + x_{2,4} + x_{3,4} + x_{4,4} + x_{5,4} + x_{6,4} = 14$
*   **Friday:** $x_{1,5} + x_{2,5} + x_{3,5} + x_{4,5} + x_{5,5} + x_{6,5} = 14$

**2. Student Availability Constraints:**
The hours worked by each student on a specific day cannot exceed their maximum available hours.
$$ x_{i,t} \le H_{i,t}, \quad \forall i \in I, t \in T $$

Explicitly (only non-zero bounds listed for brevity, zeros imply $x_{i,t}=0$):
*   **Student 1:** $x_{1,1} \le 6, \quad x_{1,3} \le 9, \quad x_{1,5} \le 7$
*   **Student 2:** $x_{2,2} \le 9, \quad x_{2,4} \le 6$
*   **Student 3:** $x_{3,1} \le 4, \quad x_{3,2} \le 8, \quad x_{3,3} \le 3, \quad x_{3,5} \le 5$
*   **Student 4:** $x_{4,1} \le 5, \quad x_{4,2} \le 5, \quad x_{4,3} \le 6, \quad x_{4,5} \le 4$
*   **Student 5:** $x_{5,1} \le 3, \quad x_{5,3} \le 4, \quad x_{5,4} \le 8$
*   **Student 6:** $x_{6,2} \le 9, \quad x_{6,4} \le 6, \quad x_{6,5} \le 3$

**3. Minimum Weekly Work Hours Constraints:**
Undergraduate students must work at least 8 hours per week. Graduate students must work at least 7 hours per week.

For Undergraduates ($i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$):
$$ \sum_{t \in T} x_{i,t} \ge 8 $$
*   $x_{1,1} + x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} + x_{1,4} + x_{1,5} \ge 8$
*   $x_{2,1} + x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} + x_{2,4} + x_{2,5} \ge 8$
*   $x_{3,1} + x_{3,2} + x_{3,3} + x_{3,4} + x_{3,5} \ge 8$
*   $x_{4,1} + x_{4,2} + x_{4,3} + x_{4,4} + x_{4,5} \ge 8$

For Graduates ($i \in \{5, 6\}$):
$$ \sum_{t \in T} x_{i,t} \ge 7 $$
*   $x_{5,1} + x_{5,2} + x_{5,3} + x_{5,4} + x_{5,5} \ge 7$
*   $x_{6,1} + x_{6,2} + x_{6,3} + x_{6,4} + x_{6,5} \ge 7$

**4. Non-negativity Constraints:**
$$ x_{i,t} \ge 0, \quad \forall i \in I, t \in T $$
