# Operations Research Model: Farm Production Planning

## 1. Decision Variables

Let the variables represent the quantities of production activities:

*   **Crop Planting (hectares):**
    *   $x_1$: Hectares of land allocated to **Soybeans**
    *   $x_2$: Hectares of land allocated to **Corn**
    *   $x_3$: Hectares of land allocated to **Wheat**

*   **Animal Rearing (head count):**
    *   $y_1$: Number of **Dairy Cows**
    *   $y_2$: Number of **Chickens**

*   **Labor Export (person-days utilized externally):**
    *   $z_1$: Person-days of labor sent out for work in **Autumn/Winter**
    *   $z_2$: Person-days of labor sent out for work in **Spring/Summer**

***

## 2. Objective Function

**Goal:** Maximize Total Annual Net Income ($Z$).

Total Income consists of:
1.  Income from Crops ($175x_1 + 300x_2 + 120x_3$)
2.  Income from Animals ($400y_1 + 2y_2$)
3.  Income from External Labor ($1.8z_1 + 2.1z_2$)

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 175x_1 + 300x_2 + 120x_3 + 400y_1 + 2y_2 + 1.8z_1 + 2.1z_2 $$

***

## 3. Operational Constraints

### A. Resource Constraints

**1. Land Constraint (Total 100 hm²)**
Crops use land directly. Cows use land for forage (1.5 hm²/cow). Chickens use no land.
$$ x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + 1.5y_1 \le 100 $$

**2. Capital/Funds Constraint (Total 15,000 yuan)**
Crops require no investment. Animals require specific investments.
*   Cows: 400 yuan/head
*   Chickens: 3 yuan/head
$$ 400y_1 + 3y_2 \le 15,000 $$

**3. Labor Constraints**
Total available labor must cover farm operations plus any labor exported.
*   **Autumn/Winter Labor (3,500 person-days):**
    *   Soybeans: 20/hm²
    *   Corn: 35/hm²
    *   Wheat: 10/hm²
    *   Cows: 100/head
    *   Chickens: 0.6/head
    *   Exported: $z_1$
    $$ 20x_1 + 35x_2 + 10x_3 + 100y_1 + 0.6y_2 + z_1 \le 3,500 $$

*   **Spring/Summer Labor (4,000 person-days):**
    *   Soybeans: 50/hm²
    *   Corn: 75/hm²
    *   Wheat: 40/hm²
    *   Cows: 50/head
    *   Chickens: 0.3/head
    *   Exported: $z_2$
    $$ 50x_1 + 75x_2 + 40x_3 + 50y_1 + 0.3y_2 + z_2 \le 4,000 $$

### B. Capacity Constraints (Infrastructure)

**1. Chicken House Capacity**
Maximum 3,000 chickens.
$$ y_2 \le 3,000 $$

**2. Cowshed Capacity**
Maximum 32 dairy cows.
$$ y_1 \le 32 $$

### C. Non-negativity Constraints

All decision variables must be non-negative.
$$ x_1, x_2, x_3, y_1, y_2, z_1, z_2 \ge 0 $$
