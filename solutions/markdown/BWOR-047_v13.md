# Optimization Model Formulation

## Model Type
**MILP** (Mixed-Integer Linear Programming)
*   **Justification**: The decision variables represent the number of ships produced, which must be non-negative integers. The objective function and constraints are linear.

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
*   $T = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of years.
*   $P \in \{N, O\}$: Set of production modes (Normal, Overtime).

**Parameters:**
*   $D_t$: Demand for ship delivery in year $t$.
    *   $D_1 = 3$, $D_2 = 3$, $D_3 = 3$
*   $S_{t,N}$: Maximum production capacity in year $t$ under Normal mode.
    *   $S_{1,N} = 2$, $S_{2,N} = 4$, $S_{3,N} = 1$
*   $S_{t,O}$: Maximum production capacity in year $t$ under Overtime mode.
    *   $S_{1,O} = 3$, $S_{2,O} = 2$, $S_{3,O} = 3$
*   $c_{t,N}$: Cost per ship (thousand yuan) in year $t$ under Normal mode.
    *   $c_{1,N} = 500$, $c_{2,N} = 600$, $c_{3,N} = 550$
*   $\Delta c$: Additional cost per ship for Overtime production (thousand yuan).
    *   $\Delta c = 70$
*   $c_{t,O}$: Cost per ship in year $t$ under Overtime mode ($c_{t,N} + \Delta c$).
    *   $c_{1,O} = 570$, $c_{2,O} = 670$, $c_{3,O} = 620$
*   $h$: Holding/storage cost per ship per year (thousand yuan).
    *   $h = 40$
*   $I_0$: Initial inventory of undelivered ships at the start of Year 1.
    *   $I_0 = 2$
*   $I_{final}$: Required ending inventory at the end of Year 3 (spare ship).
    *   $I_{final} = 1$

## Decision Variables

Let $x_{t,N}$ be the number of ships produced in year $t$ under **Normal** production.
Let $x_{t,O}$ be the number of ships produced in year $t$ under **Overtime** production.
Let $I_t$ be the inventory of undelivered ships at the **end** of year $t$.

**Domains:**
*   $x_{t,N} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for all $t \in T$
*   $x_{t,O} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for all $t \in T$
*   $I_t \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for all $t \in T$

## Objective Function

Minimize total cost (production cost + holding cost):

$$
\min Z = \sum_{t=1}^{3} \left( c_{t,N} x_{t,N} + c_{t,O} x_{t,O} + h I_t \right)
$$

Substituting values:
$$
\min Z = (500 x_{1,N} + 570 x_{1,O} + 40 I_1) + (600 x_{2,N} + 670 x_{2,O} + 40 I_2) + (550 x_{3,N} + 620 x_{3,O} + 40 I_3)
$$

## Operational Constraints

**1. Inventory Balance Constraints:**
The inventory at the end of year $t$ equals the beginning inventory plus production minus demand.
*   Beginning inventory for year $t$ is $I_{t-1}$ (with $I_0 = 2$).
*   $I_t = I_{t-1} + x_{t,N} + x_{t,O} - D_t$

Explicitly:
*   **Year 1:** $I_1 = 2 + x_{1,N} + x_{1,O} - 3 \implies I_1 = x_{1,N} + x_{1,O} - 1$
*   **Year 2:** $I_2 = I_1 + x_{2,N} + x_{2,O} - 3$
*   **Year 3:** $I_3 = I_2 + x_{3,N} + x_{3,O} - 3$

**2. Production Capacity Constraints:**
*   **Year 1:**
    *   $x_{1,N} \le 2$
    *   $x_{1,O} \le 3$
*   **Year 2:**
    *   $x_{2,N} \le 4$
    *   $x_{2,O} \le 2$
*   **Year 3:**
    *   $x_{3,N} \le 1$
    *   $x_{3,O} \le 3$

**3. Ending Inventory Requirement:**
The problem states the shipyard wishes to have one spare ship in storage at the end of the third year.
*   $I_3 = 1$

*(Note: Since $I_3$ is fixed to 1, the inequality $I_3 \ge 1$ is not needed, but $I_1, I_2 \ge 0$ is ensured by the non-negativity domain of $I_t$.)*

## Feasibility Sanity Check
*   Total Demand = $3 + 3 + 3 = 9$ ships.
*   Initial Stock = 2 ships.
*   Required End Stock = 1 ship.
*   Total Production Required = $9 - 2 + 1 = 8$ ships.
*   Total Max Capacity = $(2+3) + (4+2) + (1+3) = 5 + 6 + 4 = 15$ ships.
*   Since $15 \ge 8$, the problem is feasible.

## Final Answer Structure
The solution will provide the optimal values for $x_{t,N}$ and $x_{t,O}$ for each year, and the resulting minimum total cost $Z$.
