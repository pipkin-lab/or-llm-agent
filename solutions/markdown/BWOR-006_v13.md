### 3. Objective Function

Maximize Profit = Total Revenue - Total Variable Costs (Processing + Raw Material).

Let $Q_p$ be the total production of product $p$.
$Q_I = x_{A_1, I} + x_{A_2, I} = x_{B_1, I} + x_{B_2, I} + x_{B_3, I}$
$Q_{II} = x_{A_1, II} + x_{A_2, II} = x_{B_1, II}$
$Q_{III} = x_{A_2, III} = x_{B_2, III}$

**Revenue:**
$Rev = 1.25 Q_I + 2.00 Q_{II} + 2.80 Q_{III}$

**Raw Material Cost:**
$Cost_{RM} = 0.25 Q_I + 0.35 Q_{II} + 0.50 Q_{III}$

**Processing Cost:**
$Cost_{Proc} = \sum_{m \in M_A} \sum_{p} c_m t_{m,p} x_{m,p} + \sum_{m \in M_B} \sum_{p} c_m t_{m,p} x_{m,p}$

Calculating coefficients for each variable in the objective function (Contribution Margin per unit on machine $m$):
Profit contribution for a unit on machine $m$ for product $p$ = $s_p - r_p - c_m t_{m,p}$.

### 4. Operational Constraints

Every unit produced in Stage A must be processed in Stage B.
*   Product I:
    $x_{A_1, I} + x_{A_2, I} = x_{B_1, I} + x_{B_2, I} + x_{B_3, I}$
*   Product II:
    $x_{A_1, II} + x_{A_2, II} = x_{B_1, II}$

    $x_{A_2, III} = x_{B_2, III}$
