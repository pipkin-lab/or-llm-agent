**Model Type:** Linear Programming (LP)

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $T = \{2003, 2004, 2005, 2006\}$: The set of years for loan disbursement.
*   $B = \{A, B, C\}$: The set of available bonds.

**Parameters (in thousands of dollars):**

*   **Loan Requirements ($D_t$):**
    *   $D_{2003} = 100$
    *   $D_{2004} = 150$
    *   $D_{2005} = 120$
    *   $D_{2006} = 110$
    *   *Total Demand:* $\sum D_t = 480$

*   **Bond Parameters:**
    *   **Bond A:**
        *   Purchase Time: Beginning of 2003
        *   Maturity Time: End of 2005 (3-year term: 2003, 2004, 2005)
        *   Return Factor ($R_A$): $1.40$
        *   Max Purchase ($U_A$): $60$
    *   **Bond B:**
        *   Purchase Time: Beginning of 2004
        *   Maturity Time: End of 2005 (2-year term: 2004, 2005)
        *   Return Factor ($R_B$): $1.25$
        *   Max Purchase ($U_B$): $90$
    *   **Bond C:**
        *   Purchase Time: Beginning of 2004
        *   Maturity Time: End of 2005 (2-year term: 2004, 2005)
        *   Return Factor ($R_C$): $1.30$
        *   Max Purchase ($U_C$): $50$

*   **Bank Account:**
    *   Annual Interest Rate ($r$): $0.04$
    *   Available: Beginning of each year, withdrawn at end of same year.

**Note on Timing:**
*   Funds are raised at the **end of 2002** (equivalent to beginning of 2003).
*   Loans are disbursed at the **beginning** of each year.
*   Bond purchases occur at the **beginning** of the specified year.
*   Bond returns and bank interest are received at the **end** of the maturity/interest year (equivalent to beginning of the next year, or available for immediate use if needed for that year's beginning? No, "withdrawal at end of same year" means available for the *next* year's beginning. However, loans are at the *beginning*. This implies a strict timing sequence within the year:
    1.  Start of Year: Receive previous year's returns (if any).
    2.  Start of Year: Disburse Loan $D_t$.
    3.  Start of Year: Purchase Bonds.
    4.  End of Year: Receive Bank Interest (on funds held during the year).

### 2. Decision Variables

Let all variables be continuous non-negative real numbers (amounts in thousands of dollars).

*   $K$: Total amount of funds raised at the end of 2002 (Objective).
*   $x_A$: Amount invested in Bond A.
*   $x_B$: Amount invested in Bond B.
*   $x_C$: Amount invested in Bond C.
*   $S_3$: Surplus funds deposited in the bank at the beginning of 2003 (after paying loan and buying Bond A).
*   $S_4$: Surplus funds deposited in the bank at the beginning of 2004 (after paying loan and buying Bonds B, C).
*   $S_5$: Surplus funds deposited in the bank at the beginning of 2005 (after paying loan).
*   $S_6$: Surplus funds remaining at the beginning of 2006 (after paying loan). Note: Since no further obligations exist, $S_6$ will likely be 0 in the optimal solution to minimize $K$, but we include it for balance completeness.

### 3. Objective Function

Minimize the total amount raised at the end of 2002.

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = K $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Year 2003 Cash Balance (Beginning of 2003)**
The initial capital $K$ must cover the 2003 loan, the purchase of Bond A, and the bank deposit $S_3$.
$$ K = D_{2003} + x_A + S_3 $$
$$ K = 100 + x_A + S_3 $$

**2. Year 2004 Cash Balance (Beginning of 2004)**
The funds available are the 2003 bank deposit plus its 4% interest. This must cover the 2004 loan, purchases of Bond B and Bond C, and the 2004 bank deposit $S_4$.
$$ 1.04 S_3 = D_{2004} + x_B + x_C + S_4 $$
$$ 1.04 S_3 = 150 + x_B + x_C + S_4 $$

**3. Year 2005 Cash Balance (Beginning of 2005)**
The funds available are:
- 2004 bank deposit plus interest: $1.04 S_4$
- Bond A maturity (3 years, bought start 2003, matures end 2005): $1.40 x_A$
- Bond B maturity (2 years, bought start 2004, matures end 2005): $1.25 x_B$
- Bond C maturity (2 years, bought start 2004, matures end 2005): $1.30 x_C$

This total must cover the 2005 loan and the 2005 bank deposit $S_5$.
$$ 1.04 S_4 + 1.40 x_A + 1.25 x_B + 1.30 x_C = D_{2005} + S_5 $$
$$ 1.04 S_4 + 1.40 x_A + 1.25 x_B + 1.30 x_C = 120 + S_5 $$

**4. Year 2006 Cash Balance (Beginning of 2006)**
The funds available are the 2005 bank deposit plus interest. This must cover the 2006 loan and any final surplus $S_6$.
$$ 1.04 S_5 = D_{2006} + S_6 $$
$$ 1.04 S_5 = 110 + S_6 $$

**5. Bond Investment Limits**
$$ 0 \le x_A \le 60 $$
$$ 0 \le x_B \le 90 $$
$$ 0 \le x_C \le 50 $$

**6. Non-negativity Constraints**
All variables must be non-negative.
$$ K \ge 0, \quad x_A, x_B, x_C \ge 0, \quad S_3, S_4, S_5, S_6 \ge 0 $$

### Summary of the Model

**Minimize**
$$ K $$

**Subject to:**
1.  $K - x_A - S_3 = 100$
2.  $1.04 S_3 - x_B - x_C - S_4 = 150$
3.  $1.04 S_4 + 1.40 x_A + 1.25 x_B + 1.30 x_C - S_5 = 120$
4.  $1.04 S_5 - S_6 = 110$
5.  $x_A \le 60$
6.  $x_B \le 90$
7.  $x_C \le 50$
8.  $K, x_A, x_B, x_C, S_3, S_4, S_5, S_6 \ge 0$
