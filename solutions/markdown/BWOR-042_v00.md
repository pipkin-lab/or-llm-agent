### 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be the quantity of goods shipped from origin $i$ to destination $j$.

The indices are defined as:
*   Origins $i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ corresponding to rows I, II, and III.
*   Destinations $j \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ corresponding to columns A, B, C, D, and E.

The decision variables are:
*   $x_{11}, x_{12}, x_{13}, x_{14}, x_{15}$ (from Origin I)
*   $x_{21}, x_{22}, x_{23}, x_{24}, x_{25}$ (from Origin II)
*   $x_{31}, x_{32}, x_{33}, x_{34}, x_{35}$ (from Origin III)

### 2. Objective Function

The goal is to **Minimize** the total transportation cost. Based on the provided cost matrix:

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = 10x_{11} + 15x_{12} + 20x_{13} + 20x_{14} + 40x_{15} + 20x_{21} + 10x_{22} + 15x_{23} + 30x_{24} + 30x_{25} + 30x_{31} + 35x_{32} + 40x_{33} + 55x_{34} + 25x_{35}
$$

### 3. Operational Constraints

**Supply Constraints (Row Sums):**
The total amount shipped from each origin cannot exceed its available supply.
1.  Origin I: $x_{11} + x_{12} + x_{13} + x_{14} + x_{15} \le 50$
2.  Origin II: $x_{21} + x_{22} + x_{23} + x_{24} + x_{25} \le 100$
3.  Origin III: $x_{31} + x_{32} + x_{33} + x_{34} + x_{35} \le 150$

*(Note: Total Supply = $50 + 100 + 150 = 300$. Total Demand = $25 + 115 + 60 + 30 + 70 = 300$. Since Supply = Demand, these constraints can also be modeled as equalities ($= $).)*

**Demand Constraints (Column Sums):**
The total amount received by each destination must satisfy its demand.
4.  Destination A: $x_{11} + x_{21} + x_{31} \ge 25$
5.  Destination B: $x_{12} + x_{22} + x_{32} \ge 115$
6.  Destination C: $x_{13} + x_{23} + x_{33} \ge 60$
7.  Destination D: $x_{14} + x_{24} + x_{34} \ge 30$
8.  Destination E: $x_{15} + x_{25} + x_{35} \ge 70$

*(Note: Since the problem is balanced, these constraints are typically modeled as equalities ($= $) in standard transportation problems to ensure all supply is used and all demand is met.)*

**Non-negativity Constraints:**
All shipping quantities must be non-negative.
$$x_{ij} \ge 0 \quad \forall i, j$$
