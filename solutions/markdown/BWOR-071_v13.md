Model Type: NON-LINEAR (Quadratic Programming / QP)

**1. Index Sets & Parameters**
*   **Products**: $i \in \{1, 2\}$ corresponding to Product A and Product B.
*   **Total Daily Capacity**: $C = 5$ hours.
*   **Processing Time per Unit**: $t_1 = 1$ hour/unit, $t_2 = 1$ hour/unit.
*   **Variable Cost per Unit**: $c_1 = 4$ yuan/unit, $c_2 = 3$ yuan/unit.
*   **Price Intercept**: $a_1 = 12$, $a_2 = 13$.
*   **Price Slope**: $b_1 = 1$, $b_2 = 2$.

**2. Decision Variables**
*   $x_1$: Quantity of Product A produced per day (Continuous, $x_1 \ge 0$).
*   $x_2$: Quantity of Product B produced per day (Continuous, $x_2 \ge 0$).

*(Note: While physical units are discrete, standard economic models with linear demand curves treat quantities as continuous to find the optimal production level. Given the small numbers, the optimal solution is likely integer or near-integer, but the mathematical formulation is continuous QP.)*

**3. Objective Function**
We aim to maximize Total Profit.
Total Revenue = $p_1 x_1 + p_2 x_2 = (12 - x_1)x_1 + (13 - 2x_2)x_2$
Total Cost = $c_1 x_1 + c_2 x_2 = 4x_1 + 3x_2$

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = (12x_1 - x_1^2) + (13x_2 - 2x_2^2) - (4x_1 + 3x_2)
$$

Simplifying the objective function:
$$
\text{Maximize } Z = -x_1^2 - 2x_2^2 + 8x_1 + 10x_2
$$

**4. Operational Constraints**
1.  **Machine Operating Time Constraint**: The machine must operate exactly 5 hours per day. Since each unit takes 1 hour:
    $$
    1x_1 + 1x_2 = 5
    $$
2.  **Non-negativity**:
    $$
    x_1 \ge 0
    $$
    $$
    x_2 \ge 0
    $$
