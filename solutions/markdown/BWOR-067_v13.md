**Model Type:** QP (Quadratic Programming)

**Mandatory Cost Inventory:**
1.  **Production Cost:** $x_t^2$ (where $x_t$ is production in month $t$). This is a non-linear cost function.
2.  **Inventory Holding Cost:** 1 yuan per unit per month.

**Parameter Completeness Check:**
-   $d_1 = 100$
-   $d_2 = 110$
-   $d_3 = 120$
-   $s_0 = 0$
-   $s_3 = 0$
-   Holding cost rate $h = 1$

All parameters are provided.

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

*   **Index Set:**
    *   $T = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of months.
*   **Parameters:**
    *   $d_t$: Demand in month $t$.
        *   $d_1 = 100$
        *   $d_2 = 110$
        *   $d_3 = 120$
    *   $h$: Inventory holding cost per unit per month.
        *   $h = 1$
    *   $s_0$: Initial inventory.
        *   $s_0 = 0$
    *   $s_3$: Final inventory requirement.
        *   $s_3 = 0$

### 2. Decision Variables

*   $x_t$: Quantity of Product A produced in month $t$ for $t \in T$.
    *   Domain: $x_t \ge 0$, continuous.
*   $I_t$: Inventory level at the end of month $t$ for $t \in \{1, 2\}$.
    *   Domain: $I_t \ge 0$, continuous.
    *   *Note: $I_3$ is fixed to 0 by the problem statement ($s_3=0$), so it is not a decision variable, or can be defined as such with a bound $I_3=0$. For clarity in the objective, we only sum holding costs for months where inventory is held, i.e., end of month 1 and end of month 2. Inventory at the end of month 3 is 0, so it incurs no future holding cost in this horizon.*

### 3. Objective Function

Minimize the total production and inventory holding costs.

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{t=1}^{3} (x_t)^2 + \sum_{t=1}^{2} h \cdot I_t $$

Substituting $h=1$:
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 + I_1 + I_2 $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**Inventory Balance Constraints:**
The inventory at the end of month $t$ is equal to the previous inventory plus production minus demand.

For $t=1$:
$$ I_1 = s_0 + x_1 - d_1 $$
$$ I_1 = 0 + x_1 - 100 $$

For $t=2$:
$$ I_2 = I_1 + x_2 - d_2 $$
$$ I_2 = I_1 + x_2 - 110 $$

For $t=3$:
The problem states the ending inventory at the end of the third month is $s_3 = 0$.
$$ s_3 = I_2 + x_3 - d_3 $$
$$ 0 = I_2 + x_3 - 120 $$
$$ \Rightarrow x_3 = 120 - I_2 $$

**Non-negativity Constraints:**
$$ x_1 \ge 0, \quad x_2 \ge 0, \quad x_3 \ge 0 $$
$$ I_1 \ge 0, \quad I_2 \ge 0 $$

**Summary of Model for Solver:**

*   **Variables:** $x_1, x_2, x_3, I_1, I_2$ (all $\ge 0$)
*   **Objective:** $\min x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 + I_1 + I_2$
*   **Subject to:**
    1.  $I_1 - x_1 = -100$  (or $x_1 - I_1 = 100$)
    2.  $I_1 - I_2 - x_2 = -110$ (or $x_2 + I_1 - I_2 = 110$)
    3.  $I_2 + x_3 = 120$
    4.  $x_1, x_2, x_3, I_1, I_2 \ge 0$
