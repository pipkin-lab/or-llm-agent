### 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be the amount (in kg) of raw material $i$ used to produce brand $j$.
Where:
*   $i \in \{A, B, C\}$
*   $j \in \{X, Y, Z\}$

Specifically:
*   $x_{AX}, x_{BX}, x_{CX}$: Amounts of A, B, C in Brand X.
*   $x_{AY}, x_{BY}, x_{CY}$: Amounts of A, B, C in Brand Y.
*   $x_{AZ}, x_{BZ}, x_{CZ}$: Amounts of A, B, C in Brand Z.

Let $P_X, P_Y, P_Z$ be the total production quantity (kg) of Brand X, Y, Z.
Note: $P_X = x_{AX} + x_{BX} + x_{CX}$, etc.

### 2. Objective Function

We aim to **Maximize Total Profit**.
Profit = Total Revenue - Total Raw Material Cost - Total Processing Cost.

**Unit Economics per kg of Product:**
*   **Brand X**:
    *   Revenue: $3.40 \cdot P_X$
    *   Processing Cost: $0.50 \cdot P_X$
    *   Material Cost: $2.00 x_{AX} + 1.50 x_{BX} + 1.00 x_{CX}$
    *   Net Contribution from X: $3.40 P_X - 0.50 P_X - (2.00 x_{AX} + 1.50 x_{BX} + 1.00 x_{CX})$
    *   Simplified: $2.90 P_X - 2.00 x_{AX} - 1.50 x_{BX} - 1.00 x_{CX}$

*   **Brand Y**:
    *   Revenue: $2.85 \cdot P_Y$
    *   Processing Cost: $0.40 \cdot P_Y$
    *   Material Cost: $2.00 x_{AY} + 1.50 x_{BY} + 1.00 x_{CY}$
    *   Net Contribution from Y: $2.45 P_Y - 2.00 x_{AY} - 1.50 x_{BY} - 1.00 x_{CY}$

*   **Brand Z**:
    *   Revenue: $2.25 \cdot P_Z$
    *   Processing Cost: $0.30 \cdot P_Z$
    *   Material Cost: $2.00 x_{AZ} + 1.50 x_{BZ} + 1.00 x_{CZ}$
    *   Net Contribution from Z: $1.95 P_Z - 2.00 x_{AZ} - 1.50 x_{BZ} - 1.00 x_{CZ}$

**Objective Function:**
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = (2.90 P_X - 2.00 x_{AX} - 1.50 x_{BX} - 1.00 x_{CX}) + (2.45 P_Y - 2.00 x_{AY} - 1.50 x_{BY} - 1.00 x_{CY}) + (1.95 P_Z - 2.00 x_{AZ} - 1.50 x_{BZ} - 1.00 x_{CZ}) $$

Substituting $P_j = \sum x_{ij}$:
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 2.90(x_{AX}+x_{BX}+x_{CX}) - 2.00 x_{AX} - 1.50 x_{BX} - 1.00 x_{CX} + 2.45(x_{AY}+x_{BY}+x_{CY}) - 2.00 x_{AY} - 1.50 x_{BY} - 1.00 x_{CY} + 1.95(x_{AZ}+x_{BZ}+x_{CZ}) - 2.00 x_{AZ} - 1.50 x_{BZ} - 1.00 x_{CZ} $$

Simplifying coefficients for each variable:
*   $x_{AX}: 2.90 - 2.00 = 0.90$
*   $x_{BX}: 2.90 - 1.50 = 1.40$
*   $x_{CX}: 2.90 - 1.00 = 1.90$
*   $x_{AY}: 2.45 - 2.00 = 0.45$
*   $x_{BY}: 2.45 - 1.50 = 0.95$
*   $x_{CY}: 2.45 - 1.00 = 1.45$
*   $x_{AZ}: 1.95 - 2.00 = -0.05$
*   $x_{BZ}: 1.95 - 1.50 = 0.45$
*   $x_{CZ}: 1.95 - 1.00 = 0.95$

**Final Objective Function:**
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 0.90 x_{AX} + 1.40 x_{BX} + 1.90 x_{CX} + 0.45 x_{AY} + 0.95 x_{BY} + 1.45 x_{CY} - 0.05 x_{AZ} + 0.45 x_{BZ} + 0.95 x_{CZ} $$

### 3. Operational Constraints

**A. Composition Constraints (Proportions)**
These constraints ensure the percentage requirements for each brand are met.

*   **Brand X**:
    1.  $A \ge 60\% \implies x_{AX} \ge 0.60 (x_{AX} + x_{BX} + x_{CX}) \implies 0.40 x_{AX} - 0.60 x_{BX} - 0.60 x_{CX} \ge 0$
    2.  $C \le 20\% \implies x_{CX} \le 0.20 (x_{AX} + x_{BX} + x_{CX}) \implies -0.20 x_{AX} - 0.20 x_{BX} + 0.80 x_{CX} \le 0$

*   **Brand Y**:
    3.  $A \ge 30\% \implies x_{AY} \ge 0.30 (x_{AY} + x_{BY} + x_{CY}) \implies 0.70 x_{AY} - 0.30 x_{BY} - 0.30 x_{CY} \ge 0$
    4.  $C \le 50\% \implies x_{CY} \le 0.50 (x_{AY} + x_{BY} + x_{CY}) \implies -0.50 x_{AY} - 0.50 x_{BY} + 0.50 x_{CY} \le 0$

*   **Brand Z**:
    5.  $C \le 60\% \implies x_{CZ} \le 0.60 (x_{AZ} + x_{BZ} + x_{CZ}) \implies -0.60 x_{AZ} - 0.60 x_{BZ} + 0.40 x_{CZ} \le 0$
    *(Note: No specific constraints on A or B for Z other than non-negativity and total balance)*

**B. Raw Material Availability Constraints**
The total usage of each raw material across all brands cannot exceed the monthly limit.

6.  **Material A**: $x_{AX} + x_{AY} + x_{AZ} \le 2000$
7.  **Material B**: $x_{BX} + x_{BY} + x_{BZ} \le 2500$
8.  **Material C**: $x_{CX} + x_{CY} + x_{CZ} \le 1200$

**C. Non-negativity Constraints**
All production and material usage amounts must be non-negative.

9.  $x_{ij} \ge 0$ for all $i \in \{A,B,C\}$ and $j \in \{X,Y,Z\}$.

### Final Linear Programming Model

**Decision Variables:**
$x_{ij}$ = kg of raw material $i$ used in brand $j$, where $i \in \{A, B, C\}$ and $j \in \{X, Y, Z\}$.

**Objective Function:**
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 0.90 x_{AX} + 1.40 x_{BX} + 1.90 x_{CX} + 0.45 x_{AY} + 0.95 x_{BY} + 1.45 x_{CY} - 0.05 x_{AZ} + 0.45 x_{BZ} + 0.95 x_{CZ} $$

**Subject to:**

**Composition Constraints:**
1.  $0.40 x_{AX} - 0.60 x_{BX} - 0.60 x_{CX} \ge 0$  (Brand X: A $\ge$ 60%)
2.  $-0.20 x_{AX} - 0.20 x_{BX} + 0.80 x_{CX} \le 0$  (Brand X: C $\le$ 20%)
3.  $0.70 x_{AY} - 0.30 x_{BY} - 0.30 x_{CY} \ge 0$  (Brand Y: A $\ge$ 30%)
4.  $-0.50 x_{AY} - 0.50 x_{BY} + 0.50 x_{CY} \le 0$  (Brand Y: C $\le$ 50%)
5.  $-0.60 x_{AZ} - 0.60 x_{BZ} + 0.40 x_{CZ} \le 0$  (Brand Z: C $\le$ 60%)

**Resource Constraints:**
6.  $x_{AX} + x_{AY} + x_{AZ} \le 2000$  (Limit A)
7.  $x_{BX} + x_{BY} + x_{BZ} \le 2500$  (Limit B)
8.  $x_{CX} + x_{CY} + x_{CZ} \le 1200$  (Limit C)

**Non-negativity:**
9.  $x_{AX}, x_{BX}, x_{CX}, x_{AY}, x_{BY}, x_{CY}, x_{AZ}, x_{BZ}, x_{CZ} \ge 0$
