    *   $D^{cum}_3 = 250$
    *   $D^{cum}_4 = 370$

**2. Decision Variables**

*   $x_t$: Number of new engines purchased in period $t$ ($t \in T$). Domain: Integer $\ge 0$.
*   $f_t$: Number of engines sent for fast repair in period $t$ ($t \in T$). Domain: Integer $\ge 0$.
    *   *Note:* Engines sent in period $t$ become available in period $t+1$.
*   $s_t$: Number of engines sent for slow repair in period $t$ ($t \in T$). Domain: Integer $\ge 0$.
    *   *Note:* Engines sent in period $t$ become available in period $t+2$.

**3. Objective Function**

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{t=1}^{6} (10 x_t + 2 f_t + 10 s_t) $$

**4. Operational Constraints**

**(1) Demand Satisfaction Constraints**
The total supply of engines in each period must exactly meet the demand.
*   For $t=1$:
    $$ x_1 = 100 $$
*   For $t=2$:
    $$ x_2 + f_1 = 70 $$
*   For $t=3$:
    $$ x_3 + f_2 + s_1 = 80 $$
*   For $t=4$:
    $$ x_4 + f_3 + s_2 = 120 $$
*   For $t=5$:
    $$ x_5 + f_4 + s_3 = 150 $$
*   For $t=6$:
    $$ x_6 + f_5 + s_4 = 140 $$

**(2) Used Engine Availability Constraints**
The cumulative number of engines sent for repair (fast and slow) up to period $t$ cannot exceed the cumulative number of used engines obtained from replacements up to period $t$.
*   For $t=1$:
    $$ f_1 + s_1 \le 100 $$
*   For $t=2$:
    $$ f_1 + f_2 + s_1 + s_2 \le 170 $$
*   For $t=3$:
    $$ f_1 + f_2 + f_3 + s_1 + s_2 + s_3 \le 250 $$

    $$ \sum_{i=1}^{4} f_i + \sum_{i=1}^{4} s_i \le 370 $$
*   For $t=5$:
    $$ \sum_{i=1}^{5} f_i + \sum_{i=1}^{5} s_i \le 520 $$
*   For $t=6$:
    $$ \sum_{i=1}^{6} f_i + \sum_{i=1}^{6} s_i \le 660 $$

**(3) Non-negativity and Integrality**
$$ x_t, f_t, s_t \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0} \quad \forall t \in \{1, \dots, 6\} $$
