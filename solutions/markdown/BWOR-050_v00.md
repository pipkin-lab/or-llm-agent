# OR Model: 200-Meter Medley Relay Team Selection

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $I$ be the set of swimmers $\{Z, Q, Zh, W, Jo\}$ corresponding to Zhao, Qian, Zhang, Wang, and Zhou.
Let $S$ be the set of strokes $\{Back, Breast, Butt, Free\}$ corresponding to Backstroke, Breaststroke, Butterfly, and Freestyle.

Define binary decision variables $x_{ij}$ for each swimmer $i \in I$ and stroke $j \in S$:

$$
x_{ij} = 
\begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if swimmer } i \text{ is assigned to stroke } j \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

## 2. Objective Function

**Minimize** the total expected time for the 200-meter medley relay. The total time is the sum of the individual times of the four swimmers assigned to the four strokes.

Let $c_{ij}$ be the time (in seconds) for swimmer $i$ in stroke $j$, as given in Table 5-2.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in S} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Where the cost matrix $C = (c_{ij})$ is:
*   **Zhao ($Z$):** Back=37.7, Breast=43.4, Butt=33.3, Free=29.2
*   **Qian ($Q$):** Back=32.9, Breast=33.1, Butt=28.5, Free=26.4
*   **Zhang ($Zh$):** Back=33.8, Breast=42.2, Butt=38.9, Free=29.6
*   **Wang ($W$):** Back=37.0, Breast=34.7, Butt=30.4, Free=28.5
*   **Zhou ($Jo$):** Back=35.4, Breast=41.8, Butt=33.6, Free=31.1

## 3. Operational Constraints

The problem is modeled as an **Assignment Problem**, which is a special case of the Transportation Problem. The constraints ensure that each stroke is covered by exactly one swimmer and each swimmer is assigned to at most one stroke.

### A. Stroke Coverage Constraints
Each of the four strokes must be performed by exactly one swimmer.

$$
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall j \in S
$$

*   **Backstroke:** $x_{Z,Back} + x_{Q,Back} + x_{Zh,Back} + x_{W,Back} + x_{Jo,Back} = 1$
*   **Breaststroke:** $x_{Z,Breast} + x_{Q,Breast} + x_{Zh,Breast} + x_{W,Breast} + x_{Jo,Breast} = 1$
*   **Butterfly:** $x_{Z,Butt} + x_{Q,Butt} + x_{Zh,Butt} + x_{W,Butt} + x_{Jo,Butt} = 1$
*   **Freestyle:** $x_{Z,Free} + x_{Q,Free} + x_{Zh,Free} + x_{W,Free} + x_{Jo,Free} = 1$

### B. Swimmer Assignment Constraints
Each swimmer can be assigned to **at most one** stroke (since there are 5 swimmers and only 4 strokes, one swimmer will sit out).

$$
\sum_{j \in S} x_{ij} \leq 1 \quad \forall i \in I
$$

*   **Zhao:** $x_{Z,Back} + x_{Z,Breast} + x_{Z,Butt} + x_{Z,Free} \leq 1$
*   **Qian:** $x_{Q,Back} + x_{Q,Breast} + x_{Q,Butt} + x_{Q,Free} \leq 1$
*   **Zhang:** $x_{Zh,Back} + x_{Zh,Breast} + x_{Zh,Butt} + x_{Zh,Free} \leq 1$
*   **Wang:** $x_{W,Back} + x_{W,Breast} + x_{W,Butt} + x_{W,Free} \leq 1$
*   **Zhou:** $x_{Jo,Back} + x_{Jo,Breast} + x_{Jo,Butt} + x_{Jo,Free} \leq 1$

### C. Integrality Constraints
$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in I, j \in S
$$
