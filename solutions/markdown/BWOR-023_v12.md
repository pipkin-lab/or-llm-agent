### 4. Operational Constraints

**A. Flow Conservation (Degree Constraints)**
Each city must have exactly one incoming arc and exactly one outgoing arc.

For all $i \in V$:
1. $\sum_{j \in V, j \neq i} x_{ji} = 1$ (In-degree = 1)
2. $\sum_{j \in V, j \neq i} x_{ij} = 1$ (Out-degree = 1)

**B. Subtour Elimination (MTZ Constraints)**
To ensure a single Hamiltonian cycle rather than multiple disjoint cycles.
The root node is $1$. The constraints are written for all pairs $(i, j)$ where $i, j \in V \setminus \{1\}$ and $i \neq j$.

For all $i, j \in \{2, 3, 4\}$ with $i \neq j$:
$$ u_i - u_j + N x_{ij} \le N - 1 $$
