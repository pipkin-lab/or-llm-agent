Here is the Operations Research model for the sales outlet allocation problem.

### 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_i$ be the number of sales outlets allocated to Region $i$, where $i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$.

*   $x_1$: Number of sales outlets in Region 1
*   $x_2$: Number of sales outlets in Region 2
*   $x_3$: Number of sales outlets in Region 3

The variables $x_1, x_2, x_3$ must be non-negative integers.

### 2. Objective Function

The goal is to **Maximize** the total monthly profit. Let $P_i(x_i)$ denote the profit generated in Region $i$ when $x_i$ outlets are established. Based on Table 7-2, the profit functions are:

*   $P_1(x_1) \in \{0, 16, 25, 30, 32\}$ for $x_1 \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$
*   $P_2(x_2) \in \{0, 12, 17, 21, 22\}$ for $x_2 \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$
*   $P_3(x_3) \in \{0, 10, 14, 16, 17\}$ for $x_3 \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$

**Objective Function:**
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = P_1(x_1) + P_2(x_2) + P_3(x_3) $$

### 3. Operational Constraints

**Resource Constraint:**
The total number of sales outlets to be established is exactly 4.
$$ x_1 + x_2 + x_3 = 4 $$

**Domain Constraints:**
The number of outlets in each region must be a non-negative integer and cannot exceed the total number of outlets available (though the sum constraint already limits this, individual bounds are standard for clarity).
$$ x_1, x_2, x_3 \in \mathbb{Z}^+ \cup \{0\} $$
$$ 0 \le x_i \le 4, \quad \forall i \in \{1, 2, 3\} $$

*   **Allocation:** 2 outlets in Region 1, 1 outlet in Region 2, 1 outlet in Region 3.
*   **Maximum Profit:** $25 + 12 + 10 = 47$.
