# Operations Research Model: Nurse Scheduling (Plan 1)

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_k$ be the number of nurses assigned to the rotation pattern starting on day $k$, where $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\}$ corresponds to Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, respectively.

Since the schedule repeats weekly and each nurse works 5 consecutive days, the total number of nurses $N$ is the sum of nurses starting on each day of the week:
$$ N = \sum_{k=1}^{7} x_k $$

We define $x_k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ (non-negative integers).

## 2. Objective Function

**Minimize** the total number of nurses required for the hospital.

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{k=1}^{7} x_k $$

## 3. Operational Constraints

### A. Shift Coverage Constraints
We must ensure that for every time period and every day of the week, the number of nurses on duty meets or exceeds the required number.

**Shift Definitions:**
*   **Shift 1 (2:00–10:00):** Covers periods **22:00–6:00** (of the *previous* day) and **6:00–10:00** (of the *current* day).
    *   *Note on 22:00–6:00:* A nurse on Shift 1 on day $d$ covers the 22:00–6:00 slot that starts on day $d-1$ and ends on day $d$. In weekly cyclic scheduling, "day $d-1$" for Monday is Sunday.
    *   *Note on 6:00–10:00:* A nurse on Shift 1 on day $d$ covers the 6:00–10:00 slot of day $d$.
*   **Shift 2 (6:00–14:00):** Covers periods **6:00–10:00** and **10:00–14:00** of day $d$.
*   **Shift 3 (10:00–18:00):** Covers periods **10:00–14:00** and **14:00–18:00** of day $d$.
*   **Shift 4 (14:00–22:00):** Covers periods **14:00–18:00** and **18:00–22:00** of day $d$.
*   **Shift 5 (18:00–2:00):** Covers periods **18:00–22:00** of day $d$ and **22:00–6:00** of day $d+1$.

**Requirement Matrix per Day:**
Let $R_{d, t}$ be the required nurses on day $d$ for time period $t$.
*   $R_{d, 6-10} = 18$
*   $R_{d, 10-14} = 20$
*   $R_{d, 14-18} = 19$
*   $R_{d, 18-22} = 17$
*   $R_{d, 22-6} = 12$ (This requirement applies to the night shift ending on day $d$. The nurses providing this coverage are those on Shift 1 of day $d$ and Shift 5 of day $d-1$).

**Formulation of Constraints by Day ($d$) and Period:**

For a specific day $d$ (where $d=1$ is Monday, ..., $d=7$ is Sunday), let $x_k$ be the nurses starting their 5-day cycle on day $k$.
A nurse starting on day $k$ works on day $d$ if $d \in \{k, k+1, k+2, k+3, k+4\}$ (mod 7).
The specific shift assigned on day $d$ depends on the offset $(d-k) \pmod 5$:
*   Offset 0: Shift 1
*   Offset 1: Shift 2
*   Offset 2: Shift 3
*   Offset 3: Shift 4
*   Offset 4: Shift 5

Let's denote $S_{d, shift}$ as the set of start-days $k$ such that a nurse starting on $k$ works `shift` on day $d$.
The number of nurses on a specific shift on day $d$ is $\sum_{k \in S_{d, shift}} x_k$.

The constraints for each time period on day $d$ are:

1.  **Period 6:00–10:00 (Requirement: 18)**
    Covered by Shift 1 and Shift 2 on day $d$.
    $$ \sum_{k \in S_{d, 1}} x_k + \sum_{k \in S_{d, 2}} x_k \ge 18 $$

2.  **Period 10:00–14:00 (Requirement: 20)**
    Covered by Shift 2 and Shift 3 on day $d$.
    $$ \sum_{k \in S_{d, 2}} x_k + \sum_{k \in S_{d, 3}} x_k \ge 20 $$

3.  **Period 14:00–18:00 (Requirement: 19)**
    Covered by Shift 3 and Shift 4 on day $d$.
    $$ \sum_{k \in S_{d, 3}} x_k + \sum_{k \in S_{d, 4}} x_k \ge 19 $$

4.  **Period 18:00–22:00 (Requirement: 17)**
    Covered by Shift 4 and Shift 5 on day $d$.
    $$ \sum_{k \in S_{d, 4}} x_k + \sum_{k \in S_{d, 5}} x_k \ge 17 $$

5.  **Period 22:00–6:00 (Requirement: 12)**
    This night shift covers the end of day $d-1$ and start of day $d$. It is covered by Shift 5 of day $d-1$ and Shift 1 of day $d$.
    $$ \sum_{k \in S_{d-1, 5}} x_k + \sum_{k \in S_{d, 1}} x_k \ge 12 $$
    *(Note: For $d=1$ (Monday), $d-1$ is Sunday ($d=7$). Indices wrap around modulo 7.)*
