# Optimal Machine Replacement Strategy: Operations Research Model

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be a binary decision variable indicating whether a machine purchased at the beginning of year $i$ is sold at the end of year $j$.

*   **Indices:**
    *   $i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: The year in which a new machine is purchased.
    *   $j \in \{i, i+1, \dots, 4\}$: The year in which the machine purchased in year $i$ is sold. Note that $j \ge i$ because a machine cannot be sold before it is purchased.
    *   Specifically, the possible purchase-sale pairs $(i, j)$ are:
        *   From Year 1: $(1,1), (1,2), (1,3), (1,4)$
        *   From Year 2: $(2,2), (2,3), (2,4)$
        *   From Year 3: $(3,3), (3,4)$
        *   From Year 4: $(4,4)$

*   **Variable Definition:**
    $$
    x_{ij} = \begin{cases} 
    1 & \text{if a machine is purchased at the start of year } i \text{ and sold at the end of year } j \\
    0 & \text{otherwise}
    \end{cases}
    $$

*Note: This formulation assumes that at the start of any year where a machine is not continuing from a previous purchase, a new one must be bought. The constraints below enforce that every year is covered by exactly one active machine.*

## 2. Objective Function

**Minimize** the total cost over the 4-year horizon.

The total cost is the sum of the costs associated with each possible purchase-sale interval $(i, j)$. The cost of purchasing a machine at the start of year $i$ and selling it at the end of year $j$ consists of:
1.  **Purchase Price** at the start of year $i$: $P_i$
2.  **Operating and Maintenance (O&M) Costs** for each year the machine is used (from year $i$ to year $j$).
    *   Let $c_k$ be the O&M cost for a machine in its $k$-th year of service.
    *   $c_1 = 0.3$
    *   $c_2 = 0.8$
    *   $c_3 = 1.5$
    *   $c_4 = 2.0$
    *   The total O&M cost for interval $(i, j)$ is $\sum_{t=i}^{j} c_{t-i+1}$.
3.  **Minus** the **Salvage Value** received at the end of year $j$ after $(j-i+1)$ years of use. Let $S_{j-i+1}$ be the salvage value after $k$ years of use.
    *   $S_1 = 2.0$
    *   $S_2 = 1.6$
    *   $S_3 = 1.3$
    *   $S_4 = 1.1$

Let $C_{ij}$ be the net cost of purchasing in year $i$ and selling in year $j$:
$$ C_{ij} = P_i + \sum_{t=i}^{j} c_{t-i+1} - S_{j-i+1} $$

The objective function is:
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{4} \sum_{j=i}^{4} C_{ij} x_{ij} $$

## 3. Operational Constraints

The core constraint is that **exactly one machine must be in operation during each year**. A machine purchased in year $i$ and sold in year $j$ is in operation during years $i, i+1, \dots, j$.

Therefore, for each year $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, the sum of all decisions $x_{ij}$ such that the interval $[i, j]$ covers year $k$ must equal 1.

*   **Year 1 Coverage:**
    $$ x_{11} + x_{12} + x_{13} + x_{14} = 1 $$
    *(Any machine active in Year 1 must have been purchased in Year 1)*

*   **Year 2 Coverage:**
    $$ x_{12} + x_{13} + x_{14} + x_{22} + x_{23} + x_{24} = 1 $$
    *(Machines active in Year 2 are either purchased in Year 1 and kept, or purchased in Year 2)*

*   **Year 3 Coverage:**
    $$ x_{13} + x_{14} + x_{23} + x_{24} + x_{33} + x_{34} = 1 $$
    *(Machines active in Year 3 are from Year 1, 2, or 3 purchases)*

*   **Year 4 Coverage:**
    $$ x_{14} + x_{24} + x_{34} + x_{44} = 1 $$
    *(Machines active in Year 4 are from Year 1, 2, 3, or 4 purchases)*

*   **Binary Constraints:**
    $$ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i, j \text{ where } 1 \le i \le j \le 4 $$
