# Warehouse Space Rental Optimization Model

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{i,j}$ be the amount of warehouse space (in $m^2$) rented for a contract signed at the beginning of **Month $i$** with a duration of **$j$ months**.

*   **Index $i$**: Start month of the contract, where $i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.
*   **Index $j$**: Duration of the contract in months, where $j \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.
*   **Feasibility Constraint on Indices**: A contract starting in month $i$ with duration $j$ is only valid if it does not exceed the 4-month planning horizon. Therefore, $x_{i,j}$ is defined only if $i + j - 1 \le 4$.
    *   Valid pairs $(i,j)$:
        *   $i=1$: $j \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$
        *   $i=2$: $j \in \{1, 2, 3\}$
        *   $i=3$: $j \in \{1, 2\}$
        *   $i=4$: $j \in \{1\}$
*   **Domain**: $x_{i,j} \ge 0$ for all valid $(i,j)$.

## 2. Objective Function

**Minimize** the total rental cost.

The cost is calculated as the sum over all valid contracts of (Area Rented $\times$ Total Cost per $m^2$ for that duration).

Let $c_j$ be the total rental fee per $m^2$ for a contract of duration $j$ months (from Table 1-15):
*   $c_1 = 28$
*   $c_2 = 45$
*   $c_3 = 60$
*   $c_4 = 73$

**Objective Function:**
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{4} \sum_{j=1}^{4-i+1} c_j \cdot x_{i,j} $$

Expanded form:
$$ Z = 28x_{1,1} + 45x_{1,2} + 60x_{1,3} + 73x_{1,4} + 28x_{2,1} + 45x_{2,2} + 60x_{2,3} + 28x_{3,1} + 45x_{3,2} + 28x_{4,1} $$

## 3. Operational Constraints

For each month $t$ ($t \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$), the total warehouse space available from all active contracts must be greater than or equal to the required space for that month.

A contract $x_{i,j}$ contributes to the capacity in month $t$ if and only if:
1.  The contract starts in or before month $t$ ($i \le t$)
2.  The contract ends in or after month $t$ ($i + j - 1 \ge t$)

**Capacity Constraints:**

**Month 1 Requirement (1,500 $m^2$):**
Contracts active in Month 1 must start at $i=1$.
$$ x_{1,1} + x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} + x_{1,4} \ge 1500 $$

**Month 2 Requirement (1,000 $m^2$):**
Contracts active in Month 2 are those starting in Month 1 (with duration $\ge 2$) and those starting in Month 2 (with duration $\ge 1$).
$$ x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} + x_{1,4} + x_{2,1} + x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} \ge 1000 $$

**Month 3 Requirement (2,000 $m^2$):**
Contracts active in Month 3 are those starting in Month 1 (with duration $\ge 3$), starting in Month 2 (with duration $\ge 2$), and starting in Month 3 (with duration $\ge 1$).
$$ x_{1,3} + x_{1,4} + x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} + x_{3,1} + x_{3,2} \ge 2000 $$

**Month 4 Requirement (1,200 $m^2$):**
Contracts active in Month 4 are those starting in Month 1 (duration 4), starting in Month 2 (duration 3), starting in Month 3 (duration 2), and starting in Month 4 (duration 1).
$$ x_{1,4} + x_{2,3} + x_{3,2} + x_{4,1} \ge 1200 $$

**Non-negativity Constraints:**
$$ x_{i,j} \ge 0 \quad \forall \text{ valid } i,j $$
