# Production Planning Problem Analysis

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_t$ denote the quantity of Product A produced in month $t$, for $t = 1, 2, 3$.

Let $s_t$ denote the ending inventory level at the end of month $t$, for $t = 1, 2, 3$.

*   $x_t \ge 0$ for all $t$
*   $s_t \ge 0$ for all $t$

## 2. Objective Function

The goal is to **Minimize** the total cost, which consists of production costs and inventory holding costs.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{t=1}^{3} \left( x_t^2 + 1 \cdot s_t \right)
$$

Where:
*   $x_t^2$ is the production cost in month $t$.
*   $1 \cdot s_t$ is the inventory holding cost for the units remaining at the end of month $t$ (cost is 1 yuan per unit per month).

## 3. Operational Constraints

**Inventory Balance Constraints (Mass Balance):**
The inventory at the end of a month equals the inventory at the beginning of the month plus the production in that month minus the demand met in that month.

$$
s_t = s_{t-1} + x_t - d_t, \quad \text{for } t = 1, 2, 3
$$

Subject to specific boundary conditions and demand values:
1.  **Month 1:** $s_1 = 0 + x_1 - 100$
2.  **Month 2:** $s_2 = s_1 + x_2 - 110$
3.  **Month 3:** $s_3 = s_2 + x_3 - 120$

**Boundary Conditions:**
*   Initial Inventory: $s_0 = 0$
*   Final Inventory: $s_3 = 0$

**Non-negativity Constraints:**
*   Production quantities must be non-negative: $x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0$
*   Inventory levels must be non-negative: $s_1, s_2, s_3 \ge 0$
