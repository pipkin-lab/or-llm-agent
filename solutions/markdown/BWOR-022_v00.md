# Operations Research Model: Spare Parts Allocation

## 1. Problem Analysis
This is a **Resource Allocation Problem** (specifically, a discrete dynamic programming or integer programming problem) where a limited budget must be distributed among three components to maximize system reliability.

*   **System Structure:** Components $A_1, A_2, A_3$ work in **series**.
*   **Reliability Metric:** The problem specifies maximizing the **sum of the logarithms** of component reliabilities. Mathematically, $\ln(R_{total}) = \ln(R_1 \cdot R_2 \cdot R_3) = \ln(R_1) + \ln(R_2) + \ln(R_3)$. Maximizing the sum of logs is equivalent to maximizing the product of the individual reliabilities.
*   **Constraint:** Total cost $\le 8$ thousand yuan.
*   **Decision:** Select the number of spare parts for each component from the available options (1, 2, or 3).

## 2. Mathematical Formulation

### Decision Variables
Let $x_i$ be the number of spare parts allocated to component $A_i$, where $i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$.
Based on the table, the domain for each variable is discrete:
$$ x_i \in \{1, 2, 3\}, \quad i = 1, 2, 3 $$

### Parameters
From Table 7-19, we define the following parameters for component $i$ given $x_i$ spares:
*   $R_i(x_i)$: Reliability of component $i$ with $x_i$ spares.
*   $C_i(x_i)$: Cost of component $i$ with $x_i$ spares (in thousand yuan).

The specific values are:
| $x$ | $R_1(x)$ | $R_2(x)$ | $R_3(x)$ | $C_1(x)$ | $C_2(x)$ | $C_3(x)$ |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1** | 0.3 | 0.2 | 0.1 | 1 | 3 | 2 |
| **2** | 0.4 | 0.5 | 0.2 | 2 | 5 | 3 |
| **3** | 0.5 | 0.9 | 0.7 | 3 | 6 | 4 |

### Objective Function
Maximize the sum of the natural logarithms of the component reliabilities:
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \ln(R_1(x_1)) + \ln(R_2(x_2)) + \ln(R_3(x_3)) $$

### Constraints
1.  **Budget Constraint:** The total cost must not exceed 8 thousand yuan.
    $$ C_1(x_1) + C_2(x_2) + C_3(x_3) \le 8 $$
2.  **Discrete Domain Constraint:**
    $$ x_1, x_2, x_3 \in \{1, 2, 3\} $$

## 3. Solution Analysis (Enumeration Method)
Since the decision space is small ($3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27$ combinations), we can evaluate all feasible solutions.

Let's list the feasible combinations $(x_1, x_2, x_3)$ where $C_1(x_1) + C_2(x_2) + C_3(x_3) \le 8$.

**Step 1: Determine Cost Combinations**
Possible costs for $A_1$: $\{1, 2, 3\}$
Possible costs for $A_2$: $\{3, 5, 6\}$
Possible costs for $A_3$: $\{2, 3, 4\}$

We iterate through possible $x_1$ and $x_2$ to find valid $x_3$.

**Case 1: $x_1 = 1$ ($C_1=1, R_1=0.3, \ln R_1 \approx -1.204$)**
*   **Subcase 1.1: $x_2 = 1$ ($C_2=3, R_2=0.2, \ln R_2 \approx -1.609$)**
    *   Remaining Budget for $A_3$: $8 - (1+3) = 4$.
    *   Possible $x_3$:
        *   $x_3=1$ ($C_3=2 \le 4$): Total Cost=6. $Z = \ln(0.3) + \ln(0.2) + \ln(0.1) = \ln(0.006) \approx -5.116$.
        *   $x_3=2$ ($C_3=3 \le 4$): Total Cost=7. $Z = \ln(0.3) + \ln(0.2) + \ln(0.2) = \ln(0.012) \approx -4.423$.
        *   $x_3=3$ ($C_3=4 \le 4$): Total Cost=8. $Z = \ln(0.3) + \ln(0.2) + \ln(0.7) = \ln(0.042) \approx -3.171$.
*   **Subcase 1.2: $x_2 = 2$ ($C_2=5, R_2=0.5, \ln R_2 \approx -0.693$)**
    *   Remaining Budget for $A_3$: $8 - (1+5) = 2$.
    *   Possible $x_3$:
        *   $x_3=1$ ($C_3=2 \le 2$): Total Cost=8. $Z = \ln(0.3) + \ln(0.5) + \ln(0.1) = \ln(0.015) \approx -4.199$.
        *   ($x_3=2$ requires cost 3, $1+5+3=9 > 8$, infeasible).
*   **Subcase 1.3: $x_2 = 3$ ($C_2=6, R_2=0.9, \ln R_2 \approx -0.105$)**
    *   Remaining Budget for $A_3$: $8 - (1+6) = 1$.
    *   Possible $x_3$:
        *   Minimum cost for $A_3$ is 2. $1 < 2$, so **No feasible solution** for this subcase.

**Case 2: $x_1 = 2$ ($C_1=2, R_1=0.4, \ln R_1 \approx -0.916$)**
*   **Subcase 2.1: $x_2 = 1$ ($C_2=3, R_2=0.2, \ln R_2 \approx -1.609$)**
    *   Remaining Budget for $A_3$: $8 - (2+3) = 3$.
    *   Possible $x_3$:
        *   $x_3=1$ ($C_3=2 \le 3$): Total Cost=7. $Z = \ln(0.4) + \ln(0.2) + \ln(0.1) = \ln(0.008) \approx -4.828$.
        *   $x_3=2$ ($C_3=3 \le 3$): Total Cost=8. $Z = \ln(0.4) + \ln(0.2) + \ln(0.2) = \ln(0.016) \approx -4.135$.
*   **Subcase 2.2: $x_2 = 2$ ($C_2=5, R_2=0.5, \ln R_2 \approx -0.693$)**
    *   Remaining Budget for $A_3$: $8 - (2+5) = 1$.
    *   Minimum cost for $A_3$ is 2. **No feasible solution**.
*   **Subcase 2.3: $x_2 = 3$ ($C_2=6$)**
    *   $2+6=8$, Remaining for $A_3$ is 0. **No feasible solution**.

**Case 3: $x_1 = 3$ ($C_1=3, R_1=0.5, \ln R_1 \approx -0.693$)**
*   **Subcase 3.1: $x_2 = 1$ ($C_2=3, R_2=0.2, \ln R_2 \approx -1.609$)**
    *   Remaining Budget for $A_3$: $8 - (3+3) = 2$.
    *   Possible $x_3$:
        *   $x_3=1$ ($C_3=2 \le 2$): Total Cost=8. $Z = \ln(0.5) + \ln(0.2) + \ln(0.1) = \ln(0.01) = -2.303 \times 2 \approx -4.605$.
*   **Subcase 3.2: $x_2 = 2$ ($C_2=5$)**
    *   $3+5=8$, Remaining for $A_3$ is 0. **No feasible solution**.
*   **Subcase 3.3: $x_2 = 3$ ($C_2=6$)**
    *   $3+6=9 > 8$. **Infeasible**.

**Comparison of Feasible Solutions:**

| $x_1$ | $x_2$ | $x_3$ | Total Cost | Reliability Product ($R_1 R_2 R_3$) | Objective $Z = \ln(\text{Product})$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 6 | $0.3 \times 0.2 \times 0.1 = 0.006$ | -5.116 |
| 1 | 1 | 2 | 7 | $0.3 \times 0.2 \times 0.2 = 0.012$ | -4.423 |
| **1** | **1** | **3** | **8** | **$0.3 \times 0.2 \times 0.7 = 0.042$** | **-3.171** |
| 1 | 2 | 1 | 8 | $0.3 \times 0.5 \times 0.1 = 0.015$ | -4.199 |
| 2 | 1 | 1 | 7 | $0.4 \times 0.2 \times 0.1 = 0.008$ | -4.828 |
| 2 | 1 | 2 | 8 | $0.4 \times 0.2 \times 0.2 = 0.016$ | -4.135 |
| 3 | 1 | 1 | 8 | $0.5 \times 0.2 \times 0.1 = 0.010$ | -4.605 |

**Maximum Value:**
The maximum objective function value is approximately **-3.171**, which corresponds to the allocation:
*   $x_1 = 1$
*   $x_2 = 1$
*   $x_3 = 3$

## 4. Final Conclusion

To maximize the equipment's operational reliability within the budget of 8 thousand yuan, the spare parts should be allocated as follows:

*   **Component $A_1$:** 1 spare part
*   **Component $A_2$:** 1 spare part
*   **Component $A_3$:** 3 spare parts

**Verification:**
*   **Total Cost:** $C_1(1) + C_2(1) + C_3(3) = 1 + 3 + 4 = 8$ thousand yuan (Within Budget).
*   **System Reliability:** $R_{total} = 0.3 \times 0.2 \times 0.7 = 0.042$.
*   **Objective Value:** $\ln(0.042) \approx -3.171$.
