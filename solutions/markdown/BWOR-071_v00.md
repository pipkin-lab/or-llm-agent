# Operations Research Model Analysis

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_1$ and $x_2$ denote the daily production quantities of Product A and Product B, respectively.

*   $x_1$: Number of units of Product A produced per day ($x_1 \ge 0$)
*   $x_2$: Number of units of Product B produced per day ($x_2 \ge 0$)

## 2. Objective Function

**Goal:** Maximize Total Profit ($Z$)

**Derivation:**
*   **Revenue for A:** $R_1 = p_1 \cdot x_1 = (12 - x_1)x_1 = 12x_1 - x_1^2$
*   **Revenue for B:** $R_2 = p_2 \cdot x_2 = (13 - 2x_2)x_2 = 13x_2 - 2x_2^2$
*   **Total Cost for A:** $C_1 = 4x_1$
*   **Total Cost for B:** $C_2 = 3x_2$
*   **Total Profit:** $Z = (R_1 + R_2) - (C_1 + C_2)$
    $$Z = (12x_1 - x_1^2 + 13x_2 - 2x_2^2) - (4x_1 + 3x_2)$$
    $$Z = -x_1^2 + 8x_1 - 2x_2^2 + 10x_2$$

**Mathematical Formulation:**
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = -x_1^2 + 8x_1 - 2x_2^2 + 10x_2 $$

## 3. Operational Constraints

1.  **Machine Time Constraint:**
    The machine operates exactly 5 hours per day. Since each unit of A requires 1 hour and each unit of B requires 1 hour, the sum of production quantities must equal the available time.
    $$ x_1 + x_2 = 5 $$

2.  **Non-negativity Constraints:**
    Production quantities cannot be negative.
    $$ x_1 \ge 0, \quad x_2 \ge 0 $$

*(Note: Implicitly, prices must remain non-negative for the market model to hold, i.e., $12-x_1 \ge 0 \Rightarrow x_1 \le 12$ and $13-2x_2 \ge 0 \Rightarrow x_2 \le 6.5$. However, since $x_1 + x_2 = 5$, both variables are naturally bounded below 5, satisfying these conditions automatically.)*

---

### Summary of the Optimization Model

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Maximize} \quad & Z = -x_1^2 + 8x_1 - 2x_2^2 + 10x_2 \\
\text{Subject to} \quad & x_1 + x_2 = 5 \\
& x_1, x_2 \ge 0
\end{aligned}
$$
