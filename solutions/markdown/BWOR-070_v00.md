# Problem Formulation: Unbounded Knapsack / Truck Loading Optimization

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_i$ be the number of units of good type $i$ to be loaded onto the truck.

*   **Domain**: $x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ (Non-negative integers) for $i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.
    *   *Note: Since the problem states "no limit on the number of items," this is an unbounded knapsack problem variant. If items are indivisible, $x_i$ must be integer. If items are divisible (bulk goods), $x_i$ would be continuous ($x_i \ge 0$). Given "types of goods" and standard OR context for such tables, we assume discrete units (integer variables).*

## 2. Objective Function

**Maximize** the total value of the cargo:

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = 3x_1 + 4x_2 + 5x_3 + 6x_4
$$

## 3. Operational Constraints

### a. Weight Capacity Constraint
The total weight of all loaded goods must not exceed the truck's maximum weight capacity of 15 tons.

$$
2x_1 + 3x_2 + 4x_3 + 5x_4 \le 15
$$

*   *Coefficients derived from Table 7-9 (Weight in tons per unit).*

### b. Volume Capacity Constraint
The total volume of all loaded goods must not exceed the truck's maximum volume capacity of 10 cubic meters.

$$
2x_1 + 2x_2 + 2x_3 + 3x_4 \le 10
$$

*   *Coefficients derived from Table 7-9 (Volume in m³ per unit).*

### c. Non-negativity and Integrality
$$
x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, \quad \forall i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}
$$
