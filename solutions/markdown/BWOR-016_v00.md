# Operations Research Model: Factory Expansion and Transportation

## 1. Decision Variables

The problem involves two types of decisions: selecting the new factory to build and determining the flow of goods from all factories to demand locations.

**Binary Variables (Factory Selection):**
Let $y_k$ be a binary variable for the new factory options ($k \in \{3, 4\}$):
$$
y_k = \begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if factory } A_k \text{ is built} \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
*Note: Since the problem asks to decide "whether to build $A_3$ **or** $A_4$" and implies a single new factory is needed to meet the supply gap, we assume exactly one of them is selected. If the problem allowed building neither (if existing capacity sufficed, which it does not here as Total Demand 1200 > Existing Supply 1000), the constraint would be $\sum y_k \ge 1$. Given the context "a new factory needs to be built", we assume $\sum y_k = 1$.*

**Continuous Variables (Transportation Flow):**
Let $x_{ij}$ be the amount of material (in kilotons/year) shipped from factory $A_i$ to demand location $B_j$.
$$
x_{ij} \ge 0, \quad \forall i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}, \forall j \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}
$$

## 2. Objective Function

**Minimize Total Annual Cost**
The total cost consists of the fixed annual production costs for the new factory and the variable transportation costs.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{k=3}^{4} F_k y_k + \sum_{i=1}^{4} \sum_{j=1}^{4} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Where:
*   $F_3 = 1200$ (Fixed cost for $A_3$)
*   $F_4 = 1500$ (Fixed cost for $A_4$)
*   $c_{ij}$ is the unit transportation cost from $A_i$ to $B_j$.

Explicitly:
$$
Z = 1200 y_3 + 1500 y_4 + \sum_{i=1}^{4} \sum_{j=1}^{4} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

## 3. Operational Constraints

### A. Supply Capacity Constraints
The total amount shipped from a factory cannot exceed its production capacity. For existing factories $A_1$ and $A_2$, capacity is always available. For new factories $A_3$ and $A_4$, production is only possible if the factory is built.

For $A_1$ (Capacity 400):
$$
\sum_{j=1}^{4} x_{1j} \le 400
$$

For $A_2$ (Capacity 600):
$$
\sum_{j=1}^{4} x_{2j} \le 600
$$

For $A_3$ (Capacity 200, conditional on $y_3$):
$$
\sum_{j=1}^{4} x_{3j} \le 200 y_3
$$

For $A_4$ (Capacity 200, conditional on $y_4$):
$$
\sum_{j=1}^{4} x_{4j} \le 200 y_4
$$

### B. Demand Satisfaction Constraints
The total amount received at each demand location must exactly meet the demand (or at least meet it, but in standard transportation problems with no inventory cost mentioned, equality is standard).

For $B_1$ (Demand 350):
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i1} = 350
$$

For $B_2$ (Demand 400):
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i2} = 400
$$

For $B_3$ (Demand 300):
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i3} = 300
$$

For $B_4$ (Demand 150):
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i4} = 150
$$

### C. Factory Selection Logic Constraint
Exactly one new factory must be built to satisfy the total demand deficit (Total Demand = 1200, Existing Supply = 1000, Deficit = 200. Both new factories have capacity 200).

$$
y_3 + y_4 = 1
$$

### D. Non-negativity and Integrality Constraints
$$
x_{ij} \ge 0 \quad \forall i, j
$$
$$
y_k \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall k \in \{3, 4\}
$$
