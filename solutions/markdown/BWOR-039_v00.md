# Production Planning Linear Programming Model

## 1. Decision Variables

Let the monthly production quantities be defined as:
*   $x_1$: Number of bundles of manuscript paper produced.
*   $x_2$: Number of dozens of diaries produced.
*   $x_3$: Number of boxes of exercise books produced.

All variables must be non-negative ($x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0$).

## 2. Objective Function

The goal is to maximize the total monthly profit.
*   Profit per bundle of manuscript paper: 2 yuan
*   Profit per dozen of diaries: 3 yuan
*   Profit per box of exercise books: 1 yuan

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 2x_1 + 3x_2 + 1x_3 $$

## 3. Operational Constraints

**A. Labor Constraint**
The factory has 100 workers. The labor productivity is 30 units per person per month for all product types.
*   Labor required for manuscript paper: $x_1 / 30$ person-months
*   Labor required for diaries: $x_2 / 30$ person-months
*   Labor required for exercise books: $x_3 / 30$ person-months

Total labor usage cannot exceed the available workforce:
$$ \frac{x_1}{30} + \frac{x_2}{30} + \frac{x_3}{30} \le 100 $$
*(Simplified: $x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \le 3000$)*

**B. Raw Material (White Base Paper) Constraint**
The monthly supply of white base paper is 30,000 kg.
*   Consumption per bundle of manuscript paper: $3\frac{1}{3} \text{ kg} = \frac{10}{3} \text{ kg}$
*   Consumption per dozen of diaries: $13\frac{1}{3} \text{ kg} = \frac{40}{3} \text{ kg}$
*   Consumption per box of exercise books: $26\frac{2}{3} \text{ kg} = \frac{80}{3} \text{ kg}$

Total raw material usage cannot exceed the available supply:
$$ \frac{10}{3}x_1 + \frac{40}{3}x_2 + \frac{80}{3}x_3 \le 30000 $$
*(Simplified: $10x_1 + 40x_2 + 80x_3 \le 90000$ or $x_1 + 4x_2 + 8x_3 \le 9000$)*

**C. Non-negativity Constraints**
$$ x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0 $$
