**Objective Identification Gate:** "The problem asks me to **MAXIMIZE** **total profit (yuan)** subject to supply/demand balance, minimum supply to Region III (≥100 tons), and full satisfaction of Region IV demand."

**Unit Conversion:** All quantities in tons, prices/costs in yuan/kg. Profit per kg = (sales_price_j − production_cost_i − transport_cost_ij). Total profit (yuan) = Σ (x_ij [tons] × 1000 [kg/ton] × net_margin_ij [yuan/kg]). I will define coefficients in **yuan/ton** by multiplying per-kg margins by 1000.

## Decision Variables

$$x_{ij} = \text{quantity shipped from plant } i \text{ to region } j \text{ (tons)}, \quad \forall i \in I,\; j \in J$$

**Domain:** $x_{ij} \geq 0$, continuous (tons is a continuous quantity in this context).

---

## Objective Function

$$\max \quad Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} \pi_{ij} \; x_{ij}$$

where $\pi_{ij} = 1000(s_j - p_i - c_{ij})$ is the net profit per ton (yuan/ton) as tabulated above.

Expanded:
$$Z = 300x_{A,I} + 800x_{A,II} + 300x_{A,III} + 600x_{A,IV} + 100x_{A,V} + 700x_{A,VI}$$
$$+ 300x_{B,I} + 200x_{B,II} - 500x_{B,III} + 300x_{B,IV} - 400x_{B,V} + 400x_{B,VI}$$
$$+ 200x_{C,I} + 600x_{C,II} + 400x_{C,III} + 400x_{C,IV} + 100x_{C,V} + 500x_{C,VI}$$
$$- 100x_{D,I} + 500x_{D,II} + 100x_{D,III} + 100x_{D,IV} - 400x_{D,V} - 300x_{D,VI}$$

**Objective Parameter Cross-Reference:** All $\pi_{ij}$ values are fully determined by the extracted parameters $s_j$, $p_i$, $c_{ij}$. No [NOT_PROVIDED] values remain.

## Operational Constraints

### 1. Supply Constraints (EQUALITY — all supply must be shipped; total supply 1000 < total demand 1250)

$$\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} = S_i, \quad \forall i \in I$$

Explicitly:
- $\sum_j x_{A,j} = 200$
- $\sum_j x_{B,j} = 300$
- $\sum_j x_{C,j} = 400$
- $\sum_j x_{D,j} = 100$

### 2. Demand Constraints

For regions where demand is **not** required to be fully satisfied (I, II, III, V, VI):
$$\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \leq D_j, \quad j \in \{I, II, III, V, VI\}$$

- $\sum_i x_{i,I} \leq 200$
- $\sum_i x_{i,II} \leq 150$
- $\sum_i x_{i,III} \leq 400$
- $\sum_i x_{i,V} \leq 150$
- $\sum_i x_{i,VI} \leq 150$

For Region IV (must be **fully** satisfied):
$$\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,IV} = D_{IV} = 100$$

### 3. Minimum Supply to Region III

$$\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,III} \geq 100$$

### 4. Non-negativity

$$x_{ij} \geq 0, \quad \forall i \in I, \; j \in J$$

## Feasibility Sanity Check

- Total supply = 1000 tons. Total demand = 1250 tons. Supply < Demand → supply-side equalities pin total flow at 1000 tons.
- Region IV requires exactly 100 tons. Region III requires at least 100 tons.
- Remaining 800 tons must be allocated to regions I (≤200), II (≤150), V (≤150), VI (≤150), and III (up to 400, min 100).
- Maximum allocatable to I+II+V+VI+III = 200+150+150+150+400 = 1050 ≥ 800. Feasible.
- **Zero-solution test:** Supply constraints are equalities ($\sum_j x_{ij} = S_i > 0$), so $x_{ij} = 0$ for all $i,j$ violates them. The trivial solution is excluded. ✓

---

## Final Magnitude Sanity Check

Consider a greedy allocation: ship to highest-margin cells.
- Best margins: $x_{A,II}=800$, $x_{A,IV}=600$, $x_{A,VI}=700$, $x_{C,II}=600$, $x_{D,II}=500$, $x_{C,VI}=500$, etc.
- A rough greedy solution: $x_{A,II}=150$ (full region II), $x_{A,IV}=50$, $x_{A,VI}=100$, $x_{A,I}=0$... Let's estimate: if we allocate ~1000 tons at average margin ~400 yuan/ton, profit ≈ 400,000 yuan. This is a reasonable magnitude for a problem involving 1000 tons at margins of 100–800 yuan/ton. The objective is in **yuan**, consistent with the problem's currency. ✓
