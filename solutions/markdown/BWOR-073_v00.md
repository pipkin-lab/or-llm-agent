# Dynamic Programming Model for Patrol Unit Assignment

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_i$ be the number of patrol units assigned to warehouse $i$, where $i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.

- **Domain**: $x_i \in \{2, 3, 4\}$ for all $i$.
- **State Variable**: $k$ represents the total number of patrol units available for allocation among the remaining warehouses.

## 2. Objective Function

**Minimize** the total expected number of accidents per year:

$$
\min \sum_{i=1}^{4} C_i(x_i)
$$

where $C_i(x_i)$ is the expected number of accidents for warehouse $i$ given $x_i$ patrol units, defined by the table:

| $x_i$ | $C_1(x_i)$ | $C_2(x_i)$ | $C_3(x_i)$ | $C_4(x_i)$ |
|-------|------------|------------|------------|------------|
| 2     | 18         | 38         | 14         | 34         |
| 3     | 16         | 36         | 12         | 31         |
| 4     | 12         | 30         | 11         | 25         |

## 3. Operational Constraints

1. **Total Units Constraint**:
   $$
   \sum_{i=1}^{4} x_i = 12
   $$

2. **Per-Warehouse Limits**:
   $$
   2 \leq x_i \leq 4, \quad \forall i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}
   $$

## 4. Dynamic Programming Formulation

### Stage Definition
- **Stage $s$**: Corresponds to assigning patrol units to warehouse $s$, where $s = 1, 2, 3, 4$ (processed in reverse order for the recursive backward pass).

### State Definition
- **State $k$**: The number of patrol units available to be distributed among warehouses $s, s+1, \dots, 4$.

### Recurrence Relation
Let $f_s(k)$ denote the **minimum** expected number of accidents for warehouses $s$ through $4$ given $k$ units to allocate.

$$
f_s(k) = \min_{x_s} \left[ C_s(x_s) + f_{s+1}(k - x_s) \right]
$$

with the following constraints on $x_s$ at stage $s$:
- $x_s \in \{2, 3, 4\}$
- $k - x_s \geq 2(5 - s)$ (enough units remaining for the minimum requirement of warehouses $s+1$ to $4$)
- $k - x_s \leq 4(5 - s)$ (not exceeding the maximum capacity of warehouses $s+1$ to $4$)

### Boundary Condition
$$
f_5(k) = 0 \quad \text{for all valid } k
$$
(No further warehouses to assign after stage 4)

### Initial State
$$
k = 12 \quad \text{(total units available at stage 1)}
$$

### Solution
The optimal assignment $(x_1^*, x_2^*, x_3^*, x_4^*)$ is recovered by tracking the argmin at each stage, starting from $f_1(12)$.
