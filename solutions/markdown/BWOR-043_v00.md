# Transportation Problem Model

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be the quantity of goods transported from origin $A_i$ to destination $B_j$.

*   $i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ representing origins $A_1, A_2, A_3, A_4$
*   $j \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$ representing destinations $B_1, B_2, B_3, B_4, B_5, B_6$
*   $x_{ij} \ge 0$

## 2. Objective Function

**Minimize** the total transportation cost, $Z$.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{4} \sum_{j=1}^{6} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Where $c_{ij}$ represents the unit transportation cost from origin $i$ to destination $j$, based on the provided table:

| $i \setminus j$ | $B_1$ | $B_2$ | $B_3$ | $B_4$ | $B_5$ | $B_6$ |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **$A_1$** | 2 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 5 |
| **$A_2$** | 4 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| **$A_3$** | 3 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 1 |
| **$A_4$** | 4 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 |

Explicitly:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Min } Z = \ & 2x_{11} + 1x_{12} + 3x_{13} + 3x_{14} + 3x_{15} + 5x_{16} \\
+ & 4x_{21} + 2x_{22} + 2x_{23} + 4x_{24} + 4x_{25} + 4x_{26} \\
+ & 3x_{31} + 5x_{32} + 4x_{33} + 2x_{34} + 4x_{35} + 1x_{36} \\
+ & 4x_{41} + 2x_{42} + 2x_{43} + 1x_{44} + 2x_{45} + 2x_{46}
\end{aligned}
$$

## 3. Operational Constraints

**A. Supply Constraints (Flow out of each origin):**
The total amount shipped from each origin cannot exceed its supply capacity.
*   $\sum_{j=1}^{6} x_{1j} = 50$
*   $\sum_{j=1}^{6} x_{2j} = 40$
*   $\sum_{j=1}^{6} x_{3j} = 60$
*   $\sum_{j=1}^{6} x_{4j} = 31$

*(Note: Total Supply = $50+40+60+31 = 181$. Total Demand = $30+50+20+40+30+11 = 181$. Since Supply = Demand, equality constraints are used.)*

**B. Demand Constraints (Flow into each destination):**
The total amount received at each destination must meet its demand.
*   $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i1} = 30$
*   $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i2} = 50$
*   $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i3} = 20$
*   $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i4} = 40$
*   $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i5} = 30$
*   $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{i6} = 11$

**C. Non-negativity Constraints:**
*   $x_{ij} \ge 0$ for all $i=1..4, j=1..6$
