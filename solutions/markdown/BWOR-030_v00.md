# Operations Research Model: Grain Trading Optimization

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $t$ denote the month index, where $t \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ corresponds to January, February, and March.

*   $P_t$: Quantity of grain purchased in month $t$ (unit: dan).
*   $S_t$: Quantity of grain sold in month $t$ (unit: dan).

**Domain Constraints:**
*   $P_t \ge 0$ for all $t$
*   $S_t \ge 0$ for all $t$

## 2. Objective Function

**Goal:** Maximize Total Profit (Total Revenue - Total Cost).

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{t=1}^{3} (\text{Revenue}_t - \text{Cost}_t)
$$

Given the price table:
*   **Month 1 (Jan):** Buy @ 2.85, Sell @ 3.10
*   **Month 2 (Feb):** Buy @ 3.05, Sell @ 3.25
*   **Month 3 (Mar):** Buy @ 2.90, Sell @ 2.95

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = (3.10 S_1 - 2.85 P_1) + (3.25 S_2 - 3.05 P_2) + (2.95 S_3 - 2.90 P_3)
$$

## 3. Operational Constraints

### A. Inventory Balance Constraints
The inventory in any month depends on the previous month's closing inventory, current purchases, and current sales.
Let $I_t$ be the closing inventory at the end of month $t$.
Initial Inventory $I_0 = 1,000$ dan.

*   **End of Month 1:**
    $$I_1 = I_0 + P_1 - S_1 = 1000 + P_1 - S_1$$
*   **End of Month 2:**
    $$I_2 = I_1 + P_2 - S_2$$
*   **End of Month 3 (Target):**
    $$I_3 = I_2 + P_3 - S_3 = 2000$$

*Note: To maintain non-negativity of physical inventory at all times, we also require $I_t \ge 0$ for $t=1,2,3$.*

### B. Warehouse Capacity Constraints
The warehouse has a maximum capacity of 5,000 dan. The closing inventory at the end of each month cannot exceed this limit.

*   $I_1 \le 5000$
*   $I_2 \le 5000$
*   $I_3 \le 5000$ (Note: Since target is 2,000, this is automatically satisfied if $I_3=2000$, but included for completeness).

### C. Sales Timing & Availability Constraints
Grain purchased in month $t$ arrives immediately but **cannot be sold until month $t+1$**. This implies that sales in month $t$ can only be made from inventory available at the *start* of month $t$ (i.e., $I_{t-1}$).

*   **Month 1 Sales:** Can only sell initial stock ($I_0$). Purchases $P_1$ arrive but cannot be sold in Jan.
    $$S_1 \le I_0 \implies S_1 \le 1000$$
*   **Month 2 Sales:** Can sell inventory available at start of Feb ($I_1$).
    $$S_2 \le I_1$$
*   **Month 3 Sales:** Can sell inventory available at start of Mar ($I_2$).
    $$S_3 \le I_2$$

*(Alternatively, these are often modeled simply by ensuring $I_{t-1} - S_t \ge 0$, which is equivalent to the inventory balance equations combined with non-negativity of intermediate states, but explicit constraints prevent logical errors in implementation.)*

### D. Financial (Capital) Constraints
The company has an initial capital of 20,000 yuan. Cash is paid upon delivery (purchase). Revenue is received upon sale. The cash position at the beginning of any month must be non-negative.

Let $C_t$ be the available cash at the **beginning** of month $t$.
*   **Beginning of Month 1:**
    $$C_1 = 20,000$$
    Constraint: $2.85 P_1 \le C_1$ (Cannot spend more than available cash)
*   **Beginning of Month 2:**
    Cash available is Initial Cash - Jan Purchases + Jan Sales Revenue.
    $$C_2 = C_1 - 2.85 P_1 + 3.10 S_1$$
    Constraint: $3.05 P_2 \le C_2$
*   **Beginning of Month 3:**
    Cash available is End of Feb Cash + Feb Sales Revenue.
    $$C_3 = C_2 - 3.05 P_2 + 3.25 S_2$$
    Constraint: $2.90 P_3 \le C_3$

*(Note: It is assumed that sales revenue is immediately available for the next month's purchases, consistent with "Cash payment upon delivery" and standard monthly settlement cycles.)*

### Summary of Non-negativity
$$P_t, S_t \ge 0 \quad \forall t \in \{1,2,3\}$$
