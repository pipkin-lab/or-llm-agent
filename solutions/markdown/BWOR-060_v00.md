# Operations Research Model: Primary School Location Selection

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $I = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$ be the set of villages.
Let $d_{ij}$ be the Euclidean distance between village $i$ and village $j$.

We define binary decision variables:
*   $y_i = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if a school is built in village } i \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$ for all $i \in I$
*   $x_{ij} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if village } i \text{ is assigned to the school in village } j \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$ for all $i, j \in I$

## 2. Objective Function

**Minimize** the total weighted walking distance for all students.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in I} (N_i \cdot d_{ij} \cdot x_{ij})
$$

Where:
*   $N_i$ is the number of students in village $i$.
*   $d_{ij} = \sqrt{(x_i - x_j)^2 + (y_i - y_j)^2}$ is the Euclidean distance between village $i$ and village $j$.

## 3. Operational Constraints

**1. School Construction Constraint**
Exactly two schools must be built.
$$
\sum_{j \in I} y_j = 2
$$

**2. Assignment Constraint**
Every village must be assigned to exactly one school.
$$
\sum_{j \in I} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i \in I
$$

**3. Linking Constraint (Flow Conservation)**
A village can only be assigned to a school if a school is actually built in that location.
$$
x_{ij} \le y_j \quad \forall i, j \in I
$$

**4. Non-assignment Constraint (Logical)**
A village cannot be assigned to a school in a different village if no school is there (covered by linking), but specifically, if $y_j=0$, then $x_{ij}=0$. The linking constraint handles this. Additionally, a village is assigned to a school (which may be its own if a school is built there).

**5. Variable Domains**
$$
y_j \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall j \in I
$$
$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i, j \in I
$$
