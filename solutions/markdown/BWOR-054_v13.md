### Model Type Declaration
**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)

*Note: While the objective function and most constraints are linear, the constraint "Each student can be assigned for duty no more than twice a week" requires binary variables to count the number of days worked. Specifically, if a student works on a day, they count as 1 toward the limit, regardless of how many hours they work that day. This necessitates binary indicator variables $y_{i,d}$.*

### Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $I = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$: Set of students.
    *   $I_{UG} = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Undergraduate students.
    *   $I_{GR} = \{5, 6\}$: Graduate students.
*   $D = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: Set of days (Monday to Friday).

**Parameters:**
*   $w_i$: Hourly wage of student $i$ (yuan/h).
    *   $w_1 = 10.0$
    *   $w_2 = 10.0$
    *   $w_3 = 9.9$
    *   $w_4 = 9.8$
    *   $w_5 = 10.8$
    *   $w_6 = 11.3$
*   $H_{i,d}$: Maximum daily work hours for student $i$ on day $d$.
    *   $H_{1,1}=6, H_{1,2}=0, H_{1,3}=9, H_{1,4}=0, H_{1,5}=7$
    *   $H_{2,1}=0, H_{2,2}=9, H_{2,3}=0, H_{2,4}=6, H_{2,5}=0$
    *   $H_{3,1}=4, H_{3,2}=8, H_{3,3}=3, H_{3,4}=0, H_{3,5}=5$
    *   $H_{4,1}=5, H_{4,2}=5, H_{4,3}=6, H_{4,4}=0, H_{4,5}=4$
    *   $H_{5,1}=3, H_{5,2}=0, H_{5,3}=4, H_{5,4}=8, H_{5,5}=0$
    *   $H_{6,1}=0, H_{6,2}=9, H_{6,3}=0, H_{6,4}=6, H_{6,5}=3$
*   $T_{open}$: Daily operating hours = 14 hours (8:00 to 22:00).
*   $L_{i}$: Minimum weekly hours for student $i$.
    *   $L_i = 8$ for $i \in I_{UG}$
    *   $L_i = 7$ for $i \in I_{GR}$
*   $M$: A sufficiently large constant for Big-M formulation (e.g., $M = 9$ or $\max(H_{i,d})$).

### Decision Variables

1.  **Continuous Variables:**
    *   $x_{i,d} \ge 0$: Number of hours student $i$ works on day $d$.
2.  **Binary Variables:**
    *   $y_{i,d} \in \{0, 1\}$: Indicator variable. $y_{i,d} = 1$ if student $i$ works on day $d$ (i.e., $x_{i,d} > 0$), and $0$ otherwise.

### Objective Function

Minimize the total weekly wage cost:

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{d \in D} w_i \cdot x_{i,d}
$$

### Operational Constraints

**1. Coverage Constraint:**
The lab must have exactly one student on duty at all times. Since the problem states "exactly one student on duty at all times" and the lab is open for 14 hours daily, the total hours worked by all students on any given day must sum to 14.

$$
\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,d} = 14 \quad \forall d \in D
$$

**2. Maximum Daily Work Hours:**
A student cannot work more than their specified maximum hours on a specific day.

$$
x_{i,d} \le H_{i,d} \quad \forall i \in I, \forall d \in D
$$

**3. Minimum Weekly Work Hours:**
Undergraduates must work at least 8 hours/week; Graduates must work at least 7 hours/week.

$$
\sum_{d \in D} x_{i,d} \ge L_i \quad \forall i \in I
$$
Where $L_i = 8$ if $i \in \{1,2,3,4\}$ and $L_i = 7$ if $i \in \{5,6\}$.

**4. Maximum Days Assigned Per Week:**
Each student can be assigned for duty no more than twice a week. This means the sum of the binary indicators $y_{i,d}$ for each student over all days must be at most 2.

$$
\sum_{d \in D} y_{i,d} \le 2 \quad \forall i \in I
$$

**5. Linking Constraints (Big-M):**
To ensure $y_{i,d} = 1$ if $x_{i,d} > 0$ and $y_{i,d} = 0$ if $x_{i,d} = 0$, we use the following constraints. Note that $H_{i,d}$ serves as a natural Big-M value for $x_{i,d}$.

$$
x_{i,d} \le H_{i,d} \cdot y_{i,d} \quad \forall i \in I, \forall d \in D
$$
*(Note: If $y_{i,d}=0$, then $x_{i,d} \le 0 \implies x_{i,d}=0$. If $x_{i,d} > 0$, then $y_{i,d}$ must be 1.)*

**6. Maximum Students Per Day:**
No more than 3 students can be scheduled for duty per day. "Scheduled for duty" implies $x_{i,d} > 0$, which corresponds to $y_{i,d} = 1$.

$$
\sum_{i \in I} y_{i,d} \le 3 \quad \forall d \in D
$$

**7. Non-negativity and Integrality:**
$$
x_{i,d} \ge 0 \quad \forall i \in I, \forall d \in D
$$
$$
y_{i,d} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in I, \forall d \in D
$$
