# Operations Research Model Analysis

## 1. Problem Understanding
The goal is to determine the production quantities of two pastry products to **maximize wholesale revenue**, subject to raw material availability, market demand caps, and relative sales volume constraints.

**Key Data Extraction from Table C-5:**
*   **Product I:**
    *   Raw Material A consumption: 1 t/thousand boxes
    *   Raw Material B consumption: 2 t/thousand boxes
    *   Wholesale Price: 30 thousand yuan/thousand boxes
*   **Product II:**
    *   Raw Material A consumption: 2 t/thousand boxes
    *   Raw Material B consumption: 1 t/thousand boxes
    *   Wholesale Price: 20 thousand yuan/thousand boxes
*   **Resource Limits:**
    *   Raw Material A supply: 6 t
    *   Raw Material B supply: 8 t
*   **Market Constraints:**
    *   Product II sales $\le$ 2 thousand boxes.
    *   Product II sales - Product I sales $\le$ 1 thousand boxes.

*Note: The cost of raw materials (9.9 and 6.6) is provided but not required for the objective function since the task specifies maximizing **revenue** (wholesale price $\times$ quantity), not profit.*

## 2. Decision Variables
Let:
*   $x_1$ = Production quantity of Product I (in thousand boxes)
*   $x_2$ = Production quantity of Product II (in thousand boxes)

## 3. Objective Function
Maximize total wholesale revenue ($Z$):

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 30x_1 + 20x_2 $$

*(Where $Z$ is in thousand yuan)*

## 4. Operational Constraints

### A. Raw Material Availability
The total consumption of each raw material must not exceed the monthly supply.

1.  **Raw Material A Constraint:**
    $$ 1x_1 + 2x_2 \leq 6 $$

2.  **Raw Material B Constraint:**
    $$ 2x_1 + 1x_2 \leq 8 $$

### B. Market Demand Constraints
1.  **Product II Sales Limit:**
    $$ x_2 \leq 2 $$

2.  **Relative Sales Volume Limit:**
    The sales volume of Product II does not exceed that of Product I by more than 1 thousand boxes.
    $$ x_2 - x_1 \leq 1 $$
    *(Rearranged for standard form: $-x_1 + x_2 \leq 1$)*

### C. Non-negativity Constraints
Production quantities cannot be negative.
$$ x_1 \geq 0, \quad x_2 \geq 0 $$

---

### Final Mathematical Model Summary

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Maximize} \quad & Z = 30x_1 + 20x_2 \\
\text{Subject to} \quad & x_1 + 2x_2 \leq 6 \quad \text{(Material A limit)} \\
& 2x_1 + x_2 \leq 8 \quad \text{(Material B limit)} \\
& x_2 \leq 2 \quad \text{(Market limit for Product II)} \\
& -x_1 + x_2 \leq 1 \quad \text{(Relative sales constraint)} \\
& x_1, x_2 \geq 0
\end{align*}
$$
