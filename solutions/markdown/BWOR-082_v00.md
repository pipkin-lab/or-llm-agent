Here is the Operations Research model for the workshop relocation problem.

### 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be a binary decision variable indicating the location of workshop $i$:

$$
x_{ij} = \begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if workshop } i \text{ is located in area } j \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

**Indices:**
*   $i \in I = \{A, B, C, D, E\}$: Set of workshops.
*   $j \in J = \{\alpha, \beta, U\}$: Set of possible locations (Town $\alpha$, Town $\beta$, and Urban Area $U$).

### 2. Objective Function

The goal is to **Minimize** the Total Net Cost, which consists of the Total Transportation Cost minus the Total Relocation Savings.

$$
\min Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{k \in I, k > i} C_{ik} \left( D_{loc(i), loc(k)} \right) - \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in \{\alpha, \beta\}} S_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Where:
*   $C_{ik}$: Annual transport volume between workshop $i$ and $k$ (from Table C-19).
*   $D_{loc(i), loc(k)}$: The transportation cost per ton between the location of workshop $i$ and the location of workshop $k$. This term is determined by the values of $x_{ij}$:
    $$ D_{loc(i), loc(k)} = \sum_{j \in J} \sum_{l \in J} D_{jl} \cdot x_{ij} \cdot x_{kl} $$
    *(Note: $D_{jl}$ is from Table C-20. If $i=k$, the cost is 0 as there is no transport between the same workshop.)*
*   $S_{ij}$: Annual cost savings if workshop $i$ is moved to satellite town $j$ (from Table C-18).
    *   $S_{i, \alpha}$ and $S_{i, \beta}$ are given in the table.
    *   $S_{i, U} = 0$ (No savings if it remains in the urban area).

**Expanded Objective Function:**
$$
\min Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{k \in I, k > i} C_{ik} \left( \sum_{j \in J} \sum_{l \in J} D_{jl} x_{ij} x_{kl} \right) - \left( 100x_{A\alpha} + 150x_{B\alpha} + 100x_{C\alpha} + 200x_{D\alpha} + 50x_{E\alpha} + 100x_{A\beta} + 200x_{B\beta} + 150x_{C\beta} + 150x_{D\beta} + 150x_{E\beta} \right)
$$
*(Note: All cost/savings values are in 10,000 yuan/year, while $D_{jl}$ is in yuan/ton. Consistency in units must be maintained in the actual calculation; typically, the savings and the transport cost resulting from the product $C_{ik}D_{jl}$ should be scaled to the same unit.)*

### 3. Operational Constraints

**Constraint 1: Assignment Constraint**
Each workshop must be located in exactly one area.
$$
\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} = 1, \quad \forall i \in I
$$

**Constraint 2: Capacity/Location Limit Constraint**
No more than 3 workshops can be located in any single area (Urban, $\alpha$, or $\beta$).
$$
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \leq 3, \quad \forall j \in J
$$

**Constraint 3: Binary Constraint**
$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall i \in I, j \in J
$$

### Summary of Parameters

**Savings $S_{ij}$ (10,000 yuan/year):**
*   To $\alpha$: A=100, B=150, C=100, D=200, E=50
*   To $\beta$: A=100, B=200, C=150, D=150, E=150
*   To Urban: 0 for all.

**Transport Volumes $C_{ik}$ (t/year):**
*   A-B: 0, A-C: 1000, A-D: 1500, A-E: 0
*   B-C: 1400, B-D: 1200, B-E: 0
*   C-D: 0, C-E: 2000
*   D-E: 700
*   (Symmetric: $C_{ki} = C_{ik}$)

**Transport Costs $D_{jl}$ (yuan/ton):**
*   Urban-Urban: 1000
*   $\alpha$-$\alpha$: 500
*   $\beta$-$\beta$: 500
*   $\alpha$-$\beta$: 1400
*   $\alpha$-Urban: 1300
*   $\beta$-Urban: 900
*   (Symmetric: $D_{lj} = D_{jl}$)
