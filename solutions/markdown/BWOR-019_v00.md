# Basketball Starting Lineup Selection Model

## 1. Decision Variables
Let $x_i$ be a binary decision variable for each player $i$ ($i = 1, \dots, 8$):
$$
x_i = 
\begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if Player } i \text{ is selected in the starting lineup} \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

## 2. Objective Function
The goal is to select the 5 players such that the sum of their heights is maximized. Let $h_i$ denote the height of player $i$.

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{8} h_i x_i
$$

Where the heights are:
- $h_1 = 1.92$
- $h_2 = 1.90$
- $h_3 = 1.88$
- $h_4 = 1.86$
- $h_5 = 1.85$
- $h_6 = 1.83$
- $h_7 = 1.80$
- $h_8 = 1.78$

## 3. Operational Constraints

**Constraint 1: Team Size**
Exactly 5 players must be selected.
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{8} x_i = 5
$$

**Constraint 2: Exactly One Center**
Players 1 and 2 are Centers. Exactly one of them must be selected.
$$
x_1 + x_2 = 1
$$

**Constraint 3: At Least One Forward**
Players 3, 4, and 5 are Forwards. At least one must be selected.
$$
x_3 + x_4 + x_5 \ge 1
$$

**Constraint 4: Conditional Selection Logic (Player 1/4 vs Player 6)**
The problem states:
- If Player 1 or Player 4 is selected, Player 6 **must** be selected.
- If Player 6 is selected, neither Player 1 nor Player 4 can be selected.

This creates a mutual exclusion and dependency. Let's analyze the logical implications:
1. $x_1 = 1 \implies x_6 = 1$
2. $x_4 = 1 \implies x_6 = 1$
3. $x_6 = 1 \implies x_1 = 0$ AND $x_4 = 0$

Combining 1 and 3: If $x_1=1$, then $x_6=1$, which implies $x_1=0$. This is a contradiction. Therefore, **Player 1 cannot be selected**.
Similarly, combining 2 and 3: If $x_4=1$, then $x_6=1$, which implies $x_4=0$. This is a contradiction. Therefore, **Player 4 cannot be selected**.

Thus, the logical constraints simplify to:
$$
x_1 = 0
$$
$$
x_4 = 0
$$

*(Note: While these can be modeled as general logical constraints, the specific "if and only if" nature derived from the bidirectional implication forces these variables to zero. In a standard ILP formulation without pre-simplification, one might write:)*
$$
x_1 \le x_6
$$
$$
x_4 \le x_6
$$
$$
x_6 \le 1 - x_1
$$
$$
x_6 \le 1 - x_4
$$
*However, given the specific heights and the requirement to select 5 players, we can also just enforce the direct result $x_1=0, x_4=0$ as a valid constraint derived from the logic.*

**Constraint 5: Mutual Exclusion of Player 2 and Player 8**
At least one of Player 2 or Player 8 must **not** be selected (they cannot both be selected).
$$
x_2 + x_8 \le 1
$$

**Constraint 6: Binary Restriction**
$$
x_i \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i = 1, \dots, 8
$$
