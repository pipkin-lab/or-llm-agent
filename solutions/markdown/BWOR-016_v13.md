## Model Type: MILP

### Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
- $I_{\text{old}} = \{A_1, A_2\}$: Existing factories (always operational).
- $I_{\text{new}} = \{A_3, A_4\}$: Candidate new factories.
- $I = I_{\text{old}} \cup I_{\text{new}} = \{A_1, A_2, A_3, A_4\}$: All potential factories.
- $J = \{B_1, B_2, B_3, B_4\}$: Demand locations.

**Parameters (all values in kt/year for quantities, thousand yuan/kt for transport costs, thousand yuan for fixed costs):**

- Production capacities $cap_i$ (kt/year):
  - $cap_{A_1} = 400$
  - $cap_{A_2} = 600$
  - $cap_{A_3} = 200$
  - $cap_{A_4} = 200$

- Annual demands $d_j$ (kt/year):
  - $d_{B_1} = 350$
  - $d_{B_2} = 400$
  - $d_{B_3} = 300$
  - $d_{B_4} = 150$

- Unit transportation costs $c_{ij}$ (thousand yuan/kt):

| $c_{ij}$ | $B_1$ | $B_2$ | $B_3$ | $B_4$ |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| $A_1$ | 2 | 9 | 3 | 4 |
| $A_2$ | 8 | 3 | 5 | 7 |
| $A_3$ | 7 | 6 | 1 | 2 |
| $A_4$ | 4 | 5 | 2 | 5 |

- Fixed annual production costs for new factories $f_i$ (thousand yuan):
  - $f_{A_3} = 1200$
  - $f_{A_4} = 1500$

**Aggregated Check:**
- Total demand $D = 350 + 400 + 300 + 150 = 1200$ kt/year.
- Total existing supply = $400 + 600 = 1000$ kt/year.
- Since $D > \text{existing supply}$, a new factory is required.
- If $A_3$ is built: total capacity = $1000 + 200 = 1200$ kt/year. Supply = Demand.
- If $A_4$ is built: total capacity = $1000 + 200 = 1200$ kt/year. Supply = Demand.

### Decision Variables

1. **Binary variables for factory selection:**
   - $y_i \in \{0, 1\}$ for $i \in I_{\text{new}} = \{A_3, A_4\}$.
     - $y_i = 1$ if factory $i$ is built, 0 otherwise.
   - Constraint: $y_{A_3} + y_{A_4} = 1$ (Exactly one new factory must be built).

2. **Continuous variables for transportation flow:**
   - $x_{ij} \geq 0$ for $i \in I, j \in J$.
     - $x_{ij}$ = quantity of material (kt/year) shipped from factory $i$ to location $j$.

### Objective Function

Minimize total annual cost (in thousand yuan), which consists of fixed construction/production costs for the new factory and variable transportation costs.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in I_{\text{new}}} f_i y_i + \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Expanded:
$$
Z = 1200 y_{A_3} + 1500 y_{A_4} + \sum_{i \in \{A_1, A_2, A_3, A_4\}} \sum_{j \in \{B_1, B_2, B_3, B_4\}} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

### Operational Constraints

**1. Factory Selection Constraint:**
Exactly one of the two new factories is built.
$$
y_{A_3} + y_{A_4} = 1
$$

**2. Supply Capacity Constraints:**
The total amount shipped from each factory cannot exceed its production capacity. For new factories, shipping is only allowed if the factory is built (enforced by $x_{ij} \leq cap_i \cdot y_i$). For existing factories, $y_i$ is effectively 1.

- For $i \in I_{\text{old}} = \{A_1, A_2\}$:
  $$
  \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq cap_i
  $$
  Specifically:
  - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{A_1 j} \leq 400$
  - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{A_2 j} \leq 600$

- For $i \in I_{\text{new}} = \{A_3, A_4\}$:
  $$
  \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq cap_i \cdot y_i
  $$
  Specifically:
  - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{A_3 j} \leq 200 \cdot y_{A_3}$
  - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{A_4 j} \leq 200 \cdot y_{A_4}$

*Note: Since total supply (1200) equals total demand (1200), the supply constraints will bind as equalities in the optimal solution, but inequalities are sufficient to model the physical limit.*

**3. Demand Satisfaction Constraints:**
All demand at each location must be met exactly.
$$
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} = d_j \quad \forall j \in J
$$
Specifically:
- $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i B_1} = 350$
- $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i B_2} = 400$
- $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i B_3} = 300$
- $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i B_4} = 150$

**4. Non-negativity and Integrality:**
- $x_{ij} \geq 0$ for all $i \in I, j \in J$
- $y_{A_3}, y_{A_4} \in \{0, 1\}$
