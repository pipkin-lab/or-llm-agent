# Assembly Line Worker Assignment Model

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be a binary decision variable where:
- $i \in \{A, B, C, D, E\}$ represents the worker.
- $j \in \{I, II, III, IV, V\}$ represents the station.

$$
x_{ij} = \begin{cases}
1 & \text{if worker } i \text{ is assigned to station } j \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

## 2. Objective Function

**Maximize** the total production efficiency of the assembly line.

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i} \sum_{j} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Where $c_{ij}$ is the efficiency (pieces/min) of worker $i$ at station $j$, given by the table:

| Worker \ Station | I | II | III | IV | V |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **A** | 2 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 7 |
| **B** | 3 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 6 |
| **C** | 2 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 1 |
| **D** | 5 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 5 |
| **E** | 3 | 7 | 6 | 2 | 4 |

Explicitly:
$$
Z = 2x_{A,I} + 3x_{A,II} + 4x_{A,III} + 1x_{A,IV} + 7x_{A,V} + 3x_{B,I} + 4x_{B,II} + 2x_{B,III} + 5x_{B,IV} + 6x_{B,V} + 2x_{C,I} + 5x_{C,II} + 3x_{C,III} + 4x_{C,IV} + 1x_{C,V} + 5x_{D,I} + 2x_{D,II} + 3x_{D,III} + 2x_{D,IV} + 5x_{D,V} + 3x_{E,I} + 7x_{E,II} + 6x_{E,III} + 2x_{E,IV} + 4x_{E,V}
$$

## 3. Operational Constraints

### A. Worker Assignment Constraints
Each worker must be assigned to exactly one station.

$$
\sum_{j \in \{I, II, III, IV, V\}} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i \in \{A, B, C, D, E\}
$$

Specifically:
- **Worker A:** $x_{A,I} + x_{A,II} + x_{A,III} + x_{A,IV} + x_{A,V} = 1$
- **Worker B:** $x_{B,I} + x_{B,II} + x_{B,III} + x_{B,IV} + x_{B,V} = 1$
- **Worker C:** $x_{C,I} + x_{C,II} + x_{C,III} + x_{C,IV} + x_{C,V} = 1$
- **Worker D:** $x_{D,I} + x_{D,II} + x_{D,III} + x_{D,IV} + x_{D,V} = 1$
- **Worker E:** $x_{E,I} + x_{E,II} + x_{E,III} + x_{E,IV} + x_{E,V} = 1$

### B. Station Staffing Constraints
Each station must be staffed by exactly one worker.

$$
\sum_{i \in \{A, B, C, D, E\}} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall j \in \{I, II, III, IV, V\}
$$

Specifically:
- **Station I:** $x_{A,I} + x_{B,I} + x_{C,I} + x_{D,I} + x_{E,I} = 1$
- **Station II:** $x_{A,II} + x_{B,II} + x_{C,II} + x_{D,II} + x_{E,II} = 1$
- **Station III:** $x_{A,III} + x_{B,III} + x_{C,III} + x_{D,III} + x_{E,III} = 1$
- **Station IV:** $x_{A,IV} + x_{B,IV} + x_{C,IV} + x_{D,IV} + x_{E,IV} = 1$
- **Station V:** $x_{A,V} + x_{B,V} + x_{C,V} + x_{D,V} + x_{E,V} = 1$

### C. Binary Constraints
All decision variables are binary.

$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in \{A, B, C, D, E\}, \forall j \in \{I, II, III, IV, V\}
$$
