Here is the Operations Research model for the Traveling Salesperson Problem (TSP) based on the provided city coordinates.

### 1. Decision Variables

Let $n = 10$ be the number of cities, indexed by $i, j \in \{1, 2, ..., 10\}$.

Define binary variable $x_{ij}$:
$$
x_{ij} = 
\begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if the route travels directly from city } i \text{ to city } j \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

### 2. Objective Function

**Minimize** the total travel distance. The distance $d_{ij}$ between city $i$ and city $j$ is calculated using the Euclidean distance formula based on the coordinates $(x_i, y_i)$ and $(x_j, y_j)$ from Table 14-8:
$$
d_{ij} = \sqrt{(x_i - x_j)^2 + (y_i - y_j)^2}
$$

The objective function is:
$$
\min Z = \sum_{i=1}^{10} \sum_{j=1, j \neq i}^{10} d_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

### 3. Operational Constraints

**A. Flow Balance (In and Out)**
The route must enter each city exactly once and leave each city exactly once.
$$
\sum_{j=1, j \neq i}^{10} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i \in \{1, ..., 10\}
$$
$$
\sum_{i=1, i \neq j}^{10} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall j \in \{1, ..., 10\}
$$

**B. Subtour Elimination (MTZ Constraints)**
To ensure the solution is a single tour visiting all cities and not multiple disconnected loops, we introduce auxiliary continuous variables $u_i$ (representing the position/order of city $i$ in the tour, where $1 \leq u_i \leq 10$).

$$
u_i - u_j + n x_{ij} \leq n - 1 \quad \forall i, j \in \{1, ..., 10\}, \quad i \neq j
$$

**C. Binary Constraints**
$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \neq j
$$

*(Note: While $u_i$ are auxiliary variables for the constraints, the primary decision variables determining the route structure are the binary $x_{ij}$.)*
