## Model Type Declaration
**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)
**Solver Note:** This is a Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) variant. It is linear in structure but requires integer variables for the routing decisions. Standard MILP solvers (e.g., Gurobi, CPLEX, HiGHS) can solve this directly.

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $N = \{1, 2, \dots, 10\}$: Set of cities.
- $i, j \in N$: Indices for origin and destination cities.
- $k \in N \setminus \{1\}$: Indices for cities excluding the root city (City 1).

**Parameters:**
- $x_i, y_i$: Coordinates of city $i$.
- $c_{ij}$: Distance between city $i$ and city $j$, defined as the Euclidean distance:
  $$c_{ij} = \sqrt{(x_i - x_j)^2 + (y_i - y_j)^2}$$
  for all $i, j \in N, i \neq j$. Note that $c_{ij} = c_{ji}$ and $c_{ii} = 0$.

**Explicit Coordinate Data:**
- City 1: $(0, 0)$
- City 2: $(5, 20)$
- City 3: $(8, 12)$
- City 4: $(7, 4)$
- City 5: $(10, 15)$
- City 6: $(12, 18)$
- City 7: $(18, 18)$
- City 8: $(18, 15)$
- City 9: $(18, 18)$
- City 10: $(20, 17)$

*(Note: The exact numeric values for $c_{ij}$ are derived from the coordinates above. For implementation, these should be calculated precisely.)*

## Decision Variables

1.  **$x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$** for all $i, j \in N, i \neq j$:
    Binary variable where $x_{ij} = 1$ if the route travels directly from city $i$ to city $j$, and $0$ otherwise.

2.  **$u_k \in \mathbb{R}$** for all $k \in N \setminus \{1\}$:
    Continuous auxiliary variable used for Miller-Tucker-Zemlin (MTZ) subtour elimination. These variables represent the "position" or "order" of each city in the tour, relative to the root city.

## Objective Function

**MINIMIZE** $Z = \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} c_{ij} x_{ij}$

*This objective minimizes the total distance traveled in the tour.*

## Operational Constraints

**1. Flow Conservation (Out-degree):**
Every city must have exactly one outgoing arc.
$$ \sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} x_{ij} = 1, \quad \forall i \in N $$

**2. Flow Conservation (In-degree):**
Every city must have exactly one incoming arc.
$$ \sum_{i \in N, i \neq j} x_{ij} = 1, \quad \forall j \in N $$

**3. Subtour Elimination (MTZ Formulation):**
To ensure a single Hamiltonian cycle and prevent disjoint subtours, we use the MTZ constraints.
Root City: $1$.
Fix $u_1 = 0$.
For all other cities $k$, bound $u_k$:
$$ 0 \leq u_k \leq 9, \quad \forall k \in N \setminus \{1\} $$

The MTZ constraints are applied to all pairs $(i, j)$ where $i, j \in N \setminus \{1\}$ and $i \neq j$:
$$ u_i - u_j + 10 \cdot x_{ij} \leq 9, \quad \forall i, j \in N \setminus \{1\}, i \neq j $$

*(Note: The standard MTZ constraint is $u_i - u_j + N x_{ij} \leq N-1$. Here $N=10$, so the RHS is 9. These constraints are NOT written for arcs entering or leaving the root node 1, as including them can cause infeasibility depending on the specific formulation, but the standard safe set is for $i,j \neq 1$.)*

**4. Variable Domains:**
- $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \forall i \neq j$
- $u_k \geq 0, \forall k \neq 1$

## Final Model Structure

**Model Type:** MILP

**Index Sets:**
- $N = \{1, \dots, 10\}$
- $i, j \in N, i \neq j$

**Parameters:**
- $c_{ij} = \sqrt{(x_i-x_j)^2 + (y_i-y_j)^2}$

**Decision Variables:**
- $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: Arc from $i$ to $j$.
- $s_i \in \{0, 1\}$: Node $i$ is Start.
- $e_i \in \{0, 1\}$: Node $i$ is End.
- $u_i \in \mathbb{R}$: MTZ auxiliary.

**Objective:**
MINIMIZE $\sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} c_{ij} x_{ij}$

**Constraints:**
1.  **Start/End Uniqueness:**
    $\sum_{i \in N} s_i = 1$
    $\sum_{i \in N} e_i = 1$
    $s_i + e_i \leq 1, \forall i \in N$

2.  **Flow Conservation (Path Structure):**
    $\sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} x_{ij} = 1 - e_i, \forall i \in N$
    $\sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} x_{ji} = 1 - s_i, \forall i \in N$

3.  **Subtour Elimination (MTZ):**
    $u_i - u_j + 10 x_{ij} \leq 9, \forall i, j \in N, i \neq j$
    $0 \leq u_i \leq 9, \forall i \in N$
    *(Note: Since the start/end are not fixed to specific nodes, the MTZ variables will naturally align with the path direction from Start to End. The constraint $u_i - u_j + N x_{ij} \leq N-1$ works for directed acyclic graphs. Since cycles are eliminated, the graph is a DAG (a single path). The $u$ variables will represent the position in the path.)*

4.  **Domains:**
    $x_{ij}, s_i, e_i \in \{0, 1\}$
    $u_i \in \mathbb{R}$
