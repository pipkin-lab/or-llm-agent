# Model Type: MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)

**Note:** The objective is non-linear (products of binary and continuous variables), making this a Mixed Integer Non-Linear Program (MINLP) in its most direct form. However, it can be exactly linearized using standard McCormick-style linearization for the product of a binary and a continuous variable, resulting in a MILP.

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
- $V = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$: Set of villages.
- $V_{i} = \{j \in V \mid j \neq i\}$: Set of villages excluding village $i$.

**Parameters:**
- $x_i, y_i$: Coordinates of village $i$ (extracted from Table 5-14).
  - $x = [0, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 8, 6]$
  - $y = [0, 3, 15, 13, 9, 6, 12, 10]$
- $s_i$: Number of students in village $i$.
  - $s = [60, 80, 100, 120, 80, 60, 40, 80]$
- $d_{ij}$: Euclidean distance between village $i$ and village $j$.
  - $d_{ij} = \sqrt{(x_i - x_j)^2 + (y_i - y_j)^2}$ for $i \neq j$, and $d_{ii} = 0$.

## Decision Variables

1. $z_i \in \{0, 1\}$ for all $i \in V$:
   - Binary variable where $z_i = 1$ if a school is built in village $i$, and $0$ otherwise.
2. $w_{ij} \ge 0$ for all $i \in V, j \in V_i$:
   - Continuous variable representing the number of students from village $j$ who are assigned to the school in village $i$.
3. $u_{ij} \ge 0$ for all $i \in V, j \in V_i$:
   - Continuous variable representing the total walking distance traveled by students from village $j$ to school $i$.

## Objective Function

Minimize the total walking distance for all students:
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V_i} u_{ij} $$

## Operational Constraints

1. **School Construction Constraint:**
   Exactly two schools must be built.
   $$ \sum_{i \in V} z_i = 2 $$

2. **Student Assignment Constraint:**
   Every student in village $j$ must be assigned to exactly one school (which must be located in a village $i$).
   $$ \sum_{i \in V_i} w_{ij} = s_j \quad \forall j \in V $$

3. **Capacity/Linking Constraint (Linearized):**
   Students from village $j$ can only be assigned to village $i$ if a school is built in village $i$. We use the standard linearization for the product $z_i w_{ij}$ (where $z_i \in \{0,1\}$ and $0 \le w_{ij} \le s_j$).
   $$ w_{ij} \le s_j z_i \quad \forall i \in V, \forall j \in V_i $$
   *(Note: The other three McCormick inequalities are redundant given the other constraints: $w_{ij} \ge 0$ is the variable bound, $w_{ij} \le s_j$ is implied by the summation in constraint 2, and $w_{ij} \ge s_j z_i - s_j(1-z_i)$ is redundant because if $z_i=0$ then $w_{ij}=0$, and if $z_i=1$ it becomes $w_{ij} \ge 0$.)*

4. **Distance Calculation Constraint (Linearized):**
   The distance traveled is the number of students times the Euclidean distance. We linearize $u_{ij} = d_{ij} w_{ij}$. Since $d_{ij}$ is a constant parameter, this is already linear in $w_{ij}$.
   $$ u_{ij} = d_{ij} w_{ij} \quad \forall i \in V, \forall j \in V_i $$

5. **Variable Bounds:**
   $$ z_i \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in V $$
   $$ 0 \le w_{ij} \le s_j \quad \forall i \in V, \forall j \in V_i $$
   $$ u_{ij} \ge 0 \quad \forall i \in V, \forall j \in V_i $$
