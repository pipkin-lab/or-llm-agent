# Operational Research Model: Flour Allocation for Maximum Benefit

### 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be the amount of flour shipped from flour mill $i$ to food processing factory $j$.

**Indices:**
*   $i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ represents the flour mills (Mill I, Mill II, Mill III).
*   $j \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ represents the food processing factories (Factory 1, Factory 2, Factory 3).

**Variable Definition:**
*   $x_{ij} \geq 0$: Continuous variable representing the quantity of flour transported from mill $i$ to factory $j$.

### 2. Objective Function

The goal is to **maximize** the total net benefit.
The net benefit per unit of flour transported from mill $i$ to factory $j$ is defined as:
$$ \text{Net Profit}_{ij} = \text{Processing Profit}_j - \text{Transportation Cost}_{ij} $$

Given Data:
*   **Processing Profits ($p_j$):**
    *   $p_1 = 12$
    *   $p_2 = 16$
    *   $p_3 = 11$
*   **Transportation Costs ($c_{ij}$):**
    *   $c_{11}=3, c_{12}=10, c_{13}=2$
    *   $c_{21}=4, c_{22}=11, c_{23}=8$
    *   $c_{31}=8, c_{32}=11, c_{33}=4$

Calculate Net Benefit Coefficients ($z_{ij} = p_j - c_{ij}$):

*   **To Factory 1 ($p_1=12$):**
    *   $z_{11} = 12 - 3 = 9$
    *   $z_{21} = 12 - 4 = 8$
    *   $z_{31} = 12 - 8 = 4$
*   **To Factory 2 ($p_2=16$):**
    *   $z_{12} = 16 - 10 = 6$
    *   $z_{22} = 16 - 11 = 5$
    *   $z_{32} = 16 - 11 = 5$
*   **To Factory 3 ($p_3=11$):**
    *   $z_{13} = 11 - 2 = 9$
    *   $z_{23} = 11 - 8 = 3$
    *   $z_{33} = 11 - 4 = 7$

**Objective Function:**
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 9x_{11} + 6x_{12} + 9x_{13} + 8x_{21} + 5x_{22} + 3x_{23} + 4x_{31} + 5x_{32} + 7x_{33} $$

### 3. Operational Constraints

**A. Supply Constraints (Mill Output Limits)**
The total amount of flour shipped from each mill cannot exceed its production capacity.

*   **Mill I (Capacity 20):**
    $$ x_{11} + x_{12} + x_{13} \leq 20 $$
*   **Mill II (Capacity 30):**
    $$ x_{21} + x_{22} + x_{23} \leq 30 $$
*   **Mill III (Capacity 20):**
    $$ x_{31} + x_{32} + x_{33} \leq 20 $$

**B. Demand Constraints (Factory Capacity/Demand)**
The total amount of flour received by each factory cannot exceed its processing capacity (or demand limit).

*   **Factory 1 (Capacity 15):**
    $$ x_{11} + x_{21} + x_{31} \leq 15 $$
*   **Factory 2 (Capacity 25):**
    $$ x_{12} + x_{22} + x_{32} \leq 25 $$
*   **Factory 3 (Capacity 20):**
    $$ x_{13} + x_{23} + x_{33} \leq 20 $$

**C. Non-negativity Constraints**
*   $$ x_{ij} \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in \{1,2,3\}, j \in \{1,2,3\} $$

***

**Note on Balance:**
*   Total Supply = $20 + 30 + 20 = 70$
*   Total Demand/Capacity = $15 + 25 + 20 = 60$
*   Since Total Supply ($70$) > Total Demand ($60$), this is an **unbalanced transportation problem** with excess supply. The constraints above correctly model this by using inequalities ($\leq$) for both supply and demand, ensuring that only 60 units will be shipped and processed in total to maximize the net benefit.
