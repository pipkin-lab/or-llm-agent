# Operations Research Model: Production Planning with Fixed Costs

## 1. Decision Variables

To model the fixed costs associated with "organizing production" (i.e., whether a product is produced at all), we require two sets of variables for each product $j \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ (corresponding to Products I, II, and III):

*   **Continuous Variable:**
    *   Let $x_j$ be the quantity of Product $j$ produced.
    *   $x_j \ge 0$ for $j = 1, 2, 3$.

*   **Binary Variable:**
    *   Let $y_j \in \{0, 1\}$ be a binary indicator variable for Product $j$.
        *   $y_j = 1$ if Product $j$ is produced (fixed cost is incurred).
        *   $y_j = 0$ if Product $j$ is not produced.

## 2. Objective Function

The goal is to **Maximize** the total profit.
Total Profit = Total Revenue - Total Variable Costs - Total Fixed Costs.

First, we calculate the unit contribution margin (Profit per unit excluding fixed costs) for each product:
*   **Product I:** $8 (\text{price}) - 4 (\text{var. cost}) = 4$
*   **Product II:** $10 (\text{price}) - 5 (\text{var. cost}) = 5$
*   **Product III:** $12 (\text{price}) - 6 (\text{var. cost}) = 6$

The fixed costs are:
*   **Product I:** $100$
*   **Product II:** $150$
*   **Product III:** $200$

**Objective Function:**
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 4x_1 + 5x_2 + 6x_3 - 100y_1 - 150y_2 - 200y_3 $$

## 3. Operational Constraints

### A. Resource Constraints
The total amount of each resource consumed by all products cannot exceed the available supply.

*   **Resource A:** $2x_1 + 4x_2 + 8x_3 \le 500$
*   **Resource B:** $2x_1 + 3x_2 + 4x_3 \le 300$
*   **Resource C:** $1x_1 + 2x_2 + 3x_3 \le 100$

### B. Fixed Cost Linking Constraints (Big-M Constraints)
If a product is not produced ($y_j = 0$), its production quantity must be zero ($x_j = 0$). If a product is produced ($y_j = 1$), the quantity $x_j$ is bounded by the maximum possible production capacity determined by the resource constraints.

We must determine an upper bound $M_j$ for each $x_j$. This is the maximum production of product $j$ if it were the *only* product produced, limited by the tightest resource constraint.

*   **For Product 1 ($x_1$):**
    *   Resource A limit: $500 / 2 = 250$
    *   Resource B limit: $300 / 2 = 150$
    *   Resource C limit: $100 / 1 = 100$
    *   Binding constraint is Resource C. Thus, $M_1 = 100$.
    *   Constraint: $x_1 \le 100y_1$

*   **For Product 2 ($x_2$):**
    *   Resource A limit: $500 / 4 = 125$
    *   Resource B limit: $300 / 3 = 100$
    *   Resource C limit: $100 / 2 = 50$
    *   Binding constraint is Resource C. Thus, $M_2 = 50$.
    *   Constraint: $x_2 \le 50y_2$

*   **For Product 3 ($x_3$):**
    *   Resource A limit: $500 / 8 = 62.5$
    *   Resource B limit: $300 / 4 = 75$
    *   Resource C limit: $100 / 3 \approx 33.33$
    *   Binding constraint is Resource C. Thus, $M_3 = 33$ (using integer floor for safety in MIP, or 33.33 if continuous relaxation is acceptable, but typically we use the strict bound). Let's use $33$ to be safe for integer production, or simply $100/3$ if continuous. Given standard OR modeling, we use the exact fractional bound or the floor. Let's use the floor value $33$ for robustness if units are discrete, but generally $M$ can be the exact limit.
    *   Constraint: $x_3 \le 33.33y_3$ (or $x_3 \le \frac{100}{3}y_3$)

### C. Non-negativity and Integrality Constraints
*   $x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0$
*   $y_1, y_2, y_3 \in \{0, 1\}$

---

## Final Mathematical Formulation

**Maximize**
$$ Z = 4x_1 + 5x_2 + 6x_3 - 100y_1 - 150y_2 - 200y_3 $$

**Subject to:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
2x_1 + 4x_2 + 8x_3 &\le 500 & (\text{Resource A}) \\
2x_1 + 3x_2 + 4x_3 &\le 300 & (\text{Resource B}) \\
1x_1 + 2x_2 + 3x_3 &\le 100 & (\text{Resource C}) \\
x_1 &\le 100 y_1 & (\text{Linking 1}) \\
x_2 &\le 50 y_2 & (\text{Linking 2}) \\
x_3 &\le 33.33 y_3 & (\text{Linking 3}) \\
x_1, x_2, x_3 &\ge 0 \\
y_1, y_2, y_3 &\in \{0, 1\}
\end{aligned}
$$
