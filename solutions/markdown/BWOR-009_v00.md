### Problem Analysis

This is a **Production-Inventory Planning** problem (specifically, a lot-sizing problem with storage costs). The goal is to determine the production quantity for each quarter such that all customer delivery requirements are met, production does not exceed capacity, and the total cost (production + storage) is minimized.

**Key Data Points:**
*   **Time Horizon:** 4 Quarters ($t = 1, 2, 3, 4$).
*   **Demand ($D_t$):** Must be met exactly at the end of each quarter.
    *   $D_1 = 15, D_2 = 20, D_3 = 25, D_4 = 20$.
    *   Total Demand = 80 units.
*   **Capacity ($C_t$):** Maximum production per quarter.
    *   $C_1 = 25, C_2 = 35, C_3 = 30, C_4 = 20$.
    *   Total Capacity = 110 units.
*   **Production Cost ($p_t$):** Per unit produced in quarter $t$.
    *   $p_1 = 12.0, p_2 = 11.0, p_3 = 11.5, p_4 = 12.5$.
*   **Holding Cost ($h$):** 0.1 thousand yuan per unit per quarter.
*   **Initial Inventory:** Assumed to be 0.
*   **Final Inventory:** Assumed to be 0 (no backorders allowed, demand must be met on time).

---

### 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{t}$ be the quantity of equipment produced in quarter $t$.
Let $I_t$ be the inventory level at the end of quarter $t$.

Alternatively, and more commonly in linear programming formulations for this specific type of problem, we can define:
Let $x_{t,s}$ be the quantity of equipment produced in quarter $t$ to satisfy the demand of quarter $s$, where $s \ge t$.
*   $x_{1,1}$: Produced in Q1 for Q1
*   $x_{1,2}$: Produced in Q1 for Q2
*   ...
*   $x_{4,4}$: Produced in Q4 for Q4

Using the $x_{t,s}$ formulation simplifies the objective function by directly associating production with the specific demand it satisfies, incorporating the holding cost based on the time difference $(s-t)$.

**Variables:**
*   $x_{t,s} \ge 0$: Number of units produced in quarter $t$ to meet the demand of quarter $s$, for all $1 \le t \le s \le 4$.

---

### 2. Objective Function

**Goal:** Minimize Total Annual Cost.

Total Cost = (Total Production Cost) + (Total Holding Cost)

For a unit produced in quarter $t$ and used in quarter $s$:
*   Production Cost: $p_t$
*   Holding Cost: It is stored for $(s - t)$ quarters. Cost = $h \times (s - t)$.
*   Total Cost per unit for this specific flow: $p_t + h(s - t)$.

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{t=1}^{4} \sum_{s=t}^{4} \left[ p_t + 0.1(s - t) \right] x_{t,s} $$

Expanding the coefficients for clarity:
*   **Q1 Production ($t=1$):**
    *   For Q1 ($s=1$): $12.0 + 0.1(0) = 12.0$
    *   For Q2 ($s=2$): $12.0 + 0.1(1) = 12.1$
    *   For Q3 ($s=3$): $12.0 + 0.1(2) = 12.2$
    *   For Q4 ($s=4$): $12.0 + 0.1(3) = 12.3$
*   **Q2 Production ($t=2$):**
    *   For Q2 ($s=2$): $11.0 + 0.1(0) = 11.0$
    *   For Q3 ($s=3$): $11.0 + 0.1(1) = 11.1$
    *   For Q4 ($s=4$): $11.0 + 0.1(2) = 11.2$
*   **Q3 Production ($t=3$):**
    *   For Q3 ($s=3$): $11.5 + 0.1(0) = 11.5$
    *   For Q4 ($s=4$): $11.5 + 0.1(1) = 11.6$
*   **Q4 Production ($t=4$):**
    *   For Q4 ($s=4$): $12.5 + 0.1(0) = 12.5$

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = 12.0x_{1,1} + 12.1x_{1,2} + 12.2x_{1,3} + 12.3x_{1,4} + 11.0x_{2,2} + 11.1x_{2,3} + 11.2x_{2,4} + 11.5x_{3,3} + 11.6x_{3,4} + 12.5x_{4,4} $$

---

### 3. Operational Constraints

**A. Demand Satisfaction Constraints**
The total quantity allocated to each quarter's demand must equal the required delivery quantity.

*   **Quarter 1 Demand:**
    $$ x_{1,1} = 15 $$
*   **Quarter 2 Demand:**
    $$ x_{1,2} + x_{2,2} = 20 $$
*   **Quarter 3 Demand:**
    $$ x_{1,3} + x_{2,3} + x_{3,3} = 25 $$
*   **Quarter 4 Demand:**
    $$ x_{1,4} + x_{2,4} + x_{3,4} + x_{4,4} = 20 $$

**B. Production Capacity Constraints**
The total production in any given quarter cannot exceed its capacity. Production in quarter $t$ is the sum of $x_{t,s}$ for all $s \ge t$.

*   **Quarter 1 Capacity:**
    $$ x_{1,1} + x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} + x_{1,4} \le 25 $$
*   **Quarter 2 Capacity:**
    $$ x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} + x_{2,4} \le 35 $$
*   **Quarter 3 Capacity:**
    $$ x_{3,3} + x_{3,4} \le 30 $$
*   **Quarter 4 Capacity:**
    $$ x_{4,4} \le 20 $$

**C. Non-negativity Constraints**
All production quantities must be non-negative.
$$ x_{t,s} \ge 0 \quad \forall t, s \text{ where } 1 \le t \le s \le 4 $$
