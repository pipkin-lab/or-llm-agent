### Decision Variables

1.  **$L_A$**: Amount of the Annual Loan taken at the beginning of Month 1 (thousand yuan).
    *   Domain: $L_A \in \mathbb{R}, L_A \ge 0$.
2.  **$B_t$**: Net cash balance at the **end** of month $t$ (thousand yuan).
    *   Domain: $B_t \in \mathbb{R}$.
    *   *Note:* The problem implies the factory must cover shortfalls, implying $B_t \ge 0$ for all $t$.
3.  **$S_t$**: Amount of Short-term Monthly Loan taken at the **beginning** of month $t$ (thousand yuan).
    *   Domain: $S_t \in \mathbb{R}, S_t \ge 0$ for $t \in \{1, \dots, 12\}$.
4.  **$D_t$**: Amount deposited at the **beginning** of month $t$ (thousand yuan).
    *   Domain: $D_t \in \mathbb{R}, D_t \ge 0$ for $t \in \{1, \dots, 12\}$.

*Clarification on Timing:*
*   **Beginning of Month $t$**: Cash from $B_{t-1}$ (where $B_0$ includes $L_A$) is available. We decide to borrow $S_t$ and deposit $D_t$.
*   **During Month $t$**: Operations occur, generating net cash flow $C_t$.
*   **End of Month $t$**:
    *   Short-term loan $S_t$ from the beginning of the month must be repaid with interest. Repayment = $S_t(1 + r_S)$.
    *   Deposit $D_t$ from the beginning of the month is withdrawn with interest. Withdrawal = $D_t(1 + r_D)$.
    *   Annual Loan interest for month $t$ is paid if $t \ge 2$. Payment = $r_A L_A$.
    *   The balance $B_t$ is calculated.

### Operational Constraints

**1. Cash Flow Balance Equations**

For each month $t$, the ending balance is calculated as:
$$ B_t = B_{t-1} + C_t + \text{Inflows}_t - \text{Outflows}_t $$

Where:
*   $B_0 = L_A$ (The Annual Loan received at the start).
*   **Inflows/Outflows Details:**
    *   **Short-term Loan ($S_t$):**
        *   Taken at beginning of month $t$: Inflow $+S_t$.
        *   Repaid at end of month $t$: Outflow $-S_t(1 + r_S)$.
        *   Net effect on $B_t$: $S_t - S_t(1 + r_S) = -r_S S_t$.
        *   *Wait, this logic assumes the loan is for exactly one month.* The problem says "repaid at the beginning of the following month".
        *   Let's refine the timing.
        *   "Taken out at the beginning of any month... repaid at the beginning of the following month."
        *   If I take $S_t$ at the beginning of Month $t$, it is available during Month $t$.
        *   It is repaid at the beginning of Month $t+1$.
        *   So, in the balance equation for Month $t$ (which ends at the end of Month $t$ / beginning of Month $t+1$):
            *   The loan $S_t$ is an INFLOW at the start of Month $t$.
            *   The repayment of $S_t$ (principal + interest) occurs at the start of Month $t+1$.
            *   Therefore, the repayment of $S_t$ should appear in the balance equation for **Month $t+1$** (as an outflow at the start) OR we can treat the balance $B_t$ as the balance *just before* the next month's start transactions?
            *   Standard convention: $B_t$ is the balance at the **end** of month $t$.
            *   Transactions at "Beginning of Month $t$" affect the cash available *during* Month $t$.
            *   Transactions at "End of Month $t$" affect the cash leaving Month $t$.
            *   Let's define the timeline for Month $t$:
                1.  Start of Month $t$:
                    *   Cash available = $B_{t-1}$.
                    *   Borrow $S_t$ (Inflow).
                    *   Deposit $D_t$ (Outflow from operating cash, into deposit account).
                    *   Repay previous short-term loan $S_{t-1}$ (Outflow: $S_{t-1}(1+r_S)$).
                    *   *Note:* The Annual Loan interest is paid "starting from the end of February". This suggests interest is paid at the **end** of the month.
                2.  During Month $t$:
                    *   Net Cash Flow $C_t$ occurs.
                3.  End of Month $t$:
                    *   Pay Annual Loan interest (if $t \ge 2$): Outflow $r_A L_A$.
                    *   Withdraw Deposit $D_t$ (Inflow: $D_t(1+r_D)$).
                    *   Resulting Balance $B_t$.

    *   Let's construct the equation for $B_t$ based on this timeline:
        $$ B_t = B_{t-1} + S_t - S_{t-1}(1+r_S) + C_t - D_t + D_t(1+r_D) - I_A(t) $$
        Where:
        *   $S_0 = 0$ (No loan from previous year in the short-term category, or rather, the Annual Loan is separate).
        *   $I_A(t)$ is the Annual Loan interest payment at end of month $t$.
            *   $I_A(t) = 0.01 L_A$ if $2 \le t \le 12$.
            *   $I_A(t) = 0$ if $t = 1$.
        *   $D_t$ is deposited at start, withdrawn at end. So net cash flow from deposit activity in month $t$ is $-D_t + D_t(1+r_D) = r_D D_t$.
        *   $S_t$ is borrowed at start, repaid at start of next month. So in month $t$, we receive $S_t$ and repay $S_{t-1}(1+r_S)$.

    *   Refined Equation:
        $$ B_t = B_{t-1} + S_t - (1+r_S)S_{t-1} + C_t + r_D D_t - r_A L_A \cdot \mathbb{I}(t \ge 2) $$
        
        *   For $t=1$:
            $$ B_1 = L_A + S_1 - 0 + C_1 + r_D D_1 - 0 $$
            $$ B_1 = L_A + S_1 + C_1 + 0.004 D_1 $$
        *   For $t \in \{2, \dots, 12\}$:
            $$ B_t = B_{t-1} + S_t - 1.015 S_{t-1} + C_t + 0.004 D_t - 0.01 L_A $$

**2. Non-Negativity Constraints**

The factory must be able to cover all cash shortfalls. This implies the cash balance cannot go negative at any point.
*   **End-of-month balance:** $B_t \ge 0$ for all $t \in \{1, \dots, 12\}$.
*   **Intra-month solvency:** The problem states "cover any cash shortfalls". Usually, this is modeled by ensuring the balance at the end of the period is non-negative, assuming flows are netted. However, if $C_t$ is large negative and occurs at the end, we might dip below zero during the month. Given the granularity (monthly), standard LP formulation assumes the balance constraint $B_t \ge 0$ is sufficient.
*   **Decision Variables:**
    *   $L_A \ge 0$
    *   $S_t \ge 0$ for all $t$
    *   $D_t \ge 0$ for all $t$

**3. Specific Constraint Indices**

*   $S_0 = 0$
*   $B_0 = L_A$
