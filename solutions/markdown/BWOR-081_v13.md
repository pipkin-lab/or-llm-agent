Model Type: LP

### Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $I$: Set of products, $I = \{1, 2\}$ (Product I, Product II)
*   $R$: Set of raw materials, $R = \{A, B\}$

**Parameters:**
*   $p_i$: Wholesale price of product $i$ (thousand yuan per thousand boxes)
    *   $p_1 = 30$
    *   $p_2 = 20$
*   $a_{r,i}$: Amount of raw material $r$ required per thousand boxes of product $i$ (kg)
    *   $a_{A,1} = 1$
    *   $a_{A,2} = 2$
    *   $a_{B,1} = 2$
    *   $a_{B,2} = 1$
*   $S_r$: Monthly supply of raw material $r$ (tons)
    *   $S_A = 6$ tons $= 6000$ kg
    *   $S_B = 8$ tons $= 8000$ kg
*   $M_{max}$: Maximum monthly sales of Product II (thousand boxes)
    *   $M_{max} = 2$
*   $\Delta_{max}$: Maximum difference in sales volume between Product II and Product I (thousand boxes)
    *   $\Delta_{max} = 1$

*Note on Units:* The problem states consumption in "kg per thousand boxes" and supply in "tons". To maintain unit consistency, supply is converted to kg ($1 \text{ ton} = 1000 \text{ kg}$). Prices and production quantities are in "thousand boxes" and "thousand yuan", which are consistent.

### Decision Variables

*   $x_1$: Production quantity of Product I (thousand boxes)
*   $x_2$: Production quantity of Product II (thousand boxes)

**Domains:**
*   $x_1 \ge 0$, continuous
*   $x_2 \ge 0$, continuous

### Objective Function

Maximize total wholesale revenue $Z$:

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 30 x_1 + 20 x_2 $$

### Operational Constraints

1.  **Raw Material A Availability:**
    The total amount of Material A used cannot exceed the monthly supply (converted to kg).
    $$ 1 x_1 + 2 x_2 \le 6000 $$

2.  **Raw Material B Availability:**
    The total amount of Material B used cannot exceed the monthly supply (converted to kg).
    $$ 2 x_1 + 1 x_2 \le 8000 $$

3.  **Market Demand Limit for Product II:**
    The monthly sales (production) of Product II does not exceed 2 thousand boxes.
    $$ x_2 \le 2 $$

4.  **Relative Sales Constraint:**
    The sales volume of Product II does not exceed that of Product I by more than 1 thousand boxes.
    $$ x_2 - x_1 \le 1 $$

5.  **Non-negativity:**
    $$ x_1 \ge 0 $$
    $$ x_2 \ge 0 $$
