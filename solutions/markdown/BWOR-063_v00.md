# Operations Research Model: Multi-Resource Allocation to Departments

## 1. Decision Variables

Let the decision variables represent the integer quantities of resources allocated to each department.

*   $x_i$: The number of units of Resource 1 allocated to Department $i$ ($i = 1, 2, 3$).
*   $y_i$: The number of units of Resource 2 allocated to Department $i$ ($i = 1, 2, 3$).

**Domain:**
*   $x_i \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ for $i = 1, 2, 3$
*   $y_i \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ for $i = 1, 2, 3$

## 2. Objective Function

**Maximize** the total profit generated across all departments.

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{3} r_i(x_i, y_i) $$

Where $r_i(x_i, y_i)$ is the profit for Department $i$ given by the specific lookup tables provided in Table 7-3:
*   $r_1(x_1, y_1)$ follows the **$r(x,y)$** table.
*   $r_2(x_2, y_2)$ follows the **$r_2(x,y)$** table.
*   $r_3(x_3, y_3)$ follows the **$r_3(x,y)$** table.

## 3. Operational Constraints

**Resource Availability Constraints:**
The total amount of each resource allocated to all departments cannot exceed the total available supply.

1.  **Resource 1 Constraint:**
    $$ \sum_{i=1}^{3} x_i \leq 3 $$
    *(Since the total available Resource 1 is $x=3$)*

2.  **Resource 2 Constraint:**
    $$ \sum_{i=1}^{3} y_i \leq 3 $$
    *(Since the total available Resource 2 is $y=3$)*

**Non-negativity and Integrality:**
*   $x_i, y_i$ must be non-negative integers.
*   Based on the table limits, $0 \leq x_i \leq 3$ and $0 \leq y_i \leq 3$.
