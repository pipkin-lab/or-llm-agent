To determine the farm's optimal operational plan, we will formulate a Linear Programming (LP) model. The goal is to maximize the total annual net income, which includes income from crops and livestock, as well as the wage income earned from any surplus labor that is not used on the farm.

### 1. Model Type
**Linear Programming (LP)**

### 2. Index Sets & Parameters

**Crops:**
*   Let $C = \{s, c, w\}$ be the set of crops: Soybeans ($s$), Corn ($c$), Wheat ($w$).

**Parameters:**

*   **Land:**
    *   Total Land Available: $L_{total} = 100$ hm².
*   **Labor:**
    *   Total Autumn/Winter Labor: $L_{AW} = 3,500$ person-days.
    *   Total Spring/Summer Labor: $L_{SS} = 4,000$ person-days.
    *   Wage Income (Surplus Labor):
        *   Spring/Summer Wage: $W_{SS} = 2.1$ yuan/person-day.
        *   Autumn/Winter Wage: $W_{AW} = 1.8$ yuan/person-day.
*   **Livestock:**
    *   Dairy Cows:
        *   Investment per cow: $I_{cow} = 400$ yuan.
        *   Land for forage per cow: $A_{cow} = 1.5$ hm².
        *   Labor (AW) per cow: $l_{cow, AW} = 100$ person-days.
        *   Labor (SS) per cow: $l_{cow, SS} = 50$ person-days.
        *   Net Income per cow: $P_{cow} = 400$ yuan.
        *   Max capacity: $U_{cow} = 32$ cows.
    *   Chickens:
        *   Investment per chicken: $I_{chick} = 3$ yuan.
        *   Land per chicken: $A_{chick} = 0$ hm².
        *   Labor (AW) per chicken: $l_{chick, AW} = 0.6$ person-days.
        *   Labor (SS) per chicken: $l_{chick, SS} = 0.3$ person-days.
        *   Net Income per chicken: $P_{chick} = 2$ yuan.
        *   Max capacity: $U_{chick} = 3,000$ chickens.
*   **Crops (from Table 1-9):**
    *   Soybeans ($s$):
        *   Labor (AW) per hm²: $a_{s, AW} = 20$.
        *   Labor (SS) per hm²: $a_{s, SS} = 50$.
        *   Net Income per hm²: $p_s = 175$.
    *   Corn ($c$):
        *   Labor (AW) per hm²: $a_{c, AW} = 35$.
        *   Labor (SS) per hm²: $a_{c, SS} = 75$.
        *   Net Income per hm²: $p_c = 300$.
    *   Wheat ($w$):
        *   Labor (AW) per hm²: $a_{w, AW} = 10$.
        *   Labor (SS) per hm²: $a_{w, SS} = 40$.
        *   Net Income per hm²: $p_w = 120$.
*   **Capital:**
    *   Total Funds Available: $F_{total} = 15,000$ yuan.
    *   Investment for crops: $0$ yuan.

### 3. Decision Variables

Let:
*   $x_s, x_c, x_w \in \mathbb{R}^+$: Area (hm²) planted with soybeans, corn, and wheat, respectively.
*   $x_{cow} \in \mathbb{R}^+$: Number of dairy cows.
*   $x_{chick} \in \mathbb{R}^+$: Number of chickens.
*   $s_{SS} \in \mathbb{R}^+$: Surplus labor (person-days) in Spring/Summer (sold to outside).
*   $s_{AW} \in \mathbb{R}^+$: Surplus labor (person-days) in Autumn/Winter (sold to outside).

*Note: While the number of animals and hectares are physically discrete/continuous, in standard LP formulations for such agricultural planning problems, variables are typically treated as continuous. If integrality for cows/chickens is strictly required, this would be a Mixed-Integer Linear Program (MILP). Given the large scale (3000 chickens), LP relaxation is standard and usually yields integer or near-integer solutions for these ratios. We will formulate as LP.*

### 4. Objective Function

Maximize Total Annual Net Income ($Z$):

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = 175 x_s + 300 x_c + 120 x_w + 400 x_{cow} + 2 x_{chick} + 2.1 s_{SS} + 1.8 s_{AW}
$$

### 5. Operational Constraints

**1. Land Constraint:**
Total land used for crops and cow forage cannot exceed 100 hm².
$$
x_s + x_c + x_w + 1.5 x_{cow} \le 100
$$

**2. Labor Constraints (Autumn/Winter):**
Total labor used by crops, cows, and chickens plus surplus labor sold cannot exceed 3,500 person-days.
$$
20 x_s + 35 x_c + 10 x_w + 100 x_{cow} + 0.6 x_{chick} + s_{AW} \le 3500
$$

**3. Labor Constraints (Spring/Summer):**
Total labor used by crops, cows, and chickens plus surplus labor sold cannot exceed 4,000 person-days.
$$
50 x_s + 75 x_c + 40 x_w + 50 x_{cow} + 0.3 x_{chick} + s_{SS} \le 4000
$$

**4. Capital/Funds Constraint:**
Total investment in livestock cannot exceed 15,000 yuan. (Crops require no special investment).
$$
400 x_{cow} + 3 x_{chick} \le 15000
$$

**5. Capacity Constraints:**
*   Cowshed capacity:
    $$ x_{cow} \le 32 $$
*   Chicken house capacity:
    $$ x_{chick} \le 3000 $$

**6. Non-negativity Constraints:**
$$
x_s, x_c, x_w, x_{cow}, x_{chick}, s_{SS}, s_{AW} \ge 0
$$

### Summary of the Formulation

**Model Type:** LP (Linear Program)

**Objective:**
$$ \text{Max } Z = 175 x_s + 300 x_c + 120 x_w + 400 x_{cow} + 2 x_{chick} + 2.1 s_{SS} + 1.8 s_{AW} $$

**Subject to:**
1.  $x_s + x_c + x_w + 1.5 x_{cow} \le 100$  (Land)
2.  $20 x_s + 35 x_c + 10 x_w + 100 x_{cow} + 0.6 x_{chick} + s_{AW} \le 3500$  (AW Labor)
3.  $50 x_s + 75 x_c + 40 x_w + 50 x_{cow} + 0.3 x_{chick} + s_{SS} \le 4000$  (SS Labor)
4.  $400 x_{cow} + 3 x_{chick} \le 15000$  (Capital)
5.  $x_{cow} \le 32$
6.  $x_{chick} \le 3000$
7.  $x_s, x_c, x_w, x_{cow}, x_{chick}, s_{SS}, s_{AW} \ge 0$
