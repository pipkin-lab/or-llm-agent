## Model Type
**MILP** (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $P = \{A, B, C\}$: Set of projects (indexed by $p \in P$)
- $K = \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of possible investment levels (in thousand yuan)

**Parameters:**
- $F = 4$: Total available capital (thousand yuan)
- $B_{p,k}$: Benefit (thousand yuan) from investing $k$ thousand yuan in project $p$. Values extracted from Table 7-18:

| $p \setminus k$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **A** | 0 | 41 | 48 | 60 | 66 |
| **B** | 0 | 42 | 50 | 60 | 66 |
| **C** | 0 | 64 | 68 | 78 | 76 |

## Decision Variables

Let $x_{p,k}$ be a binary variable for each project $p$ and investment level $k$:
$$
x_{p,k} = \begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if } k \text{ thousand yuan is invested in project } p \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
Domain: $x_{p,k} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall p \in P, \forall k \in K$

## Objective Function

Maximize the total benefit:
$$
\text{MAXIMIZE } Z = \sum_{p \in P} \sum_{k \in K} B_{p,k} \cdot x_{p,k}
$$

## Operational Constraints

1. **Exclusive Investment Level per Project**: Each project must be assigned to exactly one investment level (including 0).
   $$
   \sum_{k \in K} x_{p,k} = 1 \quad \forall p \in P
   $$

2. **Total Capital Constraint**: The total investment across all projects must not exceed the available capital.
   $$
   \sum_{p \in P} \sum_{k \in K} k \cdot x_{p,k} \le F
   $$
   Substituting $F=4$:
   $$
   \sum_{p \in P} \sum_{k \in K} k \cdot x_{p,k} \le 4
   $$

3. **Variable Domains**:
   $$
   x_{p,k} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall p \in P, \forall k \in K
   $$
