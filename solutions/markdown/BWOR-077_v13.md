**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)

**Problem Analysis:**
1.  **Structure:** This is an **Assembly Line Balancing / Assignment** problem. The line is **serial** (items pass through Station I $\rightarrow$ II $\rightarrow$ III $\rightarrow$ IV $\rightarrow$ V).
2.  **Objective:** Maximize **overall production capacity**. In a serial system, the throughput is limited by the **bottleneck** (the slowest station). Therefore, the objective is to **maximize the minimum efficiency** among the five assigned stations. This is a **Bottleneck / Min-Max** problem (Rule 15).
3.  **Variables:**
    *   $x_{w,s}$: Binary variable indicating if worker $w$ is assigned to station $s$.
    *   $Z$: Continuous variable representing the minimum production rate (bottleneck) of the line.
4.  **Constraints:**
    *   Each worker is assigned to exactly one station.
    *   Each station is assigned exactly one worker.
    *   The efficiency of *each* assigned station must be at least $Z$.

**Parameter Extraction:**
*   Set of Workers $W = \{A, B, C, D, E\}$
*   Set of Stations $S = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ (corresponding to I, II, III, IV, V)
*   Efficiency Matrix $E_{w,s}$ (pieces/min):
    *   $E_{A,1}=2, E_{A,2}=3, E_{A,3}=4, E_{A,4}=1, E_{A,5}=7$
    *   $E_{B,1}=3, E_{B,2}=4, E_{B,3}=2, E_{B,4}=5, E_{B,5}=6$
    *   $E_{C,1}=2, E_{C,2}=5, E_{C,3}=3, C_{C,4}=4, E_{C,5}=1$
    *   $E_{D,1}=5, E_{D,2}=2, E_{D,3}=3, E_{D,4}=2, E_{D,5}=5$
    *   $E_{E,1}=3, E_{E,2}=7, E_{E,3}=6, E_{E,4}=2, E_{E,5}=4$

**Formulation:**

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

*   **Sets:**
    *   $W = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: Index set of workers (1=A, 2=B, 3=C, 4=D, 5=E).
    *   $S = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: Index set of stations (1=I, 2=II, 3=III, 4=IV, 5=V).
*   **Parameters:**
    *   $E_{w,s}$: Production efficiency (pieces/min) of worker $w$ at station $s$.
        *   $E = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 & 4 & 1 & 7 \\ 3 & 4 & 2 & 5 & 6 \\ 2 & 5 & 3 & 4 & 1 \\ 5 & 2 & 3 & 2 & 5 \\ 3 & 7 & 6 & 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$

### 2. Decision Variables

*   $x_{w,s} \in \{0, 1\}$ for all $w \in W, s \in S$:
    *   $x_{w,s} = 1$ if worker $w$ is assigned to station $s$.
    *   $x_{w,s} = 0$ otherwise.
*   $Z \in \mathbb{R}$:
    *   Represents the minimum production rate (bottleneck capacity) of the assembly line (pieces/min).

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize the overall production capacity (bottleneck rate):

$$ \text{Maximize } Z $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**Worker Assignment Constraints (Each worker assigned to exactly one station):**
$$ \sum_{s \in S} x_{w,s} = 1 \quad \forall w \in W $$

**Station Assignment Constraints (Each station assigned exactly one worker):**
$$ \sum_{w \in W} x_{w,s} = 1 \quad \forall s \in S $$

**Bottleneck Capacity Constraints (The assigned worker's efficiency at each station must be at least the line's throughput $Z$):**
$$ \sum_{w \in W} E_{w,s} x_{w,s} \geq Z \quad \forall s \in S $$

**Non-negativity/Domain Constraints:**
$$ x_{w,s} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall w \in W, s \in S $$
$$ Z \geq 0 $$

**Note on Solution Logic (Sanity Check):**
To maximize $Z$, we want the minimum efficiency across the 5 stations to be as high as possible.
Let's check possible values for $Z$. The efficiencies are integers.
If $Z=4$:
- Station 1 needs a worker with $E \ge 4$: Candidates D(5).
- Station 2 needs a worker with $E \ge 4$: Candidates B(4), C(5), E(7).
- Station 3 needs a worker with $E \ge 4$: Candidates A(4), E(6).
- Station 4 needs a worker with $E \ge 4$: Candidates B(5).
- Station 5 needs a worker with $E \ge 4$: Candidates A(7), B(6), D(5), E(4).

Let's try to assign workers for $Z=4$:
- St 1: Must be D (only D has 5, others are 2,3,2,3). So $x_{D,1}=1$.
- St 4: Must be B (only B has 5, others are 1,2,2,2). So $x_{B,4}=1$.
- Remaining workers: A, C, E.
- Remaining stations: 2, 3, 5.
- St 2 needs $\ge 4$: C(5) or E(7).
- St 3 needs $\ge 4$: A(4) or E(6).
- St 5 needs $\ge 4$: A(7), E(4). (B and D are used).

Let's test assigning:
- If St 2 = E (7), St 3 = A (4), St 5 = C (1) -> Fail (C has 1 at St 5).
- If St 2 = C (5), St 3 = E (6), St 5 = A (7) -> Success.
  - St 1: D (5) $\ge 4$
  - St 2: C (5) $\ge 4$
  - St 3: E (6) $\ge 4$
  - St 4: B (5) $\ge 4$
  - St 5: A (7) $\ge 4$
  
  This assignment yields a bottleneck of 4.
  Can we achieve $Z=5$?
  - St 1 needs $\ge 5$: Only D(5). So $x_{D,1}=1$.
  - St 4 needs $\ge 5$: Only B(5). So $x_{B,4}=1$.
  - St 2 needs $\ge 5$: C(5) or E(7).
  - St 3 needs $\ge 5$: E(6). (A has 4). So St 3 MUST be E. $x_{E,3}=1$.
  - St 5 needs $\ge 5$: A(7). (C has 1, E is used, D used, B used). So St 5 MUST be A. $x_{A,5}=1$.
  - Remaining Worker: C.
  - Remaining Station: 2.
  - Check St 2 with C: $E_{C,2} = 5 \ge 5$. Success.
  
  Assignment for $Z=5$:
  - St 1: D (5)
  - St 2: C (5)
  - St 3: E (6)
  - St 4: B (5)
  - St 5: A (7)
  
  Minimum efficiency is 5.
  
  Can we achieve $Z=6$?
  - St 1 needs $\ge 6$: Max is 5 (D). Impossible.
  
  Therefore, the optimal $Z$ is 5.

**Final Answer:** The optimal overall production capacity is **5 pieces/min**.
