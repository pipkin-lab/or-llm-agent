**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $P = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$: Set of available players.
*   $P_C = \{1, 2\}$: Set of players who are Centers.
*   $P_F = \{3, 4, 5\}$: Set of players who are Forwards.
*   $P_G = \{6, 7, 8\}$: Set of players who are Guards.

**Parameters:**
*   $h_i$: Height of player $i$ (in meters).
    *   $h_1 = 1.92$
    *   $h_2 = 1.90$
    *   $h_3 = 1.88$
    *   $h_4 = 1.86$
    *   $h_5 = 1.85$
    *   $h_6 = 1.83$
    *   $h_7 = 1.80$
    *   $h_8 = 1.78$
*   $N = 5$: Number of players in the starting lineup.

### 2. Decision Variables

*   $x_i \in \{0, 1\}$ for all $i \in P$:
    *   $x_i = 1$ if player $i$ is selected for the starting lineup.
    *   $x_i = 0$ otherwise.

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize the total height of the selected lineup:

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i \in P} h_i x_i $$

Expanded:
$$ Z = 1.92x_1 + 1.90x_2 + 1.88x_3 + 1.86x_4 + 1.85x_5 + 1.83x_6 + 1.80x_7 + 1.78x_8 $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**Constraint 1: Lineup Size**
Exactly 5 players must be selected.
$$ \sum_{i \in P} x_i = 5 $$

**Constraint 2: Exactly One Center**
Players 1 and 2 are the only centers. Exactly one of them must be selected.
$$ x_1 + x_2 = 1 $$

**Constraint 3: At Least One Forward**
Players 3, 4, and 5 are forwards. At least one must be selected.
$$ x_3 + x_4 + x_5 \ge 1 $$

**Constraint 4: Logical Implications (Player 1, 4, and 6)**
*   If Player 1 is selected, Player 6 must be selected: $x_1 \le x_6$
*   If Player 4 is selected, Player 6 must be selected: $x_4 \le x_6$
*   If Player 6 is selected, Player 1 cannot be selected: $x_1 + x_6 \le 1$
*   If Player 6 is selected, Player 4 cannot be selected: $x_4 + x_6 \le 1$

*Note: The conditions "If P1 then P6" and "If P6 then not P1" are logically equivalent to $x_1 \neq x_6$ (XOR-like behavior where they cannot both be 0 or both be 1? No, let's analyze carefully).*
*   $x_1 \le x_6$ means if $x_1=1, x_6=1$.
*   $x_1 + x_6 \le 1$ means if $x_6=1, x_1=0$ AND if $x_1=1, x_6=0$? No.
    *   If $x_1=1 \implies x_6=1$ (from $x_1 \le x_6$).
    *   But $x_1 + x_6 \le 1$ forbids $x_1=1, x_6=1$.
    *   Therefore, $x_1$ cannot be 1. So $x_1$ must be 0.
    *   Let's check the logic again.
        *   Condition: "If P1 selected, P6 selected" ($x_1 \le x_6$)
        *   Condition: "If P6 selected, P1 not selected" ($x_1 + x_6 \le 1$)
        *   Combining these: If $x_1=1$, then $x_6=1$. But if $x_6=1$, $x_1=0$. Contradiction. Thus $x_1$ must be 0.
        *   Similarly for P4: $x_4 \le x_6$ and $x_4 + x_6 \le 1 \implies x_4$ must be 0.
    *   So, effectively, Players 1 and 4 cannot be selected at all under these mutual exclusion/implication rules.
    *   However, we should formulate the constraints as stated rather than simplifying the logic immediately, to maintain model integrity.

    The linear constraints are:
    $$ x_1 - x_6 \le 0 $$
    $$ x_4 - x_6 \le 0 $$
    $$ x_1 + x_6 \le 1 $$
    $$ x_4 + x_6 \le 1 $$

**Constraint 5: Player 2 and Player 8 Mutual Exclusion**
At least one of Player 2 or Player 8 must not be selected (they cannot both be in the lineup).
$$ x_2 + x_8 \le 1 $$

**Non-negativity and Integrality:**
$$ x_i \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall i \in P $$

**Maximize:**
$$ Z = 1.92x_1 + 1.90x_2 + 1.88x_3 + 1.86x_4 + 1.85x_5 + 1.83x_6 + 1.80x_7 + 1.78x_8 $$
