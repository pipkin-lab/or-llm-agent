Here is the Operations Research model for the T Factory production planning problem.

### 1. Decision Variables

Let the time horizon be $t \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$ representing months January through June.
The units are measured in "hundreds of units" to match the cost parameters and table data.

*   **Production Quantity:**
    Let $x_t$ be the quantity produced in month $t$ (in hundreds of units).
    *   $x_t \ge 0$, continuous or integer depending on granularity (typically continuous in LP formulations unless specified otherwise, but given "units" are discrete, $x_t$ could be integer. However, standard LP models treat these as continuous unless scale is very small. Given costs are per 100 units, we treat $x_t$ as a continuous variable representing hundreds of units).

*   **Inventory Level:**
    Let $I_t$ be the inventory level at the **end** of month $t$ (in hundreds of units).
    *   $I_t \ge 0$.

*   **Fixed Cost Indicator (Binary Variable):**
    Let $y_t$ be a binary variable indicating whether production occurs in month $t$.
    *   $y_t = 1$ if $x_t > 0$ (production takes place).
    *   $y_t = 0$ if $x_t = 0$ (no production).
    *   $y_t \in \{0, 1\}$.

### 2. Objective Function

The goal is to **Minimize** the total cost, which consists of three components:
1.  **Variable Production Cost:** 10,000 yuan per 100 units. Since $x_t$ is in hundreds of units, the coefficient is 10,000.
2.  **Fixed Operating Cost:** 4,000 yuan per month if production occurs.
3.  **Storage Cost:** 1,000 yuan per 100 units per month. This applies to the inventory held at the end of each month (which is stored for the next month). *Note: Typically, storage cost is incurred on the inventory held during the month. The problem states "storage cost is 1,000 yuan per 100 units per month." Standard convention implies cost is paid on the ending inventory of month $t$ which is available for month $t+1$. We sum storage costs for $t=1$ to $5$ (inventory at end of month 6 is 0, so it doesn't matter, but logically storage is for the duration of the month).*

**Total Cost ($Z$):**

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{t=1}^{6} (10000 x_t + 4000 y_t) + \sum_{t=1}^{5} 1000 I_t
$$

*(Note: Storage cost for $I_6$ is 0 because $I_6=0$. We can include $t=6$ in the sum as it adds 0, or exclude it. Excluding it is cleaner.)*

$$
Z = 10000 \sum_{t=1}^{6} x_t + 4000 \sum_{t=1}^{6} y_t + 1000 \sum_{t=1}^{5} I_t
$$

### 3. Operational Constraints

**A. Inventory Balance Constraints (Flow Conservation)**
For each month $t$, the inventory at the end of the month is the previous month's inventory plus current production minus current demand.
Let $d_t$ be the demand in month $t$.
*   $d_1=1, d_2=2, d_3=5, d_4=3, d_5=2, d_6=1$ (in hundreds of units).
*   Initial inventory $I_0 = 0$.
*   Final inventory requirement $I_6 = 0$.

$$
I_t = I_{t-1} + x_t - d_t \quad \forall t \in \{1, \dots, 6\}
$$

Specifically:
1.  $I_1 = 0 + x_1 - 1$
2.  $I_2 = I_1 + x_2 - 2$
3.  $I_3 = I_2 + x_3 - 5$
4.  $I_4 = I_3 + x_4 - 3$
5.  $I_5 = I_4 + x_5 - 2$
6.  $I_6 = I_5 + x_6 - 1 = 0$ (Final constraint explicitly sets end inventory to 0)

**B. Capacity Constraints**

*   **Production Capacity:** The factory can produce at most 400 units per month. Since $x_t$ is in hundreds of units, 400 units = 4 hundreds.
    $$
    x_t \le 4 \quad \forall t \in \{1, \dots, 6\}
    $$

*   **Warehouse Capacity:** The warehouse can hold at most 300 units. Since $I_t$ is in hundreds of units, 300 units = 3 hundreds.
    $$
    I_t \le 3 \quad \forall t \in \{1, \dots, 6\}
    $$

**C. Linking Constraints (Fixed Cost Activation)**
If production is positive, $y_t$ must be 1. If $y_t=0$, production must be 0. We use the Big-M method. The maximum possible production is 4 (hundreds).

$$
x_t \le 4 y_t \quad \forall t \in \{1, \dots, 6\}
$$

**D. Non-negativity and Integrality**

$$
x_t \ge 0 \quad \forall t
$$
$$
I_t \ge 0 \quad \forall t
$$
$$
y_t \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall t
$$

*(Note: If $x_t$ is treated as continuous, the model is a Mixed Integer Linear Program (MILP) due to the binary variables $y_t$. If strict integer units are required, $x_t$ and $I_t$ should be integers, making it a Mixed Integer Linear Program with integer variables as well.)*
