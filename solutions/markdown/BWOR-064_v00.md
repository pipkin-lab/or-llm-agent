# Operations Research Model: Factory Renovation Investment Allocation

## 1. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be a binary decision variable, where:
*   $i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ represents the factory index (Factory 1, Factory 2, Factory 3).
*   $j$ represents the specific renovation plan index available for factory $i$.
*   $x_{ij} = 1$ if plan $j$ is selected for factory $i$.
*   $x_{ij} = 0$ if plan $j$ is not selected for factory $i$.

Based on Table 7-4, the available plans and their associated investment costs ($c_{ij}$) and returns ($r_{ij}$) are:

**Factory 1:**
*   $j=1$: $c_{1,1}=0, r_{1,1}=0$
*   $j=2$: $c_{1,2}=1, r_{1,2}=5$
*   $j=3$: $c_{1,3}=2, r_{1,3}=6$

**Factory 2:**
*   $j=1$: $c_{2,1}=0, r_{2,1}=0$
*   $j=2$: $c_{2,2}=2, r_{2,2}=8$
*   $j=3$: $c_{2,3}=3, r_{2,3}=9$
*   $j=4$: $c_{2,4}=4, r_{2,4}=12$

**Factory 3:**
*   $j=1$: $c_{3,1}=0, r_{3,1}=0$
*   $j=2$: $c_{3,2}=1, r_{3,2}=3$

## 2. Objective Function

The goal is to **maximize** the total expected return.

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i} \sum_{j} r_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Expanded explicitly:
$$
\text{Maximize } Z = (0 x_{1,1} + 5 x_{1,2} + 6 x_{1,3}) + (0 x_{2,1} + 8 x_{2,2} + 9 x_{2,3} + 12 x_{2,4}) + (0 x_{3,1} + 3 x_{3,2})
$$

## 3. Operational Constraints

### Constraint 1: Budget Limit
The total investment across all selected plans must not exceed the available capital of 5 million yuan.

$$
\sum_{i} \sum_{j} c_{ij} x_{ij} \leq 5
$$

Expanded explicitly:
$$
1 x_{1,2} + 2 x_{1,3} + 2 x_{2,2} + 3 x_{2,3} + 4 x_{2,4} + 1 x_{3,2} \leq 5
$$
*(Note: Plans with 0 investment do not affect the LHS)*

### Constraint 2: Mutually Exclusive Plans per Factory
For each factory, exactly one plan must be selected (including the "do nothing" plan $j=1$).

**For Factory 1:**
$$
x_{1,1} + x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} = 1
$$

**For Factory 2:**
$$
x_{2,1} + x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} + x_{2,4} = 1
$$

**For Factory 3:**
$$
x_{3,1} + x_{3,2} = 1
$$

### Constraint 3: Binary Restrictions
All decision variables must be binary (0 or 1).

$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \text{for all valid } i, j
$$
