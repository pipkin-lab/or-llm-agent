Model Type: LP

### Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $I = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of warehouses (corresponding to $A_1, A_2, A_3$).
*   $J = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: Set of regions (corresponding to $B_1, B_2, B_3, B_4, B_5$).

**Parameters:**
*   $S = 270$: Total monthly production capacity of the sugar factory (tons).
*   $U_i$: Storage capacity of warehouse $i$ (tons).
    *   $U_1 = 50$
    *   $U_2 = 100$
    *   $U_3 = 150$
*   $D_j$: Demand of region $j$ (tons).
    *   $D_1 = 25$
    *   $D_2 = 105$
    *   $D_3 = 60$
    *   $D_4 = 30$
    *   $D_5 = 70$
*   $C_{ij}$: Transportation and storage cost per ton from warehouse $i$ to region $j$.
    *   Row 1 ($A_1$): $C_{1,1}=10, C_{1,2}=15, C_{1,3}=20, C_{1,4}=20, C_{1,5}=40$
    *   Row 2 ($A_2$): $C_{2,1}=20, C_{2,2}=40, C_{2,3}=15, C_{2,4}=30, C_{2,5}=30$
    *   Row 3 ($A_3$): $C_{3,1}=30, C_{3,2}=35, C_{3,3}=40, C_{3,4}=55, C_{3,5}=25$

**Feasibility Check:**
*   Total Supply ($S$) = 270 tons.
*   Total Demand ($\sum D_j$) = $25 + 105 + 60 + 30 + 70 = 290$ tons.
*   Total Warehouse Capacity ($\sum U_i$) = $50 + 100 + 150 = 300$ tons.
*   Since Total Supply (270) < Total Demand (290), not all demand can be met.
*   Since Total Supply (270) < Total Warehouse Capacity (300), the factory will produce exactly 270 tons, and these must be fully allocated to regions. The warehouse capacity constraints will act as upper bounds on flow through each warehouse.

### Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be the amount of sugar (in tons) transported from warehouse $i$ to region $j$.

*   **Domain**: $x_{ij} \ge 0$ and continuous for all $i \in I, j \in J$.
    *   (Note: While sugar is a physical good, in large-scale transportation LPs, it is standard to treat it as a continuous variable unless integer constraints are explicitly requested. Given the scale (hundreds of tons), continuous variables are appropriate for an LP).

### Objective Function

Minimize the total transportation cost.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{3} \sum_{j=1}^{5} C_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Expanded form:
$$
Z = 10x_{11} + 15x_{12} + 20x_{13} + 20x_{14} + 40x_{15} + 20x_{21} + 40x_{22} + 15x_{23} + 30x_{24} + 30x_{25} + 30x_{31} + 35x_{32} + 40x_{33} + 55x_{34} + 25x_{35}
$$

### Operational Constraints

1.  **Supply Constraint (Factory Production):**
    The total amount shipped from all warehouses must equal the total production capacity, as all produced sugar must be shipped (and supply < demand).
    $$
    \sum_{i=1}^{3} \sum_{j=1}^{5} x_{ij} = 270
    $$

2.  **Warehouse Capacity Constraints:**
    The total amount shipped from each warehouse cannot exceed its storage capacity.
    $$
    \sum_{j=1}^{5} x_{1j} \le 50 \quad (\text{for } A_1)
    $$
    $$
    \sum_{j=1}^{5} x_{2j} \le 100 \quad (\text{for } A_2)
    $$
    $$
    \sum_{j=1}^{5} x_{3j} \le 150 \quad (\text{for } A_3)
    $$

3.  **Region Demand Constraints:**
    The total amount received by each region cannot exceed its demand. (Note: Since total supply < total demand, these are inequalities allowing for unmet demand).
    $$
    \sum_{i=1}^{3} x_{i1} \le 25 \quad (\text{for } B_1)
    $$
    $$
    \sum_{i=1}^{3} x_{i2} \le 105 \quad (\text{for } B_2)
    $$
    $$
    \sum_{i=1}^{3} x_{i3} \le 60 \quad (\text{for } B_3)
    $$
    $$
    \sum_{i=1}^{3} x_{i4} \le 30 \quad (\text{for } B_4)
    $$
    $$
    \sum_{i=1}^{3} x_{i5} \le 70 \quad (\text{for } B_5)
    $$

4.  **Non-negativity:**
    $$
    x_{ij} \ge 0 \quad \forall i \in I, j \in J
    $$
