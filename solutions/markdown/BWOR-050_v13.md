# 200-meter Medley Relay Team Selection

## Model Type: MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Programming)

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $S = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of strokes. Indexing: $1 = \text{Backstroke}$, $2 = \text{Breaststroke}$, $3 = \text{Butterfly}$, $4 = \text{Freestyle}$.
- $A = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: Set of athletes. Indexing: $1 = \text{Zhao}$, $2 = \text{Qian}$, $3 = \text{Zhang}$, $4 = \text{Wang}$, $5 = \text{Zhou}$.

**Parameters:**
- $c_{s,a}$: The 50-meter swimming time (in seconds) for athlete $a$ in stroke $s$. Extracted from Table 5-2:

| $c_{s,a}$ | Zhao ($a=1$) | Qian ($a=2$) | Zhang ($a=3$) | Wang ($a=4$) | Zhou ($a=5$) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Backstroke** ($s=1$) | 37.7 | 32.9 | 33.8 | 37.0 | 35.4 |
| **Breaststroke** ($s=2$) | 43.4 | 33.1 | 42.2 | 34.7 | 41.8 |
| **Butterfly** ($s=3$) | 33.3 | 28.5 | 38.9 | 30.4 | 33.6 |
| **Freestyle** ($s=4$) | 29.2 | 26.4 | 29.6 | 28.5 | 31.1 |

## Decision Variables

- $x_{s,a} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable for all $s \in S, a \in A$.
  - $x_{s,a} = 1$ if athlete $a$ is assigned to swim stroke $s$.
  - $x_{s,a} = 0$ otherwise.

## Objective Function

Minimize the total expected relay time:

$$ \min Z = \sum_{s \in S} \sum_{a \in A} c_{s,a} x_{s,a} $$

## Operational Constraints

**1. Stroke Assignment Constraints:**
Each stroke must be swum by exactly one athlete.
$$ \sum_{a \in A} x_{s,a} = 1, \quad \forall s \in S $$

**2. Athlete Assignment Constraints:**
Each athlete can swim at most one stroke (since there are 5 athletes and 4 strokes, one athlete will be a non-swimmer/bench).
$$ \sum_{s \in S} x_{s,a} \leq 1, \quad \forall a \in A $$

**3. Variable Domains:**
$$ x_{s,a} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall s \in S, a \in A $$
