# Model Type: MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Program)

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $I = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of products, where $1=$ Shirt, $2=$ T-shirt, $3=$ Casual Wear.

**Parameters (extracted from Table 5-10 and text):**

| Parameter | Description | $i=1$ (Shirt) | $i=2$ (T-shirt) | $i=3$ (Casual Wear) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $a_{labor, i}$ | Labor per unit | 3 | 2 | 6 |
| $b_{mat, i}$ | Material per unit | 4 | 3 | 6 |
| $P_i$ | Selling price per unit | 120 | 80 | 180 |
| $C_{var, i}$ | Variable cost per unit | 60 | 40 | 80 |
| $F_i$ | Fixed cost per week (if produced) | 2,000 | 1,500 | 1,000 |

**Resource Capacities:**
- $L_{total} = 150$ (Weekly labor availability)
- $M_{total} = 160$ (Weekly material availability)

**Derived Parameters:**
- Unit Contribution Margin $m_i = P_i - C_{var, i}$:
  - $m_1 = 120 - 60 = 60$
  - $m_2 = 80 - 40 = 40$
  - $m_3 = 180 - 80 = 100$

## Decision Variables

1.  $x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$: Number of units of product $i$ produced per week.
2.  $y_i \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable for product $i$, where:
    -   $y_i = 1$ if product $i$ is produced (equipment operated).
    -   $y_i = 0$ if product $i$ is not produced (equipment idle).

## Objective Function

Maximize total weekly profit. Profit is the sum of total contribution margins minus total fixed costs.

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} (m_i x_i - F_i y_i)
$$

Substituting values:
$$
\text{Maximize } Z = 60 x_1 + 40 x_2 + 100 x_3 - 2000 y_1 - 1500 y_2 - 1000 y_3
$$

## Operational Constraints

**1. Production-Linking Constraints (Fixed Cost Logic)**
If any quantity of product $i$ is produced, the equipment must be turned on ($y_i=1$). If the equipment is off ($y_i=0$), production must be zero. We use a sufficiently large upper bound $M_i$ for each product. A valid $M_i$ is the maximum possible production quantity limited by the scarcest resource if that product were produced alone.
-   Max $x_1$ (limited by labor): $150/3 = 50$; (limited by material): $160/4 = 40$. So $M_1 = 40$.
-   Max $x_2$ (limited by labor): $150/2 = 75$; (limited by material): $160/3 \approx 53.3$. So $M_2 = 53$ (integer floor).
-   Max $x_3$ (limited by labor): $150/6 = 25$; (limited by material): $160/6 \approx 26.6$. So $M_3 = 25$.

$$
x_i \le M_i y_i \quad \forall i \in I
$$
Specifically:
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_1 &\le 40 y_1 \\
x_2 &\le 53 y_2 \\
x_3 &\le 25 y_3
\end{aligned}
$$

**2. Resource Availability Constraints**
Total labor and material usage cannot exceed weekly availability.

$$
\sum_{i \in I} a_{labor, i} x_i \le L_{total} \implies 3x_1 + 2x_2 + 6x_3 \le 150
$$

$$
\sum_{i \in I} b_{mat, i} x_i \le M_{total} \implies 4x_1 + 3x_2 + 6x_3 \le 160
$$

**3. Non-negativity and Integrality**
$$
x_i \ge 0, \quad x_i \in \mathbb{Z} \quad \forall i \in I
$$
$$
y_i \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in I
$$
