Based on the problem description, here is the formal Operations Research model.

### 1. Decision Variables
Let $x_i$ be the number of units of Product $i$ produced and shipped, where $i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$.
Since the number of products must be whole numbers, $x_i$ are non-negative integers.

### 2. Objective Function
The goal is to maximize the total profit. Based on Table 7-8, the profit coefficients are 100 for Product 1, 140 for Product 2, and 180 for Product 3.

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 100x_1 + 140x_2 + 180x_3 $$

### 3. Operational Constraints
**Transportation Capacity Constraint:**
The total weight of the shipped products cannot exceed the transportation capacity of 10 tons. The weights are 2 tons per piece for Product 1, 3 tons per piece for Product 2, and 4 tons per piece for Product 3.

$$ 2x_1 + 3x_2 + 4x_3 \le 10 $$

**Non-negativity and Integrality Constraints:**
The number of products produced cannot be negative and must be an integer.

$$ x_1, x_2, x_3 \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0} $$
