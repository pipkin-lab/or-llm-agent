## Mathematical Optimization Model

**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Program)

---

### Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
- $R = \{1, 2, 3\}$: set of regions
- $K = \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$: set of possible numbers of sales outlets per region

**Parameters (from Table 7-2):**

| Region $r$ | $p_{r,0}$ | $p_{r,1}$ | $p_{r,2}$ | $p_{r,3}$ | $p_{r,4}$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 16 | 25 | 30 | 32 |
| 2 | 0 | 12 | 17 | 21 | 22 |
| 3 | 0 | 10 | 14 | 16 | 17 |

- $N = 4$: total number of sales outlets to be allocated.

---

### Decision Variables

For each region $r \in R$ and each possible allocation $k \in K$:

$$x_{r,k} \in \{0, 1\}$$

where $x_{r,k} = 1$ if exactly $k$ sales outlets are assigned to region $r$, and $0$ otherwise.

---

### Objective Function

$$\text{MAXIMIZE} \quad Z = \sum_{r \in R} \sum_{k \in K} p_{r,k} \cdot x_{r,k}$$

Expanded explicitly:

$$Z = 0 \cdot x_{1,0} + 16 \cdot x_{1,1} + 25 \cdot x_{1,2} + 30 \cdot x_{1,3} + 32 \cdot x_{1,4} + 0 \cdot x_{2,0} + 12 \cdot x_{2,1} + 17 \cdot x_{2,2} + 21 \cdot x_{2,3} + 22 \cdot x_{2,4} + 0 \cdot x_{3,0} + 10 \cdot x_{3,1} + 14 \cdot x_{3,2} + 16 \cdot x_{3,3} + 17 \cdot x_{3,4}$$

---

### Operational Constraints

**1. Exactly one allocation level per region (each region gets exactly one number of outlets):**

$$\sum_{k=0}^{4} x_{r,k} = 1, \quad \forall r \in \{1, 2, 3\}$$

i.e.:
- $\sum_{k=0}^{4} x_{1,k} = 1$
- $\sum_{k=0}^{4} x_{2,k} = 1$
- $\sum_{k=0}^{4} x_{3,k} = 1$

**2. Total number of outlets equals 4:**

$$\sum_{r \in R} \sum_{k \in K} k \cdot x_{r,k} = 4$$

i.e.:

$$0 \cdot x_{1,0} + 1 \cdot x_{1,1} + 2 \cdot x_{1,2} + 3 \cdot x_{1,3} + 4 \cdot x_{1,4} + 0 \cdot x_{2,0} + 1 \cdot x_{2,1} + 2 \cdot x_{2,2} + 3 \cdot x_{2,3} + 4 \cdot x_{2,4} + 0 \cdot x_{3,0} + 1 \cdot x_{3,1} + 2 \cdot x_{3,2} + 3 \cdot x_{3,3} + 4 \cdot x_{3,4} = 4$$

**3. Binary domain constraints:**

$$x_{r,k} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall r \in R, \; \forall k \in K$$

---

### Solution

This is a classic **integer resource allocation** problem. With 4 outlets distributed among 3 regions, the feasible allocations $(a, b, c)$ where $a+b+c=4$ and $a,b,c \geq 0$ are all compositions of 4 into 3 non-negative parts. Evaluating the profit for each:

| Allocation $(a,b,c)$ | Profit = $p_{1,a} + p_{2,b} + p_{3,c}$ |
|---|---|
| (4,0,0) | 32 + 0 + 0 = 32 |
| (0,4,0) | 0 + 22 + 0 = 22 |
| (0,0,4) | 0 + 0 + 17 = 17 |
| (3,1,0) | 30 + 12 + 0 = 42 |
| (3,0,1) | 30 + 0 + 10 = 40 |
| (1,3,0) | 16 + 21 + 0 = 37 |
| (0,3,1) | 0 + 21 + 10 = 31 |
| (2,2,0) | 25 + 17 + 0 = 42 |
| (2,0,2) | 25 + 0 + 14 = 39 |
| (0,2,2) | 0 + 17 + 14 = 31 |
| (1,2,1) | 16 + 17 + 10 = 43 |
| (2,1,1) | 25 + 12 + 10 = 47 |
| (1,1,2) | 16 + 12 + 14 = 42 |
| (0,1,3) | 0 + 12 + 16 = 28 |
| (1,0,3) | 16 + 0 + 16 = 32 |
| (0,2,2) | 31 |
| (2,2,0) | 42 |
| (3,1,0) | 42 |
| (1,2,1) | 43 |
| (2,1,1) | **47** |

**Optimal Solution:**

- **Region 1:** 2 sales outlets
- **Region 2:** 1 sales outlet
- **Region 3:** 1 sales outlet

**Maximum monthly profit = 25 + 12 + 10 = 47** (in the units given in the table).
