### Model Type Declaration
**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)
**Solver Requirement:** A standard linear solver (e.g., CBC, CPLEX, Gurobi) is sufficient. The objective function is linear in the decision variables (using pre-computed logarithmic values), and the constraints are linear.

---

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $C = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of components, where $1 \rightarrow A_1$, $2 \rightarrow A_2$, $3 \rightarrow A_3$.
*   $K = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of possible spare part quantities for each component.

**Parameters:**
*   $B = 8$: Total budget limit (thousand yuan).
*   $c_{c,k}$: Cost (thousand yuan) of assigning $k$ spare parts to component $c$.
*   $r_{c,k}$: Reliability of component $c$ with $k$ spare parts.
*   $\log r_{c,k}$: Natural logarithm of the reliability (objective coefficient).

**Data Extraction from Table 7-19:**

| $k$ (Spares) | $c_{1,k}$ ($A_1$ Cost) | $c_{2,k}$ ($A_2$ Cost) | $c_{3,k}$ ($A_3$ Cost) | $r_{1,k}$ ($A_1$ Rel) | $r_{2,k}$ ($A_2$ Rel) | $r_{3,k}$ ($A_3$ Rel) | $\ln(r_{1,k})$ | $\ln(r_{2,k})$ | $\ln(r_{3,k})$ |

### 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_{c,k}$ be a binary decision variable:
$$
x_{c,k} = \begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if component } c \text{ is allocated exactly } k \text{ spare parts} \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
Domain: $x_{c,k} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall c \in C, k \in K$.

---

### 3. Objective Function

The problem asks to maximize the equipment's operational reliability. Since the components work in series, the total reliability is the product of individual reliabilities: $R_{total} = r_{1, k_1} \cdot r_{2, k_2} \cdot r_{3, k_3}$.
Maximizing $R_{total}$ is equivalent to maximizing $\ln(R_{total}) = \sum_{c=1}^{3} \ln(r_{c, k_c})$.

$$
\text{MAXIMIZE } Z = \sum_{c \in C} \sum_{k \in K} \ln(r_{c,k}) \cdot x_{c,k}
$$

Substituting the specific values:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{MAXIMIZE } Z = \quad & (-1.2040)x_{1,1} + (-0.9163)x_{1,2} + (-0.6931)x_{1,3} \\
+ & (-1.6094)x_{2,1} + (-0.6931)x_{2,2} + (-0.1054)x_{2,3} \\
+ & (-2.3026)x_{3,1} + (-1.6094)x_{3,2} + (-0.3567)x_{3,3}
\end{aligned}
$$

---

### 4. Operational Constraints

**Constraint 1: Allocation Uniqueness**
Each component must have exactly one allocation level (1, 2, or 3 spares).
$$
\sum_{k \in K} x_{c,k} = 1 \quad \forall c \in C
$$
Explicitly:
1.  $x_{1,1} + x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} = 1$
2.  $x_{2,1} + x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} = 1$
3.  $x_{3,1} + x_{3,2} + x_{3,3} = 1$

**Constraint 2: Budget Limit**
The total cost of spare parts must not exceed the total investment of 8 thousand yuan.
$$
\sum_{c \in C} \sum_{k \in K} c_{c,k} \cdot x_{c,k} \le B
$$
Explicitly:
$$
1x_{1,1} + 2x_{1,2} + 3x_{1,3} + 3x_{2,1} + 5x_{2,2} + 6x_{2,3} + 2x_{3,1} + 3x_{3,2} + 4x_{3,3} \le 8
$$

**Constraint 3: Variable Domains**
$$
x_{c,k} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall c \in \{1, 2, 3\}, k \in \{1, 2, 3\}
$$
