# Operations Research Model: Workforce Recruitment Optimization

## 1. Decision Variables

Let the decision variables represent the number of workers recruited for each specific task configuration. Since workers are assigned to specific tasks in fixed team compositions, we define variables for each possible team type for each task.

Let:
*   $x_{1a}$: Number of "Technician-only" teams for Task 1.
*   $x_{1b}$: Number of "1 Technician + 2 Laborers" teams for Task 1.
*   $x_{2a}$: Number of "Technician-only" teams for Task 2.
*   $x_{2b}$: Number of "Laborer-only" teams for Task 2.
*   $x_{3a}$: Number of "5 Laborers" teams for Task 3.
*   $x_{3b}$: Number of "1 Technician + 3 Laborers" teams for Task 3.

All decision variables $x_{ij} \ge 0$ and must be integers (assuming discrete teams/people).

## 2. Objective Function

**Goal:** Minimize the total weekly wage cost.

First, calculate the weekly cost per team for each configuration:
*   **Task 1:**
    *   Team $x_{1a}$ (1 Tech): $1 \times 100 = 100$ yuan
    *   Team $x_{1b}$ (1 Tech + 2 Lab): $1 \times 100 + 2 \times 80 = 260$ yuan
*   **Task 2:**
    *   Team $x_{2a}$ (1 Tech): $1 \times 100 = 100$ yuan
    *   Team $x_{2b}$ (1 Lab): $1 \times 80 = 80$ yuan
*   **Task 3:**
    *   Team $x_{3a}$ (5 Lab): $5 \times 80 = 400$ yuan
    *   Team $x_{3b}$ (1 Tech + 3 Lab): $1 \times 100 + 3 \times 80 = 340$ yuan

**Objective Function:**
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = 100x_{1a} + 260x_{1b} + 100x_{2a} + 80x_{2b} + 400x_{3a} + 340x_{3b} $$

## 3. Operational Constraints

### A. Workforce Availability Constraints
The total number of technicians and laborers recruited cannot exceed the upper limits. We sum the workers of each type across all team types.

**Technician Limit (Max 400):**
*   $x_{1a}$ uses 1 Tech
*   $x_{1b}$ uses 1 Tech
*   $x_{2a}$ uses 1 Tech
*   $x_{3b}$ uses 1 Tech
$$ x_{1a} + x_{1b} + x_{2a} + x_{3b} \le 400 $$

**Laborer Limit (Max 800):**
*   $x_{1b}$ uses 2 Laborers
*   $x_{2b}$ uses 1 Laborer
*   $x_{3a}$ uses 5 Laborers
*   $x_{3b}$ uses 3 Laborers
$$ 2x_{1b} + x_{2b} + 5x_{3a} + 3x_{3b} \le 800 $$

### B. Task Completion Constraints (Effective Working Time)
The total effective working hours provided by the assigned teams must meet or exceed the required hours for each task.
*   Effective hours per week per Technician = 42
*   Effective hours per week per Laborer = 36

**Task 1 (Required: 10,000 hours):**
*   Team $x_{1a}$ provides: $1 \text{ Tech} \times 42 = 42$ hours
*   Team $x_{1b}$ provides: $(1 \text{ Tech} \times 42) + (2 \text{ Lab} \times 36) = 42 + 72 = 114$ hours
$$ 42x_{1a} + 114x_{1b} \ge 10,000 $$

**Task 2 (Required: 20,000 hours):**
*   Team $x_{2a}$ provides: $1 \text{ Tech} \times 42 = 42$ hours
*   Team $x_{2b}$ provides: $1 \text{ Lab} \times 36 = 36$ hours
$$ 42x_{2a} + 36x_{2b} \ge 20,000 $$

**Task 3 (Required: 30,000 hours):**
*   Team $x_{3a}$ provides: $5 \text{ Lab} \times 36 = 180$ hours
*   Team $x_{3b}$ provides: $(1 \text{ Tech} \times 42) + (3 \text{ Lab} \times 36) = 42 + 108 = 150$ hours
$$ 180x_{3a} + 150x_{3b} \ge 30,000 $$

### C. Non-negativity and Integrality Constraints
$$ x_{1a}, x_{1b}, x_{2a}, x_{2b}, x_{3a}, x_{3b} \in \mathbb{Z}^+ \cup \{0\} $$

---

## Final Mathematical Model Summary

**Minimize:**
$$ Z = 100x_{1a} + 260x_{1b} + 100x_{2a} + 80x_{2b} + 400x_{3a} + 340x_{3b} $$

**Subject to:**
1.  **Technician Capacity:**
    $$ x_{1a} + x_{1b} + x_{2a} + x_{3b} \le 400 $$
2.  **Laborer Capacity:**
    $$ 2x_{1b} + x_{2b} + 5x_{3a} + 3x_{3b} \le 800 $$
3.  **Task 1 Hours:**
    $$ 42x_{1a} + 114x_{1b} \ge 10,000 $$
4.  **Task 2 Hours:**
    $$ 42x_{2a} + 36x_{2b} \ge 20,000 $$
5.  **Task 3 Hours:**
    $$ 180x_{3a} + 150x_{3b} \ge 30,000 $$
6.  **Integrality:**
    $$ x_{ij} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0} \quad \forall i, j $$
