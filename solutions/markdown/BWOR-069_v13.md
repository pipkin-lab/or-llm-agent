Model Type: MILP

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $I$: Set of product types, $I = \{1, 2, 3\}$

**Parameters:**
*   $w_i$: Weight of one piece of product $i$ (tons/piece)
    *   $w_1 = 2$
    *   $w_2 = 3$
    *   $w_3 = 4$
*   $p_i$: Profit of one piece of product $i$ (yuan/piece)
    *   $p_1 = 100$
    *   $p_2 = 140$
    *   $p_3 = 180$
*   $W_{max}$: Total transportation capacity limit (tons)
    *   $W_{max} = 10$

### 2. Decision Variables

*   $x_i$: Number of pieces of product $i$ to produce and ship.
    *   Domain: $x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for all $i \in I$ (Integer, non-negative, as products are discrete units).

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize total profit:

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} p_i x_i $$

Substituting the values:
$$ Z = 100x_1 + 140x_2 + 180x_3 $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**Transportation Capacity Constraint:**
The total weight of all shipped products must not exceed the transportation capacity.

$$ \sum_{i \in I} w_i x_i \le W_{max} $$

Substituting the values:
$$ 2x_1 + 3x_2 + 4x_3 \le 10 $$

**Non-negativity and Integrality Constraints:**
$$ x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0 \quad \text{and integer} $$
