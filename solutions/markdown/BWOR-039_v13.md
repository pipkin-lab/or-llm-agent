## Decision Variables

$$x_i \geq 0, \quad i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$$

- $x_1$: monthly production quantity of manuscript paper (bundles) — **continuous**
- $x_2$: monthly production quantity of diaries (dozens) — **continuous**
- $x_3$: monthly production quantity of exercise books (boxes) — **continuous**

*Note: Although bundles/dozens/boxes are discrete units, this is a standard production planning LP. The optimal solution will be at a vertex; integrality is not enforced as the problem does not require integer production quantities and the standard formulation treats these as continuous aggregate quantities.*

## Objective Function

$$\text{MAXIMIZE } Z = 2\,x_1 + 3\,x_2 + 1\,x_3$$

## Operational Constraints

**1. Labor constraint (total worker-months required ≤ available workers):**
$$\frac{1}{30}\,x_1 + \frac{1}{30}\,x_2 + \frac{1}{30}\,x_3 \leq 100$$

Equivalently: $x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \leq 3000$

**2. Raw material constraint (total white base paper consumed ≤ monthly supply):**
$$\frac{10}{3}\,x_1 + \frac{40}{3}\,x_2 + \frac{80}{3}\,x_3 \leq 30000$$

Equivalently: $10\,x_1 + 40\,x_2 + 80\,x_3 \leq 90000$

**3. Non-negativity bounds:**
$$x_1 \geq 0, \quad x_2 \geq 0, \quad x_3 \geq 0$$

## Feasibility Sanity Check

- **Labor-only bound:** If only labor were binding, max total units = 3000.
- **Material-only bound:** If only material were binding, max total units (if all product 1) = $90000/10 = 9000$.
- **Combined:** The labor constraint is tighter in terms of total units. Both constraints are simultaneously satisfiable (e.g., $x_1 = x_2 = x_3 = 0$ is feasible).
- **Objective magnitude check:** At the greedy solution (all diaries, $x_2 = 3000$ from labor), profit = $3 \times 3000 = 9000$ yuan. Material used = $40 \times 3000 / 3 = 40000 > 30000$ — infeasible. The true optimum will be lower, consistent with a problem of this scale (thousands of yuan profit from 100 workers and 30 tons of paper).
