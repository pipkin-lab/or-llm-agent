# Production Scheduling Model

## 1. Decision Variables
Let $x_{i,t}$ be the production quantity of product $i$ in quarter $t$.
Let $I_{i,t}$ be the ending inventory of product $i$ at the end of quarter $t$.
Let $L_{i,t}$ be the quantity of product $i$ that is late-delivered (backordered) at the end of quarter $t$ (i.e., demand met from future production).

*   **Indices:**
    *   $i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ representing Products I, II, and III.
    *   $t \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ representing Quarters 1 through 4.

*   **Variable Definitions:**
    *   $x_{i,t} \geq 0$: Units of product $i$ produced in quarter $t$.
    *   $I_{i,t} \geq 0$: Units of product $i$ held in inventory at the end of quarter $t$.
    *   $L_{i,t} \geq 0$: Units of product $i$ short (late delivery) at the end of quarter $t$.

*   **Specific Constraint Variable:**
    *   $x_{1,2} = 0$: Product I cannot be produced in Quarter 2.

## 2. Objective Function
**Minimize** the total cost of late delivery penalties and inventory holding costs.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{3} \sum_{t=1}^{4} (C^L_i L_{i,t} + C^I_i I_{i,t})
$$

Where:
*   $C^L_1 = 20$, $C^L_2 = 20$, $C^L_3 = 10$ (Penalty cost per unit per quarter).
*   $C^I_1 = 5$, $C^I_2 = 5$, $C^I_3 = 5$ (Inventory holding cost per unit per quarter).

## 3. Operational Constraints

### A. Inventory Balance Constraints
For each product $i$ and quarter $t$, the inventory balance is determined by the previous inventory, current production, and current demand. The difference is split into ending inventory ($I_{i,t}$) and backorders/late deliveries ($L_{i,t}$).

$$
I_{i,t} - L_{i,t} = I_{i,t-1} - L_{i,t-1} + x_{i,t} - D_{i,t} \quad \forall i \in \{1,2,3\}, t \in \{1,2,3,4\}
$$

*   **Initial Conditions ($t=0$):**
    *   $I_{i,0} = 0$ (No initial inventory).
    *   $L_{i,0} = 0$ (No initial backorders).
*   **Demand Data ($D_{i,t}$):**
    *   Product I: $D_{1,1}=1500, D_{1,2}=1000, D_{1,3}=2000, D_{1,4}=1200$
    *   Product II: $D_{2,1}=1500, D_{2,2}=1500, D_{2,3}=1200, D_{2,4}=1500$
    *   Product III: $D_{3,1}=1000, D_{3,2}=2000, D_{3,3}=1500, D_{3,4}=2500$

### B. Final Inventory Requirement
By the end of Q4, there must be an inventory of 150 units for each product. This implies that all backorders must be cleared ($L_{i,4}=0$) and the net inventory is 150.

$$
I_{i,4} - L_{i,4} = 150 \quad \forall i \in \{1,2,3\}
$$
$$
L_{i,4} = 0 \quad \forall i \in \{1,2,3\}
$$

### C. Production Capacity Constraints
The total working hours used for production in each quarter cannot exceed the available 15,000 hours.
*   Hours per unit: Product I = 2, Product II = 4, Product III = 3.

$$
2x_{1,t} + 4x_{2,t} + 3x_{3,t} \leq 15,000 \quad \forall t \in \{1,2,3,4\}
$$

### D. Product-Specific Production Restriction
Product I cannot be produced in Quarter 2.

$$
x_{1,2} = 0
$$

### E. Non-negativity Constraints
All production, inventory, and backorder variables must be non-negative.

$$
x_{i,t} \geq 0, \quad I_{i,t} \geq 0, \quad L_{i,t} \geq 0 \quad \forall i, t
$$
