### Formal Mathematical Model

Although the problem can be solved by direct calculation as shown above, the request asks to "Analyze and model this OR problem". Given the structure, an analytical model is most appropriate.

**1. Index Sets & Parameters**
*   $R = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of routes.
*   $O_r$: Origin city of route $r$.
*   $D_r$: Destination city of route $r$.
*   $f_r$: Required number of sailings (departures) per day for route $r$.
*   $T_{ij}$: Sailing time (in days) from city $i$ to city $j$.
*   $t_{port}$: Time required for loading/unloading (1 day).

**2. Intermediate Parameters**
*   $C_r$: Total cycle time (in days) for one complete round trip on route $r$.
    $$ C_r = T_{O_r, D_r} + t_{port} + T_{D_r, O_r} + t_{port} = T_{O_r, D_r} + T_{D_r, O_r} + 2 $$

**3. Decision Variables**
*   $N_r$: Number of ships assigned to route $r$ (Integer).
*   $N_{total}$: Total number of ships (Integer).

**4. Objective Function**
Minimize the total number of ships deployed:
$$ \min Z = \sum_{r \in R} N_r $$

**5. Constraints**
For each route $r$, the capacity provided by $N_r$ ships must meet the daily demand $f_r$.
The service rate of one ship is $1/C_r$ trips/day.
The total service rate of $N_r$ ships is $N_r / C_r$.
Constraint:
$$ \frac{N_r}{C_r} \ge f_r \quad \forall r \in R $$
$$ N_r \ge f_r \cdot C_r \quad \forall r \in R $$
$$ N_r \in \mathbb{Z}^+ \quad \forall r \in R $$
