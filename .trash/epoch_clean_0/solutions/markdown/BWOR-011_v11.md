# Mathematical Optimization Model

## Model Type Declaration
**LP (Linear Programming)**

The problem involves allocating continuous quantities of electricity from two power stations to three cities. All decision variables represent physical quantities of electricity (units), which can be treated as continuous. The objective function and all constraints are linear.

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $I = \{1, 2\}$: Set of power stations. Station 1 is "Station I", Station 2 is "Station II".
- $J = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of cities.

**Parameters (Supplies):**
- $S_1 = 400$: Maximum supply capacity of Station I (units)
- $S_2 = 450$: Maximum supply capacity of Station II (units)
- Total Supply = $400 + 450 = 850$ units

**Parameters (Demands/Boundaries):**
- City 1: $L_1 = 290$, $U_1 = 320$ (Supply can be reduced by up to 30 units from original 320)
- City 2: $L_2 = 250$, $U_2 = 250$ (Fixed at 250 units)
- City 3: $L_3 = 270$, $U_3 = 350$ (At least 270, up to 350)

**Parameters (Costs in Yuan per Unit):**
| From \ To | City 1 | City 2 | City 3 |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Station I** | $c_{1,1} = 15$ | $c_{1,2} = 18$ | $c_{1,3} = 22$ |
| **Station II** | $c_{2,1} = 21$ | $c_{2,2} = 25$ | $c_{2,3} = 16$ |

## Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be the amount of electricity (in units) shipped from power station $i \in I$ to city $j \in J$.

- **Domain:** $x_{ij} \ge 0$ for all $i \in I, j \in J$.
- **Variables:**
  - $x_{11}, x_{12}, x_{13}$: Units sent from Station I to Cities 1, 2, 3 respectively.
  - $x_{21}, x_{22}, x_{23}$: Units sent from Station II to Cities 1, 2, 3 respectively.

## Objective Function

Minimize the total cost of electricity distribution:

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = 15x_{11} + 18x_{12} + 22x_{13} + 21x_{21} + 25x_{22} + 16x_{23}
$$

## Operational Constraints

**1. Supply Constraints (Flow Conservation at Stations)**
The problem states: "uses up all available electricity from both stations". This implies the total outflow from each station must equal its maximum capacity.
- Station I: $x_{11} + x_{12} + x_{13} = 400$
- Station II: $x_{21} + x_{22} + x_{23} = 450$

**2. Demand Constraints (Flow Conservation at Cities)**
The total inflow to each city must satisfy its specific lower and upper bounds.
- City 1: $x_{11} + x_{21} \ge 290$ AND $x_{11} + x_{21} \le 320$
- City 2: $x_{12} + x_{22} = 250$
- City 3: $x_{13} + x_{23} \ge 270$ AND $x_{13} + x_{23} \le 350$

**3. Non-negativity Constraints**
$x_{ij} \ge 0$ for all $i \in \{1, 2\}, j \in \{1, 2, 3\}$.

---
**Feasibility Check:**
- Total Supply = 850.
- Minimum Total Demand = $290 + 250 + 270 = 810$.
- Maximum Total Demand = $320 + 250 + 350 = 920$.
- Since $810 \le 850 \le 920$, a feasible solution exists where all supply is used and all demand bounds are respected.
