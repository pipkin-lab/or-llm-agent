**Model Type:** LP (Linear Programming)

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $t \in T = \{1, 2, \dots, 12\}$: The set of months in the current year.
*   $t \in T_{next} = \{13\}$: The beginning of the next year (Month 13).

**Parameters (in thousand yuan):**
*   $C_t$: Net cash flow for month $t$. A negative value indicates a net outflow (deficit) from operations.
    *   $C_1 = -12$
    *   $C_2 = -10$
    *   $C_3 = -8$
    *   $C_4 = -10$
    *   $C_5 = -4$
    *   $C_6 = 5$
    *   $C_7 = -7$
    *   $C_8 = -2$
    *   $C_9 = 15$
    *   $C_{10} = 12$
    *   $C_{11} = -7$
    *   $C_{12} = 45$

*   $r_{annual}$: Monthly interest rate for the Annual Loan = $0.01$ (1%).
*   $r_{short}$: Monthly interest rate for the Short-term Loan = $0.015$ (1.5%).
*   $r_{dep}$: Monthly interest rate for Deposits = $0.004$ (0.4%).
*   $L_{annual}$: Principal amount of the Annual Loan (Decision Variable).

**Timing Conventions:**
*   **Beginning of Month $t$**: Time point just before the cash flow $C_t$ occurs.
*   **End of Month $t$**: Time point just after the cash flow $C_t$ occurs and after any end-of-month transactions.
*   **Cash Flow $C_t$**: Occurs throughout month $t$ or is netted at the end of month $t$. To ensure solvency, we assume the cash on hand at the **end** of month $t$ (after covering $C_t$) must be non-negative.
*   **Annual Loan**:
    *   Principal $L_{annual}$ is received at the **beginning of Month 1** (start of the year).
    *   Interest payments are made at the **end** of each month $t$ for $t=2, \dots, 12$.
    *   Principal + Final Interest payment is made at the **end of Month 12** (which is the beginning of the next year, but effectively the last cash outflow of the current year's accounting). *Correction based on problem text*: "repay the principal plus the final interest payment at the end of January next year". January next year is Month 13. However, the objective is to maximize cash available at the *beginning* of next year. Usually, in these problems, "end of January next year" implies the very end of the 12-month cycle or just after. Let's look closely: "Starting from the end of February... pay 1% interest each month... repay principal plus final interest at end of January next year".
    *   Months with interest payments: End of Feb (Month 2), End of Mar (Month 3), ..., End of Dec (Month 12).
    *   Repayment of Principal + Final Interest: End of January Next Year (Month 13).
    *   *Crucial Interpretation*: The problem asks to maximize cash available at the **beginning of next year**. This is effectively the end of Month 12. Does the repayment at "end of January next year" affect the cash available at the "beginning of next year"?
        *   "Beginning of next year" = Start of Month 13.
        *   "End of January next year" = End of Month 13.
        *   Therefore, the principal and the interest for the first month of the *next* year (or the final installment) are paid *after* the time horizon of our objective function. The objective is cash *at the beginning of next year*. Thus, the principal $L_{annual}$ and the interest due at the end of Jan next year are **not** deducted from the final cash balance. Only the interest payments made *during* the current year (End of Month 2 through End of Month 12) reduce the cash available at the beginning of the next year.
        *   Wait, let's re-read carefully: "repay the principal plus the final interest payment at the end of January next year". This implies the loan term is roughly 1 year. The cash flow impact on the *current* year's ending balance (beginning of next year) includes interest payments made at the end of months 2 through 12. The principal and the *last* interest chunk (for January next year) happen later. So, $L_{annual}$ is not subtracted from the final objective value.

*   **Short-term Loan**:
    *   Taken at **beginning** of month $t$.
    *   Repaid (Principal + Interest) at **beginning** of month $t+1$.
    *   This effectively creates a 1-month loan.

*   **Deposit**:
    *   Made at **beginning** of month $t$.
    *   Withdrawn at **end** of month $t$ (which is the same time as the beginning of $t+1$ for the next period's start, but specifically, the interest is earned for that month).
    *   Interest is credited at the **end** of month $t$.

### 2. Decision Variables

*   $L_{annual} \ge 0$: Principal amount of the annual loan (thousand yuan).
*   $S_t \ge 0$: Amount borrowed via Short-term Loan at the **beginning** of month $t$ (thousand yuan).
*   $D_t \ge 0$: Amount deposited at the **beginning** of month $t$ (thousand yuan).
*   $B_t \ge 0$: Cash balance at the **end** of month $t$ (thousand yuan). This represents the cash available to carry over to the next month. Note: $B_{12}$ is the cash at the end of Month 12, which is the beginning of the next year.

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize the total cash available at the beginning of next year. This corresponds to the cash balance at the end of Month 12 ($B_{12}$).

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = B_{12}
$$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**A. Annual Loan Constraints:**
1.  **Receipt**: The annual loan principal is received at the beginning of Month 1.
2.  **Interest Payments**:
    *   Interest is paid at the end of months $t$ for $t \in \{2, 3, \dots, 12\}$.
    *   Monthly interest amount = $0.01 \times L_{annual}$.
    *   These payments reduce the cash available at the end of those months.

**B. Short-term Loan Constraints:**
1.  **Receipt**: $S_t$ is received at the beginning of month $t$.
2.  **Repayment**: $S_t$ plus interest ($1.015 S_t$) is paid at the beginning of month $t+1$.
    *   This repayment occurs *before* the cash flow $C_{t+1}$ is applied in month $t+1$.
    *   Effectively, in the cash balance equation for month $t+1$, we subtract $1.015 S_t$.

**C. Deposit Constraints:**
1.  **Deposit**: $D_t$ is placed at the beginning of month $t$.
2.  **Withdrawal + Interest**: $D_t$ plus interest ($1.004 D_t$) is received at the end of month $t$.
    *   This amount adds to the cash balance at the end of month $t$.

**D. Cash Balance Equations:**

We define the cash balance at the **end** of month $t$, denoted $B_t$.
The cash flow dynamics for a generic month $t$ (where $1 < t \le 12$) are:
*   **Start of Month $t$ Cash**: This is the balance from the previous month, $B_{t-1}$.
*   **Inflows at Start of Month $t$**:
    *   Short-term loan received: $S_t$.
    *   Deposit placed: $D_t$ (this is an outflow from operational cash, moving to deposit account, but let's track "Operational Cash" vs "Total Cash". It is easier to track a single cash pool where deposits are just a use of cash that returns later).
    *   Let's refine the state variable. Let $B_t$ be the **operational cash** available at the end of month $t$.
    *   At the **beginning** of month $t$, the operational cash is $B_{t-1}$.
    *   Transactions at **beginning** of month $t$:
        *   Receive Short-term Loan: $+S_t$.
        *   Make Deposit: $-D_t$ (Cash leaves operational account, goes to deposit).
        *   *Note*: The Annual Loan principal is only received at Start of Month 1.
    *   Transactions during month $t$:
        *   Net Operational Cash Flow: $+C_t$.
    *   Transactions at **end** of month $t$:
        *   Pay Annual Interest (if $t \ge 2$): $-0.01 L_{annual}$.
        *   Receive Deposit Maturity: $+1.004 D_t$.
        *   Repay Previous Short-term Loan: The loan $S_{t-1}$ taken at start of month $t-1$ is repaid at the beginning of month $t$. Wait, the problem says "repaid at the beginning of the following month".
            *   Loan $S_{t-1}$ taken at Start Month $t-1$.
            *   Repaid at Start Month $t$.
            *   So, at the **beginning** of month $t$, we pay $1.015 S_{t-1}$.

Let's rewrite the balance equation for **End of Month $t$** ($B_t$) based on **Start of Month $t$** ($B_{t-1}$):

$$
B_t = B_{t-1} + \text{Inflows}_{\text{start}} - \text{Outflows}_{\text{start}} + C_t + \text{Inflows}_{\text{end}} - \text{Outflows}_{\text{end}}
$$

**For Month 1 ($t=1$):**
*   Start Cash: $B_0 = 0$ (Assume no initial cash, or it's netted into the flow. The problem doesn't specify initial cash, so we assume 0 or that $L_{annual}$ covers the start. Let's assume $B_0=0$).
*   Start of Month 1:
    *   Receive Annual Loan: $+L_{annual}$.
    *   Receive Short-term Loan: $+S_1$.
    *   Make Deposit: $-D_1$.
*   During Month 1:
    *   Net Flow: $+C_1$ ($-12$).
*   End of Month 1:
    *   *No* Annual Interest payment (starts End of Feb).
    *   Receive Deposit Maturity: $+1.004 D_1$.
    *   *No* Short-term Loan repayment (first one is Start of Month 2).

$$
B_1 = 0 + L_{annual} + S_1 - D_1 + C_1 + 1.004 D_1
$$
$$
B_1 = L_{annual} + S_1 + 0.004 D_1 + C_1
$$
Constraint: $B_1 \ge 0$.

**For Month $t$ where $t \in \{2, 3, \dots, 12\}$:**
*   Start Cash: $B_{t-1}$.
*   Start of Month $t$:
    *   Receive Short-term Loan: $+S_t$.
    *   Make Deposit: $-D_t$.
    *   Repay Previous Short-term Loan ($S_{t-1}$): $-1.015 S_{t-1}$.
    *   *Note*: Annual Loan is already received. No new annual principal.
*   During Month $t$:
    *   Net Flow: $+C_t$.
*   End of Month $t$:
    *   Pay Annual Interest: $-0.01 L_{annual}$.
    *   Receive Deposit Maturity: $+1.004 D_t$.

$$
B_t = B_{t-1} + S_t - D_t - 1.015 S_{t-1} + C_t - 0.01 L_{annual} + 1.004 D_t
$$
$$
B_t = B_{t-1} + S_t - 1.015 S_{t-1} + 0.004 D_t + C_t - 0.01 L_{annual}
$$
Constraint: $B_t \ge 0$ for all $t=1,\dots,12$.

**Special Case for Month 12 End / Next Year Beginning:**
The objective is $B_{12}$.
Does the Annual Loan principal or the "final interest" affect $B_{12}$?
*   The problem states: "repay the principal plus the final interest payment at the end of January next year".
*   $B_{12}$ is the cash at the **end of December** (or beginning of January).
*   The repayment happens at the **end of January**.
*   Therefore, the principal $L_{annual}$ and the interest for January (which would be part of the "final interest") are **not** paid before $B_{12}$ is determined. They are paid later.
*   Thus, $B_{12}$ is indeed the final cash available at the start of the next year.

**Summary of Constraints:**

1.  **Non-negativity of Balances**:
    $$ B_t \ge 0, \quad \forall t \in \{1, \dots, 12\} $$
    $$ L_{annual} \ge 0 $$
    $$ S_t \ge 0, \quad \forall t \in \{1, \dots, 12\} $$
    $$ D_t \ge 0, \quad \forall t \in \{1, \dots, 12\} $$

2.  **Cash Balance Recursions**:

    **Month 1:**
    $$ B_1 = L_{annual} + S_1 + 0.004 D_1 - 12 $$

    **Month $t$ ($2 \le t \le 12$):**
    $$ B_t = B_{t-1} + S_t - 1.015 S_{t-1} + 0.004 D_t + C_t - 0.01 L_{annual} $$

    *Note on $S_{13}$*: Do we take a loan at the beginning of Month 13? No, the problem concerns the "upcoming year". The short-term loans are for covering shortfalls within the year. A loan at the beginning of Month 13 would be repaid at the beginning of Month 14, which is outside the horizon. More importantly, if there is a shortfall in Month 12, it must be covered by $S_{12}$ (taken at start of Month 12) or previous balances. Taking a loan at the start of Month 13 doesn't help cover the Month 12 cash flow $C_{12}$ or the end-of-month obligations. However, could we take a loan $S_{12}$ at the start of Month 12 to cover the $C_{12}$ outflow? Yes. Is there a need for $S_{13}$? No, because we don't need to cover a "Month 13" flow in this model. The variables $S_t$ are defined for $t=1$ to $12$.

### Final Model Formulation

**Model Type:** LP

**Sets:**
$T = \{1, \dots, 12\}$

**Parameters:**
$C = [-12, -10, -8, -10, -4, 5, -7, -2, 15, 12, -7, 45]$
$r_a = 0.01$
$r_s = 1.015$ (Repayment factor)
$r_d = 1.004$ (Maturity factor)

**Variables:**
$L_{annual} \in \mathbb{R}^+$
$S_t \in \mathbb{R}^+, \forall t \in T$
$D_t \in \mathbb{R}^+, \forall t \in T$
$B_t \in \mathbb{R}^+, \forall t \in T$

**Objective:**
$$ \text{Maximize } B_{12} $$

**Constraints:**

1.  **Month 1 Balance:**
    $$ L_{annual} + S_1 + 0.004 D_1 - B_1 = 12 $$
    *(Derived from $B_1 = L_{annual} + S_1 + 0.004 D_1 - 12$)*

2.  **Month $t$ Balance ($t = 2, \dots, 12$):**
    $$ B_{t-1} + S_t - 1.015 S_{t-1} + 0.004 D_t - 0.01 L_{annual} - B_t = -C_t $$
    
    *Verification for $t=2$:*
    $B_1 + S_2 - 1.015 S_1 + 0.004 D_2 - 0.01 L_{annual} - B_2 = -(-10) = 10$.
    $B_2 = B_1 + S_2 - 1.015 S_1 + 0.004 D_2 - 0.01 L_{annual} - 10$. Correct.

    *Verification for $t=6$ ($C_6=5$):*
    $B_5 + S_6 - 1.015 S_5 + 0.004 D_6 - 0.01 L_{annual} - B_6 = -5$.
    $B_6 = B_5 + S_6 - 1.015 S_5 + 0.004 D_6 - 0.01 L_{annual} + 5$. Correct.

3.  **Non-negativity:**
    $$ B_t \ge 0, \forall t \in T $$
    $$ S_t \ge 0, \forall t \in T $$
    $$ D_t \ge 0, \forall t \in T $$
    $$ L_{annual} \ge 0 $$
