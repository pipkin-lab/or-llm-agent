**Model Type:** Linear Programming (LP)

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
*   $P = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of products (I, II, III).
*   $A = \{A_1, A_2\}$: Set of machines for Stage A.
*   $B = \{B_1, B_2, B_3\}$: Set of machines for Stage B.

**Parameters:**

*   **Processing Times ($t_{p,m}$)**: Hours required to process one unit of product $p$ on machine $m$.
    *   Stage A:
        *   $t_{1, A_1} = 5$, $t_{2, A_1} = 10$, $t_{3, A_1} = 0$ (Not allowed)
        *   $t_{1, A_2} = 7$, $t_{2, A_2} = 9$, $t_{3, A_2} = 12$
    *   Stage B:
        *   $t_{1, B_1} = 6$, $t_{2, B_1} = 8$, $t_{3, B_1} = 0$ (Not allowed)
        *   $t_{1, B_2} = 4$, $t_{2, B_2} = 0$ (Not allowed), $t_{3, B_2} = 11$
        *   $t_{1, B_3} = 7$, $t_{2, B_3} = 0$ (Not allowed), $t_{3, B_3} = 0$ (Not allowed)

*   **Available Hours ($H_m$)**:
    *   $H_{A_1} = 6000$
    *   $H_{A_2} = 10000$
    *   $H_{B_1} = 4000$
    *   $H_{B_2} = 7000$
    *   $H_{B_3} = 4000$

*   **Processing Cost Rates ($c_m$)** (Dollars per hour):
    *   $c_{A_1} = 0.05$
    *   $c_{A_2} = 0.03$
    *   $c_{B_1} = 0.06$
    *   $c_{B_2} = 0.11$
    *   $c_{B_3} = 0.05$

*   **Raw Material Cost ($r_p$)** (Dollars per unit):
    *   $r_1 = 0.25$
    *   $r_2 = 0.35$
    *   $r_3 = 0.50$

*   **Selling Price ($s_p$)** (Dollars per unit):
    *   $s_1 = 1.25$
    *   $s_2 = 2.00$
    *   $s_3 = 2.80$

### 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_{p, m}$ be the number of units of product $p$ processed on machine $m$.

**Stage A Variables:**
*   $x_{1, A_1}$: Units of Product I processed on $A_1$
*   $x_{2, A_1}$: Units of Product II processed on $A_1$
*   $x_{1, A_2}$: Units of Product I processed on $A_2$
*   $x_{2, A_2}$: Units of Product II processed on $A_2$
*   $x_{3, A_2}$: Units of Product III processed on $A_2$
    *   *(Note: Product III cannot use $A_1$, so $x_{3, A_1}$ is excluded from the model as it is structurally zero/not allowed.)*

**Stage B Variables:**
*   $x_{1, B_1}$: Units of Product I processed on $B_1$
*   $x_{2, B_1}$: Units of Product II processed on $B_1$
*   $x_{1, B_2}$: Units of Product I processed on $B_2$
*   $x_{3, B_2}$: Units of Product III processed on $B_2$
*   $x_{1, B_3}$: Units of Product I processed on $B_3$
    *   *(Note: Product II can only use $B_1$, Product III can only use $B_2$. Other combinations are excluded.)*

**Total Production Variables (Auxiliary for objective/clarity):**
Let $X_p$ be the total production of product $p$.
$X_1 = x_{1, A_1} + x_{1, A_2}$
$X_2 = x_{2, A_1} + x_{2, A_2}$
$X_3 = x_{3, A_2}$

**Domain:**
All $x_{p, m} \ge 0$ and continuous (assuming divisible units; if integer units are required, they would be integer variables, but typically production planning allows continuous aggregation for large scales. Given the large hours, continuous is standard unless specified "integer units". The problem asks for a "production plan", usually implying continuous flow or large batch sizes. We assume continuous).

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize Total Profit $Z$.
Profit = Total Revenue - Total Raw Material Cost - Total Processing Cost.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = & \sum_{p \in P} (s_p - r_p) X_p - \sum_{m \in A \cup B} c_m \left( \sum_{p \in P} t_{p,m} x_{p,m} \right)
\end{aligned}
$$

Substituting the specific variables and values:

**Revenue - Raw Material (Contribution Margin per unit):**
*   Product I: $1.25 - 0.25 = 1.00$
*   Product II: $2.00 - 0.35 = 1.65$
*   Product III: $2.80 - 0.50 = 2.30$

**Total Contribution from Sales:**
$1.00(x_{1, A_1} + x_{1, A_2}) + 1.65(x_{2, A_1} + x_{2, A_2}) + 2.30(x_{3, A_2})$
*(Note: We use Stage A variables to represent total production count for the revenue/RAW cost part, as every unit must pass through Stage A. Alternatively, we can sum over Stage B variables, but Stage A covers all products uniformly.)*

**Total Processing Cost:**
*   $A_1$: $0.05(5 x_{1, A_1} + 10 x_{2, A_1})$
*   $A_2$: $0.03(7 x_{1, A_2} + 9 x_{2, A_2} + 12 x_{3, A_2})$
*   $B_1$: $0.06(6 x_{1, B_1} + 8 x_{2, B_1})$
*   $B_2$: $0.11(4 x_{1, B_2} + 11 x_{3, B_2})$
*   $B_3$: $0.05(7 x_{1, B_3})$

**Expanded Objective Function:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Maximize } Z = & \ 1.00 x_{1, A_1} + 1.00 x_{1, A_2} + 1.65 x_{2, A_1} + 1.65 x_{2, A_2} + 2.30 x_{3, A_2} \\
& - [0.25 x_{1, A_1} + 0.50 x_{2, A_1} + 0.21 x_{1, A_2} + 0.27 x_{2, A_2} + 0.36 x_{3, A_2}] \\
& - [0.36 x_{1, B_1} + 0.48 x_{2, B_1} + 0.44 x_{1, B_2} + 1.21 x_{3, B_2} + 0.35 x_{1, B_3}]
\end{aligned}
$$

Combining coefficients for each variable:

*   $x_{1, A_1}$: $1.00 - 0.25 = 0.75$
*   $x_{2, A_1}$: $1.65 - 0.50 = 1.15$
*   $x_{1, A_2}$: $1.00 - 0.21 = 0.79$
*   $x_{2, A_2}$: $1.65 - 0.27 = 1.38$
*   $x_{3, A_2}$: $2.30 - 0.36 = 1.94$
*   $x_{1, B_1}$: $-0.36$
*   $x_{2, B_1}$: $-0.48$
*   $x_{1, B_2}$: $-0.44$
*   $x_{3, B_2}$: $-1.21$
*   $x_{1, B_3}$: $-0.35$

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = 0.75 x_{1, A_1} + 1.15 x_{2, A_1} + 0.79 x_{1, A_2} + 1.38 x_{2, A_2} + 1.94 x_{3, A_2} - 0.36 x_{1, B_1} - 0.48 x_{2, B_1} - 0.44 x_{1, B_2} - 1.21 x_{3, B_2} - 0.35 x_{1, B_3}
$$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Flow Balance Constraints (Conservation of Product)**
The number of units of each product entering Stage B must equal the number of units completed in Stage A.

*   **Product I:**
    $$x_{1, B_1} + x_{1, B_2} + x_{1, B_3} = x_{1, A_1} + x_{1, A_2}$$
*   **Product II:**
    $$x_{2, B_1} = x_{2, A_1} + x_{2, A_2}$$
    *(Note: Product II can only use $B_1$)*
*   **Product III:**
    $$x_{3, B_2} = x_{3, A_2}$$
    *(Note: Product III can only use $A_2$ and $B_2$)*

**2. Machine Capacity Constraints**
The total processing time on each machine must not exceed available hours.

*   **Machine $A_1$:**
    $$5 x_{1, A_1} + 10 x_{2, A_1} \le 6000$$
*   **Machine $A_2$:**
    $$7 x_{1, A_2} + 9 x_{2, A_2} + 12 x_{3, A_2} \le 10000$$
*   **Machine $B_1$:**
    $$6 x_{1, B_1} + 8 x_{2, B_1} \le 4000$$
*   **Machine $B_2$:**
    $$4 x_{1, B_2} + 11 x_{3, B_2} \le 7000$$
*   **Machine $B_3$:**
    $$7 x_{1, B_3} \le 4000$$

**3. Non-negativity Constraints**
$$x_{1, A_1}, x_{2, A_1}, x_{1, A_2}, x_{2, A_2}, x_{3, A_2}, x_{1, B_1}, x_{2, B_1}, x_{1, B_2}, x_{3, B_2}, x_{1, B_3} \ge 0$$
