# Mathematical Optimization Model: Warehouse Inventory & Trading

## 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
*   $K = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of months (January to April).

**Parameters:**
*   $I_0 = 500$: Initial inventory in the warehouse at the start of Month 1.
*   $C_{buy}(k)$: Buying price in month $k$ (units: currency/unit).
    *   $C_{buy}(1) = 10, C_{buy}(2) = 9, C_{buy}(3) = 11, C_{buy}(4) = 15$
*   $C_{sell}(k)$: Selling price in month $k$ (units: currency/unit).
    *   $C_{sell}(1) = 12, C_{sell}(2) = 9, C_{sell}(3) = 13, C_{sell}(4) = 17$
*   $Cap = 1000$: Maximum warehouse capacity (units).

## 2. Decision Variables

Since the problem explicitly states "product quantities are in integers", we use Integer variables.

*   $B_k$: Integer variable representing the number of units **purchased** in month $k$.
    *   Domain: $B_k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for all $k \in K$.
    *   *Note: Purchases in month 4 are only possible if they can be sold in month 4 or later. Since the horizon ends at month 4, purchasing in month 4 is only useful if sold in month 4 (impossible due to lag) or month 5 (outside horizon). Thus, $B_4$ will likely be 0, but we keep it for structural consistency or bound it to 0 if we assume no sales occur after month 4. Given the "start of following month" rule, goods bought in month 4 are available in month 5. Since there is no month 5, $B_4$ cannot be sold. Therefore, $B_4 = 0$ is optimal. We include it in the model but it will naturally be 0.*
*   $S_k$: Integer variable representing the number of units **sold** in month $k$.
    *   Domain: $S_k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for all $k \in K$.
*   $I_k$: Integer variable representing the inventory level **at the end** of month $k$.
    *   Domain: $I_k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for all $k \in K$.

## 3. Objective Function

**Maximize Total Profit**

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{k=1}^{4} \left( C_{sell}(k) \cdot S_k - C_{buy}(k) \cdot B_k \right)
$$

Expanding with given values:
$$
Z = (12 S_1 - 10 B_1) + (9 S_2 - 9 B_2) + (13 S_3 - 11 B_3) + (17 S_4 - 15 B_4)
$$

## 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Inventory Balance Constraints**
The inventory at the end of month $k$ equals the inventory from the previous month plus purchases made in the *previous* month (since current month purchases are only available next month) minus sales made in the current month.

*   For Month 1:
    $$ I_1 = I_0 + B_0 - S_1 $$
    *(Assume $B_0 = 0$ as no purchases are mentioned prior to the start.)*
    $$ I_1 = 500 - S_1 $$

*   For Month $k$ ($k = 2, 3, 4$):
    $$ I_k = I_{k-1} + B_{k-1} - S_k $$

    Explicitly:
    $$ I_2 = I_1 + B_1 - S_2 $$
    $$ I_3 = I_2 + B_2 - S_3 $$
    $$ I_4 = I_3 + B_3 - S_4 $$

**2. Warehouse Capacity Constraints**
Inventory at the end of each month must not exceed the warehouse capacity.
$$ I_k \le 1000, \quad \forall k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\} $$

**3. Non-negativity and Integrality**
$$ B_k, S_k, I_k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, \quad \forall k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\} $$

**4. Logical Feasibility (Implicit)**
*   $S_k$ cannot exceed available inventory at the start of the month. This is enforced by the balance equations combined with $I_k \ge 0$.
    *   Start of Month 1 Inventory = 500. So $S_1 \le 500$.
    *   Start of Month 2 Inventory = $I_1$. So $S_2 \le I_1$.
    *   Start of Month 3 Inventory = $I_2$. So $S_3 \le I_2$.
    *   Start of Month 4 Inventory = $I_3$. So $S_4 \le I_3$.
    *   These are automatically satisfied if $I_k \ge 0$ and the balance equations hold.

## 5. Model Verification & Notes

*   **Integer Variables**: The problem explicitly states "product quantities are in integers". Although the scale is small (max 1000), the explicit instruction dictates an Integer Linear Programming (ILP) formulation.
*   **Lag Effect**: The constraint $I_k = I_{k-1} + B_{k-1} - S_k$ correctly implements the rule that "goods purchased in a given month are only available starting the following month".
*   **Month 4 Purchase**: $B_4$ appears in the objective with a negative cost ($-15 B_4$) but does not contribute to any sales within the 4-month horizon (it would be available in Month 5). Therefore, the optimizer will always set $B_4 = 0$.
*   **Profit Calculation**: The objective maximizes Revenue minus Cost. Since storage costs are ignored, no additional penalty terms are needed for $I_k$.
