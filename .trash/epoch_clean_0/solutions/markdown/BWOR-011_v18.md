**Model Type:** LP (Linear Program)

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $I = \{I, II\}$: Set of power stations.
*   $J = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of cities.

**Parameters:**

*   **Supply Capacities ($S_i$) in units:**
    *   $S_I = 400$
    *   $S_{II} = 450$
    *   Total Supply = $850$ units.

*   **Demand Requirements for City $j$ ($D_j$):**
    *   **City 1:** Min $= 290$, Max $= 320$. (Problem states: "reduced by up to 30 units" from 320).
    *   **City 2:** Min $= 250$, Max $= 250$. (Problem states: "fixed at 250 units").
    *   **City 3:** Min $= 270$, Max $= 350$. (Problem states: "at least 270 units, up to 350 units").

*   **Unit Costs ($c_{ij}$) in Yuan per unit:**
    *   $c_{I,1} = 15$, $c_{I,2} = 18$, $c_{I,3} = 22$
    *   $c_{II,1} = 21$, $c_{II,2} = 25$, $c_{II,3} = 16$

### 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be the amount of electricity (in units) supplied from Power Station $i$ to City $j$.

*   **Domain:** $x_{ij} \ge 0$ for all $i \in I, j \in J$.
*   **Type:** Continuous (Electricity is treated as a continuous resource in standard transportation LPs unless discrete integer units are explicitly specified; given the magnitudes (hundreds of units), continuous is the standard modeling choice).

### 3. Objective Function

Minimize the total cost of electricity supply:

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = 15x_{I,1} + 18x_{I,2} + 22x_{I,3} + 21x_{II,1} + 25x_{II,2} + 16x_{II,3}
$$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**A. Supply Constraints (Full Utilization)**
The problem states: "uses up all available electricity from both stations". Therefore, supply constraints are equalities.

1.  **Station I:**
    $$ x_{I,1} + x_{I,2} + x_{I,3} = 400 $$

2.  **Station II:**
    $$ x_{II,1} + x_{II,2} + x_{II,3} = 450 $$

**B. Demand Constraints (City Requirements)**
The total supply received by each city must satisfy its minimum and maximum requirements.

1.  **City 1 (Min 290, Max 320):**
    $$ 290 \le x_{I,1} + x_{II,1} \le 320 $$

2.  **City 2 (Fixed at 250):**
    $$ x_{I,2} + x_{II,2} = 250 $$

3.  **City 3 (Min 270, Max 350):**
    $$ 270 \le x_{I,3} + x_{II,3} \le 350 $$

**C. Non-negativity Constraints**
$$ x_{ij} \ge 0 \quad \forall i \in \{I, II\}, j \in \{1, 2, 3\} $$

### 5. Feasibility Sanity Check

*   **Total Supply:** $400 + 450 = 850$ units.
*   **Total Demand Range:**
    *   Min Demand: $290 (\text{City 1}) + 250 (\text{City 2}) + 270 (\text{City 3}) = 810$ units.
    *   Max Demand: $320 (\text{City 1}) + 250 (\text{City 2}) + 350 (\text{City 3}) = 920$ units.
*   Since Total Supply (850) is within the Total Demand range [810, 920], a feasible solution exists where all supply is used and all demand bounds are respected.
