# Production-Inventory Planning Problem

## Model Type: MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Programming)

---

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Set:**
- $t \in T = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$ — months (January through June)

**Parameters (all in consistent units):**

The table provides delivery quantities in **hundreds of units**. We work in units of **100 units** (i.e., 1 unit in the model = 100 actual units).

| Parameter | Symbol | Value | Units |
|-----------|--------|-------|-------|
| Delivery demand in month $t$ | $d_t$ | $d_1=1, d_2=2, d_3=5, d_4=3, d_5=2, d_6=1$ | hundreds of units |
| Production capacity | $C$ | $4$ | hundreds of units per month (400 units / 100) |
| Warehouse storage capacity | $W$ | $3$ | hundreds of units (300 units / 100) |
| Variable production cost | $c_v$ | $100$ | yuan per hundred units (10,000 yuan / 100 units) |
| Fixed production cost (if producing) | $c_f$ | $400$ | yuan per month (4,000 yuan / 10) |
| Storage cost | $h$ | $10$ | yuan per hundred units per month (1,000 yuan / 100) |

Total demand = $1+2+5+3+2+1 = 14$ hundreds of units.

## Decision Variables

| Variable | Definition | Domain |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| $x_t$ | Production quantity in month $t$ (hundreds of units) | $x_t \geq 0$, continuous |
| $I_t$ | Inventory at end of month $t$ (hundreds of units) | $I_t \geq 0$, continuous |
| $y_t$ | Binary indicator: $y_t = 1$ if production occurs in month $t$, else $0$ | $y_t \in \{0, 1\}$ |

---

## Objective Function

**MINIMIZE** total cost (in thousand yuan):

$$
\min \sum_{t=1}^{6} \left( c_v \cdot x_t + c_f \cdot y_t + h \cdot I_t \right)
$$

Substituting values:

$$
\min \sum_{t=1}^{6} \left( 10 \cdot x_t + 4 \cdot y_t + 1 \cdot I_t \right)
$$

**Objective Parameter Cross-Reference:**
- $c_v = 10$: "production cost for every 100 units is 10,000 yuan" → 10 thousand yuan per hundred units ✓
- $c_f = 4$: "fixed operating expenses of 4,000 yuan" → 4 thousand yuan ✓
- $h = 1$: "storage cost is 1,000 yuan per 100 units per month" → 1 thousand yuan per hundred units per month ✓

---

## Operational Constraints

### 1. Inventory Balance (for each month $t = 1, \ldots, 6$):

$$
I_{t-1} + x_t - d_t = I_t, \quad t = 1, \ldots, 6
$$

with $I_0 = 0$ (no initial inventory).

Expanded:
- $t=1$: $x_1 - 1 = I_1$
- $t=2$: $I_1 + x_2 - 2 = I_2$
- $t=3$: $I_2 + x_3 - 5 = I_3$
- $t=4$: $I_3 + x_4 - 3 = I_4$
- $t=5$: $I_4 + x_5 - 2 = I_5$
- $t=6$: $I_5 + x_6 - 1 = I_6$

### 2. Zero Final Inventory:

$$
I_6 = 0
$$

### 3. Production Capacity:

$$
x_t \leq C \cdot y_t = 4 \, y_t, \quad t = 1, \ldots, 6
$$

This enforces that if $x_t > 0$, then $y_t = 1$.

### 4. Storage Capacity:

$$
I_t \leq W = 3, \quad t = 1, \ldots, 6
$$

(Note: $I_6 = 0$ is already constrained, so the storage cap is binding for $t = 1, \ldots, 5$.)

### 5. Variable Bounds:

$$
x_t \geq 0, \quad I_t \geq 0, \quad t = 1, \ldots, 6
$$
$$
y_t \in \{0, 1\}, \quad t = 1, \ldots, 6
$$

## Feasibility Sanity Check

- **Total demand** = 14 (hundreds of units).
- **Total production capacity** over 6 months = $6 \times 4 = 24$ (hundreds of units). Since $24 \geq 14$, total capacity is sufficient.
- **Peak demand** in month 3 is 5, but capacity is 4 per month. However, inventory can be built up in prior months. Maximum storage is 3.
- Let's verify a feasible production plan exists:
  - Month 1: produce 4, deliver 1 → $I_1 = 3$ (at storage cap)
  - Month 2: produce 4, deliver 2 → $I_2 = 3 + 4 - 2 = 5$ > 3 ❌

  Try another:
  - Month 1: produce 3, deliver 1 → $I_1 = 2$
  - Month 2: produce 4, deliver 2 → $I_2 = 2 + 4 - 2 = 4$ > 3 ❌

  - Month 1: produce 2, deliver 1 → $I_1 = 1$
  - Month 2: produce 4, deliver 2 → $I_2 = 1 + 4 - 2 = 3$ (at cap)
  - Month 3: produce 4, deliver 5 → $I_3 = 3 + 4 - 5 = 2$
  - Month 4: produce 3, deliver 3 → $I_4 = 2 + 3 - 3 = 2$
  - Month 5: produce 2, deliver 2 → $I_5 = 2 + 2 - 2 = 2$
  - Month 6: produce 1, deliver 1 → $I_6 = 2 + 1 - 1 = 2$ ≠ 0 ❌

  Adjust:
  - M1: prod 2, $I_1=1$
  - M2: prod 4, $I_2=3$
  - M3: prod 4, $I_3=2$
  - M4: prod 2, $I_4=1$
  - M5: prod 2, $I_5=1$
  - M6: prod 0, $I_6=0$ ✓

  Total production = $2+4+4+2+2+0 = 14$ = total demand ✓. All storage ≤ 3 ✓. All production ≤ 4 ✓. **Feasible.**

## Final Compact Formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & \sum_{t=1}^{6} \left( 10\, x_t + 4\, y_t + 1\, I_t \right) \\[6pt]
\text{s.t.} \quad & I_0 + x_t - d_t = I_t, \quad t = 1,\ldots,6 \\
& I_0 = 0,\quad I_6 = 0 \\
& x_t \leq 4\, y_t, \quad t = 1,\ldots,6 \\
& 0 \leq I_t \leq 3, \quad t = 1,\ldots,5 \\
& x_t \geq 0, \quad t = 1,\ldots,6 \\
& y_t \in \{0,1\}, \quad t = 1,\ldots,6
\end{aligned}
$$

where $d = (1, 2, 5, 3, 2, 1)$ (in hundreds of units), and all costs are in thousand yuan.
