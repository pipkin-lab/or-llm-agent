**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)

### Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
*   $C = \{A, B, C, D, E\}$: Set of customers.
*   $J = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of trucks.

**Parameters:**
*   $d_i$: Demand for customer $i$ (units).
    *   $d_A = 1$
    *   $d_B = 2$
    *   $d_C = 3$
    *   $d_D = 4$
    *   $d_E = 8$
*   $cap_j$: Capacity of truck $j$ (units).
    *   $cap_1 = 2$
    *   $cap_2 = 6$
    *   $cap_3 = 8$
    *   $cap_4 = 11$
*   $c_j$: Cost of using truck $j$.
    *   $c_1 = [NOT\_PROVIDED]$
    *   $c_2 = [NOT\_PROVIDED]$
    *   $c_3 = [NOT\_PROVIDED]$
    *   $c_4 = [NOT\_PROVIDED]$

### Decision Variables

*   $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable, where $x_{ij} = 1$ if truck $j$ is assigned to deliver to customer $i$, and $0$ otherwise.
*   $z_j \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable, where $z_j = 1$ if truck $j$ is used (i.e., assigned to at least one customer), and $0$ otherwise.

### Objective Function

Minimize the total transportation cost:

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{j \in J} c_j z_j $$

### Operational Constraints

**1. Customer Service Constraints:**
Each customer must be served by exactly one truck.

$$ \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i \in C $$

**2. Linking Constraints:**
A truck is considered "used" if it serves at least one customer. Since the objective is to minimize cost and $c_j > 0$, the solver will naturally set $z_j=0$ if no $x_{ij}=1$. We enforce this logically:

$$ x_{ij} \le z_j \quad \forall i \in C, \forall j \in J $$

*(Note: We do not need the constraint $\sum x_{ij} \le M z_j$ because the individual linking constraints $x_{ij} \le z_j$ for all $i$ are sufficient to force $z_j=1$ if any $x_{ij}=1$.)*

**3. Truck Capacity Constraints:**
The total demand of customers assigned to a truck cannot exceed its capacity. Note that if a truck is not used ($z_j=0$), all $x_{ij}$ for that truck must be 0 due to constraint 2, satisfying the capacity constraint trivially.

$$ \sum_{i \in C} d_i x_{ij} \le cap_j \quad \forall j \in J $$

**4. Conflict Constraints:**
*   A truck cannot deliver to both Customer A and Customer C.
*   A truck cannot deliver to both Customer B and Customer D.

$$ x_{A,j} + x_{C,j} \le 1 \quad \forall j \in J $$
$$ x_{B,j} + x_{D,j} \le 1 \quad \forall j \in J $$

**5. Variable Domains:**
$$ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in C, \forall j \in J $$
$$ z_j \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall j \in J $$
