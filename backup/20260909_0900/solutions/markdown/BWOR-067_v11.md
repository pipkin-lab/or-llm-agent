## 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $t$: Set of months, $t \in \{1, 2, 3\}$

**Parameters:**
*   $d_t$: Demand in month $t$.
    *   $d_1 = 100$
    *   $d_2 = 110$
    *   $d_3 = 120$
*   $s_0$: Initial inventory at the beginning of month 1.
    *   $s_0 = 0$
*   $s_3$: Required ending inventory at the end of month 3.
    *   $s_3 = 0$
*   $h$: Inventory holding cost per unit per month.
    *   $h = 1$
*   $c(x)$: Production cost function for producing quantity $x$ in a month.
    *   $c(x) = x^2$

## 2. Decision Variables

*   $x_t$: Quantity of Product A produced in month $t$ (Continuous, $x_t \ge 0$).
*   $s_t$: Inventory level at the end of month $t$ (Continuous, $s_t \ge 0$).

*Note: Since the problem involves aggregate production quantities (hundreds of units) and does not explicitly require integer units, and given the quadratic cost structure which is typically solved via convex optimization (quadratic programming) where LP relaxation is standard for large scale, we treat variables as continuous. However, since the cost function is quadratic ($x^2$), this is technically a Quadratic Programming (QP) problem. If restricted to linear solvers, a Piecewise Linear Approximation (PWLA) would be required, but the standard mathematical formulation retains the quadratic term.*

## 3. Objective Function

Minimize the total cost, which is the sum of production costs and inventory holding costs over the 3 months.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{t=1}^{3} x_t^2 + \sum_{t=1}^{3} h \cdot s_t
$$

Substituting $h=1$:
$$
Z = x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 + s_1 + s_2 + s_3
$$

*Note: Standard practice for inventory problems specifies holding costs are incurred on the **ending** inventory of each period (or average inventory). The prompt states "inventory holding cost is 1 yuan per unit per month". Usually, this applies to the stock carried over to the next period, i.e., $s_t$. The initial stock $s_0$ is 0, so no cost is incurred for it. The final stock $s_3$ is 0, so no cost is incurred for it. Thus, the sum is over $s_1, s_2, s_3$ (with $s_3=0$ effectively).*

## 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Inventory Balance Constraints:**
The inventory at the end of month $t$ equals the inventory from the previous month plus production in month $t$ minus demand in month $t$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s_1 &= s_0 + x_1 - d_1 \\
s_2 &= s_1 + x_2 - d_2 \\
s_3 &= s_2 + x_3 - d_3
\end{aligned}
$$

Substituting known values ($s_0=0, d_1=100, d_2=110, d_3=120$):

$$
\begin{aligned}
s_1 &= x_1 - 100 \\
s_2 &= s_1 + x_2 - 110 \\
s_3 &= s_2 + x_3 - 120
\end{aligned}
$$

**2. Boundary Conditions:**
The problem specifies the ending inventory at the end of the third month is 0.

$$
s_3 = 0
$$

**3. Non-negativity Constraints:**
Production quantities and inventory levels must be non-negative.

$$
x_t \ge 0, \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\}
$$
$$
s_t \ge 0, \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\}
$$

*(Note: Since $s_3=0$ is fixed, the constraint $s_3 \ge 0$ is redundant with the equality constraint, but $s_1 \ge 0$ and $s_2 \ge 0$ ensure no backlogging is allowed.)*

### Summary of the Final Mathematical Model

**Variables:**
$x_1, x_2, x_3 \in \mathbb{R}^+$ (Production quantities)
$s_1, s_2 \in \mathbb{R}^+$ (Ending inventories for months 1 and 2; $s_3=0$)

**Objective:**
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 + s_1 + s_2 $$
*(Note: $s_3=0$, so its holding cost is 0. If holding cost applies to average inventory, the formulation would differ, but "per unit per month" on ending stock is the standard interpretation unless "average" is specified. Given $s_0=0, s_3=0$, we sum holding costs for the stock held during the month, which is typically represented by the ending stock $s_t$ in discrete time models for the cost incurred in period $t$ or carried into $t+1$. Let's assume the cost is incurred on the stock held at the end of period $t$.)*

**Constraints:**
1.  $s_1 = x_1 - 100$
2.  $s_2 = s_1 + x_2 - 110$
3.  $0 = s_2 + x_3 - 120$  (From $s_3=0$)
4.  $x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0$
5.  $s_1, s_2 \ge 0$
