*   $M_{d, i, k}$: Binary parameter, 1 if nurses who started their week on day $k$ work shift $i$ on day $d$, 0 otherwise.

**2. Decision Variables**
*   $S_k$: Integer variable representing the number of nurses who start their 5-day work week on day $k$ (where $k \in D$).
    *   $S_k \ge 0, \forall k \in D$.

**3. Objective Function**
Minimize the total number of nurses:
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{k=1}^{7} S_k $$

The total nurses covering period $p$ on day $d$ is $\sum_{i \in P} (\text{Nurses on Shift } i \text{ on Day } d \times \text{Covers}_{i, p})$.
