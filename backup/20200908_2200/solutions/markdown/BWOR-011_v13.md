### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $S = \{1, 2\}$: Set of power stations (I, II).
*   $C = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of cities.

**Parameters:**

*   **Supply Capacities (Units):**
    *   $Cap_1 = 400$ (Station I)
    *   $Cap_2 = 450$ (Station II)
    *   $TotalSupply = 850$

*   **Demand Constraints (Units):**
    *   **City 1:** Min $D_{1, min} = 290$, Max $D_{1, max} = 320$
    *   **City 2:** Min $D_{2, min} = 250$, Max $D_{2, max} = 250$ (Fixed)
    *   **City 3:** Min $D_{3, min} = 270$, Max $D_{3, max} = 350$

*   **Unit Costs (Yuan per Unit) $c_{s,c}$:**
    *   $c_{1,1} = 15$ (Station I $\to$ City 1)
    *   $c_{1,2} = 18$ (Station I $\to$ City 2)
    *   $c_{1,3} = 22$ (Station I $\to$ City 3)
    *   $c_{2,1} = 21$ (Station II $\to$ City 1)
    *   $c_{2,2} = 25$ (Station II $\to$ City 2)
    *   $c_{2,3} = 16$ (Station II $\to$ City 3)

### 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_{s,c}$ be the amount of electricity (in units) supplied from Station $s$ to City $c$.

*   **Domain:** $x_{s,c} \ge 0$ for all $s \in S, c \in C$.
*   **Type:** Continuous (Electricity is a divisible resource; standard LP formulation applies).

### 3. Objective Function

Minimize the total transportation cost:

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{s \in S} \sum_{c \in C} c_{s,c} x_{s,c}
$$

Expanded form:
$$
Z = 15x_{1,1} + 18x_{1,2} + 22x_{1,3} + 21x_{2,1} + 25x_{2,2} + 16x_{2,3}
$$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Supply Capacity Constraints (Full Utilization Required)**
The problem states the plan must "use up all available electricity from both stations". Therefore, supply from each station must equal its maximum capacity.

$$
\sum_{c \in C} x_{1,c} = 400 \quad \text{(Station I)}
$$
$$
\sum_{c \in C} x_{2,c} = 450 \quad \text{(Station II)}
$$

**2. Demand Constraints (City Limits)**
The total supply to each city must satisfy its specific minimum and maximum requirements.

*   **City 1:**
    $$
    290 \le \sum_{s \in S} x_{s,1} \le 320
    $$
    *(Linearized as two inequalities: $\sum x_{s,1} \ge 290$ and $\sum x_{s,1} \le 320$)*

*   **City 2 (Fixed):**
    $$
    \sum_{s \in S} x_{s,2} = 250
    $$

*   **City 3:**
    $$
    270 \le \sum_{s \in S} x_{s,3} \le 350
    $$
    *(Linearized as two inequalities: $\sum x_{s,3} \ge 270$ and $\sum x_{s,3} \le 350$)*

**3. Non-negativity:**
$$
x_{s,c} \ge 0 \quad \forall s \in \{1, 2\}, c \in \{1, 2, 3\}
$$

---

### Model Verification & Consistency Check

1.  **Total Supply vs. Total Demand:**
    *   Total Supply = $400 + 450 = 850$.
    *   Minimum Total Demand = $290 + 250 + 270 = 810$.
    *   Maximum Total Demand = $320 + 250 + 350 = 920$.
    *   Since $810 \le 850 \le 920$, a feasible solution exists where all supply is used and all city bounds are respected.

2.  **Unit Consistency:** All values are in "units" and "Yuan per Unit". No conversion needed.

3.  **Objective Direction:** Minimization of cost.

4.  **Variable Bounds:** All variables are bounded by the supply capacities (max 450) and demand limits.

### Final Mathematical Formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Minimize } \quad & 15x_{1,1} + 18x_{1,2} + 22x_{1,3} + 21x_{2,1} + 25x_{2,2} + 16x_{2,3} \\
\text{Subject to:} \quad & \\
& x_{1,1} + x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} = 400 \\
& x_{2,1} + x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} = 450 \\
& x_{1,1} + x_{2,1} \ge 290 \\
& x_{1,1} + x_{2,1} \le 320 \\
& x_{1,2} + x_{2,2} = 250 \\
& x_{1,3} + x_{2,3} \ge 270 \\
& x_{1,3} + x_{2,3} \le 350 \\
& x_{s,c} \ge 0 \quad \forall s,c
\end{aligned}
$$
