To model this problem, we must recognize that the benefit function is non-linear and discrete (stepwise). We cannot simply multiply investment amount by a constant rate. Instead, we use a **Discrete Investment Model** (a form of Integer Programming / Knapsack variant).

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
*   $P = \{A, B, C\}$: Set of projects.
*   $K = \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of possible investment levels (in thousand yuan).

**Parameters:**
*   $B_{p, k}$: Benefit obtained from project $p$ if $k$ thousand yuan is invested.
    *   Project A: $B_{A,0}=0, B_{A,1}=41, B_{A,2}=48, B_{A,3}=60, B_{A,4}=66$
    *   Project B: $B_{B,0}=0, B_{B,1}=42, B_{B,2}=50, B_{B,3}=60, B_{B,4}=66$
    *   Project C: $B_{C,0}=0, B_{C,1}=64, B_{C,2}=68, B_{C,3}=78, B_{C,4}=76$
*   $C_{total} = 4$: Total available capital (thousand yuan).

### 2. Decision Variables

Since the investment levels are discrete integers from 0 to 4, we define binary variables to indicate which investment level is chosen for each project.

*   $x_{p, k} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable, where:
    *   $x_{p, k} = 1$ if project $p$ is allocated exactly $k$ thousand yuan.
    *   $x_{p, k} = 0$ otherwise.

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize the total benefit across all projects and all selected investment levels.

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{p \in P} \sum_{k \in K} B_{p, k} x_{p, k} $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**Constraint 1: Capital Budget Constraint**
The sum of investments allocated to all projects must not exceed the total available capital (4 thousand yuan).

$$ \sum_{p \in P} \sum_{k \in K} k \cdot x_{p, k} \le 4 $$

**Constraint 2: Single Investment Level per Project**
Each project must be assigned exactly one investment level (including the level 0, which means no investment). We cannot invest in multiple levels of the same project simultaneously (e.g., we cannot say we invested 1 unit AND 2 units separately; we must choose the aggregate level).

$$ \sum_{k \in K} x_{p, k} = 1 \quad \forall p \in P $$

**Constraint 3: Domain/Binary Constraints**

$$ x_{p, k} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall p \in P, \forall k \in K $$

---

### Mathematical Formulation Summary

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Maximize} \quad & Z = 41x_{A,1} + 48x_{A,2} + 60x_{A,3} + 66x_{A,4} \\
& + 42x_{B,1} + 50x_{B,2} + 60x_{B,3} + 66x_{B,4} \\
& + 64x_{C,1} + 68x_{C,2} + 78x_{C,3} + 76x_{C,4} \\
\text{Subject to:} \quad & 1x_{A,1} + 2x_{A,2} + 3x_{A,3} + 4x_{A,4} \\
& + 1x_{B,1} + 2x_{B,2} + 3x_{B,3} + 4x_{B,4} \\
& + 1x_{C,1} + 2x_{C,2} + 3x_{C,3} + 4x_{C,4} \le 4 \\
& x_{A,0} + x_{A,1} + x_{A,2} + x_{A,3} + x_{A,4} = 1 \\
& x_{B,0} + x_{B,1} + x_{B,2} + x_{B,3} + x_{B,4} = 1 \\
& x_{C,0} + x_{C,1} + x_{C,2} + x_{C,3} + x_{C,4} = 1 \\
& x_{p, k} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall p \in \{A, B, C\}, k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}
\end{aligned}
$$

*(Note: The terms with $k=0$ have 0 benefit and 0 cost, so they are often omitted from the objective and budget constraint sum, but they are included in the "sum to 1" constraint to ensure a valid state is selected.)*
