### 3. Objective Function

**Dynamic Programming Approach:**
The goal is to find the minimum total distance of the tour:
$$ Z = f(V \setminus \{1\}, j) + d_{j1} $$
where the minimum is taken over all $j \in \{2, 3, 4\}$. Specifically:
$$ Z = \min_{j \in \{2,3,4\}} \left( f(\{2,3,4\}, j) + d_{j1} \right) $$

**MILP Equivalent (if using a solver):**
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V, j \neq i} d_{ij} x_{ij} $$
