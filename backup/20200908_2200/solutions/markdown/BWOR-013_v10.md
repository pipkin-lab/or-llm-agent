## 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $P = \{A, B, C\}$: Set of products.

**Parameters:**
- $F$: Total available fund = 1000 (thousand yuan).
- $r_p$: Forecasted Return on Investment (ROI) for product $p$ (expressed as a decimal).
  - $r_A = 0.05$
  - $r_B = 0.07$
  - $r_C = 0.10$
- $L_A$: Minimum investment required for Product A = 300 (thousand yuan).
- $U_{share}$: Maximum proportion of the total fund allowed for any single product = 0.35.
- $R_{reserve}$: Amount reserved for emergency use = 10% of $F$ = 100 (thousand yuan).

## 2. Decision Variables

- $x_p$: Amount of investment allocated to product $p$ (in thousand yuan), for $p \in P$.
  - **Domain**: $x_p \ge 0$ (Continuous variables).
  - *Note*: Since the scale is in thousands of yuan and the problem does not specify discrete unit constraints (e.g., integer number of shares or projects), and given the "large scale" nature of financial flows, continuous variables are appropriate for an LP formulation.

## 3. Objective Function

Maximize the total investment profit (return) $Z$:

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 0.05 x_A + 0.07 x_B + 0.10 x_C $$

## 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Fund Allocation & Reserve Constraint:**
The total invested amount plus the reserved emergency fund must not exceed the total available fund. Equivalently, the sum of investments must be less than or equal to the total fund minus the reserved amount.
$$ x_A + x_B + x_C \le F - R_{reserve} $$
$$ x_A + x_B + x_C \le 1000 - 100 $$
$$ x_A + x_B + x_C \le 900 $$

**2. Minimum Investment in Product A:**
$$ x_A \ge 300 $$

**3. Maximum Investment per Product (Risk Diversification):**
No single product may receive more than 35% of the **total fund** ($F = 1000$).
$$ x_p \le 0.35 \times F, \quad \forall p \in P $$
$$ x_A \le 350 $$
$$ x_B \le 350 $$
$$ x_C \le 350 $$

**4. Non-negativity:**
$$ x_p \ge 0, \quad \forall p \in P $$

---

### Model Summary

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Maximize} \quad & Z = 0.05 x_A + 0.07 x_B + 0.10 x_C \\
\text{Subject to:} \quad 
& x_A + x_B + x_C \le 900 \\
& x_A \ge 300 \\
& x_A \le 350 \\
& x_B \le 350 \\
& x_C \le 350 \\
& x_A, x_B, x_C \ge 0
\end{aligned}
$$
