Here is the formal mathematical optimization model for the cargo loading problem.

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
- $C = \{A, B, C\}$: Set of cargo types.
- $K = \{F, M, R\}$: Set of ship compartments (Front, Middle, Rear).

**Parameters:**

*Compartment Limits (from Table 1-24):*
- $W_{max, F} = 2000$ t, $V_{max, F} = 4000$ $m^3$
- $W_{max, M} = 3000$ t, $V_{max, M} = 5000$ $m^3$
- $W_{max, R} = 1500$ t, $V_{max, R} = 1500$ $m^3$

*Cargo Data (from Table 1-25):*
- $Q_A = 600$, $v_A = 10$, $w_A = 8$, $r_A = 1000$
- $Q_B = 1000$, $v_B = 5$, $w_B = 6$, $r_B = 700$
- $Q_C = 800$, $v_C = 7$, $w_C = 4$, $r_C = 600$

*Balance Tolerances:*
- $\delta_{FM} = 0.15$ (15%)
- $\delta_{RM} = 0.15$ (15%)
- $\delta_{FR} = 0.10$ (10%)

**Derived Parameters (Target Ratios of Max Loads):**
- Ratio Front/Middle: $\rho_{FM} = \frac{W_{max, F}}{W_{max, M}} = \frac{2000}{3000} = \frac{2}{3}$
- Ratio Rear/Middle: $\rho_{RM} = \frac{W_{max, R}}{W_{max, M}} = \frac{1500}{3000} = \frac{1}{2}$
- Ratio Front/Rear: $\rho_{FR} = \frac{W_{max, F}}{W_{max, R}} = \frac{2000}{1500} = \frac{4}{3}$

### 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_{c, k}$ be the number of pieces of cargo type $c$ loaded in compartment $k$.
Since the problem asks for "How many pieces" and quantities are relatively small (hundreds), we must use **INTEGER** variables to ensure physical feasibility (cannot load 0.5 pieces).

$$x_{c, k} \in \mathbb{Z}_{+} \quad \forall c \in C, k \in K$$

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize total revenue $Z$:

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{c \in C} \sum_{k \in K} r_c \cdot x_{c, k} $$

Substituting values:
$$ Z = 1000 \left( \sum_{k \in K} x_{A,k} \right) + 700 \left( \sum_{k \in K} x_{B,k} \right) + 600 \left( \sum_{k \in K} x_{C,k} \right) $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**A. Supply Constraints (Total Quantity per Cargo Type)**
The total number of pieces of each cargo type loaded across all compartments cannot exceed the available quantity.

$$ \sum_{k \in K} x_{A, k} \le 600 $$
$$ \sum_{k \in K} x_{B, k} \le 1000 $$
$$ \sum_{k \in K} x_{C, k} \le 800 $$

**B. Volume Constraints (Per Compartment)**
The total volume of cargo in each compartment must not exceed the compartment's volume capacity.

*Front:*
$$ 10 x_{A, F} + 5 x_{B, F} + 7 x_{C, F} \le 4000 $$

*Middle:*
$$ 10 x_{A, M} + 5 x_{B, M} + 7 x_{C, M} \le 5000 $$

*Rear:*
$$ 10 x_{A, R} + 5 x_{B, R} + 7 x_{C, R} \le 1500 $$

**C. Weight Capacity Constraints (Per Compartment)**
The total weight of cargo in each compartment must not exceed the compartment's maximum allowable load.

*Front:*
$$ 8 x_{A, F} + 6 x_{B, F} + 4 x_{C, F} \le 2000 $$

*Middle:*
$$ 8 x_{A, M} + 6 x_{B, M} + 4 x_{C, M} \le 3000 $$

*Rear:*
$$ 8 x_{A, R} + 6 x_{B, R} + 4 x_{C, R} \le 1500 $$

**D. Balance Constraints**
Let $W_F, W_M, W_R$ be the total weights in Front, Middle, and Rear compartments respectively:
$$ W_F = 8 x_{A, F} + 6 x_{B, F} + 4 x_{C, F} $$
$$ W_M = 8 x_{A, M} + 6 x_{B, M} + 4 x_{C, M} $$
$$ W_R = 8 x_{A, R} + 6 x_{B, R} + 4 x_{C, R} $$

The constraints state that the actual ratio must be within $\pm \delta$ of the target ratio $\rho$.
Mathematically: $(1-\delta)\rho \le \frac{W_i}{W_j} \le (1+\delta)\rho$.
Since $W_j > 0$ (assuming non-empty middle/rear, or handled via bounds), we can linearize these as:
1. $W_i - (1+\delta)\rho W_j \le 0$
2. $(1-\delta)\rho W_j - W_i \le 0$

**Constraint D1: Front vs Middle (15% deviation from 2/3)**
Target Range: $[\frac{2}{3}(0.85), \frac{2}{3}(1.15)] = [0.5667, 0.7667]$

1. $W_F - 0.7667 W_M \le 0 \implies 8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F} - 0.7667(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \le 0$
2. $0.5667 W_M - W_F \le 0 \implies 0.5667(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) - (8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) \le 0$

**Constraint D2: Rear vs Middle (15% deviation from 1/2)**
Target Range: $[\frac{1}{2}(0.85), \frac{1}{2}(1.15)] = [0.425, 0.575]$

1. $W_R - 0.575 W_M \le 0 \implies 8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R} - 0.575(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \le 0$
2. $0.425 W_M - W_R \le 0 \implies 0.425(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) - (8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) \le 0$

**Constraint D3: Front vs Rear (10% deviation from 4/3)**
Target Range: $[\frac{4}{3}(0.90), \frac{4}{3}(1.10)] = [1.2, 1.4667]$

1. $W_F - 1.4667 W_R \le 0 \implies 8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F} - 1.4667(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) \le 0$
2. $1.2 W_R - W_F \le 0 \implies 1.2(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) - (8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) \le 0$

**E. Non-negativity and Integrality**
$$ x_{c, k} \ge 0, \quad x_{c, k} \in \mathbb{Z} \quad \forall c \in C, k \in K $$
