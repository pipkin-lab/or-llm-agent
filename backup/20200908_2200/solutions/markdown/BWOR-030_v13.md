    *   $P^S_2 = 3.25$
    *   $P^S_3 = 2.95$

## 2. Decision Variables

*   $x_t$: Quantity of grain purchased in month $t$ (dan).
*   $y_t$: Quantity of grain sold in month $t$ (dan).
*   $I_t$: Inventory level of grain at the **end** of month $t$ (dan).
*   $K_t$: Cash balance available at the **end** of month $t$ (yuan).

**Domains:**

## 3. Objective Function

The problem does not explicitly state the objective (Maximize Profit? Maximize Final Capital?). In trading problems, the goal is typically to **Maximize the final cash balance** (or profit) at the end of the horizon. Since there is no cost for holding inventory other than capital tie-up, and the target is a specific end inventory, the implicit objective is to **Maximize the Cash Balance at the end of Month 3 ($K_3$)**.

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = K_3 $$

## 4. Operational Constraints

### A. Inventory Balance Constraints
For each month $t \in \{1, 2, 3\}$:
$$ I_t = I_{t-1} + x_t - y_t $$
Where $I_0 = 1,000$.

### B. Sales Availability Constraints
Grain sold in month $t$ must come from the inventory available at the beginning of month $t$ (since current month purchases cannot be sold until the next month).
$$ y_t \le I_{t-1}, \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\} $$
*Note: This ensures that $x_t$ does not contribute to $y_t$.*

### C. Warehouse Capacity Constraints
The inventory at the end of each month must not exceed warehouse capacity.
$$ I_t \le 5,000, \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\} $$

### D. Cash Flow Constraints
Cash balance updates based on purchases (outflow) and sales (inflow).
$$ K_t = K_{t-1} - P^B_t x_t + P^S_t y_t, \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\} $$
Where $K_0 = 20,000$.

*Note on Timing*: "Cash payment upon delivery."
- Purchase in month $t$: Cash leaves in month $t$.
- Sale in month $t$: Cash enters in month $t$.

### E. Final Inventory Target
The problem states: "The target inventory at the end of March is 2,000 dan."
This is a hard constraint.
$$ I_3 = 2,000 $$

### F. Non-negativity and Domain Constraints
$$ x_t \ge 0, \quad y_t \ge 0, \quad I_t \ge 0, \quad K_t \ge 0, \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\} $$
