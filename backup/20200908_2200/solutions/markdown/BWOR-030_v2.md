# Optimization Model for Grain Trading

## 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $t \in \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of months (January, February, March).
*   Note: Sales from inventory purchased in month $t$ occur in month $t+1$.

**Parameters:**
*   $C_{cap} = 5,000$: Warehouse capacity (dan).
*   $I_0 = 1,000$: Initial inventory at start of Month 1 (dan).
*   $K_0 = 20,000$: Initial available capital (yuan).
*   $I_{target} = 2,000$: Required inventory at end of Month 3 (dan).
*   $p_t$: Purchase price in month $t$ (yuan/dan).
    *   $p_1 = 2.85$
    *   $p_2 = 3.05$
    *   $p_3 = 2.90$
*   $s_t$: Selling price in month $t$ (yuan/dan).
    *   $s_1 = 3.10$ (Applicable to sales in Month 1, which come from prior inventory)
    *   $s_2 = 3.25$ (Applicable to sales in Month 2, which come from Month 1 purchases)
    *   $s_3 = 2.95$ (Applicable to sales in Month 3, which come from Month 2 purchases)
    *   *Note: Grain purchased in Month 3 cannot be sold within the first quarter (sales would occur in Month 4, outside the horizon), so $s_4$ is not needed for revenue calculation within the period, but the inventory must meet the target.*

## 2. Decision Variables

Let the following variables be defined for each month $t \in \{1, 2, 3\}$:

*   $x_t \ge 0$: Quantity of grain purchased in month $t$ (dan).
*   $y_t \ge 0$: Quantity of grain sold in month $t$ (dan).
*   $I_t \ge 0$: Inventory level at the **end** of month $t$ (dan).

## 3. Objective Function

The goal is to maximize the total profit.
Profit = Total Revenue from Sales - Total Cost of Purchases.

*Note on Initial Inventory*: The problem states the company "has" 1,000 dan. Usually, in such trading problems, the objective is to maximize the *incremental* profit or the final net wealth. Given the phrasing "wholesale... target inventory", and standard OR formulations for trading, we maximize the net cash flow generated by the trading activities (Sales - Purchases). The initial inventory is a resource constraint, not a cost to be recovered in the objective function unless "Total Wealth" is specified. However, strictly speaking, if we are maximizing profit *generated by the company's decisions*, it is Revenue minus Purchase Costs.

Let's define the objective as **Maximize Net Profit**:

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{t=1}^{3} (s_t \cdot y_t) - \sum_{t=1}^{3} (p_t \cdot x_t) $$

Substituting values:
$$ Z = (3.10 y_1 + 3.25 y_2 + 2.95 y_3) - (2.85 x_1 + 3.05 x_2 + 2.90 x_3) $$

*(Self-Correction/Refinement on "Profit" vs "Wealth"):*
If the problem implies that the initial capital and inventory have value, one might argue for Final Wealth. However, "Profit" in trading contexts usually refers to the gain from the trading operations. The constraint on capital and inventory ensures feasibility. We will proceed with Maximize (Sales Revenue - Purchase Cost).

## 4. Operational Constraints

### A. Inventory Balance Constraints
The inventory at the end of a month is the previous inventory plus purchases minus sales.
*   **Month 1:**
    $$ I_1 = I_0 + x_1 - y_1 $$
    $$ I_1 = 1,000 + x_1 - y_1 $$
*   **Month 2:**
    $$ I_2 = I_1 + x_2 - y_2 $$
*   **Month 3:**
    $$ I_3 = I_2 + x_3 - y_3 $$

### B. Sales Timing Constraint
Grain purchased in month $t$ can only be sold starting in month $t+1$.
*   **Month 1 Sales ($y_1$):** Can only be sold from existing inventory $I_0$. Purchases $x_1$ arrive at the end of Month 1 (or are available for next month). The problem states "Grain arrives in the month it is purchased, but can only be sold starting in the next month."
    *   Therefore, $y_1$ must be satisfied by $I_0$.
    *   Constraint: $y_1 \le I_0 = 1,000$
    *   (Note: $I_1$ includes $x_1$, so $x_1$ is not available for $y_1$).
*   **Month 2 Sales ($y_2$):** Can be sold from inventory available at start of Month 2, which is $I_1$.
    *   Constraint: $y_2 \le I_1$
    *   (Note: $I_1 = 1,000 + x_1 - y_1$. Since $y_1 \le 1,000$, $I_1 \ge x_1$. Thus $x_1$ is available. $x_2$ is not available for $y_2$).
*   **Month 3 Sales ($y_3$):** Can be sold from inventory available at start of Month 3, which is $I_2$.
    *   Constraint: $y_3 \le I_2$
    *   (Note: $x_3$ is not available for $y_3$).

*Alternative Interpretation Check:* Does "arrives in the month it is purchased" mean it's in the warehouse at the *start*? No, "can only be sold starting in the next month" clarifies that $x_t$ is not part of the sellable stock for month $t$. It is part of the sellable stock for month $t+1$.

So the explicit constraints are:
1.  $y_1 \le 1,000$
2.  $y_2 \le I_1$
3.  $y_3 \le I_2$

### C. Warehouse Capacity Constraints
The inventory at the end of each month cannot exceed the warehouse capacity.
1.  $I_1 \le 5,000$
2.  $I_2 \le 5,000$
3.  $I_3 \le 5,000$

### D. Capital Constraints
The company has 20,000 yuan available. "Cash payment upon delivery."
This implies that the cumulative cost of purchases up to any month $t$ must not exceed the initial capital plus the cumulative revenue from sales up to month $t$ (assuming sales proceeds are immediately available for subsequent purchases).

Let $K_t$ be the available cash at the end of month $t$.
$K_0 = 20,000$
$K_t = K_{t-1} + s_t y_t - p_t x_t$

Constraints:
1.  **End of Month 1:**
    $$ 20,000 + 3.10 y_1 - 2.85 x_1 \ge 0 $$
    *(Or more simply: Cumulative Purchases - Cumulative Sales $\le$ Initial Capital)*
    $$ 2.85 x_1 - 3.10 y_1 \le 20,000 $$
2.  **End of Month 2:**
    $$ 2.85 x_1 + 3.05 x_2 - (3.10 y_1 + 3.25 y_2) \le 20,000 $$
3.  **End of Month 3:**
    $$ 2.85 x_1 + 3.05 x_2 + 2.90 x_3 - (3.10 y_1 + 3.25 y_2 + 2.95 y_3) \le 20,000 $$

*Note:* Since we are maximizing profit, and the objective function is effectively the negative of the LHS of the final capital constraint (plus initial capital), the constraint that is most binding is usually the final one or intermediate ones if cash flow is tight. It is safest to include cumulative cash flow constraints for each month to ensure the company doesn't run out of cash mid-quarter.

### E. Target Inventory Constraint
The target inventory at the end of March is 2,000 dan.
$$ I_3 = 2,000 $$

### F. Non-negativity
$$ x_t \ge 0, \quad y_t \ge 0, \quad I_t \ge 0 \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\} $$

## Summary of the Linear Programming Model

**Sets:**
$t = \{1, 2, 3\}$

**Parameters:**
$I_0 = 1000, K_0 = 20000, C_{cap} = 5000, I_{target} = 2000$
$p = [2.85, 3.05, 2.90]$
$s = [3.10, 3.25, 2.95]$

**Variables:**
$x_t \ge 0$ (Purchase in month $t$)
$y_t \ge 0$ (Sale in month $t$)
$I_t \ge 0$ (End-of-month inventory in month $t$)

**Objective:**
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{t=1}^{3} s_t y_t - \sum_{t=1}^{3} p_t x_t $$

**Subject to:**

1.  **Inventory Balance:**
    $$ I_1 = 1000 + x_1 - y_1 $$
    $$ I_2 = I_1 + x_2 - y_2 $$
    $$ I_3 = I_2 + x_3 - y_3 $$

2.  **Sales Availability (Timing):**
    $$ y_1 \le 1000 $$
    $$ y_2 \le I_1 $$
    $$ y_3 \le I_2 $$

3.  **Capacity:**
    $$ I_1 \le 5000 $$
    $$ I_2 \le 5000 $$
    $$ I_3 \le 5000 $$

4.  **Cash Flow (Cumulative Cost $\le$ Initial Capital + Cumulative Revenue):**
    $$ 2.85 x_1 - 3.10 y_1 \le 20000 $$
    $$ 2.85 x_1 + 3.05 x_2 - 3.10 y_1 - 3.25 y_2 \le 20000 $$
    $$ 2.85 x_1 + 3.05 x_2 + 2.90 x_3 - 3.10 y_1 - 3.25 y_2 - 2.95 y_3 \le 20000 $$

5.  **Target Inventory:**
    $$ I_3 = 2000 $$
