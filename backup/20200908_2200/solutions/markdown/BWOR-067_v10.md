To formulate the optimization model for this production planning problem, we must carefully handle the non-linear production cost and the inventory balance dynamics.

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $t \in \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of months (periods).
*   $T = 3$: Total number of periods.

**Parameters:**
*   $d_t$: Demand in month $t$.
    *   $d_1 = 100$
    *   $d_2 = 110$
    *   $d_3 = 120$
*   $h$: Inventory holding cost per unit per month.
    *   $h = 1$
*   $s_0$: Initial inventory at the beginning of month 1.
    *   $s_0 = 0$
*   $s_T$: Final inventory at the end of month $T$.
    *   $s_3 = 0$

### 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_t$ be the production quantity of Product A in month $t$.
Let $s_t$ be the ending inventory level at the end of month $t$.

**Domains:**
*   $x_t \ge 0, \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\}$
*   $s_t \ge 0, \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ (Inventory cannot be negative; backorders are not mentioned, so we assume no backlogging).

*Note on Integrality:* Since the problem involves continuous production costs ($x^2$) and aggregate quantities (100s of units), and does not explicitly require integer units, we treat variables as **Continuous**. This is standard for such aggregate planning problems where LP/Convex Quadratic Programming relaxation is expected.

### 3. Objective Function

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which consists of production costs and inventory holding costs.

*   **Production Cost:** The cost in month $t$ is $x_t^2$.
*   **Holding Cost:** The cost is incurred on the inventory held at the *end* of the month. The cost in month $t$ is $h \cdot s_t$.

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{t=1}^{3} (x_t^2 + h \cdot s_t) $$

Substituting parameters:
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 + 1 \cdot (s_1 + s_2 + s_3) $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**Inventory Balance Constraints:**
The inventory at the end of a month is equal to the inventory from the previous month plus current production minus current demand.

For $t = 1$:
$$ s_1 = s_0 + x_1 - d_1 $$
$$ s_1 = 0 + x_1 - 100 \implies x_1 - s_1 = 100 $$

For $t = 2$:
$$ s_2 = s_1 + x_2 - d_2 $$
$$ s_2 = s_1 + x_2 - 110 \implies -s_1 + s_2 + x_2 = 110 $$
*(Alternatively: $s_1 - s_2 + x_2 = 110$)*

For $t = 3$:
$$ s_3 = s_2 + x_3 - d_3 $$
$$ 0 = s_2 + x_3 - 120 \implies s_2 + x_3 = 120 $$

**Boundary Conditions:**
*   $s_0 = 0$ (Given)
*   $s_3 = 0$ (Given)

**Non-negativity Constraints:**
$$ x_t \ge 0, \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\} $$
$$ s_t \ge 0, \quad \forall t \in \{1, 2, 3\} $$

---

### Final Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
$$ x_1, x_2, x_3, s_1, s_2, s_3 \in \mathbb{R}_+ $$

**Objective Function:**
$$ \min Z = x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 + s_1 + s_2 + s_3 $$

**Subject to:**

1.  **Inventory Balance:**
    $$ x_1 - s_1 = 100 $$
    $$ -s_1 + s_2 + x_2 = 110 $$
    $$ s_2 + x_3 = 120 $$
    *(Note: The last equation incorporates $s_3=0$ and $s_2$ link. Specifically: $s_3 = s_2 + x_3 - 120 \Rightarrow 0 = s_2 + x_3 - 120$)*

2.  **Non-negativity:**
    $$ x_1, x_2, x_3, s_1, s_2, s_3 \ge 0 $$

*(Self-Correction/Refinement on Constraints for Clarity in Solver Format):*
It is often cleaner to write the inventory balance as:
1.  $s_1 = x_1 - 100$
2.  $s_2 = s_1 + x_2 - 110$
3.  $0 = s_2 + x_3 - 120$

These are linear equality constraints. The objective is a convex quadratic function (sum of squares with positive coefficients plus linear terms). This is a standard Convex Quadratic Programming (QP) problem.
