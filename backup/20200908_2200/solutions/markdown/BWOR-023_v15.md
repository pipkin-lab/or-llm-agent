To model the Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) for 4 cities, we can formulate it as a Mixed Integer Linear Programming (MILP) problem. Although the prompt mentions solving via Dynamic Programming, the request is to construct a formal mathematical optimization model. For TSP, the standard formulation uses binary variables for edges and the Miller-Tucker-Zemlin (MTZ) constraints to eliminate subtours.

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $V = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of cities (nodes).
*   $A = \{(i, j) \mid i, j \in V, i \neq j\}$: Set of directed arcs.

**Parameters:**
*   $c_{ij}$: Distance (cost) from city $i$ to city $j$. Based on Table 7-24:
    *   Row 1 ($i=1$): $c_{12}=6, c_{13}=7, c_{14}=9$
    *   Row 2 ($i=2$): $c_{21}=8, c_{23}=9, c_{24}=7$
    *   Row 3 ($i=3$): $c_{31}=5, c_{32}=8, c_{34}=8$
    *   Row 4 ($i=4$): $c_{41}=6, c_{42}=5, c_{43}=5$
    *   $c_{ii} = 0$ (not used in arcs)
*   $n = |V| = 4$: Number of cities.
*   $s = 1$: Start/End city (City 1).

### 2. Decision Variables

*   $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable for all $(i, j) \in A$.
    *   $x_{ij} = 1$ if the path travels directly from city $i$ to city $j$.
    *   $x_{ij} = 0$ otherwise.
*   $u_i \in \mathbb{R}$: Continuous auxiliary variable for all $i \in V \setminus \{s\}$ (i.e., $i \in \{2, 3, 4\}$).
    *   These variables are used in the MTZ subtour elimination constraints. We fix $u_s = 0$ without loss of generality.

### 3. Objective Function

Minimize the total distance of the tour:

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V, j \neq i} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**Flow Balance Constraints:**
Ensure every city has exactly one incoming arc and one outgoing arc.

$$
\sum_{j \in V, j \neq i} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i \in V
$$
$$
\sum_{i \in V, i \neq j} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall j \in V
$$

**Subtour Elimination Constraints (Miller-Tucker-Zemlin):**
Prevent the formation of cycles that do not include all nodes.

$$
u_i - u_j + n x_{ij} \leq n - 1 \quad \forall i, j \in V \setminus \{s\}, i \neq j
$$
*Note: The constraint is typically applied for $i, j \neq s$. Since $s=1$, this applies to pairs in $\{2, 3, 4\}$.*

**Variable Bounds & Definitions:**

$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall (i, j) \in A
$$
$$
0 \leq u_i \leq n - 1 \quad \forall i \in V \setminus \{s\}
$$
$$
u_s = 0
$$

***

**Model Verification & Notes:**
1.  **Integrality**: The problem involves discrete paths (visit city or not), so $x_{ij}$ must be binary.
2.  **MTZ Bounds**: The bounds for $u_i$ are set to $[0, n-1]$ (i.e., $[0, 3]$) to ensure the linearization is valid and tight enough for small $n$.
3.  **Start Node**: The formulation is symmetric in terms of tour length, but fixing the start node $s=1$ and setting $u_1=0$ simplifies the MTZ constraints. The flow balance constraints ensure a single cycle passing through all nodes.
