### 2. Decision Variables

**Binary Variables:**
*   $y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: Equals 1 if Job $i$ is scheduled in position $j$ of the sequence, 0 otherwise.

**Continuous Variables:**
*   $S_{A,j} \ge 0$: Start time of the job in position $j$ on Machine A.
*   $S_{B,j} \ge 0$: Start time of the job in position $j$ on Machine B.
*   $C_{max} \ge 0$: Makespan (total completion time).

### 3. Objective Function

Minimize the total processing time (Makespan):

$$ \min Z = C_{max} $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Job Assignment Constraints**
Each job must be assigned to exactly one position, and each position must contain exactly one job.

$$ \sum_{j=1}^{12} y_{ij} = 1, \quad \forall i \in I $$

$$ \sum_{i=1}^{12} y_{ij} = 1, \quad \forall j \in P $$

**2. Machine Sequencing Constraints**
The start time of a job on a machine must be after the finish time of the previous job in the sequence on that same machine.

*For Machine A:*
$$ S_{A,j} \ge S_{A,j-1} + \sum_{k=1}^{12} a_k y_{k,j-1}, \quad \forall j \in P, j > 1 $$
$$ S_{A,1} \ge 0 $$

*For Machine B:*
$$ S_{B,j} \ge S_{B,j-1} + \sum_{k=1}^{12} b_k y_{k,j-1}, \quad \forall j \in P, j > 1 $$
$$ S_{B,1} \ge 0 $$

**3. Routing Constraints**
These constraints enforce the specific operation order for each job type.

*For Jobs in $I_A$ (A $\to$ B):*
The job must start on B after it finishes on A.
Note: $\sum_{i \in I_A} a_i y_{ij}$ is the processing time on A for the job in position $j$ if it is in $I_A$, otherwise 0.

$$ S_{B,j} \ge S_{A,j} + \sum_{i \in I_A} a_i y_{ij}, \quad \forall j \in P $$

*For Jobs in $I_B$ (B $\to$ A):*
The job must start on A after it finishes on B.
Note: $\sum_{i \in I_B} b_i y_{ij}$ is the processing time on B for the job in position $j$ if it is in $I_B$, otherwise 0.

$$ S_{A,j} \ge S_{B,j} + \sum_{i \in I_B} b_i y_{ij}, \quad \forall j \in P $$

**4. Makespan Definition**
The makespan is the maximum of the finish times of all jobs on all machines.

$$ C_{max} \ge S_{A,j} + \sum_{k=1}^{12} a_k y_{k,j}, \quad \forall j \in P $$

$$ C_{max} \ge S_{B,j} + \sum_{k=1}^{12} b_k y_{k,j}, \quad \forall j \in P $$
