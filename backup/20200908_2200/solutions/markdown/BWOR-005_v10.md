# Cargo Loading Optimization Model

## 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $C = \{A, B, C\}$: Set of cargo types.
*   $K = \{F, M, R\}$: Set of ship compartments (Front, Middle, Rear).

**Parameters:**

*From Table 1-24 (Compartment Limits):*
*   $W_{max, F} = 2000$ t (Max load Front)
*   $W_{max, M} = 3000$ t (Max load Middle)
*   $W_{max, R} = 1500$ t (Max load Rear)
*   $V_{max, F} = 4000$ $m^3$ (Max volume Front)
*   $V_{max, M} = 5000$ $m^3$ (Max volume Middle)
*   $V_{max, R} = 1500$ $m^3$ (Max volume Rear)

*From Table 1-25 (Cargo Data):*
*   $Q_A = 600$, $Q_B = 1000$, $Q_C = 800$ (Available quantities)
*   $v_A = 10$, $v_B = 5$, $v_C = 7$ ($m^3$/piece)
*   $w_A = 8$, $w_B = 6$, $w_C = 4$ (t/piece)
*   $p_A = 1000$, $p_B = 700$, $p_C = 600$ ($/piece)

*Balance Constraints Parameters:*
*   Target Weight Ratios based on Max Loads:
    *   $r_{F/M}^{target} = \frac{W_{max, F}}{W_{max, M}} = \frac{2000}{3000} = \frac{2}{3}$
    *   $r_{R/M}^{target} = \frac{W_{max, R}}{W_{max, M}} = \frac{1500}{3000} = \frac{1}{2}$
    *   $r_{F/R}^{target} = \frac{W_{max, F}}{W_{max, R}} = \frac{2000}{1500} = \frac{4}{3}$
*   Deviation Limits:
    *   $\delta_{FM} = 0.15$ (15%)
    *   $\delta_{RM} = 0.15$ (15%)
    *   $\delta_{FR} = 0.10$ (10%)

## 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_{i,k}$ be the number of pieces of cargo type $i$ loaded in compartment $k$.

*   $x_{i,k} \ge 0$ for all $i \in C, k \in K$.
*   **Integrality Note:** While cargo pieces are discrete, standard linear programming formulations for cargo loading problems of this scale (total potential pieces ~2400, weights in tons) typically treat variables as **continuous** to allow for a tractable LP solution. Given the large scale and the nature of the question ("formulate the linear programming model"), we define:
    $$ x_{i,k} \in \mathbb{R}_+ \quad \forall i \in C, k \in K $$

## 3. Objective Function

Maximize total revenue:

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i \in C} \sum_{k \in K} p_i x_{i,k}
$$

Explicitly:
$$
Z = 1000(x_{A,F} + x_{A,M} + x_{A,R}) + 700(x_{B,F} + x_{B,M} + x_{B,R}) + 600(x_{C,F} + x_{C,M} + x_{C,R})
$$

## 4. Operational Constraints

### A. Volume Constraints
The total volume of cargo in each compartment must not exceed the compartment's volume capacity.

$$
\sum_{i \in C} v_i x_{i,k} \le V_{max, k} \quad \forall k \in K
$$

*   **Front:** $10 x_{A,F} + 5 x_{B,F} + 7 x_{C,F} \le 4000$
*   **Middle:** $10 x_{A,M} + 5 x_{B,M} + 7 x_{C,M} \le 5000$
*   **Rear:** $10 x_{A,R} + 5 x_{B,R} + 7 x_{C,R} \le 1500$

### B. Weight Constraints
The total weight of cargo in each compartment must not exceed the compartment's weight capacity.

$$
\sum_{i \in C} w_i x_{i,k} \le W_{max, k} \quad \forall k \in K
$$

*   **Front:** $8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F} \le 2000$
*   **Middle:** $8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M} \le 3000$
*   **Rear:** $8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R} \le 1500$

### C. Cargo Supply Constraints
The total number of pieces of each cargo type loaded across all compartments cannot exceed the available supply.

$$
\sum_{k \in K} x_{i,k} \le Q_i \quad \forall i \in C
$$

*   **Cargo A:** $x_{A,F} + x_{A,M} + x_{A,R} \le 600$
*   **Cargo B:** $x_{B,F} + x_{B,M} + x_{B,R} \le 1000$
*   **Cargo C:** $x_{C,F} + x_{C,M} + x_{C,R} \le 800$

### D. Balance (Stability) Constraints
Let $W_k = \sum_{i \in C} w_i x_{i,k}$ denote the actual total weight in compartment $k$.

The problem states that the ratio of actual weights must not deviate by more than a specified percentage from the ratio of the maximum allowable loads.

Let $R_{k_1, k_2}^{target} = \frac{W_{max, k_1}}{W_{max, k_2}}$.
The constraint is:
$$
\frac{| \frac{W_{k_1}}{W_{k_2}} - R_{k_1, k_2}^{target} |}{R_{k_1, k_2}^{target}} \le \delta
$$
This simplifies to:
$$
(1 - \delta) R_{k_1, k_2}^{target} \le \frac{W_{k_1}}{W_{k_2}} \le (1 + \delta) R_{k_1, k_2}^{target}
$$
Multiplying by $W_{k_2}$ (which is positive if we assume the ship is loaded, or handled via the weight constraints ensuring non-negativity and capacity) gives linear inequalities:
$$
(1 - \delta) R_{k_1, k_2}^{target} W_{k_2} \le W_{k_1} \le (1 + \delta) R_{k_1, k_2}^{target} W_{k_2}
$$

**1. Front/Middle Balance ($\delta = 0.15$, Target Ratio $2/3$):**
$$
\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)(1 - 0.15) W_M \le W_F \le \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)(1 + 0.15) W_M
$$
$$
\frac{17}{30} W_M \le W_F \le \frac{23}{30} W_M
$$
Substituting $W_F$ and $W_M$:
$$
\frac{17}{30} (8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) - (8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) \le 0
$$
$$
(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - \frac{23}{30} (8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \le 0
$$

**2. Rear/Middle Balance ($\delta = 0.15$, Target Ratio $1/2$):**
$$
\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)(1 - 0.15) W_M \le W_R \le \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)(1 + 0.15) W_M
$$
$$
\frac{17}{40} W_M \le W_R \le \frac{23}{40} W_M
$$
Substituting $W_R$ and $W_M$:
$$
\frac{17}{40} (8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) - (8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) \le 0
$$
$$
(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) - \frac{23}{40} (8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \le 0
$$

**3. Front/Rear Balance ($\delta = 0.10$, Target Ratio $4/3$):**
$$
\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)(1 - 0.10) W_R \le W_F \le \left(\frac{4}{3}\right)(1 + 0.10) W_R
$$
$$
\frac{16}{15} W_R \le W_F \le \frac{22}{15} W_R
$$
Substituting $W_F$ and $W_R$:
$$
\frac{16}{15} (8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) - (8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) \le 0
$$
$$
(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - \frac{22}{15} (8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) \le 0
$$

### E. Non-negativity
$$
x_{i,k} \ge 0 \quad \forall i \in C, k \in K
$$
