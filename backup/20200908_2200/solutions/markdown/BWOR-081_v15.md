# Mathematical Optimization Model

## 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
- $P = \{1, 2\}$: Set of products (Product I, Product II)
- $R = \{A, B\}$: Set of raw materials

**Parameters:**

| Parameter | Description | Value | Unit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $a_{A,1}$ | Material A consumed per unit of Product I | 1 | t / thousand boxes |
| $a_{A,2}$ | Material A consumed per unit of Product II | 2 | t / thousand boxes |
| $a_{B,1}$ | Material B consumed per unit of Product I | 2 | t / thousand boxes |
| $a_{B,2}$ | Material B consumed per unit of Product II | 1 | t / thousand boxes |
| $S_A$ | Monthly supply of Material A | 6 | tons |
| $S_B$ | Monthly supply of Material B | 8 | tons |
| $p_1$ | Wholesale price of Product I | 30 | thousand yuan / thousand boxes |
| $p_2$ | Wholesale price of Product II | 20 | thousand yuan / thousand boxes |
| $D_2$ | Maximum sales limit for Product II | 2 | thousand boxes |
| $\Delta_{21}$ | Max difference limit (Product II - Product I) | 1 | thousand boxes |

*Note: The problem asks to maximize wholesale revenue. The cost of raw materials is not subtracted in the objective function as the question specifically asks for "maximum possible wholesale revenue", not profit. If profit were required, the material costs ($9.9 \times S_A + 6.6 \times S_B$) would be subtracted, but "revenue" strictly refers to income from sales.*

## 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_1$ and $x_2$ be the production/sales quantities of Product I and Product II, respectively.

- $x_1$: Monthly production quantity of Product I (thousand boxes)
- $x_2$: Monthly production quantity of Product II (thousand boxes)

**Domains:**
- $x_1 \ge 0$ (Continuous)
- $x_2 \ge 0$ (Continuous)

*Scale Check: The quantities are in "thousand boxes" and supply is in "tons". This is a standard aggregate production planning scale. Continuous variables are appropriate as fractional thousand boxes (e.g., 1.5 thousand boxes = 1500 boxes) are physically possible and standard for LP formulations of this type.*

## 3. Objective Function

Maximize total wholesale revenue $Z$:

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = 30 x_1 + 20 x_2
$$

## 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Raw Material A Availability:**
Total consumption of Material A must not exceed monthly supply.
$$
1 x_1 + 2 x_2 \le 6
$$

**2. Raw Material B Availability:**
Total consumption of Material B must not exceed monthly supply.
$$
2 x_1 + 1 x_2 \le 8
$$

**3. Market Demand Limit for Product II:**
Sales of Product II do not exceed 2 thousand boxes.
$$
x_2 \le 2
$$

**4. Sales Balance Constraint:**
The sales volume of Product II does not exceed that of Product I by more than 1 thousand boxes.
$$
x_2 - x_1 \le 1
$$

**5. Non-negativity:**
$$
x_1 \ge 0, \quad x_2 \ge 0
$$

---

### Final Model Summary

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Maximize} \quad & Z = 30 x_1 + 20 x_2 \\
\text{Subject to} \quad & 1 x_1 + 2 x_2 \le 6 \\
& 2 x_1 + 1 x_2 \le 8 \\
& x_2 \le 2 \\
& -x_1 + x_2 \le 1 \\
& x_1, x_2 \ge 0
\end{aligned}
$$
