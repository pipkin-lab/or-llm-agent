# Mathematical Optimization Model: Livestock Feed Formulation

## 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $I = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: Set of feed types.

**Parameters (per kg of feed):**
- $p_i$: Protein content (g)
- $m_i$: Mineral content (g)
- $v_i$: Vitamin content (mg)
- $c_i$: Price (dollars)

**Data Table:**

| Feed $i$ | $p_i$ (g) | $m_i$ (g) | $v_i$ (mg) | $c_i$ ($/kg) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1 | 3 | 1.0 | 0.5 | 0.2 |
| 2 | 2 | 0.5 | 1.0 | 0.7 |
| 3 | 1 | 0.2 | 0.2 | 0.4 |
| 4 | 6 | 2.0 | 2.0 | 0.3 |
| 5 | 18 | 0.5 | 0.8 | 0.8 |

**Nutritional Requirements (per day, per animal):**
- $P_{req} = 700$ g protein
- $M_{req} = 30$ g minerals
- $V_{req} = 100$ mg vitamins

## 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_i$ be the amount of feed type $i$ used (in kg) per day per animal.

- **Domain**: $x_i \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ for all $i \in I$.
- **Note on Integrality**: Feed is a divisible resource (continuous). The problem asks for a "feed selection plan" (ratios/amounts), not the number of discrete bags. Therefore, continuous variables are appropriate.

## 3. Objective Function

Minimize the total cost of the feed mixture.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{5} c_i x_i
$$

Substituting values:
$$
Z = 0.2 x_1 + 0.7 x_2 + 0.4 x_3 + 0.3 x_4 + 0.8 x_5
$$

## 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Protein Constraint:**
The total protein provided must be at least 700 g.
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{5} p_i x_i \ge 700
$$
$$
3 x_1 + 2 x_2 + 1 x_3 + 6 x_4 + 18 x_5 \ge 700
$$

**2. Minerals Constraint:**
The total minerals provided must be at least 30 g.
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{5} m_i x_i \ge 30
$$
$$
1.0 x_1 + 0.5 x_2 + 0.2 x_3 + 2.0 x_4 + 0.5 x_5 \ge 30
$$

**3. Vitamins Constraint:**
The total vitamins provided must be at least 100 mg.
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{5} v_i x_i \ge 100
$$
$$
0.5 x_1 + 1.0 x_2 + 0.2 x_3 + 2.0 x_4 + 0.8 x_5 \ge 100
$$

**4. Non-negativity Constraints:**
$$
x_i \ge 0, \quad \forall i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}
$$

## Final Model Summary

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Minimize} \quad & 0.2 x_1 + 0.7 x_2 + 0.4 x_3 + 0.3 x_4 + 0.8 x_5 \\
\text{Subject to:} \quad & 3 x_1 + 2 x_2 + 1 x_3 + 6 x_4 + 18 x_5 \ge 700 \quad (\text{Protein}) \\
& 1.0 x_1 + 0.5 x_2 + 0.2 x_3 + 2.0 x_4 + 0.5 x_5 \ge 30 \quad \; (\text{Minerals}) \\
& 0.5 x_1 + 1.0 x_2 + 0.2 x_3 + 2.0 x_4 + 0.8 x_5 \ge 100 \quad \; (\text{Vitamins}) \\
& x_i \ge 0, \quad i = 1, \dots, 5
\end{aligned}
$$
