Based on the problem description, this is a **Goal Programming** problem. The company has three prioritized objectives:
1.  **Priority 1 ($P_1$):** Minimize stockouts (unmet demand). The penalty weight for Product 3 is twice that of Products 1 and 2.
2.  **Priority 2 ($P_2$):** Minimize equipment overloading (excess hours).
3.  **Priority 3 ($P_3$):** Ensure working capital usage does not exceed the limit (minimize capital overage).

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $I = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of products.
*   $T = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of planning stages (periods).

**Parameters:**
*   $D_{i,t}$: Demand for product $i$ in stage $t$.
    *   From Table 4-16:
        *   $D_{1,1}=500, D_{2,1}=750, D_{3,1}=900$
        *   $D_{1,2}=680, D_{2,2}=800, D_{3,2}=800$
        *   $D_{1,3}=800, D_{2,3}=950, D_{3,3}=1000$
*   $a_{i}$: Equipment working hours required per unit of product $i$.
    *   $a_1 = 2.0, a_2 = 1.0, a_3 = 3.1$
*   $b_{i}$: Working capital occupied per unit of product $i$ (yuan).
    *   $b_1 = 40, b_2 = 20, b_3 = 55$
*   $H_{t}$: Total available equipment working hours in stage $t$.
    *   $H_{t} = 5000$ for all $t \in T$.
*   $C_{t}$: Total available working capital in stage $t$.
    *   $C_{t} = 93000$ for all $t \in T$.
*   $w_{i}$: Weight for stockout penalty in Priority 1.
    *   "Importance of timely delivery for Product 3 is twice that of Products 1 and 2".
    *   $w_1 = 1, w_2 = 1, w_3 = 2$.

### 2. Decision Variables

**Structural Variables:**
*   $x_{i,t}$: Quantity of product $i$ produced in stage $t$. Domain: $x_{i,t} \ge 0$ (Continuous, as aggregate production quantities are typically treated as continuous in large-scale LPs unless specified otherwise; scale > 1000).
*   $I_{i,t}$: Inventory level of product $i$ at the end of stage $t$. Domain: $I_{i,t} \ge 0$.

**Deviation Variables (for Goal Programming):**
*   **Priority 1 (Stockouts/Underproduction):**
    *   $u_{i,t}$: Under-production (stockout) of product $i$ in stage $t$ (amount by which production + beginning inventory falls short of demand). Domain: $u_{i,t} \ge 0$.
    *   $v_{i,t}$: Over-production (excess inventory) of product $i$ in stage $t$ (amount by which production + beginning inventory exceeds demand). Domain: $v_{i,t} \ge 0$.
    *   *Note: In standard goal programming for demand satisfaction, we usually balance: Production + BegInv - EndInv = Demand + Under - Over. Alternatively, we can define the balance equation directly.*
*   **Priority 2 (Equipment Overloading):**
    *   $e_{t}^{+}$: Excess equipment hours (overloading) in stage $t$. Domain: $e_{t}^{+} \ge 0$.
    *   $e_{t}^{-}$: Unused equipment hours in stage $t$. Domain: $e_{t}^{-} \ge 0$.
*   **Priority 3 (Capital Overloading):**
    *   $c_{t}^{+}$: Excess working capital used in stage $t$. Domain: $c_{t}^{+} \ge 0$.
    *   $c_{t}^{-}$: Unused working capital in stage $t$. Domain: $c_{t}^{-} \ge 0$.

**Initial/Final Inventory Conditions:**
*   $I_{i,0} = 0$ for all $i$.
*   $I_{i,3} = 0$ for all $i$ (Final inventory is zero).

### 3. Objective Function

The objective is to minimize the weighted sum of deviations according to their priorities. Since this is a preemptive (lexicographic) goal programming problem, we can either solve sequentially or use a single objective with large weights $M_1 \gg M_2 \gg M_3$ or simply state the lexicographic order. Here, we define the lexicographic minimization:

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \{ Z_1, Z_2, Z_3 \} \quad \text{lexicographically} $$

Where:
1.  **$Z_1$ (Priority 1: Minimize Stockouts):**
    $$ Z_1 = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{t \in T} w_i \cdot u_{i,t} $$
    *(Note: We only penalize under-production $u_{i,t}$ because the goal is to "meet demand". Over-production $v_{i,t}$ is not penalized in Priority 1, but might be implicitly handled by inventory constraints or just allowed. However, since final inventory must be 0, excessive production in early periods just increases holding, which isn't explicitly penalized in P1. Wait, if we produce more than demand, it goes to inventory. If we produce less, it's a stockout. The goal is "timely delivery to meet demand", so we minimize the shortfall.)*

2.  **$Z_2$ (Priority 2: Minimize Equipment Overloading):**
    $$ Z_2 = \sum_{t \in T} e_{t}^{+} $$
    *(We minimize the excess hours, i.e., the amount by which usage exceeds the limit.)*

3.  **$Z_3$ (Priority 3: Minimize Capital Overloading):**
    $$ Z_3 = \sum_{t \in T} c_{t}^{+} $$
    *(We minimize the excess capital usage, i.e., the amount by which usage exceeds the limit.)*

### 4. Operational Constraints

**A. Inventory Balance Constraints (Linking Production, Demand, and Deviations)**
For each product $i$ and stage $t$:
$$ x_{i,t} + I_{i,t-1} - I_{i,t} = D_{i,t} + u_{i,t} - v_{i,t} $$
Where:
*   $I_{i,0} = 0$
*   $I_{i,3} = 0$
*   $u_{i,t}, v_{i,t} \ge 0$
*   This equation states: Beginning Inventory + Production - Ending Inventory = Demand + Underproduction - Overproduction.
    *   If $x + I_{beg} < D_{end}$, then $u_{i,t}$ becomes positive (stockout).
    *   If $x + I_{beg} > D_{end}$, then $v_{i,t}$ becomes positive (excess goes to inventory or is simply "over" relative to current demand, but since $I_{i,t}$ captures the carryover, $v_{i,t}$ essentially represents the amount by which the flow into the period exceeded the demand *before* adjusting for the ending inventory variable. Actually, a simpler standard form is:
    $$ x_{i,t} + I_{i,t-1} + v_{i,t} - u_{i,t} - I_{i,t} = D_{i,t} $$
    Let's refine this. Usually, in goal programming for production smoothing/delivery:
    $$ \text{Supply} = \text{Demand} + \text{Under} - \text{Over} $$
    $$ x_{i,t} + I_{i,t-1} - I_{i,t} = D_{i,t} + u_{i,t} - v_{i,t} $$
    This is correct. $u_{i,t}$ is the unmet demand (stockout). $v_{i,t}$ is the excess production relative to demand (which effectively builds up inventory beyond what is needed for current demand, but since $I_{i,t}$ is a variable, $v_{i,t}$ will be 0 if we just use inventory to balance. Wait. If $x+I_{beg} > D$, then $I_{end}$ can just be that difference. In that case, $u=0, v=0$ is possible?
    Let's check: If $x+I_{beg} - I_{end} = D$. Then $u=v=0$.
    If $x+I_{beg} < D$, we cannot have $I_{end} \ge 0$ satisfy the equality unless we allow $I_{end}$ to be negative (backlog) or introduce $u$.
    Standard formulation:
    $$ x_{i,t} + I_{i,t-1} - I_{i,t} + u_{i,t} - v_{i,t} = D_{i,t} $$
    Here, if Supply < Demand, $u_{i,t}$ takes the difference. If Supply > Demand, $v_{i,t}$ takes the difference? No, if Supply > Demand, we just set $I_{i,t} = I_{i,t-1} + x_{i,t} - D_{i,t}$ and $u_{i,t}=v_{i,t}=0$.
    So, $v_{i,t}$ is only non-zero if we *force* it to be. In many formulations, $v_{i,t}$ is not needed if we just want to minimize under-production $u_{i,t}$. The "over-production" is naturally absorbed into $I_{i,t}$.
    However, if the problem implies that we want to avoid *both* stockouts and *excess inventory* in the same period relative to demand (i.e., produce exactly what is demanded if possible), then we would minimize $u+v$. But the goal is "Timely delivery to meet demand, minimizing stockouts". It does **not** say "minimize inventory". It only mentions stockouts.
    Therefore, we only need $u_{i,t}$ (under-production) in the objective.
    Constraint:
    $$ x_{i,t} + I_{i,t-1} - I_{i,t} + u_{i,t} = D_{i,t} $$
    With $I_{i,t} \ge 0$, $u_{i,t} \ge 0$.
    If $x_{i,t} + I_{i,t-1} < D_{i,t}$, then $u_{i,t} > 0$ and $I_{i,t}=0$ (cannot have negative inventory).
    If $x_{i,t} + I_{i,t-1} \ge D_{i,t}$, then $u_{i,t}=0$ and $I_{i,t} = x_{i,t} + I_{i,t-1} - D_{i,t}$.
    This formulation correctly captures stockouts. We do not need $v_{i,t}$ for the objective, but it might be useful for tracking. Let's stick to the minimal variables required.
    So, **$v_{i,t}$ is removed** from the model as it is not penalized and can be subsumed by the inventory variable.

**B. Equipment Working Hours Constraints (Priority 2 Goal)**
For each stage $t$:
$$ \sum_{i \in I} a_i x_{i,t} + e_{t}^{-} - e_{t}^{+} = H_{t} $$
Where $H_t = 5000$.
$e_{t}^{+}$ is the overloading (excess). $e_{t}^{-}$ is the underutilization.
Objective $Z_2$ minimizes $\sum e_{t}^{+}$.

**C. Working Capital Constraints (Priority 3 Goal)**
For each stage $t$:
$$ \sum_{i \in I} b_i x_{i,t} + c_{t}^{-} - c_{t}^{+} = C_{t} $$
Where $C_t = 93000$.
$c_{t}^{+}$ is the excess capital usage.
Objective $Z_3$ minimizes $\sum c_{t}^{+}$.

**D. Non-negativity and Domain Bounds**
$$ x_{i,t} \ge 0 $$
$$ I_{i,t} \ge 0 $$
$$ u_{i,t} \ge 0 $$
$$ e_{t}^{+} \ge 0, e_{t}^{-} \ge 0 $$
$$ c_{t}^{+} \ge 0, c_{t}^{-} \ge 0 $$

**E. Final Inventory Constraint**
$$ I_{i,3} = 0 \quad \forall i \in I $$
$$ I_{i,0} = 0 \quad \forall i \in I $$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Sets:**
$I = \{1, 2, 3\}$ (Products)
$T = \{1, 2, 3\}$ (Stages)

**Parameters:**
$D_{i,t}$: Demand matrix
$a_i$: Equipment hours per unit $(2.0, 1.0, 3.1)$
$b_i$: Capital per unit $(40, 20, 55)$
$H_t = 5000$
$C_t = 93000$
$w_i$: Stockout weights $(1, 1, 2)$

**Variables:**
$x_{i,t} \ge 0$: Production
$I_{i,t} \ge 0$: Inventory
$u_{i,t} \ge 0$: Stockout (unmet demand)
$e_{t}^{+} \ge 0, e_{t}^{-} \ge 0$: Equipment excess/deficit
$c_{t}^{+} \ge 0, c_{t}^{-} \ge 0$: Capital excess/deficit

**Objective (Lexicographic Minimization):**
1.  Min $Z_1 = 1 \cdot \sum_{t} (u_{1,t} + u_{2,t}) + 2 \cdot \sum_{t} u_{3,t}$
2.  Min $Z_2 = \sum_{t} e_{t}^{+}$
3.  Min $Z_3 = \sum_{t} c_{t}^{+}$

**Constraints:**
1.  **Inventory Balance:**
    $$ x_{i,t} + I_{i,t-1} - I_{i,t} + u_{i,t} = D_{i,t}, \quad \forall i \in I, t \in T $$
    (With $I_{i,0}=0, I_{i,3}=0$)
2.  **Equipment Hours:**
    $$ \sum_{i \in I} a_i x_{i,t} + e_{t}^{-} - e_{t}^{+} = 5000, \quad \forall t \in T $$
3.  **Working Capital:**
    $$ \sum_{i \in I} b_i x_{i,t} + c_{t}^{-} - c_{t}^{+} = 93000, \quad \forall t \in T $$
4.  **Non-negativity:**
    All variables $\ge 0$.
