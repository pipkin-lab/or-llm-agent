Based on the problem description, this is a **Transportation Problem** with additional constraints. The goal is to maximize profit, which is calculated as Revenue minus (Production Cost + Transportation Cost).

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
- $P = \{A, B, C, D\}$: Set of plants (sources).
- $R = \{I, II, III, IV, V, VI\}$: Set of regions (destinations).

**Parameters:**
- $S_p$: Supply capacity of plant $p$ (tons).
  - $S_A = 200, S_B = 300, S_C = 400, S_D = 100$.
- $D_r$: Demand of region $r$ (tons).
  - $D_I = 200, D_{II} = 150, D_{III} = 400, D_{IV} = 100, D_V = 150, D_{VI} = 150$.
  - *Note: Total Supply = 1000 tons. Total Demand = 1150 tons. Since Supply < Demand, this is a supply-constrained problem. Not all demand can be met. We must decide which demands to satisfy.*
- $c^p_p$: Unit production cost per kg (yuan/kg) at plant $p$.
  - $c^p_A = 1.2, c^p_B = 1.4, c^p_C = 1.1, c^p_D = 1.5$.
- $v_r$: Unit sales price per kg (yuan/kg) in region $r$.
  - $v_I = 2.0, v_{II} = 2.4, v_{III} = 1.8, v_{IV} = 2.2, v_V = 1.6, v_{VI} = 2.0$.
- $t_{p,r}$: Unit transportation cost per kg from plant $p$ to region $r$ (yuan/kg).
  - From Table 3-18:
    - $t_{A, \cdot} = [0.5, 0.4, 0.3, 0.4, 0.3, 0.1]$
    - $t_{B, \cdot} = [0.3, 0.8, 0.9, 0.5, 0.6, 0.2]$
    - $t_{C, \cdot} = [0.7, 0.7, 0.3, 0.7, 0.4, 0.4]$
    - $t_{D, \cdot} = [0.6, 0.4, 0.2, 0.6, 0.5, 0.8]$

**Derived Parameters (Profit Coefficients):**
Let $z_{p,r}$ be the net profit per kg for producing at plant $p$ and selling to region $r$.
$$ z_{p,r} = v_r - c^p_p - t_{p,r} $$

### 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_{p,r}$ be the amount (in tons) shipped from plant $p$ to region $r$.
Domain: $x_{p,r} \ge 0, \forall p \in P, r \in R$.

*Scale Check:* The problem involves "tons" and "kg" costs. Since production/supply/demand are in tons and costs are per kg, we can either:
1. Convert all quantities to kg (multiply tons by 1000).
2. Keep quantities in tons and convert costs to yuan/ton (multiply yuan/kg by 1000).
Let's keep $x_{p,r}$ in **tons** and define the objective coefficient $Z_{p,r}$ in **yuan/ton**.
$$ Z_{p,r} = z_{p,r} \times 1000 $$
Alternatively, to keep numbers smaller, we can define $x_{p,r}$ in **thousands of tons**? No, let's stick to standard **tons** and **yuan/ton** for clarity, or just keep **yuan/kg** and convert $x$ to **kg**.
Let's use **kg** for $x_{p,r}$ to align directly with the given cost units.
Supply $S_p$ (kg): $A=200,000$, $B=300,000$, $C=400,000$, $D=100,000$.
Demand $D_r$ (kg): $I=200,000$, etc.

Actually, it is often cleaner to keep $x$ in **tons** and treat the profit coefficient as **thousand yuan/ton** or just **yuan/ton**.
Let's define $x_{p,r}$ in **tons**.
Objective Coefficient $C_{p,r} = 1000 \times z_{p,r}$ (yuan/ton).

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize Total Profit $Z$:
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{p \in P} \sum_{r \in R} C_{p,r} x_{p,r} $$

Where $C_{p,r}$ are the profit per ton values calculated above (multiplied by 1000).
For example:
$C_{A,II} = 800$ yuan/ton.
$C_{B,III} = -500$ yuan/ton.

### 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Supply Constraints (Plant Capacities)**
The total amount shipped from each plant cannot exceed its output capacity. Since Supply (1000t) < Demand (1150t), these will likely be binding equalities (produce max possible to maximize profit, assuming positive net profits exist for some routes, which they do). However, strictly speaking, it's an inequality.
$$ \sum_{r \in R} x_{p,r} \le S_p, \quad \forall p \in P $$

**2. Demand Constraints (Region Limits)**
The total amount received by each region cannot exceed its demand.
$$ \sum_{p \in P} x_{p,r} \le D_r, \quad \forall r \in R $$

**3. Specific Constraint: Region III**
"At least 100 tons must be supplied to Region III".
$$ \sum_{p \in P} x_{p,III} \ge 100 $$
*(Note: This is in tons. If $x$ is in kg, it would be $\ge 100,000$. Let's stick to tons for the model variables for readability, assuming the solver handles the scaling or we just note the units. I will specify units as **tons** in the variable definition).*

**4. Specific Constraint: Region IV**
"The demand in Region IV must be fully satisfied".
$$ \sum_{p \in P} x_{p,IV} = D_{IV} = 100 $$

**5. Non-negativity**
$$ x_{p,r} \ge 0, \quad \forall p \in P, r \in R $$

### Summary of Mathematical Model

**Variables:**
$x_{p,r} \ge 0$ : Tons shipped from plant $p$ to region $r$.

**Objective:**
$$ \text{Max } Z = \sum_{p,r} 1000 \cdot (v_r - c^p_p - t_{p,r}) x_{p,r} $$

**Constraints:**
1.  **Plant Supply:**
    $\sum_{r \in R} x_{A,r} \le 200$
    $\sum_{r \in R} x_{B,r} \le 300$
    $\sum_{r \in R} x_{C,r} \le 400$
    $\sum_{r \in R} x_{D,r} \le 100$

2.  **Region Demand (Max):**
    $\sum_{p \in P} x_{p,I} \le 200$
    $\sum_{p \in P} x_{p,II} \le 150$
    $\sum_{p \in P} x_{p,III} \le 400$
    $\sum_{p \in P} x_{p,IV} = 100$  *(Fully satisfied)*
    $\sum_{p \in P} x_{p,V} \le 150$
    $\sum_{p \in P} x_{p,VI} \le 150$

3.  **Region III Minimum:**
    $\sum_{p \in P} x_{p,III} \ge 100$

*(Self-Correction/Refinement on Units)*: The problem states costs in **yuan/kg** and quantities in **tons**.
If $x_{p,r}$ is in **tons**, then:
Revenue = $x_{p,r} \times 1000 \text{ kg/ton} \times v_r \text{ yuan/kg} = 1000 v_r x_{p,r}$
Cost = $x_{p,r} \times 1000 \text{ kg/ton} \times (c^p_p + t_{p,r}) \text{ yuan/kg} = 1000 (c^p_p + t_{p,r}) x_{p,r}$
Net Profit = $1000 (v_r - c^p_p - t_{p,r}) x_{p,r}$

So the objective coefficient is indeed $1000 \times z_{p,r}$.

**Final Check on "Fully Satisfied" vs "Max Demand":**
For Region IV, the constraint is equality: $\sum x_{p,IV} = 100$.
For other regions, it is inequality: $\sum x_{p,r} \le D_r$.
For Region III, it is a lower bound: $\sum x_{p,III} \ge 100$ (and implicitly $\le 400$ from the demand constraint).
