# Optimization Model: Construction Task Assignment

## 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $I = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: Set of construction companies ($A_i$).
- $J = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: Set of new stores to be constructed ($B_j$).

**Parameters:**
- $c_{ij}$: Cost (in thousand yuan) for construction company $A_i$ to build store $B_j$. Values are given in Table 5-10:

| $c_{ij}$ | $B_1$ | $B_2$ | $B_3$ | $B_4$ | $B_5$ |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **$A_1$** | 4 | 8 | 7 | 15 | 12 |
| **$A_2$** | 7 | 9 | 17 | 14 | 10 |
| **$A_3$** | 6 | 9 | 12 | 8 | 7 |
| **$A_4$** | 6 | 7 | 14 | 6 | 10 |
| **$A_5$** | 6 | 9 | 12 | 10 | 6 |

## 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_{ij}$ be a binary decision variable defined as:
$$
x_{ij} = 
\begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if company } A_i \text{ is assigned to build store } B_j \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
Domain: $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$ for all $i \in I, j \in J$.

*Note: Since each store is a discrete project that must be assigned to exactly one company and each company can take at most one project, this is a standard Assignment Problem requiring binary (integer) variables. Fractional assignment (e.g., 0.5 of a store) is physically impossible.*

## 3. Objective Function

Minimize the total construction cost:
$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^{5} \sum_{j=1}^{5} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

## 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Each store is built by exactly one company:**
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{5} x_{ij} = 1, \quad \forall j \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}
$$

**2. Each company is assigned to at most one store:**
$$
\sum_{j=1}^{5} x_{ij} \leq 1, \quad \forall i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}
$$
*(Note: Since the number of companies equals the number of stores (5=5) and every store must be assigned, the "at most" constraint effectively becomes "exactly one" in any feasible solution. However, $\leq 1$ is the standard formulation for assignment problems.)*

**3. Binary Constraints:**
$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall i \in I, j \in J
$$
