### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $I = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of origins (supply nodes).
*   $J = \{A, B, C\}$: Set of destinations (demand nodes).

**Parameters:**
*   $S_i$: Supply capacity at origin $i$.
    *   $S_1 = 20$
    *   $S_2 = 40$
    *   $S_3 = 30$
*   $D_j$: Demand at destination $j$.
    *   $D_A = 30$
    *   $D_B = 20$
    *   $D_C = 20$
*   $c_{ij}$: Unit transportation cost from origin $i$ to destination $j$.
    *   Row 1: $c_{1A}=1, c_{1B}=2, c_{1C}=2$
    *   Row 2: $c_{2A}=1, c_{2B}=4, c_{2C}=5$
    *   Row 3: $c_{3A}=2, c_{3B}=3, c_{3C}=3$
*   $h_i$: Unit storage cost at origin $i$ for unsupplied goods.
    *   $h_1 = 5$
    *   $h_2 = 4$
    *   $h_3 = 3$
*   $L_i$: Minimum shipment requirement from origin $i$.
    *   $L_1 = 0$ (Not specified, assume 0)
    *   $L_2 = 38$
    *   $L_3 = 27$

### 2. Decision Variables

*   $x_{ij} \ge 0$: Quantity shipped from origin $i$ to destination $j$.
*   $y_i \ge 0$: Quantity of goods stored (not shipped) at origin $i$.

*Note: Since this is an aggregate transportation problem with large scale (total supply 90, total demand 70), continuous variables are appropriate (LP relaxation).*

### 3. Objective Function

Minimize the total cost, which includes transportation costs for shipped goods and storage costs for unsupplied goods.

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij} + \sum_{i \in I} h_i y_i $$

Expanded form:
$$ Z = 1x_{1A} + 2x_{1B} + 2x_{1C} + 1x_{2A} + 4x_{2B} + 5x_{2C} + 2x_{3A} + 3x_{3B} + 3x_{3C} + 5y_1 + 4y_2 + 3y_3 $$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Supply Constraints (Flow Balance at Origins):**
The total amount shipped out from an origin plus the amount stored cannot exceed the available supply.
$$ \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} + y_i \le S_i, \quad \forall i \in I $$

*   Origin 1: $x_{1A} + x_{1B} + x_{1C} + y_1 \le 20$
*   Origin 2: $x_{2A} + x_{2B} + x_{2C} + y_2 \le 40$
*   Origin 3: $x_{3A} + x_{3B} + x_{3C} + y_3 \le 30$

**2. Demand Constraints (Flow Balance at Destinations):**
The total amount received at a destination must exactly meet the demand.
$$ \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} = D_j, \quad \forall j \in J $$

*   Dest A: $x_{1A} + x_{2A} + x_{3A} = 30$
*   Dest B: $x_{1B} + x_{2B} + x_{3B} = 20$
*   Dest C: $x_{1C} + x_{2C} + x_{3C} = 20$

**3. Minimum Shipment Constraints:**
The total quantity shipped from specific origins must meet the lower bounds.
$$ \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \ge L_i, \quad \forall i \in I \text{ where } L_i > 0 $$

*   Origin 2: $x_{2A} + x_{2B} + x_{2C} \ge 38$
*   Origin 3: $x_{3A} + x_{3B} + x_{3C} \ge 27$

**4. Non-negativity Constraints:**
$$ x_{ij} \ge 0, \quad \forall i \in I, j \in J $$
$$ y_i \ge 0, \quad \forall i \in I $$
