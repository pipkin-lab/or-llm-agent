### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $P = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of products.
  - $p=1$: Manuscript paper (unit: bundle)
  - $p=2$: Diaries (unit: dozen)
  - $p=3$: Exercise books (unit: box)

**Parameters:**
- $W_{max} = 100$: Total available workers.
- $R_{max} = 30,000$: Total available raw material (kg).
- $a_{p}$: Labor productivity (units per worker per month).
  - $a_1 = 30$ bundles/worker
  - $a_2 = 30$ dozens/worker
  - $a_3 = 30$ boxes/worker
- $r_{p}$: Raw material consumption (kg per unit).
  - $r_1 = 3 \frac{1}{3} = \frac{10}{3} \approx 3.333$ kg/bundle
  - $r_2 = 13 \frac{1}{3} = \frac{40}{3} \approx 13.333$ kg/dozen
  - $r_3 = 26 \frac{2}{3} = \frac{80}{3} \approx 26.667$ kg/box
- $\pi_{p}$: Profit per unit (yuan).
  - $\pi_1 = 2$ yuan/bundle
  - $\pi_2 = 3$ yuan/dozen
  - $\pi_3 = 1$ yuan/box

### 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_p$ be the production quantity of product $p$ in one month.

- $x_1 \ge 0$: Number of bundles of manuscript paper to produce.
- $x_2 \ge 0$: Number of dozens of diaries to produce.
- $x_3 \ge 0$: Number of boxes of exercise books to produce.

*Note on Integrality:* While physical units are discrete, in standard linear programming formulations for production planning with aggregate resources (100 workers, 30,000 kg), continuous variables are typically used to find the optimal allocation ratio. The problem asks for a "production plan" based on resource constraints, which is a classic LP problem. Unless "integer" is explicitly requested, we treat $x_p$ as continuous ($x_p \in \mathbb{R}^+$).

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize total monthly profit $Z$:

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = 2x_1 + 3x_2 + 1x_3
$$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**1. Labor Constraint:**
The total labor required must not exceed the available workforce.
Labor required for product $p$ is $\frac{x_p}{a_p}$ (workers).
$$
\frac{x_1}{30} + \frac{x_2}{30} + \frac{x_3}{30} \le 100
$$
Simplifying (multiply by 30):
$$
x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \le 3000
$$

**2. Raw Material Constraint:**
The total raw material consumed must not exceed the available supply.
$$
\frac{10}{3}x_1 + \frac{40}{3}x_2 + \frac{80}{3}x_3 \le 30,000
$$
Simplifying (multiply by 3/10 to clear denominators and scale, or just keep as is for clarity; let's multiply by 3 to remove fractions):
$$
10x_1 + 40x_2 + 80x_3 \le 90,000
$$
Dividing by 10:
$$
x_1 + 4x_2 + 8x_3 \le 9,000
$$

**3. Non-negativity Constraints:**
$$
x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0
$$

---

### Final Mathematical Model

**Maximize:**
$$ Z = 2x_1 + 3x_2 + x_3 $$

**Subject to:**
$$ x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \le 3000 \quad \text{(Labor)} $$
$$ x_1 + 4x_2 + 8x_3 \le 9000 \quad \text{(Raw Material)} $$
$$ x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0 $$
