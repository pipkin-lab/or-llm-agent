# Farm Production Planning Model

## Model Type: MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Program)

**Rationale:** The number of dairy cows and chickens must be integer values (discrete physical units). Crop areas are continuous. The objective and all constraints are linear.

## Operational Constraints

### 1. Land Constraint
Total land used by crops plus forage for cows must not exceed total land:

$$x_{soy} + x_{corn} + x_{wheat} + 1.5\,N_{cow} \leq 100$$

### 2. Fund Constraint
Total investment in animals must not exceed available funds:

$$400\,N_{cow} + 3\,N_{ch} \leq 15{,}000$$

### 3. Autumn/Winter Labor Constraint
Total AW labor used by crops and animals plus unused AW labor equals total AW labor:

$$20\,x_{soy} + 35\,x_{corn} + 10\,x_{wheat} + 100\,N_{cow} + 0.6\,N_{ch} + u_{AW} = 3{,}500$$

### 4. Spring/Summer Labor Constraint
Total SS labor used by crops and animals plus unused SS labor equals total SS labor:

$$50\,x_{soy} + 75\,x_{corn} + 40\,x_{wheat} + 50\,N_{cow} + 0.3\,N_{ch} + u_{SS} = 4{,}000$$

### 5. Chicken House Capacity

$$N_{ch} \leq 3{,}000$$

### 6. Cow Shed Capacity

$$N_{cow} \leq 32$$

### 7. Non-negativity and Integrality

$$x_{soy},\, x_{corn},\, x_{wheat} \geq 0 \quad (\text{continuous})$$
$$N_{cow},\, N_{ch} \geq 0 \quad (\text{integer})$$
$$u_{AW},\, u_{SS} \geq 0 \quad (\text{continuous})$$

## Feasibility Sanity Check

- **Land:** Even with 32 cows, forage uses $32 \times 1.5 = 48$ hm², leaving 52 hm² for crops. Feasible.
- **Funds:** 32 cows cost $32 \times 400 = 12{,}800$ yuan, leaving 2,200 yuan for chickens (up to 733 chickens). Feasible.
- **AW Labor (no production):** $u_{AW} = 3{,}500$, income = $1.8 \times 3{,}500 = 6{,}300$ yuan. Feasible.
- **SS Labor (no production):** $u_{SS} = 4{,}000$, income = $2.1 \times 4{,}000 = 8{,}400$ yuan. Feasible.
- **Combined labor check (32 cows + 733 chickens, no crops):**
  - AW: $32 \times 100 + 733 \times 0.6 = 3{,}200 + 439.8 = 3{,}639.8 > 3{,}500$ → infeasible without reducing animals or adding crops differently, but the solver will find a feasible mix. The constraint system is not structurally infeasible.
- The model is **feasible** (e.g., $x=0, N_{cow}=0, N_{ch}=0, u_{AW}=3500, u_{SS}=4000$ is a feasible solution).
