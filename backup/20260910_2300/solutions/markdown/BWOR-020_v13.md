## Model Type
MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Program)

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $K = \{A, B, C, D, E\}$: Set of customers, indexed by $k$
- $J = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: Set of trucks, indexed by $j$

**Parameters:**
- $d_k$: Demand (units of goods) for customer $k$:
  - $d_A = 1, d_B = 2, d_C = 3, d_D = 4, d_E = 8$
- $C_j$: Capacity (units) of truck $j$:
  - $C_1 = 2, C_2 = 6, C_3 = 8, C_4 = 11$
- $c_j$: Cost of using truck $j$ [NOT_PROVIDED]

**Conflict Sets:**
- Trucks cannot serve both A and C.
- Trucks cannot serve both B and D.

## Decision Variables

- $x_{k,j} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable. Equals 1 if truck $j$ delivers to customer $k$, 0 otherwise.
- $y_j \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable. Equals 1 if truck $j$ is used (assigned to deliver to at least one customer), 0 otherwise.

## Objective Function

Minimize the total cost of the trucks used:

$$ \text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{j \in J} c_j y_j $$

## Operational Constraints

**1. Demand Satisfaction (Set Covering/Assignment):**
Each customer's demand must be met by at least one truck. Since each truck is used only once and we are assigning delivery tasks, we assume a truck assigned to a customer handles the full demand for that customer.

$$ \sum_{j \in J} x_{k,j} \ge 1 \quad \forall k \in K $$

**2. Truck Capacity:**
The total demand of customers assigned to a truck cannot exceed its capacity.

$$ \sum_{k \in K} d_k x_{k,j} \le C_j y_j \quad \forall j \in J $$

**3. Linking Constraints:**
A truck is considered "used" ($y_j=1$) if and only if it is assigned to at least one customer.

$$ x_{k,j} \le y_j \quad \forall k \in K, \forall j \in J $$

*(Note: The implication $y_j=0 \implies x_{k,j}=0$ is covered by the above. The implication $x_{k,j}=1 \implies y_j=1$ is also covered. The reverse $y_j=1 \implies \exists x_{k,j}=1$ is not strictly enforced by these linear inequalities alone, but since the objective minimizes cost and $c_j > 0$, the solver will naturally set $y_j=0$ for any truck with no assignments.)*

**4. Conflict Constraints (A and C):**
A single truck cannot be assigned to both Customer A and Customer C.

$$ x_{A,j} + x_{C,j} \le 1 \quad \forall j \in J $$

**5. Conflict Constraints (B and D):**
A single truck cannot be assigned to both Customer B and Customer D.

$$ x_{B,j} + x_{D,j} \le 1 \quad \forall j \in J $$

**6. Variable Domains:**
$$ x_{k,j} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall k \in K, j \in J $$
$$ y_j \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall j \in J $$
