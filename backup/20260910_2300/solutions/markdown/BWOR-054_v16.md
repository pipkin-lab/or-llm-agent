## Decision Variables

**Primary continuous variables:**
- $x_{i,t} \geq 0$, **continuous**: Number of hours student $i$ works on day $t$
  - Domain: $0 \leq x_{i,t} \leq a_{i,t}$ for all $i \in I, t \in T$

**Binary scheduling variables:**
- $y_{i,t} \in \{0, 1\}$, **binary**: Equals 1 if student $i$ is scheduled for duty on day $t$, 0 otherwise
  - Domain: $y_{i,t} \in \{0, 1\}$ for all $i \in I, t \in T$

## Operational Constraints

### 1. Coverage Constraint (Exactly one student on duty at all times)
Since there must be exactly one student on duty at all times and the lab is open 16 hours daily, the total hours assigned on each day must equal 16:

$$\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,t} = 16, \quad \forall t \in T$$

### 2. Maximum Daily Work Hours (Linking $x$ and $y$)
A student can only work on a day if they are scheduled for that day:

$$x_{i,t} \leq a_{i,t} \cdot y_{i,t}, \quad \forall i \in I, \forall t \in T$$

This ensures $x_{i,t} = 0$ if $y_{i,t} = 0$, and $x_{i,t} \leq a_{i,t}$ if $y_{i,t} = 1$.

### 3. Minimum Weekly Hours – Undergraduate Students
Each undergraduate student must work at least 8 hours per week:

$$\sum_{t \in T} x_{i,t} \geq 8, \quad \forall i \in I_{ug} = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$$

### 4. Minimum Weekly Hours – Graduate Students
Each graduate student must work at least 7 hours per week:

$$\sum_{t \in T} x_{i,t} \geq 7, \quad \forall i \in I_{grad} = \{5, 6\}$$

### 5. Maximum Duty Shifts Per Student Per Week
Each student can be assigned for duty no more than twice a week:

$$\sum_{t \in T} y_{i,t} \leq 2, \quad \forall i \in I$$

### 6. Maximum Students Scheduled Per Day
No more than 3 students can be scheduled for duty per day:

$$\sum_{i \in I} y_{i,t} \leq 3, \quad \forall t \in T$$

### 7. Non-negativity and Domain Bounds

$$x_{i,t} \geq 0, \quad \forall i \in I, \forall t \in T$$

$$y_{i,t} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall i \in I, \forall t \in T$$

The upper bounds on $x_{i,t}$ are already enforced by Constraint 2 combined with $a_{i,t}$ values (which include 0 for infeasible days).
