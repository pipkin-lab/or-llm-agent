To formulate the mathematical model for Jinyuan Company's production planning problem, we must first analyze the structure of the problem. This is a multi-objective optimization problem with hierarchical priorities (Goal Programming).

### 1. Problem Analysis & Parameter Extraction

**Sets:**
*   $P = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of products.
*   $T = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of production stages (periods).

**Parameters (from Tables 4-16 and 4-17):**
*   **Demand ($D_{p,t}$):**
    *   $D_{1,1}=500, D_{2,1}=750, D_{3,1}=900$
    *   $D_{1,2}=680, D_{2,2}=800, D_{3,2}=800$
    *   $D_{1,3}=800, D_{2,3}=950, D_{3,3}=1000$
*   **Equipment Hours per unit ($a_{p}$):**
    *   $a_1 = 2.0, a_2 = 1.0, a_3 = 3.1$
*   **Working Capital per unit ($b_{p}$):**
    *   $b_1 = 40, b_2 = 20, b_3 = 55$
*   **Resource Limits per Stage:**
    *   Max Equipment Hours ($H_{max}$): 5000 hours
    *   Max Working Capital ($C_{max}$): 93,000 yuan

**Objectives (Prioritized):**
1.  **Priority 1:** Minimize stockouts. Importance of Product 3 is twice that of Products 1 and 2. This implies weighted deviation minimization.
2.  **Priority 2:** Minimize overloading of processing equipment. This implies minimizing the positive deviation (excess) of equipment usage beyond the limit.
3.  **Priority 3:** Ensure working capital usage does not exceed the limit. This is a hard constraint or the lowest priority goal. Given the phrasing "Ensure that...", it is often treated as a hard constraint in strict hierarchy, or the lowest priority soft constraint. However, "Ensure" usually implies a hard constraint in standard OR terminology unless specified as a "goal to be met as closely as possible". Let's look closer. "Minimize overloading... Ensure capital... does not exceed". Usually, "ensure" means $Capital \le Limit$. If it were a goal, it would say "minimize excess capital". Therefore, Working Capital will be modeled as a **Hard Constraint**.

**Inventory Logic:**
*   Initial Inventory = 0.
*   Final Inventory = 0.
*   Let $x_{p,t}$ be the production quantity of product $p$ in stage $t$.
*   Let $I_{p,t}$ be the inventory of product $p$ at the end of stage $t$.
*   Let $S_{p,t}$ be the shortage (stockout) of product $p$ in stage $t$.
*   Balance Equation: $I_{p,t-1} + x_{p,t} + S_{p,t} = D_{p,t} + I_{p,t}$? No, standard balance is:
    $I_{p,t} = I_{p,t-1} + x_{p,t} - D_{p,t} + S_{p,t}$?
    Actually, it's easier to define deviations from demand.
    Let $u_{p,t}$ be the under-fulfillment (stockout) and $v_{p,t}$ be the over-fulfillment (excess inventory carried over or wasted, but since final inv is 0 and we want to meet demand, we track stockouts specifically).
    
    Let's refine the inventory/stockout variables.
    Let $x_{p,t}$ be production.
    Let $I_{p,t}$ be ending inventory.
    Constraint: $I_{p,t} = I_{p,t-1} + x_{p,t} - D_{p,t}$ is only valid if $I_{p,t} \ge 0$.
    If we allow stockouts, we introduce a variable $s_{p,t}$ (shortage).
    Equation: $I_{p,t} + s_{p,t} = I_{p,t-1} + x_{p,t} - D_{p,t}$ is incorrect.
    
    Correct Mass Balance with Shortage:
    $I_{p,t} = I_{p,t-1} + x_{p,t} - (D_{p,t} - s_{p,t})$
    Where $s_{p,t}$ is the unmet demand (stockout) in period $t$.
    Rearranging: $I_{p,t} - s_{p,t} = I_{p,t-1} + x_{p,t} - D_{p,t}$
    With $I_{p,0} = 0$ and $I_{p,3} = 0$.
    Also, $I_{p,t} \ge 0, s_{p,t} \ge 0$.
    Note: It is generally not optimal to have both $I_{p,t} > 0$ and $s_{p,t} > 0$ simultaneously in a standard goal programming context minimizing stockouts, but the linear constraints allow it. The objective function minimizing $s_{p,t}$ will drive one of them to zero naturally if costs are aligned. Here, we minimize $s_{p,t}$.

    **Priority 1 Objective:** Minimize weighted stockouts.
    Weights: $w_1 = 1, w_2 = 1, w_3 = 2$.
    $Z_1 = \sum_{t=1}^{3} (1 \cdot s_{1,t} + 1 \cdot s_{2,t} + 2 \cdot s_{3,t})$

    **Priority 2 Objective:** Minimize equipment overloading.
    Let $E_t$ be equipment usage in stage $t$: $E_t = \sum_{p=1}^3 a_p x_{p,t}$.
    Limit $H_{max} = 5000$.
    Let $e^+_t$ be the excess (overload) and $e^-_t$ be the underutilization.
    $E_t + e^-_t - e^+_t = 5000$.
    We want to minimize overload, so we minimize $\sum e^+_t$.
    Note: Usually, we don't care about underutilization unless specified. The problem says "Minimize overloading", so we only penalize $e^+_t$.

    **Priority 3 Constraint:** Working Capital.
    $W_t = \sum_{p=1}^3 b_p x_{p,t} \le 93000$.

### 2. Model Formulation

**Model Type:** Linear Goal Programming (LP with lexicographic objective)

**Decision Variables:**
1.  $x_{p,t} \ge 0$: Quantity of product $p$ produced in stage $t$.
2.  $I_{p,t} \ge 0$: Ending inventory of product $p$ at stage $t$.
3.  $s_{p,t} \ge 0$: Stockout (unmet demand) of product $p$ in stage $t$.
4.  $e^+_t \ge 0$: Excess equipment hours (overload) in stage $t$.
5.  $e^-_t \ge 0$: Unused equipment hours (slack) in stage $t$.

**Parameters:**
*   $D_{p,t}$: Demand as listed in Table 4-16.
*   $a_p$: Equipment hours per unit as listed in Table 4-17 ($2.0, 1.0, 3.1$).
*   $b_p$: Capital per unit as listed in Table 4-17 ($40, 20, 55$).
*   $H_{max} = 5000$.
*   $C_{max} = 93000$.
*   Weights for Priority 1: $w_1=1, w_2=1, w_3=2$.

**Objective Function (Lexicographic):**
Minimize $Z = [Z_1, Z_2]$
Where:
*   $Z_1 = \sum_{t=1}^{3} (1 \cdot s_{1,t} + 1 \cdot s_{2,t} + 2 \cdot s_{3,t})$
*   $Z_2 = \sum_{t=1}^{3} e^+_t$
*(Note: Priority 3 is a hard constraint, so it is not in the objective function but in the constraints. If it were a soft goal, it would be $Z_3 = \sum f^+_t$ where $f^+_t$ is capital excess, but "Ensure... does not exceed" implies $Capital \le Limit$.)*

**Constraints:**

1.  **Inventory Balance (for each $p \in P, t \in T$):**
    $$I_{p,t} - s_{p,t} = I_{p,t-1} + x_{p,t} - D_{p,t}$$
    With $I_{p,0} = 0$ and $I_{p,3} = 0$.

2.  **Equipment Capacity (for each $t \in T$):**
    $$\sum_{p=1}^{3} a_p x_{p,t} + e^-_t - e^+_t = 5000$$
    (We minimize $e^+_t$ in $Z_2$. $e^-_t$ is free to absorb underutilization).

3.  **Working Capital Limit (Hard Constraint, for each $t \in T$):**
    $$\sum_{p=1}^{3} b_p x_{p,t} \le 93000$$

4.  **Non-negativity:**
    $$x_{p,t}, I_{p,t}, s_{p,t}, e^+_t, e^-_t \ge 0$$

### 3. Mathematical Model Structure

**Model Type:** LP (Linear Programming) - specifically Lexicographic Goal Programming.

**Index Sets:**
*   $P = \{1, 2, 3\}$ (Products)
*   $T = \{1, 2, 3\}$ (Stages)

**Parameters:**
*   $D_{1,1}=500, D_{2,1}=750, D_{3,1}=900$
*   $D_{1,2}=680, D_{2,2}=800, D_{3,2}=800$
*   $D_{1,3}=800, D_{2,3}=950, D_{3,3}=1000$
*   $a_1=2.0, a_2=1.0, a_3=3.1$
*   $b_1=40, b_2=20, b_3=55$
*   $H_{max}=5000$
*   $C_{max}=93000$
*   Weights $w_1=1, w_2=1, w_3=2$

**Decision Variables:**
*   $x_{p,t} \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ : Production quantity of product $p$ in stage $t$.
*   $I_{p,t} \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ : Ending inventory of product $p$ at stage $t$.
*   $s_{p,t} \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ : Stockout quantity of product $p$ in stage $t$.
*   $e^+_t \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ : Equipment overload in stage $t$.
*   $e^-_t \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ : Equipment idle time in stage $t$.

**Objective Function:**
Minimize lexicographically:
1.  $Z_1 = \sum_{t=1}^{3} (1 \cdot s_{1,t} + 1 \cdot s_{2,t} + 2 \cdot s_{3,t})$
2.  $Z_2 = \sum_{t=1}^{3} e^+_t$

**Constraints:**

1.  **Inventory Balance:**
    For $t=1$:
    $$I_{p,1} - s_{p,1} = x_{p,1} - D_{p,1} \quad \forall p \in P$$
    For $t=2$:
    $$I_{p,2} - s_{p,2} = I_{p,1} + x_{p,2} - D_{p,2} \quad \forall p \in P$$
    For $t=3$:
    $$I_{p,3} - s_{p,3} = I_{p,2} + x_{p,3} - D_{p,3} \quad \forall p \in P$$
    Boundary Conditions:
    $$I_{p,0} = 0 \quad \forall p \in P$$
    $$I_{p,3} = 0 \quad \forall p \in P$$
    *(Note: The constraint for $t=3$ becomes $0 - s_{p,3} = I_{p,2} + x_{p,3} - D_{p,3} \implies s_{p,3} = I_{p,2} + x_{p,3} - D_{p,3}$)*

2.  **Equipment Capacity:**
    For $t=1$:
    $$2.0 x_{1,1} + 1.0 x_{2,1} + 3.1 x_{3,1} + e^-_1 - e^+_1 = 5000$$
    For $t=2$:
    $$2.0 x_{1,2} + 1.0 x_{2,2} + 3.1 x_{3,2} + e^-_2 - e^+_2 = 5000$$
    For $t=3$:
    $$2.0 x_{1,3} + 1.0 x_{2,3} + 3.1 x_{3,3} + e^-_3 - e^+_3 = 5000$$

3.  **Working Capital Constraint:**
    For $t=1$:
    $$40 x_{1,1} + 20 x_{2,1} + 55 x_{3,1} \le 93000$$
    For $t=2$:
    $$40 x_{1,2} + 20 x_{2,2} + 55 x_{3,2} \le 93000$$
    For $t=3$:
    $$40 x_{1,3} + 20 x_{2,3} + 55 x_{3,3} \le 93000$$

4.  **Non-negativity:**
    $$x_{p,t}, I_{p,t}, s_{p,t}, e^+_t, e^-_t \ge 0 \quad \forall p \in P, t \in T$$
