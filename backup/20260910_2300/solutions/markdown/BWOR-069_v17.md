# Integer Programming Formulation: Product Shipment Optimization

## Model Type Declaration
**MILP** (Mixed Integer Linear Program). Since products are shipped in discrete "pieces" (units), the decision variables are integer. The objective and constraints are linear.

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Set:**
- $I = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of product types.

**Parameters (from Table 7-8):**

| Parameter | Description | Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $w_i$ | Weight of product $i$ (tons/piece) | $w_1 = 2, \quad w_2 = 3, \quad w_3 = 4$ |
| $p_i$ | Profit of product $i$ (yuan/piece) | $p_1 = 100, \quad p_2 = 140, \quad p_3 = 180$ |
| $W_{max}$ | Total transportation capacity limit (tons) | $10$ |

## Decision Variables

- $x_i$: Number of pieces of product $i$ to be shipped.
  - **Domain:** $x_i \in \mathbb{Z}^+$ (Non-negative integers) for $i \in I$.
  - *Note: Discrete physical units (pieces) require integer variables.*

## Objective Function

Maximize the total profit obtained from shipping the products.

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} p_i x_i $$

Substituting the parameter values:

$$ Z = 100 x_1 + 140 x_2 + 180 x_3 $$

## Operational Constraints

**1. Transportation Capacity Constraint:**
The total weight of all shipped products must not exceed the total transportation capacity of 10 tons.

$$ \sum_{i \in I} w_i x_i \le W_{max} $$

Substituting the parameter values:

$$ 2 x_1 + 3 x_2 + 4 x_3 \le 10 $$

**2. Non-negativity and Integrality Constraints:**

$$ x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 0 \quad \text{and} \quad x_1, x_2, x_3 \in \mathbb{Z} $$

---

### Summary of the Final Model

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Maximize} \quad & Z = 100 x_1 + 140 x_2 + 180 x_3 \\
\text{Subject to:} \quad & 2 x_1 + 3 x_2 + 4 x_3 \le 10 \\
& x_1, x_2, x_3 \in \mathbb{Z}^+
\end{align*}
$$
