# Model Type: MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Program)

## Analysis

This is an **assembly line balancing/assignment problem** where 5 workers are assigned to 5 stations in a **serial** system. Since it is a serial assembly line, the overall production capacity is determined by the **bottleneck** (the slowest station), not the sum of station rates. The goal is to maximize the minimum station throughput.

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $W = \{A, B, C, D, E\}$: Set of workers, indexed by $w$
- $S = \{I, II, III, IV, V\}$: Set of stations, indexed by $s$

**Parameters (Efficiency in pieces/min):**

| $e_{w,s}$ | $s=I$ | $s=II$ | $s=III$ | $s=IV$ | $s=V$ |
|:---------:|:-----:|:------:|:-------:|:------:|:-----:|
| $w=A$    | 2     | 3      | 4       | 1      | 7     |
| $w=B$    | 3     | 4      | 2       | 5      | 6     |
| $w=C$    | 2     | 5      | 3       | 4      | 1     |
| $w=D$    | 5     | 2      | 3       | 2      | 5     |
| $w=E$    | 3     | 7      | 6       | 2      | 4     |

## Decision Variables

- $x_{w,s} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable, equals 1 if worker $w$ is assigned to station $s$, 0 otherwise.
- $Z \geq 0$: Continuous variable representing the overall production capacity (bottleneck rate) of the assembly line in pieces/min.

## Objective Function

$$\max Z$$

## Operational Constraints

**1. Each worker is assigned to exactly one station:**
$$\sum_{s \in S} x_{w,s} = 1, \quad \forall w \in W$$

**2. Each station is assigned exactly one worker:**
$$\sum_{w \in W} x_{w,s} = 1, \quad \forall s \in S$$

**3. Bottleneck (throughput) constraints — the overall capacity $Z$ cannot exceed the rate of any station:**
$$\sum_{w \in W} e_{w,s} \cdot x_{w,s} \geq Z, \quad \forall s \in S$$

**4. Non-negativity and integrality bounds:**
$$x_{w,s} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall w \in W, \forall s \in S$$
$$Z \geq 0$$

## Feasibility Sanity Check

- Total workers = 5, Total stations = 5 → perfect matching is always feasible.
- The bottleneck constraint ensures $Z$ is bounded above by the maximum possible minimum station rate.
- Since all efficiencies are positive integers, $Z$ will take a value from the set of possible station rates.
- The model is a standard assignment problem with a bottleneck objective, which is well-posed and always feasible.
