# Operations Research Model: Factory Production Planning with Temporary Workers

## Model Type: LP (Linear Program)

## Problem Analysis

The factory produces three products using labor and raw material (white base paper). We need to determine the optimal production plan and whether to hire temporary workers (and how many).

**Key Parameters Extracted:**
- Regular workers available: 100
- Monthly raw material supply: 30,000 kg
- Labor productivity (per person per month):
  - Manuscript paper: 30 bundles
  - Diaries: 30 dozens
  - Exercise books: 30 boxes
- Raw material consumption:
  - Manuscript paper: $3\frac{1}{3} = \frac{10}{3}$ kg per bundle
  - Diaries: $13\frac{1}{3} = \frac{40}{3}$ kg per dozen
  - Exercise books: $26\frac{2}{3} = \frac{80}{3}$ kg per box
- Profit per unit:
  - Manuscript paper: 2 yuan per bundle
  - Diaries: 3 yuan per dozen
  - Exercise books: 1 yuan per box
- Temporary worker wage: 40 yuan per person per month

**Key Insight on Labor:** Each worker produces 30 units of any single product type per month. Therefore, producing $x_i$ units of product $i$ requires $x_i / 30$ person-months of labor for that product. Total labor required = $(x_1 + x_2 + x_3)/30$ person-months.

Since the productivity is identical across all products (30 units/person/month), the total labor needed depends only on total output. Let $L$ be total person-months of labor available. Then:

$$x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \leq 30L$$

where $L = 100 + T$ (regular + temporary workers).

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
- $i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$: Product index
  - $i=1$: Manuscript paper
  - $i=2$: Diaries
  - $i=3$: Exercise books

## Decision Variables

| Variable | Domain | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| $x_1$ | $x_1 \geq 0$ | Monthly production of manuscript paper (bundles) |
| $x_2$ | $x_2 \geq 0$ | Monthly production of diaries (dozens) |
| $x_3$ | $x_3 \geq 0$ | Monthly production of exercise books (boxes) |
| $T$ | $T \geq 0$ | Number of temporary workers hired |

**Note on variable types:** Production quantities can be fractional in the LP relaxation (bundles, dozens, boxes are divisible in aggregate planning). $T$ represents number of workers; in practice this should be integer, but we first solve the LP relaxation. If the optimal $T$ is fractional, we round and verify. Given the structure, the LP solution will guide the integer decision.

Actually, since $T$ represents discrete persons, we should consider $T$ as a continuous variable in the LP to find the optimal *level* of labor, and then interpret the result. The problem asks "how many is optimal," implying we need a specific number. Let us formulate with $T$ continuous first, then examine integrality.

## Objective Function

**Maximize net profit** (revenue profit minus cost of temporary workers):

$$\max \quad Z = 2x_1 + 3x_2 + 1x_3 - 40T$$

## Operational Constraints

### 1. Raw Material Constraint
Total raw material consumed cannot exceed monthly supply:

$$\frac{10}{3}x_1 + \frac{40}{3}x_2 + \frac{80}{3}x_3 \leq 30000$$

Simplifying (multiply by 3):
$$10x_1 + 40x_2 + 80x_3 \leq 90000$$

### 2. Labor Constraint
Total production must not exceed the total labor capacity. Each unit of any product requires $1/30$ person-months. Total labor available is $(100 + T)$ person-months:

$$x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \leq 30(100 + T) = 3000 + 30T$$

### 3. Non-negativity
$$x_1, x_2, x_3, T \geq 0$$

## Complete Formulation

$$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\max \quad & Z = 2x_1 + 3x_2 + x_3 - 40T \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & 10x_1 + 40x_2 + 80x_3 \leq 90000 \quad \text{(raw material)} \\
& x_1 + x_2 + x_3 - 30T \leq 3000 \quad \text{(labor)} \\
& x_1, x_2, x_3, T \geq 0
\end{aligned}
}$$

## Feasibility Sanity Check

- If $T=0$: max total production = 3000 units. Raw material limit: e.g., all manuscript paper: $x_1 = 90000/10 = 9000$ bundles, but labor caps at 3000. All diaries: labor cap 3000, material = $40 \times 3000 = 120000 > 90000$, infeasible. So binding constraint interaction matters.
- The model is feasible (e.g., $x_1=x_2=x_3=T=0$).

## Interpretation for Answering the Question

The LP will determine the optimal $T^*$. If $T^* > 0$, the factory should hire temporary workers, and the optimal number is $\lceil T^* \rceil$ or the integer value (verify by checking $T^*$ and $T^*+1$ or $T^*$ and $T^*-1$ in the full problem). If $T^* = 0$, no temporary workers should be hired.
