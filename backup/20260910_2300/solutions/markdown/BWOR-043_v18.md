# Transportation Problem Formulation

## MANDATORY COST INVENTORY (PRE-MODELING STEP)

The problem provides a unit transportation cost matrix $c_{ij}$ for all origin-destination pairs. Every entry in the cost table is an objective coefficient:

| | B1 | B2 | B3 | B4 | B5 | B6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 2 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 5 |
| A2 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| A3 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 1 |
| A4 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 |

All 24 cost parameters are explicitly provided. No `[NOT_PROVIDED]` values.

## Feasibility Sanity Check

- **Total Supply:** $50 + 40 + 60 + 31 = 181$
- **Total Demand:** $30 + 50 + 20 + 40 + 30 + 11 = 181$

Total supply equals total demand. This is a **balanced** transportation problem. Standard equality constraints apply on both supply and demand sides.

---

## Model Type

**LP** (Linear Program). All variables are continuous (transportation quantities are not inherently discrete; the standard transportation problem is solved as an LP). The constraint matrix is totally unimodular, so an LP solver will yield integer optimal solutions given integer data.

---

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $I = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ — set of origins (A1, A2, A3, A4)
- $J = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$ — set of destinations (B1, B2, B3, B4, B5, B6)

**Parameters (all numeric values extracted from Table 3-8):**

Supply $s_i$ for each origin $i \in I$:
$$s_1 = 50, \quad s_2 = 40, \quad s_3 = 60, \quad s_4 = 31$$

Demand $d_j$ for each destination $j \in J$:
$$d_1 = 30, \quad d_2 = 50, \quad d_3 = 20, \quad d_4 = 40, \quad d_5 = 30, \quad d_6 = 11$$

Unit transportation cost $c_{ij}$ for each $(i, j) \in I \times J$:

| $i \backslash j$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 5 |
| 2 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 3 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 1 |
| 4 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 |

---

## Decision Variables

$$x_{ij} \geq 0, \quad \forall i \in I, \; j \in J$$

where $x_{ij}$ = amount shipped from origin $i$ to destination $j$.

**Domain:** Continuous, non-negative.

---

## Objective Function

**Minimize** total transportation cost:

$$\min \sum_{i=1}^{4} \sum_{j=1}^{6} c_{ij} \, x_{ij}$$

Expanded:

$$\min \; 2x_{11} + 1x_{12} + 3x_{13} + 3x_{14} + 3x_{15} + 5x_{16} + 4x_{21} + 2x_{22} + 2x_{23} + 4x_{24} + 4x_{25} + 4x_{26} + 3x_{31} + 5x_{32} + 4x_{33} + 2x_{34} + 4x_{35} + 1x_{36} + 4x_{41} + 2x_{42} + 2x_{43} + 1x_{44} + 2x_{45} + 2x_{46}$$

**Objective Parameter Cross-Reference:** All 24 cost coefficients $c_{ij}$ are matched to explicit values in Table 3-8. No `[NOT_PROVIDED]` parameters.

---

## Operational Constraints

**Supply Constraints** (each origin ships exactly its available supply, since total supply = total demand):

$$\sum_{j=1}^{6} x_{ij} = s_i, \quad \forall i \in I$$

Specifically:
$$x_{11} + x_{12} + x_{13} + x_{14} + x_{15} + x_{16} = 50$$
$$x_{21} + x_{22} + x_{23} + x_{24} + x_{25} + x_{26} = 40$$
$$x_{31} + x_{32} + x_{33} + x_{34} + x_{35} + x_{36} = 60$$
$$x_{41} + x_{42} + x_{43} + x_{44} + x_{45} + x_{46} = 31$$

**Demand Constraints** (each destination receives exactly its required demand):

$$\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_{ij} = d_j, \quad \forall j \in J$$

Specifically:
$$x_{11} + x_{21} + x_{31} + x_{41} = 30$$
$$x_{12} + x_{22} + x_{32} + x_{42} = 50$$
$$x_{13} + x_{23} + x_{33} + x_{43} = 20$$
$$x_{14} + x_{24} + x_{34} + x_{44} = 40$$
$$x_{15} + x_{25} + x_{35} + x_{45} = 30$$
$$x_{16} + x_{26} + x_{36} + x_{46} = 11$$

**Non-negativity:**
$$x_{ij} \geq 0, \quad \forall i \in I, \; j \in J$$
