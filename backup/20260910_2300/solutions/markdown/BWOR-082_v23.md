# Model Type: MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Sets:**
- $W = \{A, B, C, D, E\}$: Set of workshops, indexed by $i, k$
- $L = \{\text{Urban}, \alpha, \beta\}$: Set of possible locations, indexed by $j, l$

**Parameters:**

**Relocation Savings $S_{i,l}$ (10,000 yuan/year):**
| | $\alpha$ | $\beta$ |
|---|---|---|
| $A$ | $100$ | $100$ |
| $B$ | $150$ | $200$ |
| $C$ | $100$ | $150$ |
| $D$ | $200$ | $150$ |
| $E$ | $50$ | $150$ |

Note: Savings from relocating to Urban area are $0$ for all workshops.

**Transport Volume $C_{ik}$ (t/year):**
| | $B$ | $C$ | $D$ | $E$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $A$ | $0$ | $1000$ | $1500$ | $0$ |
| $B$ | $-$ | $1400$ | $1200$ | $0$ |
| $C$ | $-$ | $-$ | $0$ | $2000$ |
| $D$ | $-$ | $-$ | $-$ | $700$ |

Note: $C_{ik} = C_{ki}$ (symmetric), and $C_{ii} = 0$. Only pairs with non-zero volume are: $(A,C)=1000$, $(A,D)=1500$, $(B,C)=1400$, $(B,D)=1200$, $(C,E)=2000$, $(D,E)=700$.

**Transport Cost per Ton $D_{j,l}$ (yuan/t):**
| | $\alpha$ | $\beta$ | Urban |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\alpha$ | $500$ | $1400$ | $1300$ |
| $\beta$ | $-$ | $500$ | $900$ |
| Urban | $-$ | $-$ | $1000$ |

Note: $D_{j,l} = D_{l,j}$ (symmetric). Same-location cost ($D_{j,j}$) is the intra-location transport cost.

**Capacity Constraint:**
- $M = 3$: Maximum number of workshops allowed in any single location.

**Unit Conversion:**
- Savings are in 10,000 yuan/year.
- Transport costs are in yuan/t.
- To unify units, convert savings to yuan/year: multiply by $10,000$.

## Decision Variables

- $x_{i,l} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable, equals 1 if workshop $i$ is located at location $l$, 0 otherwise.

**Domain:** $x_{i,l} \in \{0, 1\}$ for all $i \in W, l \in L$.

## Objective Function

**MINIMIZE** total annual cost = (total transport cost) − (total relocation savings)

Expressed in yuan/year:

$$
\min \sum_{i \in W} \sum_{k \in W} \sum_{j \in L} \sum_{l \in L} C_{ik} \cdot D_{j,l} \cdot x_{i,j} \cdot x_{k,l} \;-\; 10000 \cdot \sum_{i \in W} \sum_{l \in \{\alpha, \beta\}} S_{i,l} \cdot x_{i,l}
$$

Since $x_{i,j} \cdot x_{k,l}$ is a product of binaries, we **linearize** using auxiliary variables:

- $y_{i,k,j,l} \in \{0,1\}$: Binary variable, equals 1 if workshop $i$ is at location $j$ AND workshop $k$ is at location $l$, 0 otherwise.

**Linearized Objective (yuan/year):**

$$
\min \sum_{i \in W} \sum_{k \in W} \sum_{j \in L} \sum_{l \in L} C_{ik} \cdot D_{j,l} \cdot y_{i,k,j,l} \;-\; 10000 \cdot \sum_{i \in W} \sum_{l \in \{\alpha, \beta\}} S_{i,l} \cdot x_{i,l}
$$

## Operational Constraints

### 1. Location Assignment (Each workshop assigned to exactly one location)
$$
\sum_{l \in L} x_{i,l} = 1, \quad \forall i \in W
$$

### 2. Capacity Constraint (No more than 3 workshops per location)
$$
\sum_{i \in W} x_{i,l} \leq 3, \quad \forall l \in L
$$

### 3. Linearization Constraints for $y_{i,k,j,l}$
For all $i, k \in W$ and $j, l \in L$:

$$
y_{i,k,j,l} \leq x_{i,j}
$$
$$
y_{i,k,j,l} \leq x_{k,l}
$$
$$
y_{i,k,j,l} \geq x_{i,j} + x_{k,l} - 1
$$
$$
y_{i,k,j,l} \geq 0
$$

Additionally, for $i = k$, since a workshop cannot be in two places simultaneously, $y_{i,i,j,l} = 0$ for $j \neq l$, and $y_{i,i,j,j} = x_{i,j}$. To simplify, we can restrict the summation to $i < k$ and exploit symmetry $C_{ik} = C_{ki}$, but the full formulation above is valid.

### 4. Variable Domains
$$
x_{i,l} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall i \in W, l \in L
$$
$$
y_{i,k,j,l} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall i, k \in W, j, l \in L
$$

## Feasibility Sanity Check

- Total workshops: 5
- Locations: 3 (Urban, $\alpha$, $\beta$)
- Max per location: 3
- Minimum distribution: e.g., 2+2+1 is feasible. No inherent infeasibility.
