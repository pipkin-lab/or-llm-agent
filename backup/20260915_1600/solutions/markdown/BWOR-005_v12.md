**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $C = \{A, B, C\}$: Set of cargo types.
*   $K = \{F, M, R\}$: Set of compartments (Front, Middle, Rear).

**Parameters (from Tables 1-24 and 1-25):**

*   **Compartment Capacities:**
    *   Max Load (Weight) $W_{max}$:
        *   $W_{F} = 2000$ t
        *   $W_{M} = 3000$ t
        *   $W_{R} = 1500$ t
    *   Max Volume $V_{max}$:
        *   $V_{F} = 4000$ $m^3$
        *   $V_{M} = 5000$ $m^3$
        *   $V_{R} = 1500$ $m^3$

*   **Cargo Characteristics:**
    *   Available Quantity $Q_c$ (pieces):
        *   $Q_A = 600$
        *   $Q_B = 1000$
        *   $Q_C = 800$
    *   Volume per piece $v_c$ ($m^3$/piece):
        *   $v_A = 10$
        *   $v_B = 5$
        *   $v_C = 7$
    *   Weight per piece $w_c$ (t/piece):
        *   $w_A = 8$
        *   $w_B = 6$
        *   $w_C = 4$
    *   Freight Rate (Revenue) $r_c$ (dollars/piece):
        *   $r_A = 1000$
        *   $r_B = 700$
        *   $r_C = 600$

*   **Balance Ratios (Derived from Max Loads):**
    *   Reference Ratio Front/Middle: $R_{FM} = W_F / W_M = 2000 / 3000 = 2/3$
    *   Reference Ratio Rear/Middle: $R_{RM} = W_R / W_M = 1500 / 3000 = 1/2$
    *   Reference Ratio Front/Rear: $R_{FR} = W_F / W_R = 2000 / 1500 = 4/3$

*   **Deviation Limits:**
    *   Limit for Front/Middle and Rear/Middle: $\delta_1 = 15\% = 0.15$
    *   Limit for Front/Rear: $\delta_2 = 10\% = 0.10$

### 2. Decision Variables

Let $x_{c,k}$ be the number of pieces of cargo type $c$ loaded in compartment $k$.

*   **Domain:** $x_{c,k} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for all $c \in C, k \in K$.
    *(Note: Cargo is measured in "pieces," which are discrete units. Therefore, integer variables are required.)*

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize total revenue:

$$
\text{MAXIMIZE } Z = \sum_{c \in C} \sum_{k \in K} r_c \cdot x_{c,k}
$$

Expanded:
$$
Z = 1000(x_{A,F} + x_{A,M} + x_{A,R}) + 700(x_{B,F} + x_{B,M} + x_{B,R}) + 600(x_{C,F} + x_{C,M} + x_{C,R})
$$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**A. Volume Constraints**
The total volume of cargo in each compartment must not exceed the compartment's volume capacity.

$$
\sum_{c \in C} v_c \cdot x_{c,k} \le V_{max,k} \quad \forall k \in K
$$

Explicitly:
1.  **Front:** $10 x_{A,F} + 5 x_{B,F} + 7 x_{C,F} \le 4000$
2.  **Middle:** $10 x_{A,M} + 5 x_{B,M} + 7 x_{C,M} \le 5000$
3.  **Rear:** $10 x_{A,R} + 5 x_{B,R} + 7 x_{C,R} \le 1500$

**B. Weight Constraints (Capacity)**
The total weight of cargo in each compartment must not exceed the compartment's maximum allowable load.

$$
\sum_{c \in C} w_c \cdot x_{c,k} \le W_{max,k} \quad \forall k \in K
$$

Explicitly:
1.  **Front:** $8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F} \le 2000$
2.  **Middle:** $8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M} \le 3000$
3.  **Rear:** $8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R} \le 1500$

**C. Cargo Availability Constraints**
The total number of pieces of each cargo type loaded across all compartments cannot exceed the available supply.

$$
\sum_{k \in K} x_{c,k} \le Q_c \quad \forall c \in C
$$

Explicitly:
1.  **Cargo A:** $x_{A,F} + x_{A,M} + x_{A,R} \le 600$
2.  **Cargo B:** $x_{B,F} + x_{B,M} + x_{B,R} \le 1000$
3.  **Cargo C:** $x_{C,F} + x_{C,M} + x_{C,R} \le 800$

**D. Maritime Safety / Balance Constraints**
Let $W_F, W_M, W_R$ be the actual total weights in Front, Middle, and Rear compartments respectively.
$W_F = 8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}$
$W_M = 8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}$
$W_R = 8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}$

The problem states that the ratio of actual loads must not deviate by more than the specified percentage from the ratio of maximum allowable loads.

1.  **Front vs. Middle (15% deviation from $2/3$):**
    $$
    (1 - 0.15) \cdot \frac{2}{3} W_M \le W_F \le (1 + 0.15) \cdot \frac{2}{3} W_M
    $$
    $$
    0.85 \cdot \frac{2}{3} W_M \le W_F \le 1.15 \cdot \frac{2}{3} W_M
    $$
    $$
    \frac{17}{30} W_M \le W_F \le \frac{23}{30} W_M
    $$
    
    Linearized Constraints:
    *   $W_F - \frac{23}{30} W_M \le 0$  (or $30 W_F - 23 W_M \le 0$)
    *   $W_F - \frac{17}{30} W_M \ge 0$  (or $30 W_F - 17 W_M \ge 0$)

2.  **Rear vs. Middle (15% deviation from $1/2$):**
    $$
    (1 - 0.15) \cdot \frac{1}{2} W_M \le W_R \le (1 + 0.15) \cdot \frac{1}{2} W_M
    $$
    $$
    0.85 \cdot \frac{1}{2} W_M \le W_R \le 1.15 \cdot \frac{1}{2} W_M
    $$
    $$
    \frac{17}{40} W_M \le W_R \le \frac{23}{40} W_M
    $$
    
    Linearized Constraints:
    *   $W_R - \frac{23}{40} W_M \le 0$ (or $40 W_R - 23 W_M \le 0$)
    *   $W_R - \frac{17}{40} W_M \ge 0$ (or $40 W_R - 17 W_M \ge 0$)

3.  **Front vs. Rear (10% deviation from $4/3$):**
    $$
    (1 - 0.10) \cdot \frac{4}{3} W_R \le W_F \le (1 + 0.10) \cdot \frac{4}{3} W_R
    $$
    $$
    0.90 \cdot \frac{4}{3} W_R \le W_F \le 1.10 \cdot \frac{4}{3} W_R
    $$
    $$
    1.2 W_R \le W_F \le 1.466... W_R \quad (\text{using fractions: } \frac{6}{5} W_R \le W_F \le \frac{11}{15} \cdot 4 W_R = \frac{44}{15} W_R)
    $$
    Let's keep exact fractions:
    Lower bound: $0.9 \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{9}{10} \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{36}{30} = \frac{6}{5} = 1.2$
    Upper bound: $1.1 \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{11}{10} \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{44}{30} = \frac{22}{15} \approx 1.467$
    
    Linearized Constraints:
    *   $W_F - \frac{22}{15} W_R \le 0$ (or $15 W_F - 22 W_R \le 0$)
    *   $W_F - \frac{6}{5} W_R \ge 0$ (or $15 W_F - 18 W_R \ge 0$)

**Substituting Weight Definitions into Balance Constraints:**

1.  $30(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 23(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \le 0$
2.  $30(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 17(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \ge 0$
3.  $40(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) - 23(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \le 0$
4.  $40(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) - 17(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \ge 0$
5.  $15(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 22(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) \le 0$
6.  $15(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 18(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) \ge 0$

**E. Non-negativity and Integrality**
$$
x_{c,k} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0} \quad \forall c \in \{A, B, C\}, k \in \{F, M, R\}
$$
