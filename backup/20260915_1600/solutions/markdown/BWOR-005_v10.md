# Linear Programming Formulation

## Model Type Declaration
**LP (Linear Programming)**
All decision variables are continuous (representing aggregate quantities of cargo pieces; integrality of "pieces" is handled implicitly as the optimal solution will naturally be near-integer or can be rounded in practice, and the problem asks for a linear formulation). The objective and all constraints are linear.

---

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $I = \{A, B, C\}$: Set of cargo types
- $J = \{F, M, R\}$: Set of compartments (Front, Middle, Rear)

**Parameters (from Table 1-24 and 1-25):**

| Parameter | Description | Value |
|---|---|---|
| $W_{max,F}$ | Max allowable load, Front (t) | 2000 |
| $W_{max,M}$ | Max allowable load, Middle (t) | 3000 |
| $W_{max,R}$ | Max allowable load, Rear (t) | 1500 |
| $V_{max,F}$ | Max volume, Front ($m^3$) | 4000 |
| $V_{max,M}$ | Max volume, Middle ($m^3$) | 5000 |
| $V_{max,R}$ | Max volume, Rear ($m^3$) | 1500 |
| $Q_A$ | Total available quantity, Cargo A (pieces) | 600 |
| $Q_B$ | Total available quantity, Cargo B (pieces) | 1000 |
| $Q_C$ | Total available quantity, Cargo C (pieces) | 800 |
| $v_A, v_B, v_C$ | Volume per piece ($m^3$/piece) | 10, 5, 7 |
| $w_A, w_B, w_C$ | Weight per piece (t/piece) | 8, 6, 4 |
| $p_A, p_B, p_C$ | Freight rate (dollar/piece) | 1000, 700, 600 |

**Balance Parameters (derived from max allowable loads):**
- Target ratio Front/Middle: $\frac{W_{max,F}}{W_{max,M}} = \frac{2000}{3000} = \frac{2}{3}$
- Target ratio Rear/Middle: $\frac{W_{max,R}}{W_{max,M}} = \frac{1500}{3000} = \frac{1}{2}$
- Target ratio Front/Rear: $\frac{W_{max,F}}{W_{max,R}} = \frac{2000}{1500} = \frac{4}{3}$
- Deviation tolerance for F/M and R/M: 15%
- Deviation tolerance for F/R: 10%

---

## Decision Variables

Let $x_{i,j}$ be the number of pieces of cargo type $i$ loaded in compartment $j$.

- $x_{A,F}, x_{B,F}, x_{C,F}$: CONTINUOUS, $\geq 0$ — Pieces of cargo A, B, C in Front
- $x_{A,M}, x_{B,M}, x_{C,M}$: CONTINUOUS, $\geq 0$ — Pieces of cargo A, B, C in Middle
- $x_{A,R}, x_{B,R}, x_{C,R}$: CONTINUOUS, $\geq 0$ — Pieces of cargo A, B, C in Rear

*(9 non-negative continuous variables total)*

---

## Objective Function

**MAXIMIZE** Total Revenue:

$$\text{Maximize } Z = 1000(x_{A,F} + x_{A,M} + x_{A,R}) + 700(x_{B,F} + x_{B,M} + x_{B,R}) + 600(x_{C,F} + x_{C,M} + x_{C,R})$$

Or equivalently, in summation form:
$$\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} p_i \cdot x_{i,j}$$

---

## Operational Constraints

### 1. Cargo Availability Constraints (Supply Limits)
Each cargo type cannot be loaded in excess of its total available quantity:

$$x_{A,F} + x_{A,M} + x_{A,R} \leq 600$$
$$x_{B,F} + x_{B,M} + x_{B,R} \leq 1000$$
$$x_{C,F} + x_{C,M} + x_{C,R} \leq 800$$

### 2. Compartment Volume Constraints
Total volume of cargo in each compartment cannot exceed the compartment's volume capacity:

**Front:**
$$10 x_{A,F} + 5 x_{B,F} + 7 x_{C,F} \leq 4000$$

**Middle:**
$$10 x_{A,M} + 5 x_{B,M} + 7 x_{C,M} \leq 5000$$

**Rear:**
$$10 x_{A,R} + 5 x_{B,R} + 7 x_{C,R} \leq 1500$$

### 3. Compartment Weight (Load) Constraints
Total weight of cargo in each compartment cannot exceed the compartment's maximum allowable load:

**Front:**
$$8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F} \leq 2000$$

**Middle:**
$$8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M} \leq 3000$$

**Rear:**
$$8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R} \leq 1500$$

### 4. Load Balance Constraints

Let $W_F$, $W_M$, $W_R$ denote the actual total weights in Front, Middle, and Rear compartments:
$$W_F = 8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}$$
$$W_M = 8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}$$
$$W_R = 8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}$$

**Constraint 4a: Front/Middle ratio deviation ≤ 15%**

Target ratio: $W_F / W_M = 2/3$. Deviation of at most 15% means:
$$\frac{W_F}{W_M} \in \left[\frac{2}{3}(1-0.15),\; \frac{2}{3}(1+0.15)\right] = \left[\frac{2}{3} \times 0.85,\; \frac{2}{3} \times 1.15\right] = \left[\frac{17}{45},\; \frac{23}{45}\right]$$

This gives two linear inequalities (assuming $W_M > 0$):
$$\frac{W_F}{W_M} \geq \frac{17}{45} \implies 45 W_F - 17 W_M \geq 0$$
$$\frac{W_F}{W_M} \leq \frac{23}{45} \implies 45 W_F - 23 W_M \leq 0$$

Substituting the weight expressions:
$$45(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 17(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \geq 0$$
$$45(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 23(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \leq 0$$

**Constraint 4b: Rear/Middle ratio deviation ≤ 15%**

Target ratio: $W_R / W_M = 1/2$. Deviation of at most 15% means:
$$\frac{W_R}{W_M} \in \left[\frac{1}{2}(1-0.15),\; \frac{1}{2}(1+0.15)\right] = \left[0.425,\; 0.575\right] = \left[\frac{17}{40},\; \frac{23}{40}\right]$$

This gives two linear inequalities:
$$\frac{W_R}{W_M} \geq \frac{17}{40} \implies 40 W_R - 17 W_M \geq 0$$
$$\frac{W_R}{W_M} \leq \frac{23}{40} \implies 40 W_R - 23 W_M \leq 0$$

Substituting:
$$40(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) - 17(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \geq 0$$
$$40(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) - 23(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \leq 0$$

**Constraint 4c: Front/Rear ratio deviation ≤ 10%**

Target ratio: $W_F / W_R = 4/3$. Deviation of at most 10% means:
$$\frac{W_F}{W_R} \in \left[\frac{4}{3}(1-0.10),\; \frac{4}{3}(1+0.10)\right] = \left[\frac{4}{3} \times 0.9,\; \frac{4}{3} \times 1.1\right] = \left[1.2,\; \frac{22}{15}\right]$$

This gives two linear inequalities (assuming $W_R > 0$):
$$\frac{W_F}{W_R} \geq 1.2 = \frac{6}{5} \implies 5 W_F - 6 W_R \geq 0$$
$$\frac{W_F}{W_R} \leq \frac{22}{15} \implies 15 W_F - 22 W_R \leq 0$$

Substituting:
$$5(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 6(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) \geq 0$$
$$15(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 22(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) \leq 0$$

## Non-Negativity

$$x_{i,j} \geq 0 \quad \forall\, i \in I,\; j \in J$$

## Feasibility Sanity Check

- **Total available volume:** $600 \times 10 + 1000 \times 5 + 800 \times 7 = 6000 + 5000 + 5600 = 16600\; m^3$
- **Total compartment volume:** $4000 + 5000 + 1500 = 10500\; m^3$
- **Total available weight:** $600 \times 8 + 1000 \times 6 + 800 \times 4 = 4800 + 6000 + 3200 = 14000\; t$
- **Total compartment weight capacity:** $2000 + 3000 + 1500 = 6500\; t$

The weight capacity (6500 t) is the binding constraint (much less than available weight of 14000 t), so not all cargo can be loaded. The balance constraints further restrict how the weight is distributed. The model is feasible (e.g., a trivial solution with very small balanced loads exists), and the problem is well-posed.
