Model Type: LP

### Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $S = \{I, II\}$: Set of power stations.
- $C = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of cities.

**Parameters:**
- $Cap_s$: Maximum supply capacity of station $s$ (units).
  - $Cap_I = 400$
  - $Cap_{II} = 450$
- $D_c^{min}$: Minimum required supply for city $c$ (units).
  - $D_1^{min} = 290$ (320 - 30)
  - $D_2^{min} = 250$ (Fixed)
  - $D_3^{min} = 270$
- $D_c^{max}$: Maximum allowable supply for city $c$ (units).
  - $D_1^{max} = 320$
  - $D_2^{max} = 250$
  - $D_3^{max} = 350$
- $C_{s,c}$: Unit cost of supplying electricity from station $s$ to city $c$ (Yuan/unit).
  - $C_{I,1} = 15, C_{I,2} = 18, C_{I,3} = 22$
  - $C_{II,1} = 21, C_{II,2} = 25, C_{II,3} = 16$

*Note on Total Supply:* The problem states "uses up all available electricity from both stations". Total Capacity = $400 + 450 = 850$ units.
*Note on Total Demand Bounds:*
Min Demand = $290 + 250 + 270 = 810$ units.
Max Demand = $320 + 250 + 350 = 920$ units.
Since Total Supply (850) is within the range [810, 920], a feasible solution exists.

### Decision Variables

Let $x_{s,c}$ be the amount of electricity supplied from station $s$ to city $c$ (units).
Domain: $x_{s,c} \geq 0$ (Continuous) for all $s \in S, c \in C$.

### Objective Function

Minimize Total Cost $Z$:
$$ \text{Minimize } Z = 15 x_{I,1} + 18 x_{I,2} + 22 x_{I,3} + 21 x_{II,1} + 25 x_{II,2} + 16 x_{II,3} $$

### Operational Constraints

**1. Supply Constraints (Use all available electricity):**
The problem explicitly states the plan must "use up all available electricity from both stations". Therefore, the supply from each station must equal its maximum capacity.
$$ x_{I,1} + x_{I,2} + x_{I,3} = 400 $$
$$ x_{II,1} + x_{II,2} + x_{II,3} = 450 $$

**2. Demand Constraints (City specific limits):**
- **City 1:** Supply can be reduced by up to 30 units from 320. Range: [290, 320].
  $$ 290 \leq x_{I,1} + x_{II,1} \leq 320 $$
- **City 2:** Supply must remain fixed at 250 units.
  $$ x_{I,2} + x_{II,2} = 250 $$
- **City 3:** Supply must be at least 270 units, up to 350 units. Range: [270, 350].
  $$ 270 \leq x_{I,3} + x_{II,3} \leq 350 $$

**3. Non-negativity:**
$$ x_{s,c} \geq 0 \quad \forall s \in S, c \in C $$
