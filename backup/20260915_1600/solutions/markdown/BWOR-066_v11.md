Model Type: MILP

### 1. Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $k$: Set of months, $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.

**Parameters:**
*   $I_0 = 500$: Initial inventory at the start of Month 1 (units).
*   $C = 1000$: Maximum warehouse capacity (units).
*   $b_k$: Buying price in month $k$ (per unit).
    *   $b_1 = 10$
    *   $b_2 = 9$
    *   $b_3 = 11$
    *   $b_4 = 15$
*   $s_k$: Selling price in month $k$ (per unit).
    *   $s_1 = 12$
    *   $s_2 = 9$
    *   $s_3 = 13$
    *   $s_4 = 17$

### 2. Decision Variables

*   $x_k$: Quantity of product purchased in month $k$.
    *   Domain: $x_k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.
*   $y_k$: Quantity of product sold in month $k$.
    *   Domain: $y_k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.
*   $I_k$: Inventory level at the **end** of month $k$.
    *   Domain: $I_k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.

*(Note: Variables are integers because the problem states "product quantities are in integers".)*

### 3. Objective Function

Maximize total profit over the 4 months. Profit is defined as total revenue from sales minus total cost of purchases.

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{k=1}^{4} (s_k y_k - b_k x_k)
$$

Substituting the specific prices:
$$
\text{Maximize } Z = (12 y_1 - 10 x_1) + (9 y_2 - 9 x_2) + (13 y_3 - 11 x_3) + (17 y_4 - 15 x_4)
$$

### 4. Operational Constraints

**Inventory Balance Constraints:**
The inventory at the end of month $k$ is the inventory from the previous month plus purchases made in month $k$ (which are available starting the *following* month, so they do not contribute to current month sales, but they do enter the warehouse balance) minus sales in month $k$.

*Clarification on Timing:*
The problem states: "If the store decides to purchase goods in a given month, those goods will only be available starting the following month."
This implies that $x_k$ (purchases in month $k$) cannot be sold in month $k$. They are added to the inventory at the end of month $k$ (or beginning of month $k+1$) and can be sold in month $k+1$ or later.

Let's define the flow carefully:
*   Start of Month 1: Inventory $I_{start,1} = 500$.
*   During Month 1: We can sell $y_1$ from the starting inventory. We purchase $x_1$.
*   End of Month 1: Inventory $I_1 = I_{start,1} - y_1 + x_1$.
    *   Constraint: $I_1 = 500 - y_1 + x_1$
*   Start of Month 2: Inventory $I_1$ is available.
*   During Month 2: We can sell $y_2$ from $I_1$. We purchase $x_2$.
*   End of Month 2: Inventory $I_2 = I_1 - y_2 + x_2$.
    *   Constraint: $I_2 = I_1 - y_2 + x_2$
*   Start of Month 3: Inventory $I_2$ is available.
*   During Month 3: We can sell $y_3$ from $I_2$. We purchase $x_3$.
*   End of Month 3: Inventory $I_3 = I_2 - y_3 + x_3$.
    *   Constraint: $I_3 = I_2 - y_3 + x_3$
*   Start of Month 4: Inventory $I_3$ is available.
*   During Month 4: We can sell $y_4$ from $I_3$. We purchase $x_4$.
*   End of Month 4: Inventory $I_4 = I_3 - y_4 + x_4$.
    *   Constraint: $I_4 = I_3 - y_4 + x_4$

**Sales Availability Constraints:**
The store can only sell what is currently in the warehouse each month.
*   Month 1: Sales $y_1$ cannot exceed initial inventory.
    $$y_1 \le 500$$
*   Month 2: Sales $y_2$ cannot exceed inventory from end of Month 1.
    $$y_2 \le I_1$$
*   Month 3: Sales $y_3$ cannot exceed inventory from end of Month 2.
    $$y_3 \le I_2$$
*   Month 4: Sales $y_4$ cannot exceed inventory from end of Month 3.
    $$y_4 \le I_3$$

*Note: The balance equations $I_k = I_{k-1} - y_k + x_k$ combined with $I_k \ge 0$ implicitly ensure that $y_k \le I_{k-1}$ (for $k>1$) and $y_1 \le 500$, provided we define the "available inventory" correctly. However, it is safer to be explicit or ensure the balance equations are structured such that sales are deducted from the *previous* period's closing inventory. Let's refine the balance equations to be standard:*

Standard Inventory Balance:
$I_k = I_{k-1} + x_k - y_k$
Where $I_0 = 500$.
This equation implies:
1.  $I_1 = 500 + x_1 - y_1$. Since $I_1 \ge 0$, we have $y_1 \le 500 + x_1$. **Wait.**
    Does $x_1$ become available for sale in Month 1?
    The problem says: "If the store decides to purchase goods in a given month, those goods will only be available starting the following month."
    Therefore, $x_1$ is **NOT** available for sale in Month 1.
    So, $y_1$ must be satisfied by $I_0$ only.
    Consequently, $I_1$ (inventory at end of Month 1) is composed of remaining initial stock plus new purchases $x_1$.
    $I_1 = I_0 - y_1 + x_1$.
    Since $y_1 \le I_0$, $I_1 \ge x_1 \ge 0$ is naturally satisfied if we enforce $y_1 \le I_0$.
    
    Let's check Month 2.
    Available inventory at start of Month 2 is $I_1$.
    $y_2$ is sold from $I_1$.
    $x_2$ is purchased and available from Month 3.
    $I_2 = I_1 - y_2 + x_2$.
    Constraint: $y_2 \le I_1$.

    So the constraints are:
    1.  $y_1 \le 500$
    2.  $I_1 = 500 - y_1 + x_1$
    3.  $y_2 \le I_1$
    4.  $I_2 = I_1 - y_2 + x_2$
    5.  $y_3 \le I_2$
    6.  $I_3 = I_2 - y_3 + x_3$
    7.  $y_4 \le I_3$
    8.  $I_4 = I_3 - y_4 + x_4$

**Capacity Constraints:**
The warehouse can hold up to 1,000 units. This applies to the inventory at any point. The maximum inventory occurs at the end of the month (after adding purchases) or beginning of the month. Since purchases are added at the end and sales happen during the month, the peak is likely end-of-month.
$I_k \le 1000$ for $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.
Also, initial inventory $I_0 = 500 \le 1000$.

**Non-negativity and Integrality:**
$x_k, y_k, I_k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for all $k$.

### Final Formulation Summary

**Variables:**
$x_k, y_k, I_k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, \quad k=1,2,3,4$

**Objective:**
$$ \text{Maximize } Z = 2 y_1 + 0 y_2 + 2 y_3 + 2 y_4 - 10 x_1 - 9 x_2 - 11 x_3 - 15 x_4 $$
*(Simplified: Profit per unit sold in M1 is $12-10=2$? No. Cost is buying price. Revenue is selling price. Profit for unit bought in M1 and sold in M1 is $12-10=2$. Profit for unit bought in M1 and sold in M2 is $9-10=-1$. The objective function linearity handles the assignment automatically via the balance constraints. We simply sum Revenue - Cost.)*
$$ Z = \sum_{k=1}^4 s_k y_k - \sum_{k=1}^4 b_k x_k $$

**Constraints:**
1.  **Sales Limits (Availability):**
    $$ y_1 \le 500 $$
    $$ y_2 \le I_1 $$
    $$ y_3 \le I_2 $$
    $$ y_4 \le I_3 $$

2.  **Inventory Balance:**
    $$ I_1 = 500 - y_1 + x_1 $$
    $$ I_2 = I_1 - y_2 + x_2 $$
    $$ I_3 = I_2 - y_3 + x_3 $$
    $$ I_4 = I_3 - y_4 + x_4 $$

3.  **Capacity Limits:**
    $$ I_1 \le 1000 $$
    $$ I_2 \le 1000 $$
    $$ I_3 \le 1000 $$
    $$ I_4 \le 1000 $$

4.  **Bounds:**
    $$ x_k \ge 0, \quad y_k \ge 0, \quad I_k \ge 0 \quad \text{for } k=1,2,3,4 $$
    $$ x_k, y_k, I_k \in \mathbb{Z} $$
