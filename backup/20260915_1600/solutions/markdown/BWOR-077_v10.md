## Model Type
**MILP** (Mixed-Integer Linear Program)

## Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
- $W = \{A, B, C, D, E\}$: Set of workers, $|W| = 5$
- $S = \{I, II, III, IV, V\}$: Set of assembly line stations, $|S| = 5$

**Parameters (Efficiency in pieces/min):**

| Worker \ Station | I | II | III | IV | V |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **A** | 2 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 7 |
| **B** | 3 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 6 |
| **C** | 2 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 1 |
| **D** | 5 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 5 |
| **E** | 3 | 7 | 6 | 2 | 4 |

**Key Modeling Note (Rule 15 - Bottleneck/Min-Max):**
The problem asks to maximize the **overall production capacity** of a **serial assembly line**. In a serial system, the overall throughput is limited by the slowest station (the bottleneck). Therefore, the objective is to maximize the *minimum* efficiency across all stations, not the sum.

## Decision Variables

1.  $x_{w,s}$: Binary variable, where:
    -   $x_{w,s} = 1$ if worker $w$ is assigned to station $s$
    -   $x_{w,s} = 0$ otherwise
    -   Domain: $x_{w,s} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall w \in W, s \in S$

2.  $Z$: Continuous variable representing the overall production capacity (bottleneck rate) of the assembly line.
    -   Domain: $Z \in \mathbb{R}, Z \geq 0$

## Objective Function

Maximize the overall production capacity (bottleneck rate):

$$ \text{MAXIMIZE} \quad Z $$

## Operational Constraints

**1. Assignment Constraints (Each worker assigned to exactly one station):**
$$ \sum_{s \in S} x_{w,s} = 1 \quad \forall w \in W $$

**2. Station Coverage Constraints (Each station operated by exactly one worker):**
$$ \sum_{w \in W} x_{w,s} = 1 \quad \forall s \in S $$

**3. Bottleneck Definition Constraints (Z is bounded by the efficiency of each station):**
For the overall capacity $Z$ to be achievable, the efficiency of *every* station must be at least $Z$. The efficiency of station $s$ is determined by the worker assigned to it.

$$ Z \leq \sum_{w \in W} e_{w,s} \cdot x_{w,s} \quad \forall s \in S $$

Where $e_{w,s}$ is the efficiency of worker $w$ at station $s$ (values from Table 8-10).

*Explicitly, these are:*
-   $Z \leq 2x_{A,I} + 3x_{B,I} + 2x_{C,I} + 5x_{D,I} + 3x_{E,I}$  (Station I)
-   $Z \leq 3x_{A,II} + 4x_{B,II} + 5x_{C,II} + 2x_{D,II} + 7x_{E,II}$ (Station II)
-   $Z \leq 4x_{A,III} + 2x_{B,III} + 3x_{C,III} + 3x_{D,III} + 6x_{E,III}$ (Station III)
-   $Z \leq 1x_{A,IV} + 5x_{B,IV} + 4x_{C,IV} + 2x_{D,IV} + 2x_{E,IV}$ (Station IV)
-   $Z \leq 7x_{A,V} + 6x_{B,V} + 1x_{C,V} + 5x_{D,V} + 4x_{E,V}$   (Station V)

**4. Non-negativity/Domain Bounds:**
$$ x_{w,s} \geq 0 \quad \forall w \in W, s \in S $$
$$ Z \geq 0 $$
*(Note: Since $x$ is binary and efficiencies are positive, $Z$ will naturally be positive.)*
