**Model Type:** MILP (Mixed Integer Linear Programming)

**Note on Integer Variables:** Since cargo is shipped in discrete "pieces" (countable units), the decision variables representing the number of pieces of each cargo type in each compartment must be integers.

### Index Sets & Parameters

**Index Sets:**
*   $K = \{1, 2, 3\}$: Set of cargo types, where $1=A, 2=B, 3=C$.
*   $C = \{F, M, R\}$: Set of ship compartments, where $F=\text{Front}, M=\text{Middle}, R=\text{Rear}$.

**Parameters (from Tables 1-24 and 1-25):**

*   $CapLoad_c$: Maximum allowable load (weight) for compartment $c$ (tons).
    *   $CapLoad_F = 2000$
    *   $CapLoad_M = 3000$
    *   $CapLoad_R = 1500$
*   $CapVol_c$: Maximum allowable volume for compartment $c$ ($m^3$).
    *   $CapVol_F = 4000$
    *   $CapVol_M = 5000$
    *   $CapVol_R = 1500$
*   $Qty_k$: Total available quantity of cargo type $k$ (pieces).
    *   $Qty_A = 600$
    *   $Qty_B = 1000$
    *   $Qty_C = 800$
*   $Vol_k$: Volume per piece of cargo type $k$ ($m^3$/piece).
    *   $Vol_A = 10$
    *   $Vol_B = 5$
    *   $Vol_C = 7$
*   $Wt_k$: Weight per piece of cargo type $k$ (t/piece).
    *   $Wt_A = 8$
    *   $Wt_B = 6$
    *   $Wt_C = 4$
*   $Price_k$: Freight rate (revenue) per piece of cargo type $k$ (dollars/piece).
    *   $Price_A = 1000$
    *   $Price_B = 700$
    *   $Price_C = 600$

**Derived Balance Parameters:**
*   Target Ratio Front/Middle: $r_{FM} = \frac{CapLoad_F}{CapLoad_M} = \frac{2000}{3000} = \frac{2}{3}$
*   Target Ratio Rear/Middle: $r_{RM} = \frac{CapLoad_R}{CapLoad_M} = \frac{1500}{3000} = \frac{1}{2}$
*   Target Ratio Front/Rear: $r_{FR} = \frac{CapLoad_F}{CapLoad_R} = \frac{2000}{1500} = \frac{4}{3}$
*   Deviation Tolerance $\delta_{FM} = 0.15$
*   Deviation Tolerance $\delta_{RM} = 0.15$
*   Deviation Tolerance $\delta_{FR} = 0.10$

### Decision Variables

Let $x_{k,c}$ be the number of pieces of cargo type $k$ loaded in compartment $c$.

*   $x_{k,c} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ for all $k \in K, c \in C$.

### Objective Function

Maximize total revenue:

$$ \text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{c \in C} Price_k \cdot x_{k,c} $$

Substituting values:
$$ Z = 1000 \sum_{c} x_{A,c} + 700 \sum_{c} x_{B,c} + 600 \sum_{c} x_{C,c} $$

### Operational Constraints

**1. Supply Constraints (Total Loaded $\le$ Available Quantity)**
The total number of pieces of each cargo type loaded across all compartments cannot exceed the available supply.

*   $\sum_{c \in C} x_{A,c} \le 600$
*   $\sum_{c \in C} x_{B,c} \le 1000$
*   $\sum_{c \in C} x_{C,c} \le 800$

**2. Volume Capacity Constraints**
The total volume of cargo in each compartment must not exceed the compartment's volume capacity.

*   $10 x_{A,F} + 5 x_{B,F} + 7 x_{C,F} \le 4000$  (Front)
*   $10 x_{A,M} + 5 x_{B,M} + 7 x_{C,M} \le 5000$  (Middle)
*   $10 x_{A,R} + 5 x_{B,R} + 7 x_{C,R} \le 1500$  (Rear)

**3. Weight Capacity Constraints**
The total weight of cargo in each compartment must not exceed the compartment's maximum allowable load.

*   $8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F} \le 2000$  (Front)
*   $8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M} \le 3000$  (Middle)
*   $8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R} \le 1500$  (Rear)

**4. Maritime Safety Balance Constraints**
Let $W_F, W_M, W_R$ be the actual total weights in Front, Middle, and Rear compartments respectively.
$W_F = 8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}$
$W_M = 8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}$
$W_R = 8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}$

The constraints are derived from the deviation requirements:

*   **Front/Middle Deviation $\le 15\%$:**
    $$ \left| \frac{W_F}{W_M} - \frac{2}{3} \right| \le 0.15 \cdot \frac{2}{3} $$
    $$ \frac{2}{3}(1 - 0.15) \le \frac{W_F}{W_M} \le \frac{2}{3}(1 + 0.15) $$
    $$ 0.85 \cdot \frac{2}{3} W_M \le W_F \le 1.15 \cdot \frac{2}{3} W_M $$
    
    Linearizing (multiplying by $W_M > 0$):
    1.  $W_F - \frac{17}{30} W_M \ge 0 \implies 30 W_F - 17 W_M \ge 0$
    2.  $W_F - \frac{23}{30} W_M \le 0 \implies 30 W_F - 23 W_M \le 0$

    Substituting $W_F, W_M$ with linear expressions in $x$:
    1.  $30(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 17(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \ge 0$
    2.  $30(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 23(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \le 0$

*   **Rear/Middle Deviation $\le 15\%$:**
    $$ \left| \frac{W_R}{W_M} - \frac{1}{2} \right| \le 0.15 \cdot \frac{1}{2} $$
    $$ 0.85 \cdot \frac{1}{2} W_M \le W_R \le 1.15 \cdot \frac{1}{2} W_M $$
    
    Linearizing:
    1.  $W_R - \frac{17}{40} W_M \ge 0 \implies 40 W_R - 17 W_M \ge 0$
    2.  $W_R - \frac{23}{40} W_M \le 0 \implies 40 W_R - 23 W_M \le 0$

    Substituting $W_R, W_M$:
    1.  $40(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) - 17(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \ge 0$
    2.  $40(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) - 23(8 x_{A,M} + 6 x_{B,M} + 4 x_{C,M}) \le 0$

*   **Front/Rear Deviation $\le 10\%$:**
    $$ \left| \frac{W_F}{W_R} - \frac{4}{3} \right| \le 0.10 \cdot \frac{4}{3} $$
    $$ 0.90 \cdot \frac{4}{3} W_R \le W_F \le 1.10 \cdot \frac{4}{3} W_R $$
    
    Calculating bounds:
    Lower: $0.9 \cdot \frac{4}{3} = 1.2 = \frac{6}{5}$
    Upper: $1.1 \cdot \frac{4}{3} = \frac{44}{30} = \frac{22}{15}$

    Linearizing:
    1.  $W_F - \frac{6}{5} W_R \ge 0 \implies 5 W_F - 6 W_R \ge 0$
    2.  $W_F - \frac{22}{15} W_R \le 0 \implies 15 W_F - 22 W_R \le 0$

    Substituting $W_F, W_R$:
    1.  $5(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 6(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) \ge 0$
    2.  $15(8 x_{A,F} + 6 x_{B,F} + 4 x_{C,F}) - 22(8 x_{A,R} + 6 x_{B,R} + 4 x_{C,R}) \le 0$

**5. Non-negativity and Integrality**
*   $x_{k,c} \ge 0$ and integer for all $k \in \{A, B, C\}, c \in \{F, M, R\}$.
