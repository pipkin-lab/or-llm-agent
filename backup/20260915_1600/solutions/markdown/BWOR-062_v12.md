## Model Type Declaration
**MILP** (Mixed-Integer Linear Program). The decision variables are integer (number of outlets per region), and the objective is linear in indicator variables.

---

## Index Sets & Parameters

- $R = \{1, 2, 3\}$: set of regions, $|R| = 3$
- $K = \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$: possible number of outlets assigned to a region
- $N = 4$: total number of sales outlets to allocate

**Parameters** (monthly profit, in problem's base units):

$p_{r,k}$ = monthly profit from region $r$ when exactly $k$ outlets are assigned:

| $r \backslash k$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 3 | 0 | 10 | 14 | 16 | 17 |

## Decision Variables

For each region $r \in R$ and each possible outlet count $k \in K$:

$$
x_{r,k} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall r \in R,\; k \in K
$$

where $x_{r,k} = 1$ if and only if exactly $k$ outlets are assigned to region $r$, and $0$ otherwise.

**Domain:** Binary (integer) variables. Since outlets are discrete physical entities, integrality is required.

---

## Objective Function

$$
\text{MAXIMIZE} \quad Z = \sum_{r \in R} \sum_{k \in K} p_{r,k} \cdot x_{r,k}
$$

## Operational Constraints

### Constraint 1: Exactly one outlet count per region (mutual exclusivity)

For each region $r \in R$:
$$
\sum_{k \in K} x_{r,k} = 1
$$

Expanded:
- Region 1: $x_{1,0} + x_{1,1} + x_{1,2} + x_{1,3} + x_{1,4} = 1$
- Region 2: $x_{2,0} + x_{2,1} + x_{2,2} + x_{2,3} + x_{2,4} = 1$
- Region 3: $x_{3,0} + x_{3,1} + x_{3,2} + x_{3,3} + x_{3,4} = 1$

### Constraint 2: Total outlets allocated equals 4

$$
\sum_{r \in R} \sum_{k \in K} k \cdot x_{r,k} = 4
$$

Expanded:
$$
0\cdot x_{1,0} + 1\cdot x_{1,1} + 2\cdot x_{1,2} + 3\cdot x_{1,3} + 4\cdot x_{1,4}
+ 0\cdot x_{2,0} + 1\cdot x_{2,1} + 2\cdot x_{2,2} + 3\cdot x_{2,3} + 4\cdot x_{2,4}
+ 0\cdot x_{3,0} + 1\cdot x_{3,1} + 2\cdot x_{3,2} + 3\cdot x_{3,3} + 4\cdot x_{3,4} = 4
$$

### Constraint 3: Binary bounds

$$
x_{r,k} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall r \in R,\; k \in K
$$
